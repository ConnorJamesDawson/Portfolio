# Sazare Ruri — combat capability

**Author-pinned, 17/12 story-time, immediately before the first fight of his
life.** Everything before this was hypothetical. This file is the reference.

---

## 1. The system

**Speed is free. Volume and density cost. The limiter is nature chakra.**

- **Not material.** Confirmed. He does not need a supply of stone.
- **Not time.** Production of a structure is **split-second** and always has been.
- **The four-second course is a bench calibration, not a combat speed** — it is a
  graded article (three deep, fine damping coarse) laid to specification on a
  single grain. It is his test cut, like a machinist's. It is deliberately slow
  because it is a *quality* operation.

**What costs is reinforcing volume with chakra.** A spike under a foot is cheap.
Guren's castle is not, and is a matter of him working out the shape rather than
of ability — he has the shape side solved.

## 2. What he can do

- **Any structure, under a second**, including under somebody without warning.
- **Range: a twenty-foot perimeter, confirmed. Beyond that untested** — untested
  means unknown, not impossible.
- **Remote actuation** of crystal already placed — proven at the invasion.
  Cannibalistic crystal is the extension of it.
- **Medical ninjutsu, sealless.** Nothing blocks him; he ran four years on a
  ward. **Chakra scalpels are his natural aptitude** given his control — a
  precision lethal instrument he has carried since about sixteen and has never
  once classified as one.
- **Wood: existing growth only.** No reference for creation, so no trees. A vine
  on an ankle, a branch that swats. Same principle as everything else — he
  matches a finished article.
- **Chakra reserves: jōnin.** Bloodline-exercised relentlessly, supplemented by
  nature chakra, and his techniques are efficient.
- **Body: genin baseline.** Untrained, competent over roofs. **Sage mode lifts
  everything.**
- **Gather mid-fight: possible, untrained, slow.**

## 3. Hard limits

- No ninjutsu, no genjutsu, no fūinjutsu — the medium is shut.
- No taijutsu training, no weapons training. Never went to an Academy.
- **One shape at a time, held entire, and there is one of him.** A hinge is two
  objects and a relationship; he has no operation for a relationship.
- **He cannot bluff.** No face, no feint, no deception.
- **He has never been hit on purpose.**
- **He has never chosen what to make.** Every article of his life arrived with
  somebody standing at the front of it holding the specification.

## 4. What stops him in a fight

Five, and all five are already true on the page:

1. **He cannot predict a fighter.** He can put a trap anywhere; he cannot tell
   you where the foot will be. He has never watched a person move with intent.
2. **Shadow clones.** Twenty feet against forty of them is nothing, and volume
   costs him while it costs Naruto nothing.
3. **He will not damage a person**, and he knows the hesitation is fatal while it
   is happening.
4. **The first real hit stops everything.**
5. **The sphere is broken at about six** — his ammunition is down, right now,
   because a man said a word to him on a roof.

**Therefore the winning move is the moment a fighter stops being unpredictable,
which is the moment he commits** — a Rasengan charge is a straight line, both
feet, no options. That is a thing a man who has never fought can still read,
because it is the same as an article under load.

## 5. SLUG SAGE MODE — first appearance

**He does not know it exists.** Jiraiya told him on 16/11 that he is *a sage*;
**nobody has ever mentioned a mode** — a state you enter, as distinct from a
thing you carry. So there is nothing for him to attempt.

**Trigger: holding.** Not being knocked into it. The fight drives him into a
static defensive position and there he does the only thing he has ever been
trained to do, which is hold — and holding, sustained, is the gather, and the
gather sustained is the mode. **Nineteen evenings of a sphere and a count.**

**Therefore the mode is the mechanism of the reef.** *The longer he holds a
position the deadlier he is* stops being a description of his tactics and
becomes a description of what is happening to him while he stands there.

**Manifestation: partial. Eyes only** — as with partial toad markings.

- **The eyes go clear.** The light goes all the way in and comes back, which is
  his own vocabulary for a well-ordered article. Opacity is disorder.
- **Plus Katsuyu's markings**, running down from the inner corner of each eye.
- **Consequence: nobody can tell what he is looking at.** The man with no face,
  who four hundred people can read off a wall at forty feet, becomes — for the
  first and only time — completely unreadable. **The lighthouse goes out**, and
  Sakura is the one who notices, because she has a column for it.

## 6. CANNIBALISTIC CRYSTAL — the first sage jutsu

**Crystal is inert.** It draws from the air and it does not grow. **Plants grow,
because a plant is its own intake — the structure is the drawing.** That is the
lesson from Mokuton applied to Shōton, and the mode is what lets him edit the
nature of the material.

**So every fragment is feedstock.** Broken crystal is still his. The ground under
him becomes progressively his, whether he is visible or not.

**This is the reef** — see `arc-shaping.md` §5b: draws → maintains → grows →
seeds, and *a thing that reproduces cannot be recalled.*

## 7. Standing rules that bind him

- **The panel says *unknown*.** The rule is in writing with her name on it: he
  does not draw except to a specification. **The sword does not come out**, and
  the panel still says *unknown* afterwards — question six answered by
  implication rather than statement.
- Never a second blade while one is out.
- Note moves, or the hand does anything at all → close, and find her.
- Governor, eleven clauses, including *if somebody takes it off my body*.
- The creed: *I am not going to get stronger. Find out how it works.*
