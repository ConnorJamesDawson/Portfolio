# Falsified — the ledger of dead rules

**What this file is.** Design rules the story has since falsified, each with
the event, date, and chapter that killed it, and the narrower statement that
is now true. The failure mode this prevents: a chapter reaches for a rule
from an older design file and writes a contradiction, because the rule was
true when written and nobody recorded its death.

**Standing procedure:**
1. **Read this ledger and the warning block in `combat.md` before planning
   or writing any chapter.**
2. **When a chapter falsifies a design rule, add the entry here in the same
   commit as the chapter.** A rule dies on the page; the ledger records the
   death; the old file gets a one-line marker pointing here. Old files are
   not rewritten — they are era documents and their reasoning is still the
   record of how the character got here.
3. In-place markers use the form:
   `> ⚠ FALSIFIED <date> (<chapter>) — see falsified.md #N.`

Story dates: ch40 = 4/2. Byakugō read 12/11. Jiraiya names him a sage 16/11.
ch63 = 16–19/12. ch64 = 20/12. ch65 = 22/12–1/1.

## #1 — "His work dies at six days"

**Dead rule:** crystal degrades; six days and the order slips; universal.
**Lived in:** `world-bible.md` (courses), `arc-shaping.md` (§ containment,
§ two axes, § the rose), early combat notes, and — repeatedly — my drafting.
**Killed by:** the starvation model, pinned across 16–20/12. The blade has
been carried **open** since 18/12 (ch63) with no degradation — 400+ hours by
ch65. Cannibalistic crystal (20/12, ch64) *grows*.
**Now true:** the six-day death is **starvation of a CLOSED article only** —
it spends what it has holding itself together. An open article draws and
does not run out. A cannibalistic article feeds and grows. "Six days" is a
property of **the close**, not of crystal.

## #2 — "Fortifier, not duelist — he never becomes fast, brave, or willing
to trade blows, and loses every fight he didn't choose"

**Dead rule:** the assassin-era thesis, stated as permanent law.
**Lived in:** `crystal-doctrine.md` (header thesis), `world-bible.md`
(combat identity), echoed in `character.md`.
**Killed by:** ch64 (20/12) — eleven exchanges with a jinchūriki, nine
touches taken, four of which could have gone through him, and he kept
working and won. ch65 (22/12–1/1) — author ruling: **the sword exists
precisely to add a duelling dimension**; Jiraiya builds the four-band style
from the second week of January (`combat.md` §8). Author ruling: **ANBU-level
by the time Akatsuki is on screen** — physical training is part of the plan,
not a betrayal of the character.
**Now true:** fortifier **first** — the style is area denial — and a duellist
under five feet **by design**. "Never fast" was always wrong about production
(split-second, see #3) and is now wrong about the body plan. What survives:
he still wins biggest on prepared ground, and he has still never fought
somebody who was lying to him.

## #3 — "Four seconds is his speed; under pressure he gets one course out"

**Dead rule:** production is slow; one course under stress; time-limited.
**Lived in:** `world-bible.md` (the courses, its guards),
`crystal-doctrine.md` rung 1b.
**Killed by:** era — true at ch15, long outgrown; pinned 17/12 in
`combat.md` §1: **the four-second course is a bench calibration**, a graded
three-deep article laid to specification. Production of a structure is
split-second and always has been.
**Now true:** speed is free; volume and density cost; the limiter is nature
chakra. The courses model itself is not dead — it is the correct HISTORY of
how he trained, and multi-course-at-speed is present-day fact.

## #4 — "He cannot compose seals. Ever."

**Dead rule:** composition categorically closed to him.
**Lived in:** old `combat.md` §3b (struck), session drafting.
**Killed by:** ch65 assessment (22/12–1/1) + author correction: a man who
deconstructs to parts becomes one of the best composers. He has composed
from first principles for eleven years — the governor, the keyed store, the
splint protocols. He never composed **in notation** for one reason only:
Urushibara was alive and asking was faster. That supply ended 4/2 (ch40).
**Now true:** he composes. What is still shut: **reading** a composed array
(*I know the alphabet and I cannot read the sentences*) and **hand seals**
(a performance, from memory, at speed — categorically different problem).

## #5 — "He cannot enter sage mode and nobody can teach him"

**Dead rule:** the mode declared unreachable and unteachable.
**Lived in:** `combat.md` §10 first draft (struck), implications in
`arc-shaping.md` §5b.
**Killed by:** ch64 (20/12) — he entered it, ~90 seconds, sustained, eyes
clear with Katsuyu's markings, logged from outside by Sakura, named by
Jiraiya. ch65 sc5 — Katsuyu spent all three of Shikkotsu's wager questions
on **how to train a sage**: the teaching is coming.
**Now true:** entered once, **cannot reproduce on demand yet** — no
procedure, no nameable trigger, no account of the conditions. Myōboku
closes that from the training side; the battery (`combat.md` §10) closes
entry-under-fire from the equipment side. He should end up with both.

## #6 — "Preparation is his Achilles heel"

**Dead rule:** deny him prep time and he is a genin.
**Killed by:** pinned 17/12; demonstrated 20/12. He makes any structure in a
split second, anywhere, without material.
**Now true:** preparation is an **economy**, not a prerequisite — placed
articles actuate at ~1/40, so prepared ground multiplies capacity ~40-fold.
Denying him preparation makes him *expensive*. **The world is the canvas.**

## #7 — "Wood is the slow layer / no wood option in a fight"

**Dead rule:** everything wood-related is off the combat table.
**Killed by:** ch65 ruling (22/12–1/1). Manipulating beats creating is his
whole method, and **existing vegetation is already there** — a vine on an
ankle is instant and near-free.
**Now true:** *creating* wood stays a bench operation (assisted, invited,
the rose). *Using* what grows is a combat layer from day one.

## #8 — "He will not damage a person / the first real hit stops everything /
he has never been hit on purpose"

**Dead rules:** three of the five entries in "what stops him in a fight."
**Killed by:** ch64 (20/12), all three in one afternoon. He put a growing
article through a boy's shin and let it feed, then had to be the one to
close it. He took real hits from somebody trying, and did not stop.
**Now true:** whatever the shin cost him — and it cost him — it is not a
mid-fight stop condition. See `combat.md` §4 for what replaces these.

## #9 — "He has never chosen what to make"

**Dead rule:** every article of his life arrived with somebody standing at
the front of it holding the specification.
**Killed by:** ch64 (20/12). The cannibalistic crystal is the first article
with **nobody at the front of it**. He wrote the specification. That is the
line — not the jutsu, the authorship.

## #10 — "Growth inside a living body — held back, possibly forever"

**Dead rule:** `crystal-doctrine.md` rung 6 — if ever used, once, late, at
enormous cost, reading as the square done to somebody else.
**Killed by:** ch64 (20/12). A live article entered a subject's leg,
parasitic, feeding on the subject's chakra, stopped only by the maker's
close — in a sanctioned spar, with the Hokage watching, and it is in an
incident report written by her.
**Now true:** growth in living tissue is real, used, and on the record —
treated by the story as an **incident**, not a technique in his kit. The
held-back line moves to where it always really was: **petrification of a
person** — the full square. That stays held, possibly forever.

## #11 — "Life in rock is infrastructure and medicine, not a combat power"

**Dead rule:** `arc-shaping.md` — the reef ruled out of combat.
**Killed by:** ch64 (20/12). Its combat debut ended the first fight of his
life: the ground was food and Naruto never once looked at the floor.
**Now true:** it is both. And the combat version is precisely the
incident-report, security-problem, *worth-more-than-the-village* version —
the danger clause (**a thing that reproduces cannot be recalled**) is not
falsified, it is now live ammunition.

## #12 — THE CREED — **REVISED ON THE PAGE, 16/3 (ch74). CLOSED.**

**Dead rule:** *I am not going to get stronger.* (act-4, the creed that
governed the book.)
**Killed by:** ch74 sc2, the north fields, **16/3 at 07:11, witnessed by
Jiraiya and Fukasaku**, entered in all four books on 20/3 with the struck
clause preserved under a single ruled line.
**What it turned out to be:** not humility and not a philosophy — **a
governor, trigger condition CAPACITY**, laid at fifteen on a boat over the
older sentence to shut it. Its author had established at thirteen and a
half that his hands come up *without a decision in them* (the square: the
spike a hundred and forty seconds early, the guards' standing order, four
of his own dead by the timing), and, unable to put a clause on a decision
that never occurs, governed the only reachable variable — how far the
hands could reach.
**Why it dies:** it did not work, and he wrote the proof. 20/12 was the
same mechanism with the clause in force and perfectly obeyed, and capacity
arrived anyway. **What stopped the twentieth being the square was the
close** — eleven years of craft, not weakness. *Staying small does not
make the accident small. It makes the accident unskilled.*
**Now true — the standing text, in the front of four books:**

> *I am going to be strong enough that nobody standing near me has to be
> lucky.*
>
> *Acceptance test: anything I hold, I can say who it is for — out loud,
> to them, on the day they ask. Where I cannot, I say that I cannot, and
> say when I will be able to, and I am held to the date.*
>
> *Find out how it works.*

**Second half untouched** — eleven years old, three of them spent under
the other one, and it never once needed help.
**Consequences that bind later chapters:** (1) growth still reads as loss
— striking it broke a promise to Kohaku, on a date, and Jiraiya explicitly
refused to absolve him and kept the absolution in his own notebook; (2)
**the acceptance test is live and is meant to catch him** — Jiraiya said
so on the day and intends to use it inside the year; (3) Myōboku's rite
applies: it will want doing again when it changes, out loud, dated,
witnessed, **and the old page is never torn out**.

## Production rules changed (author rulings, this pass)

  A tall ceiling, not a quota — chapters end where they end.
- **Before writing any chapter, read:** the `combat.md` warning block, this
  ledger, the latest §C entries in `threads.md`, and the fixed-facts block

## Still standing — verified this audit, do not over-correct

- The limiter is **nature chakra** — not material, not time.
- **Hold ceiling: six minutes** now, fifteen at best. The battery is a fast
  fill, not a bigger tank. Nothing in December raised the ceiling.
- **One shape at a time, built.** Opening is one act on N objects — that is
  why twenty-six at once — but *building* is still one article, held entire.
- The **range price curve** (20ft ordinary → 60ft nothing); placed-article
  actuation at ~1/40 does not rise with distance.
- He **cannot read a composed array**, and **hand seals are shut**.
- **He cannot bluff.** No face, no feint.
- **Shadow clones are still the hard counter** — volume costs him, bodies
  cost Naruto nothing.
- **Battery costs:** days to charge; he is in the circuit on discharge
  (hilt principle); the object is worth more than anything in the village.
- **Nobody can teach him the Shōton itself.** Senjutsu now has teachers, the
  style has Jiraiya, the body has Kakashi — the crystal is still his alone.
- **The crowd wound** — a crowd facing one thing — untested since, and
  standing.
