# Timeline — the standing ledger

**Purpose.** Act 4 lost control of its clock. Eleven months went past in one
sentence at the end of ch33, another eleven in the first line of ch34, three
more between ch36 and ch37, and a sixteen-month surplus arc was planned on top
of that. Twenty-nine of the thirty available months were spent as connective
tissue between emotional beats. This file exists so that does not happen again.

---

## THE RULE

**Time advances in days and weeks. A skip longer than one month requires a
reason on the page and must be entered in this file before it is written.**

Skips are not transitions. They are expenditure. The timeskip is a fixed budget
of thirty months and it has been almost entirely spent — every month written
past is a month of canon-quiet village life that cannot be recovered, because
the far side of it is the Akatsuki era and the story stops being about a
hospital.

Corollary: **do not close a chapter on a line like "eleven months later."** It
reads as elegance and costs a quarter of the act.

## SAKURA — the correction

**She has been absent from ch30–37 and should not have been.** She is Tsunade's
apprentice at that hospital for most of that stretch, and Tsunade discusses her
actual working life with him every Thursday. That is an authorial omission.

**The half that is not an omission:** it is entirely in character that he has
never spoken to her. He went two years without asking Ochiai's name. He does not
initiate; he treats people as terrain until somebody assigns him a role. Passing
the Hokage's apprentice in a corridor forty times across two years without
opening his mouth is precisely the chapter-three failure ch37 diagnosed.

**The fix is not to retro-write seven chapters.** Foreground her from ch38 with
the acknowledgement built in — he has seen her repeatedly for two years, never
spoken, and now knows why. That converts the omission into characterisation
instead of pretending it was planned.

**The thread that has been waiting since ch21:** Sakura is the one who gave him
the word. On a road, at sixteen, off the top of her head in nine seconds —
*tree-walking, it's the second control exercise* — the first name anybody ever
handed him for anything. And per beat 18 she is the safe echo of what pulled
Tsunade toward him: exceptional control she does not know is her best asset.
