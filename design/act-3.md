# Act 3 — The War Ends Without Him

**STUB — not drafted.** Recorded so the structure doesn't carry a silent hole
between the road and the bridge.

---

## What this act has to do

Act 2 leaves him solvent, capable, hunted, and funding a war. Act 4 needs him
drifting with no reason left, chasing Zabuza's shadow because there is nothing
else to walk toward.

Act 3 is what removes the reason.

## The spine

**Mei Terumī wins, and he is in another country when it happens.**

Ao's Byakugan discovery, the rebellion's turn, Yagura's fall, the Fifth
Mizukage — all canon, all off-screen, all delivered to him by a stranger in a
bar who has no idea it means anything to him.

Everything he lost was for a war other people won. The purges stop. Kiri
reforms. The country that killed his family becomes, over about eighteen months,
**fine** — and starts apologising, publicly, to people who are still alive to
receive it.

There is nothing left to send money to. There is nothing to go back for. He is
nineteen and unemployed in the only sense that mattered.

## Why Guren's offer lands here

Not in Act 2 — here, at the bottom. The war is over, the purpose is gone, and
the only person alive who could teach him properly is still making the same
patient, kind, undemanding offer.

Whether he refuses cleanly, refuses late, or nearly takes it is the act's
central choice. He was warned in Act 2 beat 4. The warning came from an
organisation that also lied to him by omission for seven years.

## Candidate beats

- The news, secondhand, from someone who doesn't know who he is.
- An attempt to go home. Kiri is reformed and does not want him — the cover held,
  and to the new administration he is exactly what the record says: a deserter.
  Nobody who could vouch for him survived.
- The reparations. Watching a state apologise to bloodline families and finding
  the Ruri listed as extinct, in a ledger, correctly.
- The scroll recovered, or nearly — the inheritance arriving when there is no
  one left to have wanted it for.
- Guren's real offer, and the answer.
- The friend who stays: last encounter, now that the regime he served is gone
  and his warrant is void. What a hunter-nin is when the war ends.

## Open

- Does he take the scroll thread to completion here or in Act 4?
- Is Guren refused, or does he go and come back? The second is riskier and more
  interesting.
- Does the friend die, reconcile, or simply stop coming?
