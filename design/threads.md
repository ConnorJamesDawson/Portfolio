# Thread ledger — plan and close point

Every live thread, what it does, and where it ends. Ordered by **when it
closes**, not by when it opened, because that is the useful view.

Companion to `timeline.md` (which holds state) and `arc-shaping.md` (which
holds the reasoning). This file holds *destinations*.

## A. CLOSING INSIDE ACT 4 — ch50–53

### The contract
**Plan:** Katsuyu offers; he asks how the first one ever happened; declines
because signing removes the *free*. She visits instead — no summoning, no
calling, no ordering, no relying.
**Closes:** ch50. Done, and it stays done.

### Wood Release is reproducible
**Plan:** he tries again, correctly, by invitation, per the term. Six seconds,
a rose, hand fine. Undramatic on purpose.
**Closes:** ch51. **This is the thesis proved** — it was always a technique,
and the proof is that it has just been taught.

### The slugs always knew
**Plan:** an elder confirms it flatly, in the register used for *the fourth
floor is holding*. Must not help him — a summon's word is not evidence and the
paper is already forbidden. He gets certainty and no evidence.
**Closes:** ch52. The confirmation is the close; the *use* of it never comes.

### Tsunade / the slugs / the confrontation
**Plan:** four layers, all true at once. She cannot stop it (sage training is
the safety measure and she set the term). Katsuyu offers her the same thing.
She refuses.
**Closes:** does not close — **changes state.** From "decided nothing" to
estranged. End ch51 on the refusal, unexplained.

### The sword
**Plan:** finishes ~month 10 at four hours a night. The maker's panel on the
inside face of the guard is still blank and he still does not know what goes
on it.
**Closes:** Act 4 close or Act 5 opening. **The panel closes later** — when
the thing is named, which is not the same event as when it is finished.

### The promotion, and R&D
**Plan:** recognition for the medical crystal work. Off floor 2, someone else
takes the role. New remit: medical devices for Konoha, on the argument that a
stone doing a third of a medic's work expands the medical force by a third at
no extra commitment. **Approval pipeline straight to Tsunade** as head of the
medical board.
**Cost, which must be paid on the page:** he loses the ward, the people, and
the patients — the last being the thing he asked for and did not cost. His
world narrows as his remit widens.
**Closes:** the act break. It does not resolve anything; it *builds the
structure Act 5 runs on.*

### Kiri
**Plan:** the category error. His mother and Kohaku died for a property they
did not have. Not *therefore meaningless* — worse: nobody had any reason to
check, and the first person with the data and the motive is four years late
and is her son.
**Closes:** spills past month 30. **Do not compress it against the timeskip.**
Act 5 opening. The heaviest beat in the arc and it gets its own chapter.

## B. CLOSING EARLY ACT 5

### Kurihara Saeko — a debt, not a romance

**It never began, and that is the point.** What is actually on the page: she
corrects him (ch43), makes nine unpaid visits with no work order (ch44 sc1),
gets the last winter whole in a noodle house (ch44 sc3), asks him twice
whether something has happened and is told **"no"** (ch44 sc6), brings her own
lens, softens two corrections, uses his given name once, and books her next
two months so she is only in that yard by contract (ch45 sc5).

He never made a move. She cleared her diary when he shut the door.

**So there is nothing to end.** ch44 sc6 was the whole story, and it should
have cost him something permanent, and it did.

**He owes her eleven seconds.** He constructed the sentence at the time and
had it in his mouth: *I did something with what you gave me that you would not
have consented to, I stopped, I have recorded it, and I am ashamed.* He said
"no" instead.

**The scene, whenever it comes, is a debt settled far too late.** Not a
beginning and not an ending. She still reads him faster than he reads himself.
She can still name Tsunade, without malice, and be right. And she gets a
better line than a breakup allows: *here is what I would have been, and here
is why it was never going to happen* — from somebody who worked it out two
years before he did.

Afterwards they go on working together, correctly and pleasantly, forever,
which proves her point exactly.

**She needs two or three proper scenes in Act 5** or she was a device. She has
the lacquered pin (thirty years too old for her, unexplained), the funeral she
did not attend, the curriculum row, and six years of a dead man. That is a
character.

**The lack-of-hurt beat still lands, and harder.** Sakura: *do you feel sad?*
— *funny, I haven't even thought about it.* Feeling nothing about a
relationship that ran its course is one diagnosis. Feeling nothing about a
thing that never started **because you said one word in a yard** is a much
worse one, and it is the accurate one.

### WHAT KICKS IT OFF — the slugs ask why he has no mate

**Placement: ch52, in the elder's conversation.** Same flat register as the
bloodline confirmation and *Katsuyu says the Hokage is not pleased with you* —
the tonal whiplash between the three is the joke.

**No social barrier, same mechanism as *are you free* and *she should ask*.**
A biological question asked biologically, by something hermaphroditic, with
genuine cross-species confusion, uninterested in everything he is tortured
about and expansive about something irrelevant.

**And nobody has ever asked him.** Not Yagi, not Kanzaki, not Ashida, not
Handa — because human beings have the sense not to. The first entity ever to
ask him about this is a slug.

**What it produces: he goes and gets data**, retrospectively, out of his own
ledgers, because that is how he works. And the evidence is all there and
logged and never read as a column. **Nine visits with no work order** — he
noticed, wrote it up, could not interpret it. Now he can.

That is the Saeko detonation: **he reads his own bench log correctly, two
years late.** Same failure as the source-medium column, the shape-theory
schoolbook, and the assumed channel — but this time it is about a person.

### AND THE ONE HE LOOKS AT ON PURPOSE

**The stair.** She came down the outside stair at a run, at eleven at night,
in her shirt, with her hair down and no haori, and at the time he was in shock
with a stone hand and registered nothing — he says himself he watched her
face.

**The memory arrives later, complete, having been stored without being looked
at**, which is how memory actually works and is the point.

**Register:** his, not generic. He does what he does with everything — an
inventory, in the only vocabulary he owns, which is fabric and load and line
and what age does to a thing. **The attempt is transparent to the reader and
opaque to him.** Mature, unhurried, and not leering, because leering is a
failure of precision and he is never imprecise.

One moment where the technical vocabulary drops and something plainer comes
out. **He crosses it out and it is still there.**

**Brief — a paragraph or two.** The restraint is what lands it.

**The development is that he lets himself look.** Ch44: *I did not decide to
do nothing. I decided not to look.* Here he looks, once, deliberately, writes
it down, and does not act on it. Growth is permission, not action.

### Sakura's grievance
**Plan:** relief and replacement, both at once, in her own POV. **Turns** at
the promotion — genuinely pleased and it genuinely costs her.
**Resolves** when she gets the contract, and **Tsunade must be the one to
move**, on her own, plausibly shaken loose by the ch51 confrontation into
noticing what she has been withholding. **Sazare never says a word**, and
Sakura never finds out he refused one.
**Closes:** early-to-mid Act 5.

### Guren
**Plan:** anime-only and canon-alive (she survives to the end). Timing puts
the Three-Tails arc shortly after the timeskip. **Not two bloodline holders
comparing power** — a woman who has spent her life being told she is special,
meeting the man who can prove she isn't, who has to decide whether telling her
is a kindness. She uses it; she does not understand it.
**Closes:** mid Act 5. She lives.

### Kabuto — the correspondence
**Plan:** exchange 2 due ~month 9, exchange 3 ~month 14 (post-timeskip). The
fee was stated in advance and accepted in writing, and it was priced wrong —
**nobody costed the fishmonger**, and nobody costed that what he actually
wants is the grammar. He has had access to Guren the whole time and is writing
to Konoha anyway, which is the tell.
**Closes:** exchange 3 is where the ask arrives. Mid Act 5.

### The paper leak
**Plan:** the notice stopped the ninety-per-cent case. Anything surviving it
is by definition the serious version, and the tare weight is still in it.
**Closes:** surfaces in Act 5, and names a door.

## C. LONG-RUNNING — mid to late Act 5 and beyond

### The romance
**Plan:** secret, and the cover is that **nothing false is ever said** —
there is a real pipeline, real dockets, real devices, real odd hours. Exactly
the inverse of his doctrine, and he will notice.
**Danger with teeth:** a concealed relationship between the Hokage and a
subordinate is an unreported security vulnerability, with a live
correspondence running. He reported an envelope in eleven minutes and
disclosed a drawer at cost. He will not report this, and will have the
comparison in his own handwriting.
**Shizune knows first and says nothing**, and it costs her something nobody
accounts for.
**State changes** (the tension must not hold one note): decided-nothing →
estranged → what Saeko's ending teaches him → the timeskip ends and her
attention goes to Naruto → war footing makes her less available, not more.

### Is it the mechanism or is it her
**Plan:** he wrote that there is no experiment, because every test destroys
the thing being tested. **Losing her is the experiment, and it is run on him
rather than by him** — which is the only way he was ever going to find out,
and it is the same signature as everything else that has worked for him.
**Closes:** whenever the loss happens. Reasons one and two (Hokage/subordinate,
the age gap) **never dissolve** — they are outweighed knowingly, with the cost
accepted, and she pays in standing as she has all along.

### Do I teach, and who
**Plan:** the arc's actual destination. Not *do I publish* — publication is
forbidden on security grounds and the slugs' word is not evidence. He teaches
**somebody with no bloodline at all**, and it works **partially**. Not fully.
Enough. Which proves both halves: the aptitude was real, and it was only ever
a head start.
**And the two answers he has already met:** Urushibara (*you can't put a
ladder in a book*) and Minato (who put the ladder in the book and it survived
him). Saeko lost two years to that argument.
**Closes:** late Act 5. The arc's end.

### The scroll — what he does with it
**Plan:** **deliberately undecided. Do not resolve in advance.** Likely tied
to Kiri: the family scroll was taken, it came back, and what he does with the
last of it is the same question as *do I teach*.
**Closes:** with the teaching question, or not at all.

### The surplus (30 kg)
**Plan:** will not complete. The operation and the new remit eat the rest.
**Closes:** by failure, mid Act 5, and somebody has to be told.

### Root
**Plan:** the quiet is the news. Danzō's line runs to the Five Kage Summit.
**Closes:** around there. Not Sazare's to close.

### The reparations claim
**Plan:** filed ch35 at +10, two to three years to determination — so it lands
deep in Act 5. Kudō writes either way.
**Closes:** on determination, and the outcome matters less than that it
arrives years after he stopped waiting for it.

### Hazama's four grave queries
**Plan:** burial register, clearance, survey, temples. **One of them should
come back with something**, years late, and it should be small and wrong-
shaped rather than an answer.
**Closes:** Act 5, tied to Kiri.

## D. SMALL, PERSONAL — pay quietly, do not build up to

  body*, left in the notebook as an open query.
