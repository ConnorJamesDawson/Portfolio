# Act 5 — The Network

**Skeleton.** Beats are indicative, not final. See `world-bible.md` for setting
and cast, `act-4.md` for what precedes this.

**Span:** the 2.5-year timeskip and into early Shippuden. MC ~19 at the opening.
**Opens:** a prisoner nobody is supposed to know about.
**Closes:** Sasuke kills Orochimaru and the network fragments.

**Function of the act:** he finally has to choose a side. Acts 1–4 happen *to*
him — used without being told, deployed, traded, licensed. Here he is given a
mission with real latitude and has to decide what he is on the way through.

**Act 4 is the midpoint of the game, not the end.** The full arc runs to the
Fourth Shinobi World War (see `act-6.md`, `act-7.md`).

---

## Canon ground

**Otogakure is not a village.** It is a network of laboratories — scattered
hideouts and bases across the Land of Sound and several other countries, with no
fixed location. The pre-Chūnin-Exams headquarters was built beneath a tree; there
was a hideout in Kusagakure, another at the edge of the Land of Waves. **Guren
canonically commands one of these bases.**

This changes what infiltration means. There is no inside to get into. He gets
access to **a cell**, and the act is moving between bases on somebody else's
say-so — never seeing the whole structure, never knowing where Orochimaru
actually is, never able to confirm the person vouching for him hasn't already
sold him.

**Sasuke defects immediately after Tsunade's inauguration** (Sasuke Recovery
Mission, chapters 172–238) and spends roughly three years in Oto before killing
Orochimaru in early Shippuden. That window is this act.

### The Tayuya divergence — deliberate, and it closes

Canon: Temari kills Tayuya with *Summoning: Quick Beheading Dance* during the
retrieval — sliced, then crushed under falling trunks. **Kabuto later reanimates
her via Edo Tensei in the Fourth Shinobi World War**, where she fights Shikamaru
again.

This act diverges: she survives the forest, barely, and is recovered.

**The constraint that makes it work: she must be dead before the Fourth War.**
Edo Tensei is non-negotiable if Act 7 is to stay intact. So she dies in this act,
as a consequence of the MC's mission — the divergence opens and closes inside one
arc, and canon reasserts itself.

**Secrecy has a concrete cause.** Temari is Suna. Telling an allied village their
confirmed kill didn't take is a diplomatic problem, which is why this lands on a
trusted doctor instead of T&I.

## Part A — The Prisoner

**1. Handed a patient.**
Tsunade, in a very small room. A survivor of the retrieval, held off the books.
Heal her.

**2. The ask underneath the ask.**
Heal her *and turn her*.

He learned medicine out of regret — because if he had known how he might have
saved people along the way. It is the one clean thing he owns, and the village is
asking him to use it as an instrument of coercion on a captive.

**This rhymes with Kohaku**, who used his love as cover for something she
believed in. He is being used, again, through the best part of himself, by people
who are probably right that it is necessary.

**3. Months.**
The relationship is built over a long convalescence and should not be warm
quickly. She is hostile, in pain, and correct about what he is doing.

**4. The route in.**
What she gives him is not a map — there is no map. It is a name, a supply route,
and a base that will vouch for him.

## Part B — Recruit, Not Defector

**5. The cover is true.**
Konoha fabricates nothing. He is a foreign national held under treaty, unable to
go home, his output claimed by the country that murdered his family, audited and
quota'd and ranked. They simply let him tell the truth and walk.

The sharpest expression of the whole design: his grievance is real, legitimate,
and therefore *useful*, so it gets deployed.

**6. Wanted, not tolerated.**
A grievance gets him a hearing; it does not get him kept. What keeps him is that
**Orochimaru's organisation is a research operation** and the MC is a self-taught
bloodline researcher who rebuilt chakra theory from scraps, taught himself
medical ninjutsu, learned to read seals, and invented a class of medical
technology nobody else has.

He is not sneaking in. He is the hire they would want.

**7. The real temptation.**
Not Guren. **Oto is the only place that would fund his work.**

Konoha licenses, audits and quotas him. Kiri takes delivery by treaty. Orochimaru
would give him a laboratory, materials, subjects, and nobody telling him to stop.
For the first time in his life an institution would value the thing he is
actually interested in and resource it properly.

He would be good there. He would be happy there, for a while. That is far more
dangerous than a friendly face.

**8. Oto as a place.**
The freshest territory in the design — canon shows Sound almost entirely through
Sasuke's training and Kabuto's labs. Who lives there. What the hierarchy is. What
it is like to be an ordinary person in a village founded by a man who collects
people.

**Third state, completing the set:** Kiri purged bloodlines, Konoha licenses
them, Oto harvests them. Only the third is honest about what it wants.

**9. Sasuke, at a distance.**
Present in the network throughout, glimpsed and never befriended. The MC has no
reason to matter to him and shouldn't.

## Part C — Guren

**10. The reveal.**
She is a Ruri. He has believed he was the last for eight years.

**11. The accusation.**
She renounced the name because the family sat by — took protection while other
clans burned, armed the hunters, never came for anyone.

**He cannot answer it, because she is right and he watched it happen.**

Second time in the game he loses an argument to someone who picked a side and can
defend it. Haku chose to be used; Guren chose to leave; the MC has never chosen
anything.

**12. Her offer.**
Not family — she rejected that. The technique should outlive the people who
dishonoured it. She will teach him properly, which is the only thing he has ever
actually wanted.

**13. The stone-name.**
He learns what she was called. Two versions, and they are different characters:
he **uses** it once, which is the cruellest thing he ever does and tells him what
he has become — or he knows it and **never says it**, which is consistent with
the rest of him.

---

## Part D — Choosing

**14. The choice.**
Report the cell and burn it, take the offer and stay, or something worse than
either. This is the first genuine decision of his life and the act should make
that explicit.

**15. Tayuya dies.**
As a consequence of his mission — his cover slips, or he picks the objective over
her.

**He saved her from Temari only to get her killed himself.** The Act 1 wound
reopened with the roles reversed: then he was a child whose intervention killed
the person he was trying to save; here he is an adult who knew better.

Canon reasserts. She is dead before the war.

**16. Sasuke kills Orochimaru.**
Early Shippuden. The network fragments and the act ends in chaos he neither
caused nor controls — the one structural constant of his entire life.

**17. Extraction.**
Out, with whatever he chose to become.

## Open questions

- **Does he take Guren's offer, even temporarily?** Going and coming back is
  riskier and considerably more interesting than a clean refusal.
- **Does Guren survive the act?** She is anime-original with an ambiguous canon
  fate, so either is available. Surviving makes her a presence in Acts 6–7.
- **What Konoha does with what he brings back**, and whether he tells them
  everything.
- **How Tayuya's death is caused** — slip, sacrifice, or choice. Only the third
  makes it his.
