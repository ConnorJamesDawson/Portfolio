# Act 5 — Who Gets Taught

**Rewritten from the ch38–53 material.** The previous skeleton was built on Oto
infiltration, which was cut when Kabuto became a correspondent rather than a
destination. Its canon ground on Otogakure survives below; its beats did not.

Companions: `threads.md` (every live thread with a close point),
`arc-shaping.md` §5b (the slug road, sage mode), `timeline.md` (state and
dates), `writing-prompt.md` (the Act 5 register).

**Span:** from the day after ch53 into early Shippuden. He is 18y10m at the
opening and turns nineteen in the eleventh month.
**Opens:** Jiraiya, four days after the worst week Tsunade has had in a decade.
**Closes:** Sasuke kills Orochimaru and the network fragments. → `act-6.md`

---

## Function of the act

**Acts 1–4 happened *to* him.** Used without being told, deployed, traded,
licensed, quota'd, promoted into a leash he was relieved by.

**Here he has something everybody wants, and has to decide who gets it.**

He has proved — and cannot publish — that a kekkei genkai is a technique. Which
means it can be taught. Which means the question at the end of his arc is not
*do I publish* but **do I teach, and who.**

And every available answer is wrong:

- **Konoha** forbids it, on security grounds he agrees with, because the paper
  names the short list of families that genuinely are heritable.
- **Kabuto** can receive the whole of it, is the only correspondent he has ever
  had, and wants it.
- **Guren** can receive it, has a claim on it by blood — except there is no
  blood, and he can prove that, and proving it takes the last thing she has.

**Nobody who should have it is available. Everybody who is available should not
have it.**

The act closes with the choice made and the consequence paid. **The right
student does not appear until Act 6**, and works only partially.

---

## What is different from Act 4 — the standing conditions

**He is out of the hospital.** Fabrication Research, Medical Corps, effective
1/12. No ward, no patients, no colleagues. Everything he makes goes through one
desk.

**He is dangerous.** Sage capacity alone puts him above ninety-five per cent of
the roster before crystal and before wood — and the three consequences are in
`writing-prompt.md`, all of them problems:

1. He stops being safe. **Being worth taking** scales with him, and there is a
   price on him already, alive and undamaged.
2. **The hug box stops working**, visibly, to everyone including him.
3. The creed holds and will look like it doesn't.

**The relationship is secret and the cover is that nothing false is ever said.**
Real pipeline, real dockets, real devices, real odd hours. Shizune runs the
diary and knows first and says nothing. And a concealed relationship between the
Hokage and a subordinate **is an unreported security vulnerability** while a
live correspondence runs — which he knows, precisely, having built his standing
with Ibiki on what he does after.

**And the register widens.** See `writing-prompt.md`. Bodies, drink, what people
say when frightened. The voice does not change.

## Canon ground

**Otogakure is not a village.** It is a network of laboratories — scattered
hideouts across the Land of Sound and several other countries, with no fixed
location. **Guren canonically commands one of these bases.**

**Sasuke defected immediately after Tsunade's inauguration** and spends roughly
three years in Oto before killing Orochimaru in early Shippuden. That window is
this act. He appears as news and never as an acquaintance; the MC has no reason
to matter to him and should not.

**Guren survives canon.** She encases herself and Rinji in crystal to protect
Yūkimaru, sinks into the lake, is revealed alive, and is pulled out by Gozu —
who looked up to her because she saved his life years earlier. She, Yūkimaru
and Gozu are seen outside Konoha after the Fourth War. **She lives.** Killing
her would be a choice, not a canon obligation.

**Yūkimaru is her whole heart** and is the reason any of her arc works.

### The Tayuya divergence — deliberate, and it closes

Canon: Temari kills Tayuya during the retrieval. **Kabuto reanimates her by Edo
Tensei in the Fourth War.**

This act diverges: she survives the forest, barely, and is recovered.

**Constraint: she must be dead before the Fourth War.** Edo Tensei is
non-negotiable if `acts-7-8.md` is to stand. So she dies inside this act, as a
consequence of his choice. The divergence opens and closes in one arc and canon
reasserts.

**Secrecy has a concrete cause.** Temari is Suna. Telling an allied village that
their confirmed kill did not take is a diplomatic problem — which is why it
lands on a trusted doctor and not on T&I.

## Part A — What He Has Become

**1. The morning after.**
He wakes in a chair in the Hokage's house and cannot explain being there.
Shizune finds him. She spells out what he has done **to her**, not what he did
wrong — **and she calls her Hime**, which is his word, which nobody else uses,
and which she has been not-saying for a year.

**2. Jiraiya.**
Four days after ch53, which is the worst possible timing and the point.

- He knows her better than anybody alive and reads the whole situation in about
  the time it takes to cross a room.
- **He is the only human in the village who would recognise what Sazare is on
  sight** — a toad sage looking at somebody trained by slugs who was never told
  what it was called.
- He is **not kind about it and is right**, and Sazare has had nobody be unkind
  and correct to him in a very long time.
- And he is the one man who can say what the road actually costs, having walked
  a different fork of it.

**3. The sword comes back from Shikkotsu.**
With a review. Six numbered questions answered by things that have no reason to
be diplomatic, and **question six answered at length** — *what am I not asking.*
Sets the combat frame without him acquiring anything.

**4. The first deployment.**
He goes out of the village as an asset for the first time since the invasion,
and **the hug box does not survive contact.** One name on the bottom of
everything is not a control on a man who can put a forest across a valley, and
everybody can see it.

## Part B — Kiri

**Spilled deliberately from Act 4. It is the heaviest beat in the whole design
and must not be compressed against anything.**

**5. The category error.**
Not a tragedy of prejudice. **His mother and Kohaku died for a property they did
not have.** Thousands did.

And the honest version is worse than *therefore it was meaningless*: nobody had
any reason to check, because there was nothing to check it against, and the
first person with both the data and the motive is four years late and is her
son.

**6. The reparations claim and the scroll.**
Filed ch35, two to three years to determination — it lands in this act. Kudō
writes either way. **What he does with the scroll is deliberately undecided and
should stay undecided until it resolves alongside the teaching question.**

**7. Hazama's four grave queries.**
Burial register, clearance, survey, temples. **One of them comes back**, years
late, small and wrong-shaped, and is not an answer.

## Part C — The Prisoner

**8. Handed a patient.**
Tsunade, in a very small room. A survivor of the retrieval, held off the books.
Heal her.

**9. The ask underneath the ask.**
Heal her **and turn her**.

He learned medicine out of regret — because if he had known how, he might have
saved people along the way. It is the one clean thing he owns, and the village
is asking him to use it as an instrument of coercion on a captive.

**This rhymes with Kohaku**, who used his love as cover for something she
believed in. He is being used, again, through the best part of himself, by
people who are probably right that it is necessary.

**And it is worse than it was in the old skeleton**, because:

- **His entire post is now producing medical articles**, and he is the only
  person alive who could build one for this purpose.
- **He can make crystal that draws** — and was told, on the record, on the
  eleventh of the eleventh month, that an open article does not go near a
  patient until it is one-way.
- **A prisoner held off the books is exactly the patient nobody would ask
  about.**

He should get as far as specifying it before he stops. Or not stop.

**10. Months.**
Built over a long convalescence and **not warm quickly**. She is hostile, in
pain, and correct about what he is doing.

**11. What she gives him.**
Not a map — there is no map. A name, a supply route, and a base that would vouch
for him. **Which he does not use to go anywhere**, and which is worth
enormously more as the thing he declines to use.

## Part D — The Ask

**12. Exchange three.**
Nine months of correspondence, agreed in writing, with the fee stated in advance
and accepted. Two exchanges true and unaltered.

**Nobody tells you what they want in the first letter.**

**13. What he wants is the grammar.**
Not crystal. He has had access to Guren the entire time and is writing to a
hospital fabricator in Konoha anyway — **which is the tell.** Guren *uses* it.
She does not know why it works, any more than Sazare did at sixteen or Tenzō
does at thirty.

**14. The temptation, and it is not a friendly face.**
**Oto is the only place that would fund his work.**

Konoha licenses, audits and quotas him and forbids the paper. Kiri takes
delivery by treaty. Orochimaru would give him a laboratory, materials, subjects,
and nobody telling him to stop. For the first time in his life an institution
would value the thing he is actually interested in and resource it properly.

**He would be good there. He would be happy there, for a while.**

And it arrives **as a letter**, which is worse than a conversation, because he
can read it eleven times.

**15. Third state, completing the set.**
Kiri purged bloodlines. Konoha licenses them. **Oto harvests them.** Only the
third is honest about what it wants.

**16. And the fee was priced wrong.**
*It will help him* was the fee Ibiki named and both of them accepted. **Nobody
costed the fishmonger**, and nobody costed that the thing being handed over was
not a finding but a method.

## Part E — Guren

**Route: the Three-Tails deployment.** Konoha sends a team; she commands a base;
the target involves crystal. **He is the obvious asset**, which is the first
time that sentence has been true about him.

**17. Not a power comparison.**
A woman who has spent her life being told she is special, meeting the man who
can prove she isn't — and who has to decide whether telling her is a kindness.

**18. The reveal: she is a Ruri.**
He has believed he was the last for eight years.

**19. The accusation.**
She renounced the name because **the family sat by** — took protection while
other clans burned, armed the hunters, never came for anyone.

**He cannot answer it, because she is right and he watched it happen.**

Second time he loses an argument to somebody who picked a side and can defend
it. Haku chose to be used; Guren chose to leave; **he has never chosen
anything** — and this act is where that stops.

**20. Her offer.**
Not family — she rejected that. **The technique should outlive the people who
dishonoured it.** She will teach him properly, which is the only thing he has
ever actually wanted.

**And he no longer needs it**, which is the cruellest available structure: she
is offering the thing he wanted at fourteen, and he has already got it off a
slug, and saying so would take the last thing she has.

**21. The thesis, pointed at her.**
There is no blood. She did not renounce a bloodline; she renounced a *family*,
and the technique was never theirs to dishonour or hers to inherit.

**He can prove it and it would cost her everything.** Whether he says it is the
same class of decision as the stone-name.

**22. The stone-name.**
He learns what she was called. Two versions and they are different characters:
he **uses** it once — the cruellest thing he ever does, and it tells him what he
has become — or he knows it and **never says it**, which is consistent with the
rest of him.

**23. Yūkimaru.**
Her whole heart, and the reason any of it works, and the thing that makes her
legible rather than a rival specimen.

---

## Part F — Choosing

**24. The choice is who gets taught.**

Not a side. Not a village. **A student.**

- Kabuto can hold the whole of it and is the enemy.
- Guren can hold it and has a claim, and taking the claim apart is the price.
- Konoha has forbidden it and is right to.
- **And the honest answer — somebody with no bloodline at all — does not exist
  yet.**

This is the first genuine decision of his life and the act should say so.

**25. Tayuya dies.**
As a consequence of his choice — his cover, his objective, or his refusal.

**He saved her from Temari only to get her killed himself.** The Act 1 wound
reopened with the roles reversed: then he was a child whose intervention killed
the person he was trying to save; here he is an adult who knew better.

**Canon reasserts. She is dead before the war.**

**26. Guren lives.**
She goes into the lake, is pulled out by Gozu, and leaves with Yūkimaru. **Do
not kill her.** She is the counterexample the thesis needs alive, and she is the
one person who could ever have been family and chose not to be.

## What must NOT happen in this act

- **He does not infiltrate anything.** The network comes to him, by letter and
  by deployment. There is no cover identity and no defection.
- **He does not publish.** The paper stays forbidden and he agrees with the
  reasoning.
- **He does not get a clean student.** The partial success is Act 6.
- **Guren does not die.**
- **The relationship does not become public**, and nothing false is ever said
  about it.
- **The retrospective does not come back.** Retired ch53. If he needs a
  comparison he uses a person.
