# Act 7 — The Rubble

**Span:** Shippuden, Pain's assault on Konoha. MC ~20.

**The event:** Pain destroys the village. Nagato revives the dead; **the buildings
stay gone.**

### He agrees with Pain

Act 6 leaves him holding Nagato's thesis — that the shinobi system manufactures
suffering and the powerful spend the weak — reached independently, from evidence,
months before Pain says it aloud.

**So he is not a bystander to the ideological confrontation.** He is the person in
the audience who already agrees with the man who just levelled his home, and he
has to watch Naruto argue him out of it.

**Naruto's answer has to work on him too, or it does not work at all.** That is
this act's real climax — not the destruction, the argument.

Do not resolve it cheaply. He has better evidence for Pain's position than almost
anyone alive, and whatever moves him has to be worth more than evidence.

**Why it matters to this character specifically:**

- **The hospital is levelled.** The place he defended during Orochimaru's
  invasion, the place he integrated through, the first thing since the Ruri
  compound he called home.
- **Second time a place he belonged to has been erased** — and the first time he
  was not a child when it happened.
- Act 1 beat 17 is the compound standing open, doors ajar, growing-frames
  smashed, nobody. This is that image again at the scale of a city.

**What's different this time:** he is not twelve, he is not helpless, and the
dead come back. Almost everything he lost in Act 1 is the thing Nagato hands back
to Konoha for free — which is either a mercy or an obscenity depending on the
hour, and he should not resolve which.

**Open:** where he is when it happens. Inside the village is obvious; returning
to find it flattened rhymes harder with Act 1.

# Act 8 — The War

**Span:** the Fourth Shinobi World War. MC ~21.
**Ends:** Madara's defeat.

### He never fights Madara

The **Logistical Support and Medical Division**, captained by Shizune, is a
canonical formation — mobile hospitals, field outposts, medical aid across the
Allied Shinobi Forces. Sakura serves in the medical unit and is explicitly barred
from the front lines.

That is his role. **He keeps people alive while other people fight**, which
requires zero power inflation and is exactly consistent with instrumental-but-
never-decisive.

### His life's work matters at scale, once

The crystals are **distributed medicine** — they work where no medic is present.
A hundred-thousand-strong allied army with far too few medics is precisely that
situation.

The thing he invented in one overloaded hospital after one invasion becomes the
thing that matters when the entire world is the hospital. This is the only moment
in the design where his ceiling is high enough to matter to history, and it is a
logistics ceiling, not a combat one.

### Two canon details that land hard on him

**The division's duties include protecting the corpses of kekkei genkai users so
Akatsuki cannot use their bodies against the Alliance.** A bloodline survivor
whose family was hunted for their blood, assigned to keep bloodline bodies from
being harvested. Very on the nose — use sparingly, but it is canon.

**Kabuto's Edo Tensei brings Tayuya back.** He healed her, turned her, and got
her killed in Act 5. Now she is a corpse puppet on the field and he has to work
around her.

### Open

- Whether Guren appears, and on which side.
- Whether he ever tells anyone what he did in Oto.
- The final image. He has no family, no village of his own, and a hospital post
  in a country that owns his output by treaty. What "winning" looks like for a
  man whose only sovereignty is choosing what to make.
