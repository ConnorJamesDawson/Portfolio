# Chapter Plan

54 chapters, ~540,000 words, four books. Chapter numbering is continuous across
the whole work; book divisions are marketing and morale, not structure.

Allocation is by **beat density**, not evenly — Act 3 is one location over a
fortnight and Act 1 is twenty-four beats across four years.

**Acts 1–4 are drafted, so their chapters are firm. Acts 5–8 are skeletons and
their chapter counts will move** once those acts are written properly.

| **2 — The Guest** | 4 | 24–33 | ~100k | The hospital post |
| **3 — The Network** | 5–6 | 34–45 | ~120k | Pain arrives |
| **4 — The War** | 7–8 | 46–54 | ~90k | Madara's defeat |

**Two genuine escape hatches, both free if built in now:**

- **Book 1 is a complete work at ~230k.** Everything Kiri closes: Zabuza dead,
  the sword refused, the past behind him, a boy walking to a country he was
  raised to hate. If the serial dies at 30% — where serials usually die — it dies
  having finished something.
- **Books 1–2 together are a complete arc at ~330k.** He arrives with nothing,
  builds something, is traded between two states, and wins the right to choose
  what to make.

---

## POV

Six **dedicated** non-protagonist chapters, marked below. Additional scene-level
switches inside protagonist chapters are at the writer's discretion, within the
roster in `writing-prompt.md`.

The protagonist holds roughly 90% of chapters and rather less of the scenes.

## Notes

**Chapter 4 is the most important structural decision in Book 1.** Putting
Kohaku's POV that early converts the rest of Act 1 from mystery into dread. The
reader spends seven chapters watching a boy be loved and used simultaneously,
knowing, unable to say anything. Holding the reveal to the end would be the
conventional choice and a much weaker one.

**Chapter 6 will be the hardest chapter in the book to write and the most
valuable.** The whole design argues that the horror is bureaucratic; this is the
only place it is shown rather than asserted.

**Chapter 15 must not be cut for pace.** It is the warm centre of Act 2, and the
fall in chapters 18–19 lands on it or on nothing.

**Chapters 30–31 carry the load Act 4 was restructured for.** The news arrives
while he is owned by two governments, and the settlement is a floor rather than
an ending. If the pacing pulls toward putting chapter 33 before chapter 31,
resist it — that was the original mistake.
