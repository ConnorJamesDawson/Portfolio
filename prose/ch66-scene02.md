# Chapter 66, Scene 2 — Brown

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the quartermaster's yard. Sazare Ruri, age
eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1460 words.*

---

Katsuyu arrived at four, on time, which after the last Friday's four
hours of argument at Shikkotsu felt like a form of apology neither of
them named.

They had a bench arrangement now, fifteen Fridays old: the flat stone
he had dressed for her at the second session, because a summon the size
of a loaf sitting on raw brick offended something in him that predated
language; the lamp at the angle she preferred; the kettle for him,
because she had observed at Friday nine that he conducted the sessions
better holding something warm, and had said so, plainly, the way she
said everything, and he had written it down and complied and it was
true.

"Before we begin," said Katsuyu, "I am instructed to tell you that the
answering is scheduled. Fukasaku-sama will deliver the first of the
three answers at Myōboku's convenience, and Myōboku's convenience has
been arranged, by me, to fall in the week your training with
Jiraiya-sama begins."

"Arranged."

"Negotiated," said Katsuyu, which from her was the whole confession.
"If the answers arrive before the training, you will have a month of
theory and no field, and you do not do well holding theory you cannot
work. If they arrive long after, the two teachings will run unwatched
in opposite corners of you and disagree where nobody can see it. So
they will arrive together, and disagree in daylight, in front of both
teachers, where a disagreement is called a syllabus."

He wrote it down. Under it he wrote: *she has been managing my
education for fifteen Fridays and has decided to stop hiding it,* and
under that, *the decision to stop hiding it is itself instruction.
Note the method.*

"Now," said Katsuyu. "The state. Tell me the plan and I will tell you
what I think of it."

"Three configurations," said Sazare. "The hold alone, first, because
it is the twentieth's condition and the twentieth is the only success
in the data. Then the hold while you observe the eyes and call any
change aloud, because on the twentieth the change was observed and I
want the observation in the loop from the start, not arriving late.
Then the hold in contact with the blade, open, because the blade has
been drawing for sixteen days and if the state is a matter of what is
passing through me, an open article is the largest tap I can stand
next to."

"And your own opinion of the plan?"

"That it is a good plan for finding something that responds to plans."

"Yes," said Katsuyu. "I thought so too. Begin anyway. Negative results
from a good plan are load-bearing."

---

He held.

The sphere and the count, nineteen evenings old and then some, the
still water brought up the way it came up now, without ceremony,
because ceremony was for things that might not come. Twenty seconds.
The yard stood around him in its usual grammar — brick, frost, the
kettle ticking as it cooled, the lamp's circle ending four feet out.

"Brown," said Katsuyu.

Forty seconds. He widened nothing, forced nothing, asked nothing. On
the twentieth there had been no asking; there had been a ditch, and
holding, and the world arriving unrequested.

"Brown."

Ninety seconds. The count ran on its own floor below him. He let the
hold go long past the point of proof, to two hundred, to three, in
case the state kept the twentieth's timetable and not his.

"Brown," said Katsuyu gently. "Rest."

The second configuration went the same, except that being watched
added a low hum of construction to the first minute — a self standing
to attention for its examiner — and he noted the hum and waited it out
and held clean from the second minute. Brown at every mark.

The third configuration he had, privately, given the best odds, and it
gave him something, and the something was not the state. Holding with
one hand on the open blade was like holding on the bank of a river —
not power, *traffic*, a sense of throughput close enough to touch. It
did not enter him. It went past him, the way the river goes past a man
standing on the bank, minding its own errand entirely.

"Brown," said Katsuyu, at the end of it. "I am sorry."

"Don't be. Three configurations, three clean negatives, one
observation about the blade that I did not have this morning. That is
a productive Friday by any standard I have ever kept." He said it in
the level register, and it was even true, and the small flat weight
under his ribs was noted, dated, and declined further comment.

He wrote the honest version while the kettle went back on.

*The state does not attend the procedure. On the twentieth it attended
the situation. Candidate differences: an opponent; fear; his chakra
environment; exhaustion; the ditch; stakes. I can reproduce none of
these on a Friday and half of them I am forbidden to reproduce at all.*

*This is what Jiraiya-sama meant. Everybody is taught, over years,
with somebody standing next to them saying when the line is crossed.
There is no procedure because I am the procedure and nobody has
written me yet.*

---

"May I raise the blade," said Katsuyu, "since it has raised itself."

"Please."

"Sixteen days open. Your month sheet went to the Corps on the first. I
have read it — I am on the circulation, which continues to surprise
the clerks. Every reading steady, no degradation, and the draw
continuous the entire period." She paused, in the way she paused when
she had arranged the next sentence some time ago. "You weighed it."

"Twice a week. It weighs what it weighed."

"So," said Katsuyu. "Sixteen days of intake. No growth. No loss of
order — the opposite, if your fourth reading is right. And no change
in mass. Where is it going, Sazare?"

The yard did the thing yards did when a question landed in them: went
on being a yard, unhelpfully.

"Either it spends as it draws," he said slowly, "the whole income on
the upkeep, a wage that arrives and is gone — or it keeps it somewhere
I have not found the scale for."

"And which do you believe?"

"The first is tidier. The first requires nothing new." He looked at
the blade where it lay in the lamp's circle, open, ordinary, sixteen
days into a life nobody had written a procedure for either. "The
fourth reading says the order has *improved*. Upkeep does not improve
a thing. Upkeep is running to stand still. Something in there is being
paid more than the rent costs, and I have not found where it banks."

"Write the question," said Katsuyu. "Do not answer it tonight."

He looked at her.

"You have a holiday on Wednesday," she said, in exactly the voice the
fourth floor is holding was conducted in. "I have watched you for
fifteen Fridays. A question written before bed is a fuse, and you are
a man who, once lit, burns to the end regardless of what else was
scheduled. Write it, cap it, and go and stand somewhere that needs
nothing from you. The question will keep. Questions of this quality
keep for centuries. That is how we still have them."

He wrote: *Q — where does an open article keep what it draws?*

He ruled a line under it, which in the grey notebook meant closed
until reopened, and he capped the pen, and if the capping took a
fraction longer than mechanics required, Katsuyu was courteous enough
to watch the lamp instead.

"Thank you for the negatives," she said, at the gate hour. "I mean
that as written. Three clean noes from a sound method are worth more
than one wobbling yes, and on the day the state finally has a reason
to attend, we will know exactly what it was not. Also—" and here she
attained the tone that, in a creature with no face to speak of, still
somehow carried the weight of a formal footnote— "I am given to
understand there will be a market."

"There is usually a market."

"I have never been to a market," said Katsuyu. "Four hundred years. I
am on the circulation for the Corps' reject tables and I have never
seen a stall. When you write Wednesday up — and you will, you are
constitutionally incapable of otherwise — I would like the market in
it. Prices. What things cost when nobody needs them. It is the kind of
data nobody thinks to bring a slug."

"I will bring you the market," said Sazare, and found that the flat
weight under his ribs had gone, and did not note where, which was
itself, had he noted it, the finding of the evening.
