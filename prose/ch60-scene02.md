# Chapter 60, Scene 2 — Provenance

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the Hokage's tower. Sazare Ruri, age eighteen.
POV: Sazare. ~1300 words.*

---

He did not say anything for some time.

She got up and went out and he heard the water in the room at the end of
the corridor and then her going down the inner stair, and the third board
went and did not go again, and he lay on his back in a bed on the second
floor of the Hokage's tower and looked at a ceiling.

*Right.*

*Do not do anything with it yet.*

*I want to have it before I start on it, because the moment I start on
it I will have turned it into a thing I have handled, and I would like
about four minutes of just having it.*

He had four minutes.

Then he started on it, because that was what he was.

---

*She has met her own specification.*

*Twenty-first of the eleventh month, that office, about twenty-five past
four in the morning. She said she would not say it back and she gave me
the reasoning and the reasoning was mine — that if she said it under
load I would go back and look at the conditions it was produced under
and find a bad provenance and write* not a clean reading *in my own book
and step over it for the rest of my life.*

*She was right. I would have. I would have done it inside a fortnight
and I would never have mentioned it.*

*And then she said what the clean conditions would be, and she said them
out loud, and she listed four.*

*Sober.*

*Slept.*

*Nothing on the table.*

*Nobody's touched anybody.*

He lay there and ran it.

*One: sober, and I would put it at four days minimum. She has not had a
drink in this building since Tuesday and there is nothing in the low
cupboard on the left because I looked on Thursday for a completely
different reason.*

*Two: slept. Eleven respirations, no catch, no surfacing, ninety minutes
minimum and I would defend three hours. Best figures I have ever taken
on her by a distance.*

*Three: nothing on the table. There is no table. There is no docket and
no article and no question in the room and nobody wanted anything.*

*Four is broken, and she broke it herself, deliberately, about a second
before she said it.*

He put a hand over his eyes.

*She hit me first so that four would be broken by her and not by me.*

*I am not certain of that. I want to be honest — that is a reading and it
is the reading I want, and I have four hundred pages on people who took
the reading they wanted.*

*But she hit me and then she said it, in that order, with about a second
between, and she is a woman who does eleven things in a quarter of a
second and has been doing it for forty years.*

*It was not an accident.*

---

*So: three of four met exactly, and the fourth broken on purpose and at
her own hand.*

*She has not said a sentence at me. She has delivered against a
specification she stated in advance, six days ago, out loud, in front of
nobody, and she has done it in daylight on an ordinary Saturday with
nothing at stake and no witness and nothing to gain.*

*It is the cleanest reading I have ever been given of anything.*

*I could put that on a felt.*

He lay on the bed for a while longer than he had intended to.

*I have got nothing to write it in and I am not going to go and get the
book.*

*It is the first thing in eighteen years I have decided not to write
down on purpose, and I would like it noted — somewhere, by somebody,
possibly nobody — that the reason is not that I am frightened of it.*

*It is that the entry would be worse than the thing.*

---

He got up at about ten to seven and stood in the middle of the room and
did the other one, because it had been sitting there since he opened his
eyes.

*How did I get in here.*

*I have got no report of it. That is the first time since I was seven
that there is an hour of my life I cannot produce, and I have had one on
the eleventh and one on the twenty-first and now this, which makes
three, all in eighteen days, which is a rate I do not like.*

*Evidence.*

He looked round the room.

*One: my coat is on that chair and it is folded. I do not fold a coat. I
have never folded a coat in my life, it goes on the back of whatever is
nearest, and Sakura has a column for it.*

*So somebody else took it off me or I was awake enough to be told.*

*Two: my boots are at the end of the frame, together, toes out. That is
mine. That is exactly mine and I do it without thinking, which means I
did that part.*

*Three: the felt roll is on the floor on that side of the bed and it was
in the other room at midnight, because I put it against the wall by the
door where I could reach it, which is what I do with it.*

*So I carried it. Nineteen inches of hilt in a roll of felt, down a
corridor, at half one in the morning, three-quarters asleep, without
being told to.*

He looked at it for a moment.

*Four: the door at the end of the corridor is open and I did not shut
it, which she now knows because I told her.*

*Five, and this is the one that settles it: there is no version of this
where I came down here.*

*I would not. Not in any state. She put me in a room and there is no
condition under which I would get out of the bed I had been put in and
walk down a corridor into hers, because I do not presume, and because I
have spent eleven years not presuming, and it is the only piece of
conduct about myself I have never once had to check.*

*So she came and got me.*

*At some point between about half twelve and half two, on her feet, in
her own building, sober, having decided to.*

He stood in the middle of the room.

*And she is not going to tell me why.*

*And if I ask her, she will produce four completely defensible reasons
in about six seconds, and one of them will be true, and I will not be
able to tell which, and she will know that I cannot.*

*So I am not going to ask.*

---

He got his coat off the chair and put it on.

*She has got an hour of the night that I have not got.*

*And I had one of hers, on the twelfth, at four inches, with both her
hands shut in my shirt for seven hours, and she was drunk and did not
have it and I have never once mentioned it and I am never going to.*

*Now it is the other way round.*

*She has got something of mine that I cannot produce, and she is going
to keep it, and I would have to ask for it and asking is the one move
that would spoil it.*

He picked up the felt roll and stood there with it in his hand for a
second, and then put it under his arm, because there was a thing he had
worked out at about four o'clock on the Thursday and had not told
anybody, and this was the morning for it.

*Fair enough.*

*She can have it.*
