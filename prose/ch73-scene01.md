# Chapter 73, Scene 1 — The Border

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the tower, the second floor. Sazare Ruri,
age eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1250 words.*

---

The border was eleven weeks old and it had never been drawn.

That was the first finding, and he arrived at it on the Wednesday
evening of the second week of the third month, sitting at the
desk in the upper room with a manuscript audit open in front of
him and no attention whatsoever available for it.

*Reconstruct the boundary. It exists — both of us have stopped
at it, hundreds of times, in the dark, with total consistency.
So it is real, and it is agreed, and it has never been stated
out loud by either party.*

*Where was it drawn, and by whom, and on what authority?*

He went at it the way he went at everything, which was
backwards from the evidence.

*It is not the shirt. The shirt went in January and stayed
gone. It is not the bath, which has been shared since the
eleventh. It is not the hands — the hands were renegotiated
on the first Thursday, unilaterally, by me, and ratified by
the recipient with a sound I have entered under a heading of
its own.*

*It is a line whose location I could draw on a diagram to
within a quarter inch and cannot name in a sentence. Beyond
it: the whole territory neither of us has any documentation
for. On this side of it: everything we have.*

*Finding: the border is the outermost limit of what I have
ever been given explicit permission for.*

*Corollary, and this is the one that has kept me at this desk
for forty minutes with an unread manuscript: I will never
cross it.*

*Not from restraint. Restraint implies wanting to and
declining, and I want to at a magnitude that has its own
weather. From ARCHITECTURE. I have never in my life taken a
thing that was not specified. Nine hundred articles, every
one to somebody's order. The scalpel required her to
instruct me by name, out loud, in theatre, in front of
witnesses, forty-eight hours after I had written* not to be
used *under a line — and I would not lift it until she did.
That is not a preference. It is the load path of the entire
man. Everything I am is built to be asked.*

*So the border cannot move from this side. It is
structurally impossible. Which means either it never moves —*

The pen stopped.

*— or she moves it.*

---

The evidence that it was going to move had been accumulating
for nine days, and he had been logging it without conclusion,
which was itself a symptom.

Instance one, the Saturday: a kiss that had ended because it
had to and not because it had finished. He knew the
difference now — that was the new instrumentation of the last
month, the managed bug's one gift; the noise no longer took
him, so he was present for everything, including the moment
where a thing stopped against a wall rather than coming to
rest. She had put her forehead against his collarbone
afterwards and breathed out, once, hard, the way a smith
sets down a hammer.

Instance two, the Monday: his own hand at the small of her
back had travelled four inches past its historic limit and
been stopped by nothing — no line, no signal, no arrival at
any obstacle — and he had halted it himself, on the old
border, out of the architecture, and she had said nothing at
all, and the nothing had been so loud he had lain awake
underneath it.

Instance three, tonight, twenty minutes ago, downstairs, in
the small office with the door shut: nothing had happened.
That was the instance. She had come round the desk to look
at the manuscript over his shoulder, and stood closer than
the manuscript required, and neither of them had moved for
about four seconds, and then she had said, in a voice with
gravel in it that he had never heard from her at close
range, "Go up. Take that with you. I'll be an hour," and he
had gone up, and had been at this desk since, and the
manuscript had not been read.

*She is at the border on her side, in the dark, with a
hammer she has set down eleven times.*

*And she is not going to say it. That is the third finding
and it is the one that matters. She does not ask for things
in words. Three years of data: not once, not for the day
out, not for the tower, not for anything. She asks in
FORMATS. The wager, the appointment, the terms witnessed,
the clause five she wrote last and has never disclosed —
every want that woman has ever expressed to me arrived
wearing paperwork, because paperwork is where she keeps the
part of herself that can be refused without either of us
having to survive the refusal bare.*

*So: she will build a format.*

*And when she does, my entire architecture — which cannot
initiate, which has never once taken an unspecified thing —
becomes, for once, an asset. Because a format is a
specification. And I have never once in my life failed to
build what I was specified.*

He wrote the last line of the entry at ten past eleven, and
underlined it, and sat looking at it, with the manuscript
unread and the whole of him listening for a step on the
stair.

*I am ready. Whenever it comes. In whatever form it comes,
in whatever ridiculous administrative costume she dresses
it in, I want her to know — and I have no way to tell her
that does not itself constitute crossing the border, so I
am writing it here where nobody will read it — that the
answer is yes, that it has been yes since a boat and a
ditch and a tower, that there is no version of the question
where the answer is anything else.*

*Ask me, Hime.*

*I have spent my whole life being unable to ask, and the
one thing I have always, always been able to do is answer.*

The step came on the stair at twenty past.

She was carrying a document. Of course she was carrying a
document — he saw it in her hand from four feet and something
in his chest went over like a wave breaking, because he knew,
before he read one word of it, exactly what he was looking
at.

"Saturday," said the fifth Hokage of the Land of Fire, in
the flat administrative voice, with her chin up and her
whole face doing something he would spend the rest of the
week failing to catalogue. "You've been training eight
weeks. Nobody's assessed you. I'm assessing you."

She put it on the desk.

*COMBAT ASSESSMENT — informal. Unofficial. Unwitnessed. Not
for any file.*

*Location: the garden of my house.*

*Terms: to be agreed on the ground.*

*Prize: to the victor. Each party to write their prize in
advance, sealed, unread. The victor opens both.*

He read it twice.

He looked up.

She was already at the door, having discovered in herself,
apparently, an urgent need to be elsewhere, and her ears —
the fifth Hokage of the Land of Fire, fifty-three years old,
the strongest woman alive — her ears were scarlet.

"Saturday," said Sazare.

"Bring the sword," said Tsunade, and left, at a pace, and
he sat alone in the upper room with a piece of paper in his
hands and his heart going like a hammer on hot iron.

*She built the format.*

*Three days.*
