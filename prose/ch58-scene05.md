# Chapter 58, Scene 5 — I Forgot

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the quartermaster's yard. Sazare Ruri, age
eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1770 words.*

---

He had four openings by Wednesday night and he threw all four of them
away on the hill on Friday evening, in order, between the bottom gate
and the yard.

*One: the account. Start at the twelfth and give her the whole week in
sequence and let the omission arrive where it actually arrived.*

*No. That is a structure. That is me arranging the facts so that she
gets to the conclusion in the right order and thinks the right thing,
and she has never once done that to me in nineteen evenings.*

*Two: lead with it. First words in the yard.*

*Also a structure. That is me getting it out of the way so I do not have
to carry it through the evening, and the reason I want it out of the way
is that I do not like carrying it, which makes it about me.*

*Three: the reason. Explain what was happening.*

*There is no version of that which is not an excuse, and it is not even a
good excuse, and the honest content of it is* I was thinking about
somebody else*, which is true and which I am not going to say to her.*

*Four: don't raise it. She has not raised it, she is not going to raise
it, and there is a completely available evening in which nothing is said
and everything is fine.*

He stopped on the path about thirty feet short of the gate for that one.

*That is the worst of the four and it is the one I want.*

---

The yard was as it always was.

Kanzaki had gone at six and had left the lamp out on the stool, which
she had been doing since the eighth month and had denied for a week
every time he mentioned it and then stopped mentioning it. The sacking
was over the far bench. There was frost coming on the flags at the
north end because it was the twentieth of the eleventh month and the sun
had gone at four.

He put his coat on the bench and stood in the middle of it.

*Right.*

He had asked eleven times and he had never once thought about the asking
until this evening, and now that he was thinking about it he found that
it was eleven words and that it cost nothing at all.

That was the part that had been sitting in him since Saturday night and
it arrived properly now, in the cold, with his hands in his pockets.

*It is eleven words in a yard.*

*It is not a journey and it is not an expense and it does not require a
scroll or a seal or a contract or anybody's permission. It has never
taken me more than four seconds in my life.*

*She has never told me how she hears it and I have never asked, which is
the only question I have ever had and left, and I left it because it was
hers.*

*Eleven words. And I did not spend them.*

He took his hands out of his pockets.

"Katsuyu," he said, "it's Ruri. If you're free, I'd like to see you."

---

She came about forty seconds later, on the corner of the bench where she
always came, at about the size she always came at.

"Good evening," said Katsuyu.

"Good evening."

"You are standing in the middle of the yard."

"Yes."

"You do not stand in the middle of the yard. You go to the bench and you
put the lamp on and you put a felt down before you speak, and you have
done that on nineteen occasions, and this evening you have put your coat
on the bench and stood on the flags."

She did not say it as an accusation. She never said anything as an
accusation. She said it the way she said *the third finger is doing
something at the second knuckle*, which was as an observation offered
to the person best placed to use it.

"There's a thing I have to say," said Sazare, "and I've built four ways
of saying it and thrown all of them away between the gate and here, and
what's left is that I haven't got a way, so I'm going to do it badly."

"Very well."

---

"I forgot you."

He said it into the cold and it went out into the yard and did not come
back.

"Last Friday was the thirteenth. It was the twelfth Friday. I did not
ask you and I did not come and nobody told you why, and you were where
you were on a Friday and nothing happened.

"I want to be exact about it, because there are several better versions
available and all of them are false.

"I did not decide against it. I did not weigh it up. I was not prevented
and I was not ill and there was no emergency, and I was at a desk in a
tower doing correspondence and I could have walked down that hill at any
point in eleven hours and said eleven words.

"I did not think about you once. The entire day went past.

"And I did not notice until nine o'clock on Saturday night when I got to
the bottom of a page in my own book and found the column empty, and I
was looking for something else at the time."

He made himself finish it.

"I have said in that room, out loud, to myself, about four times, that I
have never assumed the twelfth. I was proud of it. It's the only thing
about my own conduct I have ever been proud of.

"And I assumed the twelfth by not noticing there was one."

---

Katsuyu was quiet for a moment.

"I see," she said.

"That's it. That's the whole of it. I'm not going to explain it because
every explanation I've got is an excuse and two of them aren't true."

"You are asking me to say something."

"...Yes."

"Then I will say the accurate thing," said Katsuyu, "and I would like
you to hear the whole of it before you decide what it is, because I
think you have already decided and I think you have it wrong.

"I did not mind."

---

He stood in a cold yard and had absolutely nowhere to put that.

"I want to be precise," she said. "That is not forgiveness. I have
thought about the word since you began speaking and it does not apply,
because forgiveness requires that something was done, and nothing was
done.

"On the thirteenth I was at the eastern edge of the forest, where I
usually am, and the day passed, and you did not ask. And I want to tell
you what I thought about it, because I do remember, and I would not
alter it to make you feel better and I would not know how.

"I thought: he has not asked.

"And then I thought about something else."

"That's—"

"It is not a rebuke and it is not a kindness," said Katsuyu. "It is what
happened. There was no wound and there was no waiting. I do not wait,
Ruri. I have no arrangement in me for waiting. I am four hundred years
old and I have spent most of it on a hillside, and a Friday is not a
unit that means anything to me except that you have used it."

---

*Right.*

*Say the difficult thing.*

"That's worse," he said.

"I do not understand."

"I know you don't. That's why it's worse and I want to try to say it
properly, and I'm going to get it wrong.

"I came up this hill this evening with a thing to pay and I have been
carrying it since Saturday night, and I built four versions of it, and
the whole of the last six days has had it in it.

"And you have just told me there is nothing to pay.

"Which should be a relief. It is not a relief. It's the opposite,
and I've been working on why for about ninety seconds and I think I've
got it."

"Please."

"If nothing was done to you," said Sazare, "then the eleven Fridays
weren't a thing either."

---

Katsuyu did not move.

"Go on," she said.

"That's the arithmetic and I don't like it and it's still the
arithmetic. If missing the twelfth was nothing, then the eleven I did
ask were nothing as well, and the whole of it is just a series of
evenings where a person turned up and then one where he didn't, and
nothing has ever been at stake in any direction.

"And I do not believe that. I do not believe it because of one sentence
you said to me in this yard in the tenth month and I have had it in
front of me since.

"You said nobody had ever asked you. You said you did not know until
that Tuesday morning that there was anything in you that wanted to be
asked.

"So there is."

He put his hands back in his pockets because they had started shaking
and it was not the cold.

"There is something in you that wanted to be asked. You said so. You
found it out at four hundred years old in a yard on a Tuesday and you
told me about it, which I have never once forgotten.

"And on the thirteenth of the eleventh month it was not asked, and you
have just told me that you thought *he has not asked* and then thought
about something else.

"I think that is true. I think you have reported it exactly.

"And I don't think it's the whole of what happened, and I don't think
you would know if it wasn't, and that is not a criticism of you — it's
that nobody has ever looked at you either."

---

The yard was very quiet.

Somewhere down the hill a gate went and somebody laughed and it was a
long way off.

"That is a considerable thing to say to me," said Katsuyu.

"I know."

"You have said it standing up, in the cold, with no felt down, having
thrown away four preparations."

"Yes."

"And you did it because you believed you owed me a debt that I have just
told you does not exist."

"Yes."

Katsuyu was silent for a while.

"Ruri," she said. "Put the lamp on. My mantle is cold and I have been
here four minutes and you have not once looked at the bench."
