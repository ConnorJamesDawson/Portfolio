# Chapter 51, Scene 3 — An Honest Cause

*Act 4, part D. Konoha, the quartermaster's yard and the tower. Sazare
Ruri, age eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1,588 words.*

---

He asked her on the Thursday, at the bench, and he asked it as a
technical question because it was one.

"Katsuyu. Could I do the wood?"

She did not answer for some time.

"Ask me the whole question," she said. "You have asked me the short
one and I think you have asked the short one on purpose."

"...Yes. All right." He put the file down. "On the fifteenth of the
eighth month I copied a rose and it worked and I could not close it and
it took my hand. The close is the whole of what went wrong. I have the
close now — I have had it since the twenty-third of the eighth month
and I have done it about six hundred times in crystal and I have never
once failed to do it deliberately since.

"I have capacity I did not have. Eleven, and it does not feel like
holding any more, and I do not know what that means.

"And since Saturday I have you *inside* it rather than beside it, which
means that if the close does not fire, there is somebody in the
operation and not watching from the felt.

"So: on your assessment, not mine — could I do it, and come out of it
with the hand I have got?"

---

"I think so," said Katsuyu.

"Say that with the number on it."

"I do not have numbers."

"Then say it in words and don't make it kinder than it is, because I
will hear the kindness and I will discount for it and then I will be
working off a figure neither of us meant."

"Very well." She settled. "I think you would close it. I think that
more than I think most things. The close you do in crystal is now
extremely deliberate and I can see the whole of it and it does not
hesitate.

"What I do not know is whether it is the same close."

Sazare went still.

"Go on."

"You learned to close *crystal*," said Katsuyu. "You have done it six
hundred times deliberately and four hundred thousand times without
knowing, and every one of those was the same operation on the same
material. I do not know whether the close is a thing you do to an
operation or a thing you do to a *kind* of operation — whether it is one
door or a door for each room."

"And if it's a door for each room."

"Then you will reach for the handle and there will not be one, and it
will be exactly the fifteenth of the eighth month again, except that
this time I will be in it with you and I will have hold of it in about
a quarter of a second rather than one and a half."

"Would that be enough?"

"I do not know," said Katsuyu. "It would be a great deal better. I
cannot tell you it would be enough and I am not going to."

---

He wrote the protocol that evening and it took him rather longer than a
protocol usually took, because he wrote it four times.

The first three were correct documents.

The fourth was the one he kept.

*Question. Is the crystal close general to the operation, or specific to
the medium?*

*Reference. Rose 7 of the twelve — dried, fourteen months, saturation
unchanged on every test to date.*

*Method. Match, as on the fifteenth of the eighth month. Produce the
smallest thing I can produce; a stem is sufficient and a flower is not
required and I am specifying that in advance because I know what I did
last time.*

*Close deliberately.*

*Product. Whatever comes.*

*Prediction. It closes. Approximately eight in ten on my assessment,
and Katsuyu will not give a number and has declined twice, which I am
recording because her declining is itself information and it is not
good information.*

Then he ruled a line and wrote a heading he had never written on a
document in his life.

*OPERATOR.*

*Sazare Ruri, 18, Konoha General (2nd fl., fabrication), left hand at
85 per cent following the same operation on 15/8.*

*Known risks to the operator: petrification, from the fingertips,
painless, decelerating and not certainly self-limiting. On the previous
occasion the line reached the mid-metacarpal in eighteen minutes and
was arrested at the carpus after six hours of work by the Hokage,
which is not a resource that exists twice and which I am not entitled
to assume.*

*Consent: mine, informed, and I have written the risk line myself
rather than putting "none identified" and underlining it.*

*Observer in the operation: Katsuyu. She has the authority to stop it
at any point, for any reason, without giving one, and if she says stop
I take my hands off mid-figure and lose the article.*

*Second observer, required, not optional: the Hokage, per the term set
on the sixteenth of the eighth month.*

---

He sat and looked at the last line for about eleven minutes.

Then he did the other thing, which was to write down what he could not
separate, because a man who would not write it down was a man who
intended to use it.

*I want to be accurate about this because I have four days of evidence
that I am not to be trusted on it.*

*On the third of this month I began building a drainage seat, which is
a good article solving a real problem, in order to manufacture a
signature. It is in the drawer under bench three, three-quarters
finished, and I could not complete it.*

*Four days later I have an honest cause to go up that hill.*

*It is honest. I have checked it four ways and I will check it again in
the morning and it survives all of them: the experiment is worth
running, the finding is worth having, the term requires her presence,
and there is no version of this that does not go through that door.*

*And I want to go through that door.*

*Both of those are true at once and I cannot separate them and I am not
going to be able to, and I have spent this evening working out what to
do about that.*

*What I am not going to do is decline to run a safe and correct
experiment in order to protect my own opinion of my motives. That is
not integrity. That is vanity with a ledger, and I would be spending a
finding to buy a feeling about myself, and I have watched other people
do exactly that and thought less of them for it.*

*So: it goes. And this entry goes with it, and it is dated, so that in
four months when I am telling myself a tidy version I will have to walk
past the untidy one.*

---

He sent the slip up on the Friday morning through Shizune, sealed,
because it named the fifteenth of the eighth month and that was not a
thing that went through an open registry.

It was six lines.

*I intend to repeat the operation of 15/8. I believe I can now close
it; I am not certain and neither is the only person who can see it.*

*Per your term I am not doing it alone. Katsuyu will be in the
operation and not beside it, which is new since Saturday and is the
reason I think it is now possible.*

*I am asking you to be present. Any evening you name. I can wait as
long as necessary and I would rather wait than have it early.*

*Protocol enclosed, including the operator section, which exists this
time.*

*— S. Ruri*

Shizune read the outside of it, and the seal, and did not read the
inside, and looked at him for about a second and a half.

"Right," she said.

"It's not a—"

"I know what it isn't." She put it in the small tray rather than the
large one. "Go to work, Sazare-kun."

---

The reply came back at four the same afternoon.

He had costed it at between three days and a fortnight, on the basis
that the Hokage of a village with a border commission running does not
answer correspondence in a working day, and he had written *three days
to a fortnight* on the back of the copy at about eleven in the morning
because he wrote everything down.

Four hours.

It was in her hand and it was two lines and there was no salutation.

*Tuesday. The 14th. Nine in the evening, the yard, and I will come to
you.*

*Bring the protocol. — T.*

He stood in the second-floor corridor and read it three times.

*Four hours.*

*Not the four hours. I want to be careful — she does not sit on
correspondence and there is a security matter in that slip and a
security matter gets answered the same day and that is the whole of the
explanation and it is sufficient.*

*And she is coming to me.*

*Every meeting I have had with her in two years and eleven months has
been in her building, in a room she chose, and I have gone up that hill
about ninety times, and the one exception was a yard at one in the
morning in the seventh month when she came over the wall about a light,
which was a security matter.*

*This is also a security matter.*

*I am aware that I have now written that twice.*
