# Chapter 64, Scene 2 — Nothing

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the second training ground. Sazare Ruri, age
eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~2060 words.*

---

Naruto covered the ten feet in about a third of a second.

That was the first thing and it was the only thing Sazare had time for,
and it was not a thought, it was an arrival: that ten feet had been a
distance eleven seconds ago and was now not one.

He went left.

He did not decide to. His left foot went and his shoulder came round and
he was four feet further out and turned about thirty degrees, and Naruto
went past him with a hand out and got two fingers on the shoulder of his
coat and laughed.

"Nice!"

*What?*

"That was *good*, that was — do that again—"

And came at him again.

---

*Eleven seconds.*

He got the figure at about the fourth exchange and had it clean, because
counting was the floor of him and had not switched off.

*Eleven seconds and I have moved four times and he has touched me three
and I have not done one single thing.*

*Six minutes of hold. Untouched. I have not spent one second of it.*

*I have got two hundred and forty small ones and five and a half walls
sitting in me doing nothing at all and he has been on top of me since
Sakura put her hand down.*

Naruto came again.

He came from the right this time and low, and Sazare had it — had it
completely, at about a third of a second, the way it had come in the yard
on Wednesday: *bar, ankle height, rounded top, eleven inches in front of
the lead foot, second and a half, costs about a two-hundredth* —

He went backwards.

---

They did it eleven times in ninety seconds and it was the worst ninety
seconds of his life and it did not have anything in it at all.

Naruto came. He went away. Naruto came again from a different line
because Naruto did not use the same line twice, and he went away, and at
about the seventh he stopped being able to go away in the direction he
wanted because there was a post behind him, and he went round the post,
and Naruto laughed again and said something admiring.

*He is not trying.*

*He is going at about — I have got no scale. I have got nothing to
measure this against. I have watched people spar about eleven times from
a window and it looked like this and I assumed that was what it was.*

*He has not thrown anything. He has not made a seal. He has come at me
eleven times with his hands and he has touched me nine, and about four of
those he could have put through me and did not, and on the sixth one he
had my wrist and let go of it.*

*He is being extremely careful with me and she told him not to be and he
is doing it anyway and he does not know he is doing it.*

---

At two minutes he made the clones.

There were four. They came out of nothing on his left and did not do
anything clever at all — they simply spread, one to each quarter, and
stood, and the original came at him from the front.

*Right.*

*Four and one.*

*Twenty feet is a perimeter and a perimeter is a circle and a circle has
got one of me at the middle of it, and five points on a circle is not a
thing I have ever had to think about in my life because a bench has one
side.*

He went backwards.

The clone behind him got hold of his collar and put him on the ground.

---

It was not hard.

That was the strangest part and he sat with it afterwards for a
considerable time: it was not hard, and it did not hurt, and there was
about nine inches of drop and a hand in his collar guiding him down, and
he went onto the packed earth on his back with his head held and the wind
half out of him and a clone of Uzumaki Naruto looking down at him with an
expression of enormous encouragement.

"You okay?"

"Yes."

"You want a second?"

"No."

He got up.

*Two minutes eleven seconds. I have been put on the floor once. I have
spent nothing.*

---

"Ruri," said Katsuyu, from the north post, in a voice that carried
exactly as far as it needed to and no further.

He did not look round.

"I know," he said.

"You have not spent anything."

"I know."

"Then—"

"I've had it eleven times," said Sazare, and it came out at a volume he
had not used in about two years. "I have had the article on every single
one of them and I have had it inside about a third of a second and it has
been *right*, and on the sixth one it was better than anything I built on
Wednesday, and I have not done one of them.

"I know what the fault is. You told me on Wednesday morning and you were
right and I have not been able to argue with it since.

"Knowing it has not done anything."

---

Naruto had stopped at about fifteen feet.

He had his hands on his knees, not because he was out of breath — he was
not remotely out of breath — but because he had stopped and did not
appear to have anything else to do with his hands.

"Hey," he said. "Can I say a thing?"

"Yes."

"You're not trying to hit me."

"No."

"No, like—" He straightened up. "I don't mean you're not *managing* it. I
mean you're not *trying*. There's a difference and I can feel it and I've
been feeling it since about the second one.

"When somebody's trying and failing, you can feel where they wanted to
be. Every time. It's the whole of it — you go at somebody and there's a
place they wanted to put their hand and they didn't get there, and you
know it, and they know it, and that's what a fight is.

"There isn't one with you.

"I've been at you eleven times and you have gone away eleven times and
there is not one single place, anywhere, that you were trying to get to."

He put his hands out.

"Where do you *want* to be?"

---

Sazare stood on packed earth eleven feet from him and had no answer at
all.

*He is sixteen and he has just described the whole of it in about forty
seconds off nothing but the feel of my body going backwards.*

*Katsuyu had it on Wednesday off nineteen evenings and seventy-two
corrections and it took her three days.*

*He has had it in ninety seconds and he has not got a word for it and he
has got it more exactly than she did.*

*Where do I want to be.*

He looked at the ground between them.

*Nowhere.*

*That is the honest answer and I have got it at two minutes and forty
seconds on a training ground with five witnesses and a slug.*

*There is no place on this ground I am trying to get to. There is nothing
I want to do to him. There is no position I am working toward and no
outcome I have specified, and I have got two hundred and forty articles
and no idea what any of them are for, because I have never once in my
life started an operation without somebody at the front holding the
paper.*

*And nobody is going to hold the paper.*

*She told me not to build one on Thursday night because I would do it in
thirty-six hours and get it wrong. She was right and I did not build one
and now I am standing here without one.*

---

"I don't know," he said.

"...You don't know?"

"That's the honest answer and I'm not being clever. I have got no
position I am trying to reach. I have got about two hundred and forty
things I could make and I have got no reason to make any of them,
because I do not want anything to happen to you."

Naruto looked at him.

"Then what are you *doing* out here?" he said.

And it was not unkind. That was the whole difficulty with it. It came out
of him at ordinary volume, in the middle of a training ground, with
complete sincerity and no edge in it anywhere, as a genuine question from
somebody who wanted to know.

"Finding out," said Sazare.

"Finding out what?"

"What I do."

"Yeah," said Naruto, "but you're not doing *anything*."

"I know," said Sazare. "That's what I do."

---

There was about four seconds of nothing.

Then, from the western treeline, quite clearly, Jiraiya said: "Go on,
kid."

And Naruto came.

---

It was different and it was different immediately and Sazare had about a
third of a second of understanding that it was different before it
stopped being a thing he could think about.

*He has stopped being careful.*

*That is not four clones spreading out. That is—*

Eleven clones.

They came at him from four sides at once and they did not do anything
clever either, and that was the part he could not get past afterwards:
there was no technique in it, there was no cleverness, it was eleven of
the same person arriving at a man who had never been hit, and the whole
of the answer was that there were eleven and one of him.

He went left because left was the only direction with nothing in it and
there was something in it.

A hand got his wrist and turned it.

Another one had his coat.

And the third one hit him in the ribs.

---

*Oh.*

That was the whole of the thought and it was the only one available and
it arrived complete.

*That is what it is.*

He went down on one knee with somebody's hand still on his wrist and the
whole of the left side of his chest doing something entirely new, and he
had, for about a second and a half, no capacity for anything whatsoever.

Then it came back.

*Sixth or seventh rib, lateral, about four inches below the axilla. Not
broken — I would know, I have felt about eleven hundred of them and a
break reports immediately and this has not.*

*Bruised at the periosteum. Intercostal on that side is going to seize
in about forty minutes and stay seized for a fortnight and there is
nothing anybody can do about it including me.*

*Breathing is compromised on the in-stroke. Not badly. Enough to matter
in about four minutes.*

*Pulse — I am not going to take my own pulse.*

He knelt on the packed earth of ground two at three minutes and eleven
seconds with a boy's hand coming off his wrist and did the last part of
it, and it was not a medical observation and it was the only one that
mattered.

*I am still here.*

---

*Eleven years.*

*Eleven years of arranging, without once deciding to, never to find out
what this was.*

*A market row for eleven weeks. A licence. A council committee in the
sixth month. Four thousand people in the ground in the Land of Water and
a boy going out through the eastern lines because there was nothing else
to do.*

*And every single time, from seven and a half years old, the answer has
been the same shape, and I have never once known what was on the other
side of it, and I have built a life whose entire foundation is a thing I
have never tested.*

*It hurts.*

*And I am on one knee and I can see and I can breathe and my hands work
and the whole of me is functioning, and in about eleven seconds I am
going to be able to stand up.*

He knelt there.

*It does not stop me.*

*That is the finding. That is the actual finding and it is three minutes
and eleven seconds into the first fight of my life and it is worth more
than the table and the range curve put together.*

*It hurts and it does not stop me and I did not know that.*

He stood up.
