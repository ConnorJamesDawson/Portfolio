# Chapter 62, Scene 5 — The Question

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the road down from the tower. Sazare Ruri, age
eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1630 words.*

---

Sakura came as far as the fork because the meeting was at eleven and it
was twenty-five to and she had eleven minutes to spend and had decided to
spend them.

They took the cart down with Naruto on one shaft and Sazare on the other
and the box between them, and it was a bright cold Saturday and the
street was full and about four people said good morning to Naruto and
nobody said anything to Sazare, which was ordinary and which he had
stopped noticing at about seventeen.

"Hey," said Naruto, at the second corner. "Why do you live in the
tower?"

---

*Right.*

*And there it is, at twenty to eleven, in a street, from about four feet
away, in the middle of a completely ordinary morning.*

*And it is not a gossip question. I want that established before I do
anything about it, because if I treat it as one I will handle it wrong.*

*That is a boy who has spent two hours in a room with no furniture in it
and has just watched a man put fourteen books on a handcart and be told
by the Hokage where they are allowed to go, and he has worked out that
the man does not live in the room with no furniture in it, and he has
asked the next question.*

*Anybody would ask it. It is the obvious one. Four hundred people in this
village would ask it if they were the kind of person who asked things.*

*Nobody has.*

*Seventeen days and nobody has asked me. Not Yagi, and she walked up a
hill to be angry on my behalf. Not Kanzaki. Not Emiko, who did not finish
a sentence in a corridor. Not Sakura, and she has fifty-one sheets.*

*Every single one of them decided not to.*

*And this one has not decided anything, because deciding is not a thing
he does, and so I am going to get the honest question that everybody else
has been carrying about for a fortnight.*

"I'm not going to tell you," said Sazare.

---

"Aw, what?"

"That's the answer. I'm sorry about it."

"That's not an answer, that's—"

"Naruto," said Sakura.

"—it's what you said to Sakura-chan, she told me, she said you did the
same thing in the—"

"*Naruto.*"

"I'm just *saying* it's not an—"

"It's a senjutsu posting," said Sakura.

---

The cart went about four more feet.

"It's on the Corps schedule," she said. "Supervised senjutsu training
with medical oversight, on-site, for a Corps sergeant with a prior
near-fatal incident in the same discipline. It went through the board on
the seventh and Shishō signed the covering note and it's got a reference
on it.

"He's a sage, Naruto. There's about four in the world and one of them
lives on the second floor of the tower now because the last time he did
it on his own his hand went to stone up to the wrist and it took Shishō
six hours to stop it.

"So he sleeps in the building where the person who can stop it sleeps.

"That's it. That's the whole thing and it's on a piece of paper with a
reference number and you could go and read it, and you're not going to,
because you'd have to ask a clerk for it and then a clerk would know you'd
asked."

---

Naruto pulled the cart for a bit.

"Oh," he said.

"Yeah."

"That's — I didn't know about the hand thing."

"Nobody knows about the hand thing," said Sakura, "and now you do, and
it's not a story you tell in the street."

"I wasn't going to tell it in the—"

"I know you weren't. I'm telling you anyway because you're going to be in
about nine rooms this week where somebody says his name and you are going
to want to be helpful."

"...Yeah, all right. Fair."

---

Sazare pulled his shaft and did not say anything at all.

*Four things and I want them in order.*

*One. Every word of that was true.*

*The posting is real. The board took it on the seventh. The reference
exists and I have seen it and it is in the drawer. The hand is real and
the six hours are real and she has just given a sixteen-year-old the
fifteenth of the eighth month in about eleven words and got every single
detail right, including the wrist, which is wrong — it was the carpus and
the carpus is the wrist and she is a medic and she said wrist because she
was talking to Naruto.*

*She has not lied to him.*

*She has not lied to him and she has not lied to me and she has not lied
to anybody, and the whole of what she has just done is take a true
document and put it in front of a question that was about something
else.*

*Which is exactly what I did in a lane at twenty past nine this morning
with a piece of crystal on my hip.*

*Two. She knew the reference.*

*Off the top of her head, in a street, at twenty to eleven, including
that it went through on the seventh.*

*That is not a thing anybody knows. That is a thing you know because you
have looked, and there is one place to look, and looking means asking a
clerk, and she has just told him that asking a clerk means a clerk knows
you asked.*

*So she has done a thing she has just told him not to do.*

*And she did it before this morning, because you do not get a board date
by asking on a Saturday.*

He pulled the cart.

*Three. She did not ask me.*

*She has had it since about the twenty-fifth of the eleventh month at the
earliest and the seventh of this month at the latest, and she has stood
on a stair with me twice since then, and she has said nothing, and this
morning she stood in a hall holding a lamp for four minutes and heard the
whole of it and said nothing.*

*And the first time she has used it, she has used it to shut down a
question about me, on my behalf, in a street, in front of the person she
is closest to in the world.*

---

*And four.*

*Four is the one I am going to have to sit with.*

*She has just built me a cover.*

*Not agreed to one. Built one — she has taken a true document and worked
out how to deploy it against the exact question people are going to ask,
and she has rehearsed it, because that came out in one piece and in the
right order and with the reference number in the middle of it, and nobody
produces a reference number in the middle of a sentence in a street
without having said it to themselves first.*

*She has been walking round with that in her mouth for some days waiting
for somebody to ask.*

*She is nineteen years old and nobody has told her anything and she has
worked out the whole of it on her own off nine hundred marks and a lamp,
and instead of coming to me with it, or to her master, or to anybody, she
has gone and got the board reference and built a form of words and
carried it about until it was needed.*

*That is not a friend keeping a secret.*

*That is an operation.*

*And she has run it, on her own, for a fortnight, for nothing, and she
is now going to go and sit in a meeting about a medical annex.*

---

They got to the fork and she stopped.

"Right," she said. "I've got nine minutes."

"Sakura," said Sazare.

"Don't."

"I haven't said anything."

"You were going to say something," said Sakura, "and it was going to have
*thank you* in it somewhere, and if you say it then I have to say *for
what*, and then either I lie to you or you tell me, and I would rather
not have either of those things happen at a fork on a Saturday."

She adjusted the lamp under her arm.

"So don't," she said. "It's fine. It's genuinely fine and I'd rather you
just took it."

*Right.*

*That is the second person this month who has built a sentence
specifically so that I cannot thank them for it, and the first one was
Shizune on the twenty-eighth of the eleventh month at half past eight in
the morning, and I stood at the end of a table and worked out that saying
it would make her acknowledge the whole of it.*

*And I decided I would find another way.*

*I have not found one.*

*It has been sixteen days and I have not found one and I have not looked,
and there are now two of them.*

"All right," he said.

"Good."

"I'm going to find another way."

"Ruri—"

"Not today. But I'd like it noted that I said it out loud at a fork, so
that in about a year, when I do it, neither of you can say I was being
sly about it."

Sakura looked at him for a second.

"That's the most *you* possible way to say thank you without saying thank
you," she said.

"Yes."

"Fine. Noted." She turned to go and then turned back. "And Ruri —"

"Yes."

"You've got that thing on your face again."

"I know."

"You've had it since the twenty-first of last month."

"I know."

"I've got a column for it now," said Sakura, and went off up the road
towards the tower with a lamp under her arm and eight minutes to spare.
