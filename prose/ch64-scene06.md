# Chapter 64, Scene 6 — The Fourth Step

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the second training ground. Sazare Ruri, age
eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1620 words.*

---

He did not build anything.

That was the whole of it and it was the only reason it worked, and he
went over it afterwards about nine hundred times and it never once became
anything more complicated than that.

*There is a piece of my box under his fourth step.*

*It has been there since I came out of the back of it at four minutes
fifty, and it was about eleven inches across then and it is about two
feet across now because it has been eating the made ground for
twenty-six seconds, and it is flat and it is grey and it is the same
colour as the earth and it is under about half an inch of dust.*

*He has not seen it. Nobody has seen it. It has been there the whole
time.*

*Making a thing at nine feet costs a second and a half.*

*Moving one costs a fortieth.*

The fourth step came down.

He closed it.

---

It took the foot at the ankle.

It came up around it in about a tenth of a second — not a spike and not a
grab, just the flat two feet of it going from *lying there* to *a shape
with an ankle in it*, and the shape had a taper on the inside because
every collar he had built on Wednesday had a taper on the inside, and it
did not break anything at all.

Uzumaki Naruto broke his own leg about a fifth of a second later.

---

*Right.*

*Say it accurately and do not let yourself off.*

*I did not break his leg. That is true and it is going to be in the
report and it is going to be the least useful true sentence anybody says
this week.*

*He was at about eleven feet a second and he had both feet committed and
his weight was three inches in front of his lead foot, and the lead foot
stopped, and the rest of him did not.*

*The tibia went about four inches above the ankle. Spiral. I heard it and
I have heard about eleven hundred of them and there is no other noise
like it.*

*And the muscle went at the same instant on the lateral side because
there is nowhere for a leg to go when the foot is fixed and the body is
still travelling, and that is a thing I have written up about forty times
in four years off a ladder or a stirrup or a badly set post.*

*I did not break his leg.*

*I fixed a point and he ran into it.*

---

The thing in his right hand went into the ground.

He had it for about a third of a second and then he did not have it any
more and nobody on that ground did, and what he had instead was a four
foot circle of packed earth that was not there and a noise he had no
comparison for.

*It was a rotation.*

*That is all I have got and it is not enough and I am going to have it
wrong. It was chakra held in a rotation, at extremely high density, in a
sphere, with nothing containing it that I could see, and it went into the
ground at about eleven feet a second because he could not stop and could
not turn.*

*And it has gone into the ground.*

*Four feet of it. Made ground and gravel down to about eighteen inches,
and all of it thrown, and the whole of whatever was in there has gone
into the—*

He stopped.

*Oh no.*

---

The crystal took it.

It took it the way the piece by the post had taken the made ground,
except that this was not made ground: it was four feet of earth with the
entire content of that rotation still in it, and the nearest live edge
was about nine inches away.

It went across in under a second.

*That is not a foot every four seconds.*

*That is — I have got no figure. That is the whole four feet and it has
gone in under a second and it is still going and it is going* outward,
*it has gone past the crater on all four sides and it is going into
ordinary ground now at about three times what it was doing eleven seconds
ago.*

*Because it has eaten.*

*It has had one mouthful of whatever that boy is made of and it is now
three times faster and I have not touched it since I opened it.*

---

Naruto came down.

He came down with his ankle fixed and his leg broken and his weight going
forward and nothing under him, and he was going to go into the packed
earth face first at about eleven feet a second from a height of about
four feet, and he had no hands out because his hands had been full a
third of a second ago.

*Head.*

*He is going into the ground head first and there is nothing to the head
that anybody has got a choice about and she said so at half past nine
this morning in front of five witnesses.*

*Nine inches.*

He put a spike in it.

Blunt. Four inches. Under the sternum, angled back along the line of
travel, and it cost him about a two-hundredth and it went up in something
under a tenth of a second and it was the only article he built in the
whole of the fight that he had costed in advance.

It caught Naruto across the chest and turned him.

---

He went into the ground on his right shoulder and his head hit about a
fifth of a second later on the temporal, at maybe a third of the speed it
would otherwise have done, and he went out immediately and completely and
did not move again.

The ground was very quiet.

*Right.*

*Assess.*

*Out. Immediate loss, no interval, no seizure activity. Airway is open
because he has gone onto his right and not his face and that is the spike
and it is the only good thing that has happened in eleven seconds.*

*Head: temporal, at about a third of terminal. Concussion, certain.
Fracture, possible and I would put it at under a tenth. Bleed, possible
and I have got no way to tell from here.*

*Leg: spiral tibia four inches above the ankle, displaced, and the
lateral compartment has gone.*

*Ankle: still in the crystal.*

*Shoulder: he took it properly, so nothing.*

*Ninety seconds to get the leg free, six minutes to have him off this
ground, and Sakura is eleven feet away and coming and she is better at
the head than I am.*

He was already moving.

---

He got four steps.

And on the fourth step, with the whole of the bowl still in the room, he
found out that it had not stopped.

*It has not stopped.*

*The fight is over. He is on the ground. He is unconscious. It is
finished and there is nothing left on this ground for anything to do and
it has not stopped.*

*It is—*

He stood on packed earth about eleven feet from a sixteen-year-old lying
on his side and had the whole of it at once, which was what the mode was
for and which he had not yet been told the mode existed.

*It is not going outward any more.*

*It went outward for about eleven seconds and it has stopped going
outward and it has turned, and every single live edge on this ground is
now going in the same direction, and I have got twenty-six of them and
they are all doing it and I did not tell any of them to.*

*They are going toward him.*

---

*Right. Why.*

*Because he is the largest thing on this ground.*

*Because a thing that eats goes to the food and there is nothing in this
bowl to eat that compares with him by any factor I can put a number on,
and I looked at him at the beginning of this and thought* I have got to
be careful about looking at him *and then I opened twenty-six articles
and did not think about it again.*

*It has had a mouthful. It got four feet of ground with that rotation in
it and it went three times faster and it has now worked out where that
came from.*

*It is not attacking him. There is no intent in it. It has not got
anything to have an intent with — it is a structure that draws, and I
took the doors off it, and it has been sitting on a hundred and twenty
feet of ordinary earth eating dust and gravel for twenty-six seconds and
somebody has put a *meal* on the floor in front of it.*

*It is a plant going to the light.*

*And it is eleven feet from his foot, and his foot is in a piece of it.*

---

He was running before he had finished the thought.

*Close it.*

*Close all of it. One act, twenty-six objects, it cost me almost nothing
to open them and it will cost nothing to shut them and I can do it from
here.*

*Do it.*

He did not do it.

He got about nine feet and he did not do it, and it was not hesitation
and it was not the fault and it was not anything that had been on that
ground all morning.

It was that at nine feet, with the whole of the bowl in the room, he
could feel the piece around Naruto's ankle.

And it was already inside.
