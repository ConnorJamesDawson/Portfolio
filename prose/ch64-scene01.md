# Chapter 64, Scene 1 — Ground Two

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the second training ground. Sakura Haruno, age
nineteen. POV: Sakura. ~1790 words.*

---

Ground two was a hundred and twenty feet of packed earth in a bowl, with
the three posts at the north end going grey where they had been going
grey since before she was born, and a treeline on three sides, and a
ditch along the eastern edge that carried about four inches of water in
the twelfth month and had a lip on it that had put more genin on their
faces than any instructor in the village.

It was ten past nine and there were six of them.

That was the thing Sakura had been turning over since half past eight,
and she had been turning it over because she had been the one sent to
tell four separate people that the ground was closed.

*Six.*

*Shishō, Jiraiya-sama, me, and three that aren't human.*

*And there should be about forty.*

---

It had gone round the village in three days. She had heard it in the
hospital and she had heard it at the market and she had heard it out of a
chūnin on the south stair who did not know she was involved, and the
version he had was that the Hokage had bet on the crystal one and that
this was known to be a death sentence and that a man in the sealing
office was giving four to one.

Forty people would have come. More.

And at seven that morning Shishō had put a closure on the ground with a
reason on it — *medical observation, Corps* — and had sent Sakura round
with it in her own hand, and there had been no argument anywhere, because
you do not argue with a closure with the Hokage's name at the bottom.

*She has known since Wednesday.*

*She has known something since Wednesday and she has been at that window
and she has not said one word about it to anybody, and this morning she
shut a training ground.*

*You do not shut a training ground to stop people watching a boy lose.*

---

Fukasaku and Shima came out of the treeline at twenty past nine and did
not appear to have arrived from anywhere.

Sakura had met them twice. She had been in the long room on the second of
the twelfth month with a pen in her hand for an hour before Shishō sent
her out, and she had spent that hour writing down things she did not
understand and had not asked one question about any of them since.

"Morning, lass," said Fukasaku.

"Good morning."

"Are you the medic?"

"I'm the medic."

"Good," he said. "There isn't going to be much for you, but I like it
when there's a medic. It settles a room."

He went and sat on the end of the middle post, which was four feet high
and which he got onto without any obvious mechanism, and Shima went and
sat next to him and looked at the ground the way Sakura had once watched
a quartermaster look at a shipment.

"How many feet across, do you reckon," said Shima.

"Hundred and twenty."

"Hm."

"Is that bad?"

"That is *interesting*," said Shima, and did not explain.

---

Katsuyu was on the north post and had been since before any of them got
there.

She had said good morning to Sakura and nothing else, and Sakura had
found herself unable to look at her for very long, because the slug on
the post was a division of the same creature that lived on the Hokage's
contract and had been in a room with her about nine times in three years,
and there was a thing about her this morning that was not right.

*She's worried.*

*I don't know how I know that. It hasn't got a face. It's about nine
inches long and it has got no expression of any kind and I have been
looking at it for eleven minutes and I am completely certain that it is
worried.*

---

Jiraiya arrived last, at twenty-five past, from the direction of the
bathhouse.

He did not sit on anything. He went along the western treeline and stood
about eleven feet inside it with his back against a trunk and his arms
folded, which was the position of a man who intends to be able to move,
and Sakura noticed that and put it away.

Shishō was at the eastern edge, above the ditch.

She had been there since ten past nine, on her own, with the haori on and
her arms folded and her hair up, and she had said about eleven words all
morning and four of them had been to Sakura and had been about where the
kit was going.

*Right.*

*I have got fifty-one sheets on Ruri and about nine hundred marks on him
and I have got about four years on her, and I know exactly what that is,
and I have got no idea what to do with it.*

*She has got a face on.*

*Not a blank one. That's the ordinary one, that's the twice-a-season one
where the whole apparatus goes still, and I have seen that about eleven
times and it means nothing is getting out.*

*This is a face she has put on. She has *chosen* one. There is an
expression on the Hokage of Konohagakure this morning and it has been
selected, and it is about nine-tenths right and there is a tenth of it
that is not sitting properly, and it has not sat properly since ten past
nine.*

*I have never once seen her do that.*

*She has bet on him in public and she is standing at the edge of a ground
she closed at seven o'clock this morning, wearing a face she picked out
of a drawer.*

---

They came at half past.

Naruto came down the western path at a run, because Naruto arrived at
everything at a run, and he had eaten something on the way and was still
finishing it, and he shouted at Jiraiya from about forty feet and got a
grunt.

Ruri came down about eleven seconds later at a walk.

He had the coat on and the felt roll on his hip and nothing else, and he
came down the path and stopped at the edge of the packed earth and looked
at the whole of the bowl for about four seconds in a way that Sakura had
seen him do to a piece of stone about a hundred times.

*He's grading it.*

*He is standing at the edge of a training ground looking at the dirt and
he is doing the thing he does at a bench.*

Then he came in and put his coat on the second post.

---

"Terms," said Tsunade.

Everybody stopped.

"Sakura's the medic and she's the only medic and if she says stop, it
stops, and that is not negotiable and it does not go to me.

"Nothing to the head that you have got a choice about. Naruto, that is
you, and I know what you are going to say and I have watched you fight
since you were nine and I am telling you anyway.

"Ruri. No scalpel."

There was a small silence.

"I know," said Ruri.

"Say it out loud."

"No scalpel, at any point, for any reason, including if I am on the
floor," he said. "It's on the sheet under a line with Thursday's date on
it and I wrote it in front of you."

"Good."

*What?*

*What is a scalpel? He's a fabricator. He hasn't got a—*

*He has got a scalpel. Of course he has got a scalpel, he did a
subperiosteal in the fourth month with clean margins and I was in the
room and I remember thinking that his control was better than mine and
being annoyed about it for a week.*

*And that is a chakra scalpel and I have never once thought about it and
neither has anybody else on that floor.*

*And she has just made him say it out loud in front of witnesses.*

---

"Anything else?" said Naruto.

"Two more." Tsunade did not move from the ditch edge. "It ends when one
of you can't continue or when Sakura calls it, and there is no other way
it ends. Nobody yields. I do not want either of you standing there at
some point deciding whether to be gracious about something."

"And the second one?"

"You don't hold back."

Naruto's head came round.

"Granny—"

"I have watched you do it for eleven years," said Tsunade, "and you do it
to everybody and you have always done it and it is the best thing about
you and it is going to be useless here.

"He has never been hit. He does not know what any of this is. And if you
go at him at half and he learns what half feels like, then in about four
months he is going to be on a road somewhere with the wrong figure in his
head, and that is on me and on you and not on him.

"So don't."

---

Sakura stood next to the kit and did the arithmetic and did not like any
of it.

*She has closed the ground. She has banned the scalpel out loud in front
of five witnesses. She has told Naruto not to hold back.*

*Every single one of those is her being careful.*

*And they do not point the same way.*

*Closing the ground is about what people might see. Banning the scalpel
is about what Ruri might do. Telling Naruto to go properly is about what
Ruri needs.*

*She is managing three different risks at once and one of them is him
being hurt and one of them is him hurting somebody and one of them is
nobody knowing which.*

She looked across at the eastern edge.

Shishō had not moved.

*And she has got money on him.*

---

"Right," said Tsunade. "Ten feet apart. Sakura calls it."

They walked out.

Naruto went out to the middle of the bowl with his hands behind his head
and turned round and grinned, and Ruri came out and stopped at ten feet
and stood there with his hands at his sides in the way he stood
everywhere, which was like a man waiting to be handed something.

*He looks wrong out there.*

*He looks completely wrong. He looks like somebody who has walked onto a
training ground by mistake looking for a corridor.*

"Hey," said Naruto. "Ruri."

"Yes."

"Good luck!"

"Thank you," said Ruri. "You as well."

Sakura put her hand up.

*Eleven seconds. Jiraiya-sama said eleven seconds and Shishō didn't argue
and neither did I.*

She brought it down.

"Go," she said.
