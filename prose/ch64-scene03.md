# Chapter 64, Scene 3 — Ninety Seconds

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the second training ground. Sazare Ruri, age
eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1820 words.*

---

He built the first one at three minutes and twenty.

It was a wall.

It went up eleven feet in front of him, four feet high and about nine
feet across, and it was eleven-deep because eleven-deep was the one that
took a hit and he had not chosen eleven-deep, it had come out of him
that way.

Three clones went round the end of it in about a second and a half.

*Forty seconds.*

He had it before they were past.

*That cost forty seconds of hold. Four-tenths of one wall out of five and
a half, for a thing that has been in existence for one and a half seconds
and has stopped nothing.*

*It is nine feet across. This bowl is a hundred and twenty. Nine feet is
not a wall on a hundred and twenty feet of open ground, it is a piece of
furniture.*

*I have just spent a ninth of everything I have got on a piece of
furniture.*

---

He built the second one at three minutes and thirty-one and he built it
badly for the opposite reason.

*Cheap. Long. Three-deep.*

It went up as a bar at ankle height, thirty feet of it, in an arc across
the front of him, and it cost him about eleven seconds and it was
exactly right and it was in exactly the wrong place.

Naruto was not coming across the front.

Naruto had gone right on the second step of the approach, the way he had
gone right on the fourth and the ninth exchange, and the arc had gone
where he had been at the moment Sazare started it, and by the time it was
there he was eleven feet off the end of it.

*A fifth of a second.*

*That is the whole of it.*

*I am building where he is. Every single one of them. The article arrives
at the place he occupied when I began, and he began moving before I began
building, and there is a fifth of a second in it and a fifth of a second
is eleven feet.*

*I have got no interval between deciding and doing. I have been told that
about four times this month as though it were an advantage.*

*It is not an advantage. It is not anything. Having no interval does not
help you if you are aiming at where somebody has already been.*

---

He got hit twice in the next eleven seconds and one of them was a knee
and he did not go down.

*Right thigh, vastus lateralis, deep. That is going to be a problem in
about four minutes and a serious one in eleven.*

He built the third one on the ground while he was still moving and it was
the first good one of the fight.

*Not where he is. Where he cannot not go.*

The ditch was on the eastern edge and the treeline was on three sides and
the posts were at the north end, and a man coming at somebody standing
four feet off the eastern ditch has about a hundred and ten degrees of
approach and not three hundred and sixty.

He put four spikes in it.

Blunt, four inches, in a scatter across the middle of the arc, at about a
two-hundredth each — and Naruto came into the arc and put his foot down
about eleven inches from the second one and went past it and the whole
thing was worth nothing.

*Four out of four missed.*

*Because I put them in a scatter, and a scatter is what you do when you
do not know, and I do not know.*

---

At four minutes he lost his temper.

He was aware of it afterwards as a finding and he wrote it up in those
words because it was the correct description and because nothing else
covered it: at about four minutes and ten seconds, on the packed earth of
ground two, with a seized intercostal and a dead thigh and eleven of the
same person coming at him, Sazare Ruri lost his temper for the first time
since he was nine years old.

He built six things in about four seconds.

Two walls, eleven-deep, at eleven feet and at twenty. A bar. Nine spikes,
reinforced, which was pointless and expensive and which he did because
they had not worked cheap. A collar. And a thing he did not have a name
for and had not built before, which went up around him at about four feet
and closed over, and which he understood about half a second after he had
made it was a *box*.

He stood inside it in the dark.

*Right.*

*Do it. Do the arithmetic.*

*Two elevens at forty each. The bar at eleven. Nine reinforced spikes at
about four each, which is thirty-six, and I made them reinforced because
I was angry. The collar at two. The box —*

He did not have a figure for the box.

He put his hand on the inside of it and got it off the volume in about
two seconds and did not enjoy any of it.

*The box is fifty-one.*

*Four seconds.*

*Two minutes and forty seconds of hold, in four seconds, on six articles,
of which one has stopped anything and it is the one I am standing inside
and it has stopped* me.

---

Something hit the outside of the box.

It hit it hard enough to move it about an inch, which was interesting,
and then it hit it again in the same place, which was the actual
information: eleven-deep took the first one and did not take the second
in the same spot.

*He is not going round it.*

*He has been going round everything for four minutes and he is not going
round this and I do not know why and I have got about eleven seconds to
work it out.*

The third one came through.

Not a hole — the whole face went, all at once, in about four pieces, and
the light came in and there was a clone in the gap with a fist still out
and an expression of complete concentration on it.

Sazare went through the back of his own box.

He did that by taking the wall out, which took no time at all and which
he had not known he could do, and he came out of the back of it at a run
with about half of it coming down behind him.

---

*Four minutes forty.*

*Capacity: about two minutes twenty.*

*Six articles built. One functional. About nine hundred pounds of graded
crystal lying on this ground doing absolutely nothing.*

He got behind the middle post.

It was a bad position and it was the only one and he had about three
seconds.

*And she told me.*

*She told me on Tuesday morning in that store in about eleven words.*

You will spend the whole of it in ninety seconds on the first four things
that frighten you.

*Ninety seconds. Not four minutes. Ninety.*

*I have done it in about eighty and I have done it in exactly the shape
she described and I had the sentence in my own book in my own hand and I
read it back on Wednesday night, and it has not made one particle of
difference.*

*She has told me twice this month that knowing does not fix a thing and I
have got two findings that say so and I have written both of them up.*

*And this is the third.*

---

Naruto came round the post at four minutes fifty-one.

Sazare put a wall between them.

It was the last eleven-deep he had and it cost forty seconds and it went
up in the gap between the post and the ditch and it was eight feet across
and it worked, entirely, for about two seconds, which was long enough to
get four feet further east and no further.

Then he was against the ditch.

The ground fell away eleven inches to about four inches of water and
there was a lip on it that had put half the village on its face, and
there was a wall on his left and the post on his right and a hundred and
twenty feet of open bowl in front of him with eleven of the same person
in it.

*Right.*

*One minute forty.*

He stopped moving.

---

He did not decide that either.

He had it clearly afterwards and he wrote it that way: that he did not
choose to stop, that he ran out of directions, that there was a wall and
a post and a ditch and a boy, and that his feet stopped because there was
nowhere for them to go, and that he stood on the eastern lip of ground
two with about a minute and forty seconds in him and did the only thing
available to a man who cannot go anywhere.

He held.

*Everything in. Nothing out. Do not spend and do not build and do not
move.*

*I have got a minute forty and I have got no idea what to do with it and
the one thing I know how to do with a quantity is carry it.*

*Nineteen evenings. Two, for seventeen weeks. Nine. Eleven. Fifteen on a
hill on the eleventh of the eleventh month with a slug on a bench and
nothing at stake.*

*Hold it.*

*It is the only thing anybody has ever taught me.*

---

Naruto stopped at about twenty feet.

He did not come in. That was the first strange thing and Sazare had it
immediately, because Naruto had not stopped voluntarily at any point in
five minutes: he came, always, from wherever he was, without any pause
in it at all, and he had just stopped at twenty feet with his weight
back.

The clones stopped too.

"Hey," said Naruto.

Sazare did not answer.

He was holding, and holding took the whole of him, and there was a thing
happening at about the fifth second of it that he had never once had in
eleven weeks on a felt in a yard with a lamp on and nothing at stake, and
he did not have a word for it and did not have any attention to spare for
finding one.

*It is coming faster.*

*That is not right. It comes at the rate it comes. It has come at the
same rate seventy-two times and it does not care what I want and it does
not care that I am in a hurry, and it is coming*

*faster*

"*Ruri*," said Naruto, from twenty feet, in a completely different voice.

And behind him, from the north post, in a voice that carried across a
hundred and twenty feet of open ground and that he had never once heard
her use:

"Do not close," said Katsuyu.
