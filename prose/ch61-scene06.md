# Chapter 61, Scene 6 — Ordinary

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the hospital, the north store. Sazare Ruri, age
eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~2000 words.*

---

The bench went in on the eighth and he laid the first course on it at
about four in the afternoon.

It came up in four seconds.

He looked at it for a moment and then did it again, on a fresh grain,
and it came up in four seconds.

He did it eleven times.

*Four. Four. Four and a quarter. Four. Three and three-quarters, once,
and I have gone back and it was a soft grain. Four. Four. Four. Four.
Four. Four.*

He put the lamp behind the eleventh one.

The light went about two-thirds of the way in and came back cloudy at
the edge.

---

He sat down on the step of the cart door with it between his fingers.

*Right.*

*Do not go fast and do not make it worse than it is.*

*This is not bad work. I want that established at the front. This is
good work. This is the standard I held from about the ninth month of
last year through to the twentieth of last month and it is better than
anything in this village and it will make a splint that holds for six
days and does the job.*

*It is exactly what I have always done.*

*It is not what I did between the twenty-first of the eleventh month and
about the second of the twelfth, which was eleven days, in which
everything came up at three and a half seconds and the light went the
whole way in and the note sat clean on a first course, and the worst
thing I made in that period was better than the best thing I had made in
four years.*

He turned it over.

*I have got about ninety of those in a tin.*

*And this is the ninety-first thing I have made and it is a four-second
course with cloud at the junctions, and there is nothing wrong with the
stock and nothing wrong with the room and nothing wrong with my hands.*

---

*So say it.*

*It has come down.*

*Not gone. I want to be precise, because I have got a strong pull
towards the dramatic version and the dramatic version is false.*

*Nothing has ended. Nothing has changed at the tower. I woke up at six
this morning next to her and we had about four minutes of an argument
about a delegation and she called me something and I have got the whole
of it and I would not give up one minute of it for anything on this
earth.*

*The state has* settled*.*

*That is the accurate word and it is the same word Shikkotsu used about
the fourth voice, and I have been sitting on this step for eleven
minutes trying to find a worse one because I want this to be somebody's
fault.*

*It is not somebody's fault. It is what an elevated state does. Nothing
in a human being holds at that level and I have got about nine pages on
that in chapter four as well, and I read them at sixteen and thought
they were about grief.*

*Eleven days. That is a very long time for it and I would put it at the
outside of the range.*

---

*Now the finding, and I am going to write it and then I am going to sit
here for a bit.*

He got the book out of his coat and wrote it on the step.

*8/12. Eleven courses, north store, new bench. 4s throughout. Light 2/3,
cloud at junctions. Note: four voices, top one wandering on nine of
eleven.*

*This is my ordinary standard and it is unchanged from the tenth month.*

*The 21/11–2/12 output was not an improvement in my work. It was a
state.*

*The state has settled and the work has returned to baseline, and the
whole of the difference is the state, and the mechanism is the one I
wrote up on the 23rd: I hold the entire shape by hand with no fixed form
anywhere in it, and my condition is therefore not a modifier on the last
tenth of the output, it is the output.*

*Which I have known for a fortnight.*

*And which I have been carrying since the twenty-third as an
interesting retrospective fact about eleven years of misfiled data.*

He stopped and looked at what he had written.

*It is not retrospective.*

---

*Say it properly.*

*If my output is a function of my state, then my best work is not
available to me on demand. It never was. It is available when I am in a
condition I cannot produce, cannot sustain, cannot predict the duration
of and cannot measure from inside.*

*I struck a protocol for it on the twenty-third with the words* not
reproducible, do not build anything on this*, and I meant it as a note
about a fortnight.*

*It is a note about my career.*

*Everything I make for the rest of my life is going to come out
somewhere on a range, and the range is about thirty per cent wide, and
the position on the range is set by something that has nothing to do
with the work.*

*Four hundred items on a windowsill.*

*And about a fifth of those are only doable at the top of the range, and
I do not know which fifth, and I cannot get to the top of the range on
purpose.*

He sat on the step in the cold with the book on his knee.

*That is the finding and it is a bad one and I have got no remedy and
Katsuyu's answer is not going to work on this one, because you cannot
wait for a thing to get bored of not happening.*

---

And then the second one arrived.

It came about four minutes later, sideways, unasked for, the way they
did, and he was watching a cat on the laundry roof at the time and had
stopped thinking about any of it.

*I have got an interest now.*

He went completely still.

*Say it. Say the whole of it and do not put a bracket round it.*

*My work is better when I am in a particular state.*

*That state is produced by her.*

*Therefore there is a version of me that has a reason to want things
from that woman which are not about her.*

*Not lying. I am not going to be able to lie and I have never once
managed it. Something much worse and much smaller: a man who notices
that a fortnight of a certain kind of happiness produced ninety of the
best articles of his life, and who begins — not decides, begins — to
handle the thing that produces it.*

*Who is a little more careful about which mornings he brings a problem
to her. Who does not raise a thing on a Tuesday because the run is going
well. Who finds himself, at some point in about four years, managing a
person for output and calling it consideration.*

*I would not know I was doing it.*

*That is the whole of the danger and it is the only part of it that
frightens me. I did not know about the take for eleven years. I did not
know about the grip. I did not know I had been recording my own state in
the materials column since I was fifteen, and it was in ink, in my own
hand, four hundred times, and I read past it about nine thousand times.*

*I do not find my own faults. That is established. It is the single most
reliably established thing about me and it took a slug four minutes to
demonstrate it.*

---

He put the pencil down on the step.

*And there is a third one and I have been avoiding it for about ninety
seconds.*

*She gave me the list.*

*Eleventh of the eleventh month, in that office, drunk, at volume. Six
items, in order, revised, with a note on the fourth one that she has
never explained to anybody.*

*Four is* what I can do for them*. The man who is going to die of
something stupid at forty-one and knows it, and the woman who can put a
hand on him and stop it is not a woman, she is an insurance policy with
a face on.*

*And she said it was the most reasonable thing on the list and the one
that makes her want to throw up.*

*I gave her eight back and I said that not one of mine was one of hers,
and I said it was the finding, and it was, and it was true when I said
it.*

*It is the twenty-third day.*

*And there is now a thing that woman does for my work.*

He sat on the step for a long time.

*It is not on the list yet.*

*It is not on the list because I have just found it and because I have
not acted on it and because as of four o'clock this afternoon it is a
hazard and not a fact.*

*And every single item on her list started as a hazard in somebody who
did not know he was one.*

---

He did the blade at six, which was the second measurement of the day,
because that was the arrangement and the arrangement was in writing.

*8/12, 18:00. Note: four voices, seated, unchanged. Light: full
traverse, unchanged. Hand: nothing. Sixth day open — 148 hours.*

*No degradation of any kind.*

He looked at that for a while, because it was the one line on the page
that was good.

*A hundred and forty-eight hours and nothing.*

*Every article I have ever made was measurably down at thirty. This
thing has been drawing continuously for six days and it is exactly what
it was on Thursday afternoon, and it will still be exactly what it was
in the fourth month, and I will still be able to hold a lamp behind it
when I am forty.*

*The one object I have ever made that does not need me to be happy.*

He put it away.

---

He locked the store at about seven and stood in the laundry yard in the
dark.

*Right. The last one.*

*Do I tell her.*

*I tell her everything. That is not a policy and it is not a virtue, it
is that I have never been able to do anything else, and it is the whole
of what I have got with that woman and I have said so out loud more than
once.*

*And this one is about her.*

*Not about my work. It looks like it is about my work and it is not —
the moment I put it in front of her, she is a woman who has been told
that her presence has a measurable effect on the output of somebody who
depends on her, and she has forty years of a list about exactly that,
and there is not one version of that conversation where item four does
not come up.*

*She would take it well. I want to be honest — she would take it far
better than I would, she would probably laugh, and then she would go
away and think about it on her own at four in the morning for about a
month.*

*And she has spent eleven days telling me I did well.*

He stood in the yard.

*Not yet.*

*I am going to tell her. I want that written down: I am going to tell
her, and it is not going to be a year, and it is not going to be after
something has gone wrong.*

*But not this week.*

He got about four steps up the alley before he stopped.

*That is the first time in eighteen years I have put* yet *on a
finding.*

*And it is the fifth thing this month that somebody has stood between me
and a procedure.*

*Except that this time it was me.*
