# Chapter 62, Scene 2 — Two Genin and a Handcart

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the east side. Sazare Ruri, age eighteen.
POV: Sazare. ~2010 words.*

---

"You're taller than I thought," said Uzumaki Naruto.

He was standing in the doorway of the room with the whole of the landing
behind him and a handcart at the bottom of the stair that he had
evidently got from somewhere without asking, and he was sixteen, and he
had been in the building for about eleven seconds.

"Sorry?"

"You're taller. Everyone talks about you like you're this little
hospital guy, like —" he did something with his shoulders that was
presumably meant to indicate a little hospital guy "— and you're not,
you're taller than Sakura-chan."

"Sakura is five feet two."

"Yeah, but she's *scary*, so she's taller."

"I heard that," said Sakura, from the stair.

"You were *supposed* to hear it, that's why I said it out loud!"

---

Sazare stood in the middle of his own room and had, in the space of
about four seconds, an entirely new experience.

*I can see all of it.*

*He came through that door and I have got the whole of him. All of it,
at once, without doing anything — the shoulders, the hands, the fact
that he has already looked at the box under the sill twice and worked
out that it is the heavy thing, the fact that he is pleased about the
handcart, the fact that he is nervous, which he is, about two per cent,
and it is the only part of him that is under any kind of management at
all.*

*I have not constructed anything.*

*I have not had to. There is no bracket. There is nothing to bracket —
he is putting the whole of it out at whatever volume it happens to have,
and it is arriving, and I am reading it the way I read a note off a
piece of stone.*

*Everybody in my life is a wall with about four windows in it and I have
spent eleven years working out what is behind them off the light.*

*This is a man standing in a field.*

*He is more legible than I am.*

He stood there.

*I have never met one.*

*Not one, in four countries, in eleven years. There has never been a
person I could read faster than they could read me, and Sakura told a
room in an outer office three weeks ago that I have no face and that the
whole village could take me off a wall at forty feet, and she was right,
and I have been that man since I was seven.*

*And this one is worse than me.*

*And I find that it is—*

He looked for the word and did not have it for about two seconds.

*Restful.*

---

"Right," said Sakura, coming past Naruto into the room. "What's the
— oh."

She stopped.

"Is this it?"

"This is it."

"No, I mean —" She turned round on the spot, once, all the way. "Where's
the rest of it?"

"There isn't a rest of it."

"Ruri, you've been in this room for two years."

"Two years and three months."

"So where's —" She made a gesture that took in the whole room and did not
find anything to land on. "Where's your *stuff*?"

"That box is fourteen books," said Sazare. "There are four more in that
pile. The tool roll is under the sill. There are two tins and a lamp and
a cup and a bowl, and the clothes are in the corner and there are two
sets and one of them is on me.

"The futon is the landlord's. The table came with the room. One of the
blankets is O-Shige's."

Sakura did not say anything.

"It's about seventeen kilos," he said, "of which fourteen is paper, and
the reason I asked for a handcart is the box and not the volume."

---

He watched it land on her.

She was extremely good — that was the thing about Sakura Haruno and it
was in his book about nine times — and she got hold of her own face
inside about a second and a half, and what came out of her mouth was
brisk and organised and about the load order.

He had the second and a half.

*Right.*

*She has just done the arithmetic and got the same answer I got at
twenty past eight and it has hit her considerably harder than it hit me,
and she is now going to be very efficient about a handcart for about
four minutes.*

*And she is going to raise it again this afternoon, in a completely
different room, when I am not expecting it.*

*I have got seventeen instances of that pattern off her and it has never
once failed.*

---

Naruto had gone straight past all of it to the box.

He got a hand under each end and picked it up as if it were nothing,
which it was not, and looked at the underneath.

"Books?"

"Books."

"All of it?"

"All of it."

"That's *heavy*." He put it down again. "Are they good?"

"They're mine."

"No, but are they *good* though? Like, would I —"

"You would not," said Sazare.

"Right." Naruto did not appear even slightly wounded. "So what's in
them?"

"Everything I've thought since I was fifteen."

There was a pause.

"That's kind of terrifying," said Naruto.

"Yes."

"Like, in a good way, but also kind of—"

"Yes," said Sazare.

Naruto grinned at him.

*And that is the second one.*

*He has just been handed a sentence that four separate people in this
village have gone quiet at, and he has said the honest thing back
inside a second, and then he has grinned, and he is not managing me and
he is not being careful and he is not doing the thing that Emiko does
where she does not finish the sentence.*

*I have had four years of people being extremely delicate about me and I
have never once minded it and I did not know it was there until it
stopped.*

---

They got the box down in two goes because Sakura would not let either of
them carry it down a stair alone, and got the futon rolled, and got the
tool roll and the tins and the lamp into a crate that Naruto had also
got from somewhere, and it took about twenty minutes, and it was going
to be one load.

Naruto sat down on the handcart shafts in the lane and looked up at him
while Sakura went back for the blankets.

"Hey," he said. "What's that?"

He was looking at the hip.

*Here it is.*

*Thirteen days. That is the first time anybody has asked in thirteen
days and I have walked past about four hundred people with it, and it
has been on the outside of the coat since Tuesday because there is no
version of carrying it under a coat that does not look like carrying it
under a coat.*

*And the first one to ask is a sixteen-year-old with no guard on him at
all in a lane on a Saturday morning.*

"It's an instrument," said Sazare.

"It's a *sword*."

"It's a hilt. There's no blade in it at the moment — the blade's drawn
when it's needed and it comes out of the hilt, and the hilt's the
article. It took two hundred and three hours and it's graded conductor
all the way through and the grading is the entire thing about it."

"Can I see?"

"No."

"Aw, come on—"

"Not because it's secret," said Sazare. "Because there's a rule about
drawing it and the rule is in writing and it's got the Hokage's name on
it, and the rule is that I don't draw a second one while one's out and
I don't draw at all except to a specification."

Naruto's face did a thing.

"That is the most boring possible reason," he said.

"Yes."

"Like, that's *so* boring. That's the kind of thing Iruka-sensei
says—"

"Yes."

"—but also you've got a *sword* and I've never seen anyone in this
village with a—" He stopped. "Wait, is it because of the crystal? Is it
made of the crystal?"

"Yes."

"*That's so cool.*"

---

Sazare stood in a lane on the east side at twenty past nine in the
morning and felt entirely sick.

*It worked.*

*It has taken eleven seconds and it has worked completely and it has
worked on the most guileless person in this village, and he is now going
to go away and tell somebody that I have got a crystal sword, and that
is what people are going to talk about.*

*I designed that.*

*I sat on a floor on the twenty-sixth of the eleventh month and worked
out, in about four minutes, that if I could not take the light off my
own face then the next best thing was to put another one beside it. And
I said it out loud in an office and she said it was the most cynical
thing I had ever said to her and I said yes and then I sat on a floor for
a bit.*

*And I have just run it on a sixteen-year-old who asked me a friendly
question in a lane.*

*He has not been deceived. Not one word of it was false. It is an
instrument, the hilt is the article, there is a rule and it is in
writing and it has got her name on it.*

*And the whole of the reason I have got it on the outside of the coat is
so that he would ask about it instead of looking at my face.*

*And he did. And he is delighted. And I feel like something that lives
under a stone.*

---

"Are you all right?" said Naruto.

"Yes."

"You went weird."

"I know."

"Like, you were fine and then you went—" He did something with his own
face that was so precisely, catastrophically accurate that Sazare had to
look at the wall.

"Yes," he said. "That's about right."

"So what was it?"

*Right.*

*Four options and three of them are lies and I have used up the whole of
my capacity for that this month on a letter.*

"I put this on the outside of my coat on purpose," said Sazare, "so that
people would ask about it."

Naruto blinked.

"Why?"

"Because there is something else about me at the moment that I would
rather they did not ask about, and I cannot do anything about that, and
I can do something about this. So I gave everybody a better question."

"...That's kind of horrible."

"Yes."

"No, like — properly horrible, that's—" Naruto sat back on the shafts.
"Did it work?"

"You asked me about the sword," said Sazare.

There was a silence of about three seconds.

Then Naruto laughed, once, and rubbed the back of his head, and said,
"Yeah, all right, fair," and did not appear to hold it against him in
any way at all, and got up off the shafts and shouted up the stairwell at
Sakura about the blankets.

---

Sazare stood in the lane.

*He has taken it entirely.*

*I have just told a sixteen-year-old that I manipulated him, thirty
seconds after doing it, in a lane, unprompted, and he has said it was
horrible and then he has said* fair *and got up.*

*And there is nothing behind it. I have been looking for the second
thing since he said it and it is not there.*

*He has weighed it, decided it was a small unpleasant thing about a
person who told him about it himself, and put it down.*

*In about three seconds.*

*I would have had that in a book for four years.*

He looked up at the second-floor window with the persimmon in front of
it.

*I am going to have to be careful with him.*

*Not because he is dangerous. Because he is the first person I have ever
met who does not require any work at all, and I have got nine days of
evidence that I do not handle low-effort things well.*
