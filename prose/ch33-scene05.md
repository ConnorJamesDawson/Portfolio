# Chapter 33, Scene 5 — A Tenth of Everything

*Act 4, part C. Konoha, the intelligence building. Sazare Ruri, age
seventeen. POV: Sazare. ~1,281 words.*

---

He went back nine days later and asked one question, and it took them
three weeks to answer it, and the answer was four lines long.

The question was: *did it arrive.*

He put it in writing because Ibiki had told him to put things in
writing and because there was no version of saying it out loud to
Hazama that did not require explaining the whole of Act Two to a woman
with pen calluses who had been extremely kind to him and did not need
it.

*Request: any record of receipt, by the Kirigakure resistance
organisation or its successor administration, of funds transmitted
through the mainland courier chain operating out of Tsuzura, Kanezaki
and Kasado between [dates], via the board-and-peg system associated
with the broker Chōsei. Subject transmitted approximately one tenth of
gross earnings monthly over thirty-one months. Subject does not
require attribution and is not making a claim. Subject wishes to
establish whether the chain functioned.*

He read it back four times before he handed it in and the sentence he
could not get right was the last one, which went through six versions
and ended up as the flattest of them.

*Subject wishes to establish whether the chain functioned.*

---

The answer came back through Ashida, in an envelope, with a T&I stamp
and a covering note from Hazama that said only *this is all of it, and
I am sorry it is not more*.

*1. The courier chain referenced is known to this section and was
assessed as operational throughout the period described. It was one of
at least four such chains. Assessment: functional, with losses.*

*2. Aggregate figures for funds moving through mainland chains in the
period exist in Kirigakure successor-administration records and were
obtained by this section in [month]. Total across all chains, all
sources, thirty-one months: approximately 41,000,000 ryō.*

*3. No attribution by source exists at any level. Contributions were
pooled at the second transfer point as a matter of operational
security. This was deliberate and is standard.*

*4. Uses recorded in aggregate only: transport, forged documentation,
bribery of harbour officials, medical, and maintenance of
approximately nine safe houses on the mainland coast.*

Sazare sat at the low table with the four lines in front of him and did
the arithmetic, because he could not not.

He had sent, over thirty-one months, a little over four hundred and
twenty thousand ryō. He knew the figure to the ryō. He had entered
every payment in a book with the date and the board it went through and
the initials of the man at the counter.

Four hundred and twenty thousand out of forty-one million.

*One per cent.*

He wrote *1%* on the paper and looked at it.

---

It took him about an hour to work out that the number was not the
point, and another hour to work out what was.

*Transport. Documents. Bribery. Medical. Nine safe houses.*

He knew what a safe house on that coast cost, because he had priced
eleven lodgings across two countries at fifteen and had never once
stopped pricing anything. He knew what a harbour official cost, to
within about twenty per cent, because he had bought one himself at
Nagihama at thirteen with money that was not his.

He got out a fresh sheet and did the only piece of work he was capable
of doing that night, which was to convert one per cent of forty-one
million into things.

It came out, on the most conservative assumptions he could construct,
at somewhere between two hundred and four hundred people.

He checked it twice and then a third time with the assumptions pushed
as hard as they would go in the unfavourable direction, and the floor
did not go below about a hundred and forty.

*A hundred and forty people got off that coast on my money.*

*Minimum. Probably more than twice that.*

*I do not know one of their names.*

*I never will. Not one. The pooling was deliberate and it was correct
and I would have insisted on it myself, and I have spent nine years
building exactly that kind of system on purpose, so that nothing could
be traced back along it to anybody.*

*It worked.*

*It worked perfectly.*

---

He put the pen down.

The thing he had not expected, and which arrived at about the second
hour of the morning while he was sitting with a cold cup of tea he had
made and not drunk, was that it was the most complete piece of work he
had ever done.

Everything else in his life had a fault in it he could name. The
crystal was six seconds and a supply chain. The medical stone repeated
eight seconds of somebody else's hands. The street had killed
twenty-two people and he had needed nineteen seconds to turn it off.
He had scored thirty-one in infection. He had closed two wounds that
should have been left open.

And for thirty-one months, in four towns, under a false trade, at a
tenth of everything, he had run a channel that never once failed, was
never once traced, was never once suspected, and delivered every
single ryō it ever carried into a system that used it to move
frightened people out of a country.

Nobody knew. Nobody was ever going to know. There was no ledger in the
world in which that appeared under his name, and there never could be,
because the whole design of it was that there could not be.

*I did one thing without a fault in it,* thought Sazare Ruri, at a low
table in a rented room at two in the morning, *and the price of doing
it that well is that it does not exist.*

*And I would build it exactly the same way again tomorrow.*

*Both of those are true and neither of them cancels the other and I do
not know what to do with a thing that is shaped like that.*

---

He wrote it up at the end, because everything got written up.

*Chain: functional. Losses acknowledged, unspecified.*

*My contribution: 420,000 ryō over 31 months. Approx. 1% of aggregate.*

*Attributable: nothing. By design. My design as much as theirs.*

*Estimated persons moved on my share: 140 floor, 200–400 likely. No
names. No possibility of names, now or ever.*

*Finding: it is the only thing I have ever done that went entirely
right, and it is the only thing I have ever done that leaves no trace
whatsoever, and those are the same fact.*

He stopped. Then he added the honest half, because Kanzaki had told him
in a yard full of scales that an audit does not improve events and he
had believed her for two years.

*I want somebody to know.*

*That is the whole of what I feel about this and I have been avoiding
writing it down for four hours. Not credit. Not the money back. I want
one person alive to know that the chain out of Kasado had a hundred and
forty people in it that were mine, and there is nobody, and the reason
there is nobody is that I was careful, and I was careful because
careful was the only way it worked.*

*Kohaku never told me what the drops were either.*

*I have built my sister's system twice. Once as her courier when I was
nine and did not know, and once as myself when I was fifteen and knew
exactly. And I have found out tonight that the second one worked as
well as the first one did.*

*I do not think that is a good thing and I cannot make it stop being
one.*
