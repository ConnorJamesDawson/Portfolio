# Chapter 69, Scene 2 — The Cover

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the north fields. Sazare Ruri, age eighteen.
POV: Sazare. ~730 words.*

---

He raised it at the field on the Tuesday, after the morning's
footwork, under the standing protocol: any new application of the
open-article principle went to the keepers before it went to paper.

"State the application," said Fukasaku, from the cloth.

He stated the application.

There was a silence of a texture the field had not previously
produced.

Jiraiya had gone very still on the reject bin — actually he had
gone still the way a dam goes still, with visible engineering
holding back visible pressure — and when he finally spoke it was
with the gravity of a man conducting a state funeral, which was
itself the tell, because Jiraiya did not do gravity except as a
container for its opposite.

"Load-bearing crystal," he said. "In the underpinnings. Of the
kunoichi of the Hidden Leaf."

"Yes."

"Self-powering. No chakra cost. Distributes the anchor load off
the shoulder grooves and the rib line."

"Yes."

"And you are bringing this to me," said Jiraiya, "for security
review."

"You have a veto and he has a veto. The articles draw. Anything
that draws sits next to the spring, and the spring has terms."

The dam held for one further second, and then Jiraiya put his face
in both hands and made a sound like a kettle being deeply moved,
and Fukasaku ignored him with four hundred years of practice and
answered the actual question.

"The firewall holds if the product stays on the public side of the
line," said the old toad. "Drawing for self-maintenance is in your
Corps files since the autumn — a stone that keeps itself is old
news wearing new cloth. What stays behind the line is storage,
grading for release into a person, and every word of the spring.
An article that holds a strap steady tells nobody anything. Keep
the seals to load work — nothing that banks, nothing that feeds —
and the mountain has no objection to brassieres."

"And I want it minuted," said Jiraiya, emerging, wiping one eye,
"that the mountain's elder just said that sentence, in a field, in
the frost, and I was present." He got off the bin, and the levity
dropped away between one step and the next in the manner Sazare
had learned to watch for, because what came after it was always
the trade. "Now the part you haven't seen, kid, because you're
honest and it takes a career of lying to see it. The month sheets.
The blade's readings. Right now, anything of yours that draws is
*interesting* — one article, one maker, one file, and interesting
files get read by exactly the people we built the terms against.
But a village where every third woman is wearing a draw-article to
work — where the clerks file crystal-draw numbers by the *crate* —
in that village, a sword that drinks a little nature energy is a
rounding error. Nobody audits the ordinary." He grinned, enormous.
"You're not compromising the spring's cover, kid. You're
*manufacturing* it. Wholesale. In lace."

"The pilot garments will not have lace. Lace is a snag hazard."

"He's going to put that in the docket," said Jiraiya, to Fukasaku,
delighted beyond any capacity to contain it. "Word for word.
They're going to read it out at the board." He clapped Sazare on
the shoulder with the hand that had thrown him four hundred times.
"Cleared, with both vetoes reserved and one condition of my own:
when the requests start — and they will start, faster than you
believe and from ranks you don't expect — you come to me before
you agree to anything for anyone outside the village. That's not
teacher's vanity. Export is where four countries start paying
attention to what the Leaf grows, and we have just spent a
fortnight deciding what they must never pay attention to."

*Entry, Tuesday. Cleared at the field. The firewall is the public
principle; the fleet of ordinary articles becomes the spring's
camouflage — he found in four seconds a value I had not seen in
four days, which is the recorded difference between an engineer
and a spy.*

*Also entered, because the book gets the whole of it: the Toad
Sage of Mount Myōboku laughed until he required the bin for
support, and then cleared the project on strategic grounds, and
both reactions were fully professional and I have stopped being
able to tell them apart, and I suspect that is the lesson.*
