# Chapter 64, Scene 9 — What You Did

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the hospital. Sazare Ruri, age eighteen.
POV: Sazare. ~2660 words.*

---

Jiraiya found him at about six, on the bench in the corridor outside
theatre two, where he had been since half past two because he had sat
down when he was told to and had not worked out what came after that.

He sat down on the other end of it.

"You never drew the sword," he said.

"No."

"Not once. I watched the whole of it and you did not put a hand on it,
and there was about eleven seconds at four minutes fifty where anybody
alive would have."

"There's no specification."

"Kid—"

"There is a rule and it is in writing and it has got her name at the
bottom," said Sazare, "and the rule is that I do not draw except to a
specification, and the panel on that instrument says *unknown* in my own
hand and it has said it since the eleventh of this month and it is on a
shelf in a dye-works where I can see it from the bench.

"I went onto that ground this morning with the best thing I have ever
made on my hip and no lawful reason to draw it, and I said so out loud in
her office on Wednesday and neither of us did anything about it for four
days."

"And?"

"And it is still *unknown*," said Sazare. "I have been sitting on this
bench for three and a half hours and I have thought about it twice and I
have not got any further than I was on Thursday.

"So that is the whole of the answer to question six and it took eleven
sheets of leaf and four elders and a training ground, and the answer is
that I do not know, and it is going to stay *unknown* until it does not,
and I have got no idea when that is."

---

Jiraiya sat with that.

"Right," he said. "Now the other thing, and I have been outside that
theatre for four hours working out how to say it and I have not got a
good version, so you are getting the bad one.

"You went into sage mode."

Sazare turned his head.

"...What?"

"On the eastern edge, at about five minutes and fifty seconds, standing
in a ditch with a wall on your left and a post on your right. You held
for about twenty seconds and then it came, and it was on you for about a
minute and a half, and it went off somewhere in the four seconds after
you closed everything."

"That's not—"

"Kid."

"I was *holding*," said Sazare. "That is the whole of what I did. I ran
out of directions and I stopped and I did the exercise, because it is the
only thing anybody has ever taught me and there was nothing else on that
ground I knew how to do.

"I did the same thing I have done on a felt in a yard seventy-two times
with a lamp on."

"Yes," said Jiraiya.

"So—"

"That is what it *is*," said Jiraiya.

---

He put his hands on his knees.

"I have been going over a conversation since Tuesday and I have got about
four things wrong in it and this is the largest one.

"On the sixteenth of last month, on the roof of that tower, at about
half five in the morning, I told you that you were a sage. I told you
there was a word and that there are four of us and I watched you take it
and I watched what it did to you, and I have been pleased about that
morning for five weeks.

"And I did not tell you there was a *state*.

"Not because it was secret. It has never been secret. It did not occur
to me — it has never once occurred to me in thirty-one years, because
every single human being who has ever done it has been taught it, by a
toad or by a snake, deliberately, over years, as a thing you are working
toward.

"Nobody arrives at it. There is no arriving. You are taught to gather and
then you are taught to gather *more*, and then one day, after about four
hundred attempts, with two old toads shouting at you, you go over a line
and somebody tells you that you have gone over it.

"There is no version where a man does it standing in a ditch on the
twentieth of the twelfth month because he was frightened and ran out of
places to go."

---

Sazare sat on a bench in a corridor.

"What is it?"

"It is what happens when you stop carrying it and start being in it,"
said Jiraiya. "That is the best I have got and I have had thirty-one
years to find a better one.

"Gathering is holding a quantity. Everybody stops there. It is a very
useful place to stop and I know four people who can do it and about
eleven hundred who cannot.

"The mode is what happens when you go on holding past the point where
holding is a thing you are doing.

"And then the whole of you is made of it for a while, and everything you
have got works better, and everything you can perceive gets larger, and
you can do things to the *nature* of what you are working with that you
cannot do otherwise."

"The crystal," said Sazare.

"The crystal."

---

*Right.*

*Say it all at once and do not soften any of it, because I am going to
have to write it tonight and it is going to be the worst entry in
fourteen books.*

*I could not make crystal grow. Crystal does not grow. It is inert; it
draws from the air and it holds what it has got and it does not extend
itself, and I have got nine hundred articles and eleven years and not one
of them has ever added a millimetre to itself.*

*This morning I took the close off twenty-six pieces and they ate a
training ground.*

*And I did not learn anything between Thursday and this morning.*

*The wood lesson has been in my hands since the fifteenth of the eighth
month — a plant is built by drawing, so the structure is the intake, and
I wrote that in the ninth month and I have got it underlined.*

*I have had the whole of the theory for four months and I could not do
it, and this morning I could, and the only thing that changed is that I
stood still for twenty seconds in a ditch.*

*It was not knowledge. It has never once been knowledge. It is a state,
and I was in it, and I did not know I was in it, and I did not know it
existed.*

He put his hands over his face.

"I can't do it again," he said.

---

"No," said Jiraiya.

"Say it properly."

"You cannot do it again," said Jiraiya, "and I want you to hear the whole
of that before you start on it, because you are going to go home tonight
and try.

"You did not enter that on purpose. You have got no procedure and no
trigger and no idea what the conditions were, and the honest list of what
was true at five minutes fifty this morning is: you had been fighting for
six minutes, you were at about a minute forty of capacity, you had a
seized intercostal and a dead thigh, you were cornered, you were
frightened, and you stopped moving.

"You cannot reproduce that. You can reproduce *stopping moving*, and you
will, tonight, on the floor of a room in a tower, and it will not work,
and you will do it about four hundred times over the next three months
and it will not work, and at some point you will get a bad one and hurt
yourself."

"So how do I—"

"You get taught," said Jiraiya. "That is the only answer anybody has ever
had. Four hundred attempts with somebody who knows what the line is
standing next to you telling you when you have gone over it.

"And the only creature in this world who can teach you is a slug who has
never taught anybody anything, who told you two days ago that your eyes
had gone clear and that you had her markings, and who let you laugh at
it."

---

*She said it.*

*Thursday evening. Eleven-minute hold, longest since the eleventh of the
eleventh month, and she said it flat, twice, in the ordinary voice, and
she said she had no framework and would not invent one at half past five
in the afternoon.*

*And I told her it was the first joke she had ever made and I offered to
write it down.*

*And she let me. She let me because she genuinely did not know, and
because I laughed, and because she has never once pushed me on anything
in nineteen evenings.*

*Two days.*

*I have got a state I cannot enter and the one being who has ever seen
the outside of it told me about it on a step on Thursday and I made a
joke.*

---

Tsunade came down the corridor at about seven.

She had been up on the ward and she had the file under her arm and she
sat down on the bench between the two of them, which put Jiraiya at one
end and Sazare at the other, and she did not say anything for about
eleven seconds.

Then she said, "The IOU."

"No," said Sazare.

"I haven't—"

"I know what you're going to say and the answer is no and I'd like to say
why before you say it, because if you say it first I will have to argue
with a thing you have already offered."

"Go on, then."

"It isn't a win."

He put his hands on his knees.

"There is a boy on the second floor of this building with a spiral tibia
and sixty per cent of a lateral compartment and a concussion, and
fourteen and a quarter inches of my work came out of his femoral
compartment in forty-one sections this afternoon, and it took me four
hours and eleven minutes and I used a thing I wrote *not to be used*
under a line on Thursday.

"Nobody is going to call that a match. Sakura never called it. There is
no result. It stopped because a medic went onto the ground, and the
reason the medic went onto the ground is that I put him there."

"That's a result," said Jiraiya.

"It is not a—"

"Kid, in about forty countries that is the *only* kind of result there
is, and I am not being clever and I am not enjoying this. You were on
that ground for six minutes and at the end of it he was on it and you
were not, and every single person who was there this morning knows
exactly what happened and about nine hundred people who were not there
are going to be told something else by Sunday.

"You have won a fight against Uzumaki Naruto.

"That is going to be true for the rest of your life whether either of us
likes it and whether or not anybody says the word."

---

Tsunade put the file on her knees.

"I'm paying it," she said.

"Hime—"

"I said the terms in front of a witness on Wednesday morning and the
terms were unconditional, any time, any purpose, no expiry, and I do not
get to ask and I do not get to refuse, and I said them out loud
specifically so that I could not do this."

"Do what?"

"Decide afterwards that it did not count," said Tsunade.

She held it out.

It was four lines on a piece of ordinary paper with a date on it and her
name at the bottom, and it did not have a reference number, and it did
not say what it was for.

He did not take it for about four seconds.

*She wrote this some time before seven o'clock this evening, after
standing in a theatre for four hours, having watched a thing happen on a
training ground this morning that has put her the colour of the underside
of a leaf.*

*She has had about eleven reasons to not do this since ten o'clock.*

*And she did it in four lines and did not put a reference on it, because
a reference is a thing people can request against.*

He took it.

"I'm not going to use it," he said.

"I know you're not."

"I want to be clear that this is not humility and it is not a gesture. I
have got a piece of paper in my coat that is worth more than this
building and I have never asked anybody for anything in my life, and I
have got no procedure at all for holding a debt that somebody owes *me*,
and I would rather have almost anything else."

"I know," said Tsunade. "That's why it's the prize."

---

Naruto woke at about twenty past eight.

Sazare was in the room because Yagi had put him in the room, on the
grounds that he was the one who knew what had come out of the leg and
that if there was going to be a fit it was going to be in the first
twelve hours, and he had been sitting in the chair by the window for an
hour and ten with a chart he had already read four times.

The boy came round the way people came round, which was badly and in
sections, and got about eleven seconds of not knowing where he was, and
then found the frame on his leg and looked at it.

Then he found Sazare in the chair.

And his whole face came apart.

"That," said Uzumaki Naruto, "was the *best* fight I have ever had."

---

Sazare sat in the chair by the window.

*Right.*

*Do not say anything yet.*

*He has got a spiral tibia and a pinned fibula and sixty per cent of a
compartment and a head injury, and he has been conscious for about
fourteen seconds, and the first sentence out of him is about the fight.*

*And he means it. There is nothing in it. I have got about nine hours of
him now and there is not one thing anywhere in that boy that is not
exactly what it looks like.*

*He is delighted.*

*I put him on that floor and he is delighted and he is going to be
delighted about it for the rest of his life, and he is going to tell
people, and he is going to tell them in a way that makes me sound like
something worth having in a village.*

*And I have got four hours and eleven minutes of his leg on a tray.*

"Hey," said Naruto. "Hey — what was that? At the end. With the — it went
all *frosty* and I couldn't—"

"I don't know," said Sazare.

"...You don't know?"

"I have been asked that four times today by people who were on that
ground and I have given all four of them the same answer, and it is the
true one and it is going to go on being the true one for some time.

"I do not know what I did.

"I know what it was called about two hours ago because a man told me in a
corridor. I do not know how I did it and I cannot do it again and there
is nobody alive who can teach me, and there is about eleven hundred
pounds of it on ground two which is going to be chalk by Friday."

Naruto thought about that.

"So you can't do it again," he said.

"No."

"Aw," said Naruto. "That's a shame. That was *great*."
