# Chapter 56, Scene 6 — Thirty-One Hours

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the room over the persimmon tree. Sazare Ruri,
age eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1190 words.*

---

He got home at about nine and lit the lamp and got the notebook out and
opened it at the day.

He did the ordinary things first, because the ordinary things were
ordinary: the count of the day's work, the four hundred and six, the
forty-nine tied as fifty, the eleven minutes, the cover. He wrote the
cover up without softening it and put *no remedy identified* under it
and did not put anything under that.

Then he came to the bottom of the page, where the column went.

The column had been at the bottom of every page since the third month
of the year he was ten.

*Ex. 1 —*

He stopped with the pencil on the paper.

---

*I have not done it.*

*Right. When.*

He went back through the book.

It did not take long, because there was nothing in it. The eleventh: a
hill, and fifteen, and a line under it. The twelfth: nothing. The
thirteenth: nothing. The fourteenth, in front of him, blank.

*Three days.*

*The longest previous gap is two, in the fifth month of last year, when
I had a fever of thirty-nine and could not hold a grain, and I wrote
down that I could not hold a grain, and I wrote it down on both days.*

*This is three and I did not notice on any of them.*

He sat back against the wall.

*And it is not the paperwork. Say it properly. It is not the paperwork,
because I have had eleven-hour days at a bench and gone up that hill
afterwards at midnight, and I have done the exercise in a corridor
outside theatre two waiting on a set of results, and I did it in a
ward chair at seventeen with a hand that would not close.*

*There has never been a day too full for two minutes.*

*There has been a day too full for two minutes three days running and
the thing filling it is not work.*

---

He went further back and did the other one, because he could feel it
coming and there was no version of the evening where he did not look.

*When did I last think about the work.*

*Not do it. Think about it. The actual question — how does the thing
hold, why does it go, what is the fault in it.*

He looked for it.

*Thursday: nothing. Not once. Four hundred and sixty acknowledgements
and an out-tray.*

*Friday: nothing. A docket with an alloy hardness wrong on the third
page, and that is materials and it is not the question, that is
arithmetic with a metal in it.*

*Today: nothing.*

*Wednesday morning: yes.*

*Wednesday morning, at about twenty past seven, lying on my side in a
bed I had no business being in, for perhaps ninety seconds.*

He put the pencil down.

*And it was about a seal on her forehead.*

---

*So that is the whole of it in one line and I am going to write the
line and I am not going to enjoy it.*

*The only time in three days that the work has been in my head, it came
in as a property of her.*

He sat and looked at the wall for a while.

*I have been assuming, since Wednesday, that the thing happening to me
is a displacement. That she has taken up the room the work was in, and
that this is a cost, and that I would have to decide whether to pay it.*

*That is not what has happened.*

*The work has not been pushed out. The work has been annexed. It went
in on Wednesday morning as a question about her and it has not been
back since in any other form, and if it comes back it is going to come
back the same way.*

*And I do not know what that does over a year.*

*I have got a creed. I have had it since I was fifteen and I have
written it in the front of four books:* I am not going to get stronger.
Find out how it works.

*I have not found out how anything works since Tuesday.*

*And I do not mind.*

*That is the finding. That is the actual finding of the last three days
and it is not the count and it is not the cover. I have gone three days
without asking the only question I have ever had, and it has not cost
me one moment of discomfort, and I did not notice until nine o'clock
tonight when I got to the bottom of a page.*

---

He wrote the column in.

*Ex. 1 — not attempted, 12th, 13th, 14th. No cause but the true one.*

And then, underneath, because it was the honest half:

*I have been happy for three days.*

*I do not have a previous entry for that word and I have looked. I have
got 'satisfied' nine times, all of them about an article. I have got
'not unhappy', twice, both in the ninth month of last year. There is
nothing else in fourteen books.*

*So I have no baseline and no comparison and I cannot tell whether this
is a recovery or the beginning of something going badly wrong, and the
two of them look identical from inside, and there is nobody I can ask,
because the three people who would know are Shizune, who is standing
between me and two procedures; Katsuyu, who—*

He stopped.

---

He sat absolutely still.

*Friday.*

*Yesterday was Friday.*

*I have asked her on eleven Fridays. Eleven, since the second, no
contract, and she has said yes eleven times and I have written down
every single one of them and I have never once assumed the twelfth, and
I have said that to myself in those words about four times, in this
room, at this table, as a thing I was rather proud of.*

*And yesterday was the twelfth Friday and I did not ask.*

*I did not decide not to ask. I did not think about it and choose. I
was in a tower at the fourth hour and then I was at a desk until seven
and then I walked home and went to sleep, and the day went past, and
she was four hundred miles away in a forest on a Friday and nobody came
and no reason was given.*

*She will not have minded. She will have assumed I was busy and she
would be right and she would not have written it down.*

*She would not have written it down.*

*I would have.*

He got up and stood in the middle of the room for a moment and then sat
down again, because there was nowhere to go and nothing to do about it
at ten o'clock at night on a Saturday.

*Add it to the entry.*

*Twelfth Friday: not asked. Not declined, not deferred, not busy.*

*Forgotten.*

*This is what it costs. It has taken three days and it has not come out
of the work.*

*It has come out of the one person who volunteered.*
