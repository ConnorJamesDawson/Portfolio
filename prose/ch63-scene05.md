# Chapter 63, Scene 5 — What She Cannot Say

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the Hokage's tower. Tsunade, age fifty-three.
POV: Tsunade. ~1740 words.*

---

She went back up at six and did four hours of files and did not take in
one word of any of them.

*Nine.*

*Nine out of nine, at a walk, on flat ground, in his own yard, from nine
feet, by a woman he sleeps next to.*

*Not one.*

She put the pen down at about eight and sat back.

*Say it in four sentences, the way you make them do it.*

*Eighteen-year-old male. Full capacity, complete technique, no combat
history of any kind. Presented with a slow approach at conversational
distance, nine repetitions, and disengaged on all nine with no defensive
or offensive response.*

*And the fifth sentence is the one I have not been able to put in my own
mouth since about eleven o'clock this morning.*

She got up and went to the window.

*The fifth sentence is that he is not going to fight.*

*Not* cannot*. Not* is not able to*. He is a sage with jōnin reserves and
an operation that puts four inches of graded article anywhere inside
forty feet in under a second, and he could have stopped me at any point
on any of the nine and he had the answer on at least six of them because
the slug watched it arrive.*

*He is not going to.*

---

*And now do the honest part, which is that you have known this for a
year and a half and have never once written it down.*

*Kiri. Fourth month of the purge year. Seven and a half years old, out
through the eastern lines, on his own, with nothing.*

*He ran.*

*And that is not a criticism and I would not let anybody say it in front
of me and I would take the head off a man who did — he was seven, and his
mother was in a frame shop, and there was nothing else on this earth for
him to do, and about four thousand people who did not run are in the
ground.*

*But it is the first entry.*

*It is the first entry in the whole of him, and everything since has been
the same shape and I have got three years of it.*

*He was cheated on a market row for eleven weeks and he paid it and
entered it under rent.*

*The council put a licence on his work in the fifth month and he did not
argue, he complied, and then he built the reject table.*

*Ariake Fumi took his throughput figure off him in front of eight people
in the sixth month and he sat there and let it happen and thanked me
afterwards for not saving him.*

*Kudō has been arguing his reparations claim for two years and he has
never once been in the room.*

*Every single one of those was correct. Every one. I would have done four
of them myself and the other two I would have got wrong.*

*And there is not one instance, anywhere, in eleven years, of that boy
moving toward a person who was moving at him.*

*He builds. He works. He goes round. He goes away.*

*He has never in his life hit anybody and I do not think he knows that
about himself.*

---

She stood at the window for a long time.

*So Friday.*

*Friday is not the problem. I want that clear before I go any further,
because I have been sitting here since six letting Friday be the problem
and Friday is nothing.*

*Naruto is not going to hurt him. That boy has never hurt anybody in a
training match in his life, he does not know how, he will go at him with
about a third of what he has got and he will spend the whole of it
grinning, and Sakura is going to be nine feet away with her hands free.*

*He will lose. He will lose in about eleven seconds or he will lose in
about four minutes and either way he will get up and he will have four
pages by Sunday and he will be pleased about it, because that is the one
thing about that boy that has never once failed.*

*Friday is the safest place in the world for this to happen.*

*That is why I said yes to it on Monday and it is why I have not tried to
stop it, and I have been telling myself for three days that I said yes
because he wanted it.*

*I said yes because I want him to find out in front of a referee.*

She put her hand flat on the frame.

*Because in about four months it is going to be a border, or a road, or
a room, and it is going to be somebody who means it, and the same nine
out of nine is going to happen with nobody counting.*

*And that is the thing I actually cannot get out of my chest.*

---

*Right. So tell him.*

She turned that over for about eleven minutes and could not get it to
stand up anywhere.

*Tell him what, exactly.*

*"Ruri, I have watched you for three years and I do not think you will
fight back."*

*And he will take it. He will take it entirely, without one word of
argument, because he takes everything, and he will go away and write four
pages about it tonight and he will be on that ground on Friday morning
having decided to correct it.*

*And a man who has decided to correct an eleven-year-old reflex in
thirty-six hours is a man who is going to do something on Friday morning
that he does not mean, at the wrong moment, to a sixteen-year-old,
because I said a sentence to him on a Thursday night.*

*That is not a risk. That is what will happen. I have watched him do it
four times with smaller things.*

She went back to the desk.

*Second option: say nothing and let Friday do it.*

*Which is what I have already chosen and it is the correct one and I hate
it, and the reason I hate it is not the fight.*

*It is that I will have watched him walk onto that ground knowing
something about him that he does not know, having decided not to tell
him.*

*Which is the sixth time in five weeks that somebody has stood between
that boy and a thing, and he has counted every one of them out loud, and
on the second of the twelfth month he told a room he was getting used to
it.*

*And every single one of the other five was me or Shizune or Sakura or
Jiraiya deciding something on his behalf for four minutes.*

*This one I would be doing for three days.*

---

She poured one and did not drink it.

*And the third thing, and this is the one I have been walking round since
about half four.*

*I have got money on him.*

*In public, at a table, in front of Jiraiya and three other people, on
Tuesday, out loud.*

*And by now it is all over this village, because Jiraiya made sure it
was, and every single person who has heard it has laughed and said the
same thing, which is that she has cursed the poor little sod, and about
four of them have stopped laughing and thought about it properly and I
know exactly which four.*

*And I did it because I could not do the other thing.*

*I was asked which way and I had about a second and a half and the
sensible money was on the boy and I knew it was on the boy and I have
been saying since Monday that Ruri is going to lose —*

*— and I could not say his name in a bet against him in front of
witnesses.*

*Forty years. Forty years of not being able to pick a winner and the one
time it has ever cost me anything I have picked with my mouth instead of
my head and it is going to be quoted in a bathhouse by Sunday.*

She looked at the cup.

*So: I believe he is going to lose. I have arranged for him to lose
somewhere safe. I have not told him the thing I know. And I have staked
money on him winning in front of the only man alive who can read me.*

*Four positions, all of them mine, and not one of them agrees with any
other.*

*And every one of them is true.*

---

The stair went at about half nine and it was his tread.

She did not turn round.

*Right. He is going to come in here with four pages of a conversion table
and a range curve and he is going to be pleased about it, and he is going
to want to show me, and the sheet is going to be extremely good.*

*And at some point in the next forty minutes he is going to ask me
something, because he has been building up to it since about five o'clock
this afternoon and I have watched him do it — he did it on the step with
the scalpel sheet and he did it again at the door and he stopped both
times.*

*And I have got no idea what it is.*

*Which is the first time in three years.*

He knocked, and she said it was open, and he came in with four pages and
a lamp.

"I've got the table," he said.

"I know you've got the table."

"It's better than I thought it would be and the range curve is not what
anybody would have guessed, and there is a thing about placed articles
that I want to say out loud to somebody before Friday, because if it is
wrong I would rather find out tonight."

"Sit down."

He sat down.

*And he is going to give me nine minutes of the finest work anybody has
done in this village this month,* she thought, *and I am going to sit
here and listen to every word of it and not say the one sentence I have
got, and then at some point he is going to ask me for whatever it is.*

*And I am going to have to have an answer ready.*

She pulled the lamp over.

"Go on, then," she said.
