# Chapter 71, Scene 7 — Do My Back

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the tower, the residence bath. Tsunade, age
fifty-three. POV: Tsunade. ~1040 words.*

---

She arrived at the Thursday bath armed, which was the new normal
and she would not have traded it for a province: the war stood at
— by her count, which differed from his in ways that were
themselves a front — nine engagements to seven in her favour, and
she had spent a satisfying quarter hour planning the evening's
offensive while signing the week's last documents.

He was in the water first, as always. Fixed point, thermal
stability, the engineer's opening. She had counter-doctrine
prepared for all of it.

She never got to deploy any of it.

Because he let her settle, let the first quarter hour go by in
the ordinary paradise the Thursdays had become — the heat, the
lamplight, the day's laxness audit already filed, the two of
them boiled soft and easy — and then, into that peace, in the
working register, level as a bench rule, the fabricator of the
Hime line said:

"Hime. Can you do my back for me, please?"

And turned around.

The request took her instruments a full second to parse,
because it had no precedent anywhere in the file. Three months
of this — the towel, the crawl, the fittings, every escalation
in the whole shared study — and the traffic had run one
direction: her hands, his systems; her confidence, his copper;
she the artillery and he the beautifully collapsing position.
He had *received* everything. He had never once, not one time,
asked.

He was asking. On purpose. In the voice.

*He has opened a front below the waterline,* reported the part
of her brain that never boiled soft, with something that in a
younger officer would have been called panic. *The entire
force structure is built for outgoing fire. There is no
doctrine for THIS, there is no—*

"Of course," said her mouth, which had standards, and her body
crossed the tub on its own recognizance.

His back. She had healed it, inspected it, catalogued its
bruises weekly at the noons — she held medical charts on every
square inch of this man. None of that, it turned out, was
doctrine for kneeling behind him in the lamplight with the
cloth in her hand and *permission*, explicit, requested,
granted, to touch him slowly for no clinical reason at all,
while he sat with his eyes shut and let her.

Her hands were not unsteady. She was fifty-three years old
and the finest medical hands of an era; her hands did not do
unsteady. But she noticed — clinically, in the register, with
the mortified fascination of a specialist contracting the
disease she'd spent months studying — that she was *pacing*
herself. Metering. Working from the shoulders down in careful
grades, the way — the exact way — a certain fabricator laid
courses, and that her breath had picked up a count it did not
require, and that somewhere around the fourth pass of the
cloth she had begun, absurdly, at her age, with her résumé,
to feel the heat arrive in her own face.

"You're quiet," observed the strategist, eyes still shut,
with the serene cruelty of a man reading a battle from inside
his own victory. "You're never quiet in the bath, Hime. Is
something the matter with my back?"

"Your back," said Tsunade, with immense dignity, "is
structurally adequate."

"Mm. The shoulders too, please. Jiraiya-sama says I carry my
guard too high. You would be assisting the training
programme. There is probably a form."

*He is doing my own moves to me,* she thought, somewhere
between outrage and a laugh and a third thing that was
frankly winning. *The clinical pretext. The straight face.
Theform joke — that's MY form joke. He has reverse-engineered
the entire towel doctrine and he is running it back at me
beat for beat, the absolute — the magnificent little—*

She finished the shoulders. She took her time about it,
because losing composedly was still a skill she owned, and
because his breathing had gone carefully level in the way
that meant the copper was up and the cost was being paid and
the position was being held anyway — both of them now, she
realised, running the same fire mission on each other
simultaneously and both of them holding, which was either
the whole relationship in one bathtub or a definition of
madness, and she had stopped being able to tell the
difference around the second Thursday and had signed a
document saying so.

And then he turned around, pink to the ears, blush fully
deployed and voice fully level — the managed bug, the love
arriving through the noise with the signal intact — took the
cloth out of her hand with a courtesy that dismantled her
last formation, and said:

"Thank you for that, Hime. Let me."

And that — the reversal of the reversal, her own offensive
handed back to her with a bow, his hands and the cloth and
three months of jeweller's patience arriving at HER
shoulders while she sat in the water having entirely lost
track of whose war this was —

— that was the exact moment, undefended, mid-surrender,
gloriously off balance, that her eyes, sliding away from
his in a tactical retreat to anywhere safer, fell on the
clothes pile by the door.

On his coat, folded in his parade-ground way.

On the corner of a small orange book, slid half out of the
inner pocket, spine up.

The water was very warm. The lamplight was perfect. And the
part of her brain that never boiled soft came all the way
to its feet, in total silence, and every unclosed file of
three months — the bath out of a clear sky, the hair out of
the blue, the trail, the back, the *curriculum order*, one
thumbnail's width, the audit is scheduled, God help the
source — every single one of them turned, aligned, and
snapped shut on the same half-inch of orange like a barrier
array finding its keystone.

The source has chapters.

The source was six feet away, in his coat, where he kept
everything load-bearing.

The source was *Icha Icha*.

"Sazare," said the Hokage of the Land of Fire, in a voice
that had gone somewhere entirely new, and felt him go
still behind her, reading the register shift the way he
read everything, one half-second too late.

"You've been reading that *FILTH*!?"
