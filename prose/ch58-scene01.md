# Chapter 58, Scene 1 — The End of the Box

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the Hokage's tower, outer office. Sazare Ruri,
age eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1220 words.*

---

He put the sixth month on Shizune's desk at eight o'clock on Tuesday
morning.

"Reconciled both ways. There were fourteen discrepancies and thirteen
of them are the clerk's and trivial and I've listed them at the front
with the page and the direction.

"The fourteenth isn't trivial and it isn't the clerk's. There's a
consignment in the sixth month that appears in the annexe ledger and
not in the quartermaster's copy, and it's four hundred and eleven ryō
of dressing stock, and it went somewhere.

"I've put it on a separate sheet with everything I can establish and
nothing I can't, and the honest position is that it's either a
transcription failure in the seventh month when the floor was relaid or
it's a theft, and I cannot tell which from a ledger and neither can
anybody else, and I'd want Kanzaki on it before anybody says the word."

Shizune looked at the separate sheet for a while.

"Nobody's looked at these ledgers in seventeen months," she said.

"No."

"I invented them on Thursday as make-work."

"Yes."

"And you've found four hundred and eleven ryō of missing dressings in
them."

"I've found a discrepancy," said Sazare. "It's not the same thing and
I'd rather it wasn't written down as the same thing."

---

Shizune put the sheet down.

"That's it, then," she said.

"That's it."

"Correspondence, dispatch, standing returns, the sixth month. Eleven
weeks in six days." She sat back. "I told you on Sunday I wouldn't
have a control any more. It's Tuesday."

"Yes."

"So what happens now?"

"I don't know," said Sazare.

"Neither do I."

They sat with that.

It was not uncomfortable, which he noted, because it would have been in
the eighth month and it would have been unbearable in the third, and
somewhere in the last five days sitting in a room with Shizune and
nothing to say had stopped being a state he had to manage.

"There's a fortnight," he said eventually. "The post starts on the
first. Fourteen days, and there's no fabrication room until there is,
and the instrument's four hundred miles away, and there's nothing on
the table."

"And you're going to come in at the fourth hour every day of it."

"Yes."

"Ruri, you're not employed here."

"I know."

"You're a sergeant in the Medical Corps. Your posting says Fabrication
Research, effective the first of the twelfth, and between now and then
you are on nothing, and if the Corps board took an interest in where
you were spending your mornings there is not one document in this
building that would help me."

"I know that as well," said Sazare.

---

Shizune rubbed her eyes.

"Right," she said. "Here's what I'm going to do and you're going to let
me do it without taking it apart.

"The desk stays. It stays because I have an assistant who has cleared
seventeen months of backlog in six days and I would be a lunatic to
give that up, and I am going to write a line to the Corps board this
morning requesting your temporary attachment to this office until the
first of the twelfth, and I am going to date it *the twelfth of the
eleventh*, which is a Thursday, which is six days ago."

He opened his mouth.

"Don't."

"That's a backdated document."

"It's a backdated *request*," said Shizune, "which will be granted,
because the person who grants it is upstairs, and because the work in
that box is the evidence, and if anybody ever queries it they will find
eleven weeks of correspondence in your hand with dates all over it.

"It is not a lie, Ruri. It is a piece of paper catching up with a fact
that has been true since Thursday morning, and the fact is that I asked
you and you came."

He sat and looked at the desk for a moment.

*She is right about the substance and the date is still wrong.*

*And it is the fourth one.*

*Wednesday: Ibiki. Friday: the form in her pocket. Saturday, or before
it, and I only found out on Monday: the paper. And now a date.*

*Four in eight days.*

*And the first three were all correct and this one is correct as well,
and I have said all right to every single one, and the thing I have got
to be careful about is not the four.*

*It is that I have stopped counting them as a surprise.*

"All right," he said.

"You've gone very quiet."

"I'm counting."

"I know you are," said Shizune. "You said you would. Say it on Monday
and I'll write that down too."

---

She wrote the line to the Corps board and he watched her do it and did
not say anything else about it.

Then, at about twenty past eight, she put the pen down.

"Can I ask you something that isn't work?"

"Yes."

"Are you all right?"

He thought about it properly, because she had asked it properly and
because Sakura had told her four days ago in this room that she would
regret asking it.

"No," he said.

Shizune waited.

"I've had a very good week and I have not been all right since Thursday
morning and both of those are true. I've stopped doing the one thing I
have done every day since I was ten and I did not notice for three
days. I forgot a Friday. I found out on Monday that the accident that
took my hand at seventeen was a different kind of accident than the one
I've had in the book for three months, and I found out from a man I'd
known ninety minutes, and I found out on the same morning that there's
a word for what I am and that four people alive are it.

"And I got given a category, and it's the first one anybody's ever put
on me that isn't about what I make, and I have been carrying it round
for two days like something in a bag."

"Ruri—"

"You asked."

"I did ask."

"Sakura told you not to."

"Sakura," said Shizune, "was extremely thorough and got one thing
wrong out of five, and I have decided I'm going to keep asking anyway,
and you are going to keep answering, and neither of us is going to
mention that we're doing it."

---

He got the acknowledgements he had brought down from upstairs and
started on them, because there was an hour before nine and there was a
stack and the stack went down.

*Friday is in three days.*

*I have not built anything to say and I have started four times.*

*Every version I have got is a structure, and a structure is a thing
you put in front of somebody so that they arrive where you want them,
and she has never once in nineteen evenings tried to make me arrive
anywhere.*

*So I am going to have to go up that hill on Friday with nothing, which
I have never done in my life, and say a true thing out loud to a
creature who is not going to think anything is wrong.*

*That is the whole problem and I have three days.*

He did four hundred and eleven acknowledgements before noon, which was
a figure he noticed, and did not enjoy.
