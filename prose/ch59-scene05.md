# Chapter 59, Scene 5 — Question Six

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the room over the persimmon tree. Sazare Ruri,
age eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1910 words.*

---

The instrument came back on the Tuesday.

It came back the way it had gone, which was with Katsuyu, in the yard,
without ceremony: a nineteen-inch hilt in a roll of felt and a bundle of
paper that was not paper at all but eleven sheets of something they
made at Shikkotsu out of a pressed leaf, and which had been written on
in an ink he could not identify with a nib that was not a nib.

"They have answered your six questions," said Katsuyu.

"All of them?"

"All of them. I would like to say two things before you read it."

"Go on."

"They are not being unkind."

"All right."

"And the sixth answer is four sheets long," said Katsuyu, "and three of
the four are about you and not about the object, and I have read it, and
I would rather you read it in your own room and not in a yard with me
sitting here."

---

He read it in his own room with the lamp on and the hilt on the felt in
front of him.

*One. Is the core correctly graded through its length, or is the
nineteen inches of twenty-one a compromise I have talked myself into?*

*Answer: It is correctly graded. It is graded better than the maker
believes. The compromise is real and it is at the eleventh inch and it
is worth about four per cent and it is not the fault he thinks it is.
He has spent time on the wrong end.*

He read that four times and then wrote *eleventh inch, 4%, wrong end*
on a fresh page and drew a box round it.

*Two. The fourth voice has come down about a tone since the seventh
month and the two middle voices have stopped beating against each
other. Is the convergence a fault?*

*Answer: No. It is the article settling and it is what settling sounds
like. He should not correct it. If he corrects it he will have to do the
whole run again and it will settle back to the same place in about
eleven months.*

*He is asking whether a thing that has got better on its own is broken.
We are told this is characteristic.*

He put the sheet down for a moment.

---

*Three. What is the failure mode? I am not asking whether it will fail.*

*Answer: The blade. Always the blade, never the hilt. The hilt will
outlast him.*

*The blade is drawn cheaply and on purpose and this is correct and we
approve of it, and it means the article cannot fail in a way that hurts
him, only in a way that leaves him without it.*

*He should therefore stop treating the blade as the article. He has
written eleven pages about the blade and four about the hilt and the
hilt is the thing he made.*

*Four. The six seconds. Burnout or duration?*

*Answer: Burnout. He knew this. He asked anyway.*

He wrote *asked anyway* in the margin and then looked at it for a while
and did not cross it out.

*Five. Can the blade be drawn twice in succession?*

*Answer: Yes. Once. The second draw is at about sixty per cent and the
third does not come, and there is no fourth in any circumstance, and the
gap required before it is available again is somewhere between four
hours and a day and we cannot be more precise because none of us has a
hand.*

*We will say plainly that we do not like this question and we have
discussed it. He is asking how many times he can do a thing whose
purpose he has not stated.*

---

*Six. What am I not asking?*

He turned the sheet over.

There were four of them.

---

*You have sent us an object and six questions.*

*Five of the six are about how it works. The sixth is a question you ask
at the bottom of every list you have ever sent us and we have answered
it briefly on four previous occasions because there was nothing in it.*

*There is something in it now.*

*You have asked us how the core is graded, how the voices sit, how it
will fail, why it stopped at six seconds and how many times it can be
drawn.*

*You have not asked us what it is for.*

*We have gone back through everything you have sent us since the eighth
month. There are two hundred and six questions. Not one of them is what
it is for. Not one is who it is to be used on, or in what circumstance,
or at whose instruction, or what happens to the person at the other end
of it.*

*We do not raise this as a moral matter. We are not qualified and it is
not our business and you did not ask.*

*We raise it because it is a design fault and it is in the article.*

He stopped and put the sheet flat on the table and did not pick it up
for about a minute.

---

*A thing that is drawn is drawn at something.*

*You have built an instrument whose entire structure is a way of
spending, and you have specified every part of the spending — the rate,
the grade, the conduction, the note, the burnout — and you have never at
any point specified the load.*

*You cannot size a thing without knowing the load. You told us this
yourself in the ninth month about a splint. You were correct and you were
emphatic and you wrote four pages.*

*So the article is not finished. It is beautifully made and it is
correctly graded and it will outlast you and it is not finished, because
there is a specification missing from the front of it, and everything
downstream of a missing specification is a guess that happens to have
worked.*

He sat back against the wall.

*They are right.*

*They are completely right and it took them eleven sheets of leaf and a
slug carrying it four hundred miles and I have had it in my hands for
seven months.*

*I have got a creed. It is in the front of four books.* I am not going
to get stronger. Find out how it works.

*Find out how it works.*

*Not what it is for. There has never been a what it is for. There has
never been one about anything — not the splints, because the splints
have a patient and the patient is the specification and Yagi gives it to
me; not the surplus, because Kanzaki gives me the load.*

*Everything I have ever made has had somebody else standing at the front
of it with the load already written down.*

*And the one thing I made entirely on my own, for no department, on no
docket, for two hundred and three hours, has got nothing at the front of
it at all.*

---

There was more.

*And there is a second half, and it is smaller, and we have argued about
whether to send it and we are sending it because you would send it to
us.*

*You keep asking us about the object.*

*The object is not the weapon.*

*It is a way of spending, and the thing being spent is you, and you have
never once measured the reservoir. You have asked us two hundred and six
questions about a hilt and none about the hand.*

*You are the article. The instrument is a tap.*

*And you close, which is your own business and which has kept you alive,
and it means that whatever you build with it is a jar with a finite
amount in it, and six seconds is what a full jar looks like when it is
poured out fast.*

*So the shape of you in a field is this, and we will say it plainly
because nobody else is going to and because the ones who would are
diplomats:*

*Six seconds, and then a man standing in the open holding a handle.*

He read that four times.

Then he got up and walked to the window and stood there for a while,
which he had done twice in a fortnight and had not done for two years
before that.

---

*Last part.*

*You asked us in the tenth month about a fifth thing in the position
where a fifth voice would be. You said two seconds, not repeated, and
that you would not repeat it.*

*We have taken the object apart with four of us and we cannot find it,
and we want to be honest about what that means, which is not that it is
not there.*

*Two of us think it is a harmonic of the core at the eleventh inch and
that it is a consequence of the compromise you did not know you had
made, and that it will go away when the article finishes settling.*

*One of us thinks it is not in the object at all.*

*The fourth will not say, and has asked that this be recorded, and she
is the oldest of us and I have known her for two hundred years and I
have seen her decline to answer perhaps four times.*

*We are recording the disagreement rather than giving you a consensus,
because a consensus here would be manufactured and you would be able to
tell.*

*Do not go looking for it on your own.*

*That is not advice about the article. Katsuyu has told us the
circumstances of the fifteenth of the eighth month and we did not know
them when you first wrote, and had we known we would have said this in
the tenth month and said it harder.*

---

He put the eleven sheets down in order on the low table and squared them
and sat and looked at them for a long time.

Then he got a fresh page.

*Review received 24/11. Eleven sheets. Six answers.*

*Actions arising, in order of size:*

*1. The instrument has no specification at the front of it. It has never
had one. I have not written one because I have never had to write one,
because somebody has always handed me the load, and this is the first
object in my life where the load is mine to state and I have not stated
it.*

*I am not going to state it tonight. I want that recorded as a decision
and not as an omission.*

*2. The eleventh inch. Four per cent. Wrong end.*

*3. Do not correct the fourth voice.*

*4. Twice. Never three times. Four hours to a day.*

*5. The fifth thing: three positions and one refusal, and the refusal is
from the one who has declined four times in two hundred years.*

*Do not go looking on my own. Agreed. Recorded. I would like it noted
that I agreed on the page and that this is the fourth time I have
written that sentence about this specific thing.*

He stopped, and then wrote the last one properly because it was the
honest half.

*6. Six seconds, and then a man standing in the open holding a handle.*

*I have known that since the eleventh of this month. I have known it
since the yard.*

*Nobody has ever said it out loud to me and I did not know how much I
needed it said until I read it off a leaf.*
