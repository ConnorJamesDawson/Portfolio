# Chapter 50, Scene 6 — Assisted Practice

*Act 4, part D. Konoha, the room over the persimmon tree. Sazare Ruri,
age eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1,466 words.*

---

He wrote the month up on the last night of the ninth, which was a
Thursday, with the window shut for the first time since the fifth month
and the persimmon starting to turn at the top where it got the wind.

He did it the way he did every month-end, which was in order, with the
work first, because the work was the part he could be trusted on.

*NINTH MONTH.*

*Reject: aggregate 9, granite 11, site rubble 12.1, own prior work 1.8.
Two hundred and nine articles. Kanzaki has entered the series break and
has entered the excess-draw figure under my name, correctly, and I have
worded it and it is worse than her wording and I have kept mine.*

*Degradation study: invalidated. Fifty measurements, eleven weeks,
against a process I no longer perform. It is not wrong, it is a
description of a man doing it badly, and I will have to run it again in
the eleventh month when the bench is clear.*

*Article 1 (instrument): 203 hours. Core at nineteen inches of
twenty-one. Ways at the fourth branch measurably wider than the third
and I can hear it — the fourth voice has come down about a tone since
the seventh month and the two middle ones have stopped beating, which
means they have converged, which means the two runs are now within a
few thousandths of each other and I did not do that on purpose.*

*Estimated completion: eleventh month. Possibly the tenth if the
evenings hold.*

*The panel on the guard is still flat.*

---

*Requisition 40-Q3/4: filed 4/9, personnel line completed. No response
from the sealing office as of this evening, which is twenty-six days,
which is not unusual for a fourth-quarter batch and which I have looked
up twice in order to be able to write "which is not unusual."*

He read that back and did not soften it.

*Katsuyu: eleven evenings of nineteen. No contract, per the arrangement
of the second. I have asked her on eleven Fridays and she has said yes
eleven times and I have not once assumed the twelfth.*

*Sakura: south stair, four minutes, sheet not produced, first time in
fifty-one.*

*The Hokage: thirty-six days.*

He stopped on that one.

He had written it because it went in the month-end and everything went
in the month-end, and he sat and looked at four words for some time
before he let himself put anything under them.

*I have not been up the hill since the twenty-fourth of the eighth
month, which was the night before the rose went. Thirty-six days.*

*In the two years and eleven months before that the longest interval
was nineteen days and that was the border commission and she was four
hundred miles away.*

*There is no interval in this. She is a fifteen-minute walk from this
table and has been for thirty-six days.*

*Neither of us has sent anything. There has been no reason to — the
notice went up in the fifth month and holds, the study reports to Yagi
by design, the surplus is on schedule and Kanzaki files it, and the
correspondence is Ibiki's and the second exchange is not due until the
eleventh. There is not one piece of business between us and there has
not been since the twenty-second of the eighth month.*

*Which is the first time that has been true since the third month of
last year, and I would like it recorded that I noticed it on the
fourteenth day and have been not-writing-it-down for twenty-two.*

---

Then he did the last part, which was the count, and it went where it
always went, at the bottom, in the ordinary place.

*Ex. 1 — assisted.*

*4th to 30th. Observer present eleven of nineteen evenings.*

*Best hold: two, for seventeen weeks, from the twenty-fourth of the
fifth month.*

*Fifth of the ninth: three, twice, repeatable.*

*Eleventh: four.*

*Nineteenth: five, and I wrote that one down and then sat on the floor
for a while.*

*Twenty-third: six.*

*Twenty-eighth: eight.*

*Tonight: nine.*

He looked at the column.

*Seventeen weeks at two. Then twenty-six days and it is nine.*

*I do not think this is me getting better at holding it. I have not
practised holding it any harder than I practised for the seventeen
weeks, and for eleven of these nineteen evenings I was doing something
else entirely and did the sphere at the end out of habit at about half
past one.*

*What changed is the grip and the take.*

*I was over-drawing by a third and over-holding by about double, which
means that every time I have attempted exercise one since the fifth
month I have been trying to hold twice as much as necessary, twice as
hard as necessary, with no report of doing either.*

*A boy of eleven wrote "easy" beside it in the margin of a school
book.*

*It was easy. It has always been easy. It was easy for everybody in
this village at twelve and it was easy for Kanzaki's nephew at ten and
it was never once easy for me, and the reason is not that I am worse at
it. It is that I have been doing it with a fault nobody could see and
nobody could tell me about, because in order to tell me somebody would
have had to be able to watch, and nobody in the world could.*

---

He put the pencil down and then picked it up again, because there was a
thing standing behind the whole page and had been standing there since
about the nineteenth, and he had been leaving it alone.

*The other observation, which I am going to write badly and then leave
badly, because I have tried four times to write it well and every
version is a version where I know more than I do.*

*Exercise one is holding chakra in the open with no material at the far
end. That is what the book says. First term, eleven years old, page
eleven, four exercises and a diagram of a hand and a compressed
sphere.*

*That is what I have been doing for eighteen weeks.*

*I do not think it is what I am doing now.*

He read that back.

*It does not feel like holding any more. It has not since about the
nineteenth. Holding is what I was doing at two, and it was work, and I
could feel the effort in it and I could feel it fail.*

*Nine does not feel like nine of anything. It feels like standing
still, and it takes no effort at all, and the reason I stop at nine is
not that I run out. It is that at about nine something else starts —
and I do not have a word for it and I do not know whether it is
important and I have not told anybody, including her.*

*Because I have nothing to say. "It feels different" is not an
observation, it is a sensation with an adjective on it, and I have
audited myself against exactly that failure for eleven years.*

*So the honest statement is:*

*I do not know what this number is a measurement of any more.*

*It was a schoolbook drill in the fifth month. It is the same drill, on
the same page, at the bottom of the same column, in the same hand.*

*And I have got no idea what I am counting.*

---

He closed the book.

*There is one person alive who could tell me.*

*She has forty-one years of a summon she has never asked a question,
three of the four in her family tree, and the only doorway in this
village to whatever this is — and she said, in a yard at two in the
morning in the seventh month, that she had never once wondered where
Wood Release gets the wood, and did not notice she had said it.*

*And I have not spoken to her in thirty-six days.*

*There is no business between us. There has not been since the
twenty-second of the eighth month. I could construct one in about four
minutes and it would be a good one and she would see through it before
I finished the first sentence, and I would rather not go up that hill
holding a manufactured reason.*

*So I am not going to.*

*I am going to sit here with a number I cannot name and wait for
something to happen that gives me an honest cause to knock on that
door, which is the least sensible research decision I have ever
recorded and I am recording it anyway.*

Outside, the persimmon had four leaves down on the sill and the ninth
month was over.

He got about five hours.
