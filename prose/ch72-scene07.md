# Chapter 72, Scene 7 — The Kiln Report

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the Hyūga compound. Hyūga Hinata, age
nineteen. POV: Hinata. ~780 words.*

---

Hinata conducted the review of the incident that evening, in her
own room, in the format Sazare-san had taught her, because the
format was the one dignity available and she intended to use it.

*Incident report. Location: the quartermaster's yard. Time:
approximately half past three. Load: arrived without warning,
through a gate, at full amplitude, announcing a yard tour.*

*System response: total structural failure in under one second.
The summary acquired a tremor. The voice produced eleven words,
four of them "the." The subject then conducted a detailed
inspection of a bank of kilns that she had personally certified
forty minutes earlier.*

*The kilns were sealed brick. There is nothing to inspect on
sealed brick. She inspected it for six minutes.*

She put her face in her hands for a while, because the format
permitted brief recesses, and then she made herself write the
part that mattered, because he had taught her that too — the
record gets the whole of it, or the record is worth nothing.

*Findings. One: the format did not fail. The format never
deployed. Every drill so far has begun from readiness — a
breath, a bridge, a prepared load. He arrived the way he
arrives everywhere: as weather. No format known to the
consultation can be raised in the time his gate-to-greeting
interval allows, because there is no interval. Training
implication: the bridge must become STANDING. Not raised for
crossings. Lived on.*

*Two, entered honestly: after the failure, I recovered. The
licence sign-off was conducted at full function, in the
working register, nineteen minutes post-incident. A year ago
the failure would have consumed the day. The consultation is
working. The kilns are shameful and the trend is real, and
the trend is the finding. He would say the trend is the
finding. I can hear him say it.*

*Three. He knows now.*

She sat with that one, listening to the compound settle around
her, the walks and the walls and the eight hundred years of
watching, and examined it for fear, and found — this was the
finding of the evening, and she turned it over twice to be
sure — none.

*He was four feet away with a clipboard. He reads rooms the
way we do. There is no version of this afternoon in which the
finest observer I have ever sat across did not identify the
street's name before the kilns were half inspected. So: he
holds it. The man whose own eleven-year architecture I watched
from across a table — who built a voice a room and thinks the
door is hidden — now holds my name the way I hold his.*

*And I am not afraid, and the reason I am not afraid is the
reason the consultation exists: I have watched what he does
with held things. He builds furniture inside them. He came
back two days after I held his and gave me load paths as an
exchange rate — unasked, unpriced, because to him a held
thing is not leverage, it is a TRUST, and trusts appreciate.*

*The clan exchanges hostages. This is not that. I do not have
the word for what this is. Perhaps: collateral, held in
kindness, both directions. Perhaps just: friends. I have not
had one outside the team before. The file will use "friend"
and review the term quarterly.*

The last entry she wrote slowly, in her most careful hand,
because it was for herself in a year, whenever the bridge
finally stood, whoever she had become by then.

*He worried I might have a fever. He recommended a medic.*

*Eleven years, and the engineering holds so completely that
the man I built it against stands four feet away and
diagnoses the weather. Sazare-san lived inside the same
walls for three years and says somebody walked into his
without knocking, and that is how the door was found. But
nobody walks into a Hyūga's walls without knocking. Nobody
ever has. The walls are the clan's whole inheritance, and I
am the heir, and so the finding of the incident, written
plainly, in the format, where only I will read it:*

*if the door is ever to be found, I will have to open it
from the inside. Myself. On purpose. There is no other
engineer for this structure.*

*The bridge must become standing.*

*Training resumes Tuesday. Amendment requested: the
no-warning case. He will say yes. He flagged it before I
did — I saw the margin of his copy. "Live load."*

*He is a good teacher, and a good friend, and his advice is,
as the pink one says, scary, and I am going to learn to
stand in weather if it takes the whole year.*

*End of report. The kilns were fine. — H.H.*
