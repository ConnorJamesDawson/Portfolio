# Chapter 65, Scene 2 — Consequences

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the Hokage's tower. Sazare Ruri, age eighteen.
POV: Sazare. ~1770 words.*

---

She had it in four piles on the desk on the morning of the
twenty-fourth, which was how she did anything she had already decided not
to soften.

"Sit down. This is going to take about eleven minutes and I am not going
to make it comfortable, and at the end of it I want you to say *yes* four
times and go to work."

"All right."

"First pile. Him."

She put a hand on it.

"Six weeks off the active list. That is my figure and it is optimistic
and I have put seven on the paper because I would rather be early than
explain a variance.

"He was on the December roster for two escort details and a border sweep
in the first week of the new year, and all three of those have gone to
other people, and one of them has gone to a man who should not be doing
it and I have signed it anyway.

"That is what it costs. Not a tragedy. It is three jobs and a bit of
scheduling and a man doing a sweep who is nine years older than the man
who should be doing it, and it lands on nobody in a way anybody will
remember."

"Understood."

"That's one. Say yes at the end."

---

"Second pile. Paper.

"There is an incident report and it is done and I wrote it myself last
night and Sakura and Yagi have both signed the medical, and it is
accurate and it is not flattering to anybody including me.

"It says: sanctioned training engagement, ground closed under a Corps
notice, medic present throughout, terms stated in front of five witnesses
and adhered to by both parties.

"It says: injury sustained by U. Uzumaki consequent on contact with a
crystal article at speed, tibia and lateral compartment, treated in
theatre two on the day by S. Ruri under my instruction.

"And it says the other thing."

She turned the top sheet round.

"*A live article entered the subject's limb and was arrested by the
operator. Duration approximately forty seconds. No procedure existed and
none exists.*"

Sazare read it twice.

"That's the whole of it," he said.

"That is the whole of it and it is going in the file, and I have thought
about every possible way of not putting it in the file and there isn't
one, and the reason there isn't one is that in about nine months somebody
is going to ask what happened and the answer had better already be
written down by me on the twenty-third of the twelfth month."

"Agreed."

"You'll want to read it properly."

"I'll read it four times," said Sazare, "and if I have got a correction I
will bring it to you tomorrow morning and it will be about a figure and
not about a word."

---

"Third pile," said Tsunade. "The box."

She did not put a hand on this one.

"Say it," he said.

"It's finished."

"I know."

"You do not know, because you have been thinking of it as *failing*, and
it has been failing since the eleventh of the eleventh month and I have
been letting it fail because a thing that is failing is still a thing.

"It is not failing. It is over.

"One name at the bottom of a docket was an adequate control on a man who
made splints. It has been visibly nothing for six weeks and on the
twentieth of this month it became a joke, and everybody who was on that
ground knows it and so do the eleven people who have heard about it since
and so do the fifteen on that council when they come back in the new
year."

"So what replaces it?"

"I don't know," said Tsunade.

---

He sat with that.

*She has never said that to me about a decision.*

*She has said* I don't know *about a diagnosis and about a supply figure
and about whether she can love again, and she said it in a briefing in
front of eleven people in the ninth month and it is item three on the
list I gave her in a chair on the eleventh.*

*She has never once said it about a thing she is going to have to do.*

"Give me the shape of it," he said.

"You're not going to like the shape."

"I'd rather have it on the twenty-fourth than in the second week of the
new year."

"All right." She sat back. "The problem is not that you are dangerous.
Everybody has got that wrong including two people on that council and
including me for about a fortnight.

"The problem is that you are *unattached*.

"You have got no chain. You are a Corps sergeant in a post that exists
for one man, reporting to a board through Yagi on paper and to me in
practice, and there is nobody between you and this desk and there never
has been.

"Which was correct when you were a fabricator. A man who makes four
hundred splints a year does not need a commander.

"You are not that any more, and the whole of what happened on the
twentieth is that a man with no chain went onto a training ground and did
a thing nobody had authorised because nobody had thought to."

"You want to attach me to something."

"I want to attach you to *somebody*," said Tsunade, "and I have got about
four candidates and I do not like any of them, and I am going to decide
in the first week of the new year and I am telling you now so that you
have got a fortnight to be unhappy about it in private."

---

*Right.*

*Say the honest thing.*

"That's correct," he said.

"...That was faster than I expected."

"It's correct and I have had it since about the eleventh," said Sazare.
"Not in those words. I have had it as *the box does not work* and I have
been carrying it round as a security problem, and you have just put the
right word on it, which is that it is not a security problem, it is an
organisational one.

"There is nobody who is supposed to say no to me.

"Shizune said no to me four times in five weeks and every one of them was
correct and not one of them was her job. Katsuyu tells me what my hands
are doing and has no standing whatsoever. You say no to me and you are
the Hokage and it should not be the Hokage.

"Yagi is the closest thing I have got and she is my superior on paper for
medical purposes and she has never once given me an instruction about
anything else, and she would tell you herself that she has not got the
first idea what to do with a sage."

He put his hands on his knees.

"So attach me. I would like it recorded that I said that on the
twenty-fourth without an argument and before I knew who it was going to
be, because in about eleven days I am probably going to have one."

---

"Fourth pile," said Tsunade.

There was nothing on the desk.

He looked at the empty place for about four seconds.

"That's Root."

"That's Root."

"And there's nothing in it."

"There is nothing in it," said Tsunade. "No request. No query. Nobody has
asked me for the incident report and nobody has asked the Corps board for
the medical and there has not been one word out of that building since
the twentieth.

"There were six people on that ground and two of them were slugs and
toads and the other four are me, Jiraiya, Sakura and you. There was a
closure notice with my name on it. There is a boy on the second floor of
the hospital with a leg in a frame and a story going round a village
about the ground coming alive.

"And a man who has had somebody in that hospital since before you were
born has not sent me a single piece of paper."

"Which means he has got the whole of it."

"Which means he had the whole of it by about four o'clock on the
twentieth," said Tsunade, "and has decided that the useful thing to do
with it is nothing at all, in a way that I can see him doing.

"He is not concealing that he knows. He is *showing* me that he knows and
that he is not going to ask, and there are about four reasons a man does
that and three of them are bad."

---

Sazare sat and looked at an empty place on a desk.

*The quiet is the news.*

*I wrote that in the ninth month about something completely different and
I have got it in a book in a dye-works, and it has been true of that man
for four months and it has just become true about me.*

*And the fourth reason. She said three of the four are bad.*

"What's the fourth reason?" he said.

"That he does not need to ask because he is going to be told."

"By whom?"

"That is the fourth reason," said Tsunade, "and it is the one I have been
sitting with since Tuesday, and I am not going to say a name in this room
because I have not got one and because if I say a name out loud on the
twenty-fourth of the twelfth month I will be looking for it for a year."

---

She squared the piles.

"Four things," she said. "Six weeks. The report. The box is finished and
you are going to be attached to somebody. And Root is quiet and the quiet
is the loud part.

"Say yes four times."

"Yes," said Sazare. "Yes. Yes. And yes, and I would like to add one to
the fourth."

"Go on."

"If you find out who," he said, "I do not want to be told, and I want the
reason on the record, which is that I have got a correspondence with an
agent of Otogakure under a written arrangement and a third exchange
answered on the eleventh of this month, and the single most valuable
thing about me at the moment is that there is nothing in my head that
anybody could want.

"Keep it that way for as long as you can."

Tsunade looked at him for a moment.

"That's the most professional thing you have ever said to me," she said.

"I know," said Sazare. "I did not enjoy any part of it."
