# Chapter 58, Scene 7 — What You Are

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the quartermaster's yard. Sazare Ruri, age
eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1620 words.*

---

He got up off the yard stone and stood there.

"I've got one more thing," he said, "and I'm going to be bad at it, and
I'd rather you let me be bad at it than help."

"That is the third time you have said that this week," said Katsuyu.

"How do you know that?"

"You said it to me on the ninth Friday and you have the same shoulders."

---

*First attempt.*

"You are the most important person in my professional life," he said,
"and I want to set out why in about five items, starting with the take,
which was the first correction and which nobody in eleven years—"

He stopped.

*No.*

*That is a list. That is a structure, and it is the same structure I
gave a woman in an office eight days ago, and it worked on her because
she is a person who wanted the data.*

*This is a creature who has never given me a structure in nineteen
evenings.*

*And it is a list about my work.*

"That's wrong," he said. "I'm going again."

"Very well."

---

*Second attempt. Say the size of it.*

"Nobody has ever done for me what you have done," said Sazare. "Not
one person. I have had four years in a hospital and three in a tower and
seven in rooms on my own, and there is not one—"

He stopped again.

*No.*

*That is a superlative. That is me ranking her against everybody else I
have ever met, which is a thing about the field and not a thing about
her, and it is the exact failure I have got four hundred pages on.*

*And the whole content of it is what I did not have. It is about the
hole. She is not a hole with something in it.*

"That's wrong as well," he said. "It's about me. I've done it twice now
and both of them were about me and I can hear it."

"You are being very hard on yourself."

"I'm being accurate."

"On this occasion," said Katsuyu, "those are the same, and I did not say
it was a fault. Go again."

---

He stood in the middle of the yard with his hands at his sides.

*Right.*

*Stop trying to name it.*

*I have not got a word. Teacher is wrong — I have had teachers and it is
not that. Friend is wrong and I wrote a manual about it and gave it to
her and it is drifting round a forest being said slightly incorrectly by
somebody who never read it. Summon is wrong and there is no contract and
that was the point.*

*I have been handed a word for myself this week by a man who watched me
for four seconds, and it has been the largest thing that has happened to
me, and he did not get it by finding a name.*

*He got it by telling me what he could see.*

*And that is her method. That is what she has done seventy-two times. She
does not explain and she does not instruct. She looks at what my hands
are doing and she says the true thing about it, flat, offered to the
person best placed to use it.*

*So do that.*

---

"I'm going to tell you what I've observed," said Sazare, "and I'm not
going to say what it means, because I don't know, and I'd rather give
you the readings."

"That is acceptable."

"First. On the second Friday you told me my take was a third over. I had
been doing it since I was seven and I had checked it eleven times and
found nothing, and you had it in about four minutes from nine feet
away.

"Nobody has ever been able to see me work. Nobody. There is not one
person alive who can look at what I am doing and tell me anything about
it, and there never was, and I had concluded by about fifteen that
there never would be and had built a whole method on the assumption.

"Second. You have never once told me I was doing well.

"Not in nineteen evenings. Not when it went from two to nine. I have got
every word you have said to me written down and there is not one line of
encouragement in any of it, and I did not notice for two months, and
when I noticed it I sat on a floor for a while, because everybody else I
have ever worked with has said *well done* and it has never once meant
anything and I did not know why until you didn't.

"Third. On the eleventh Friday I put a thing on the felt that had taken
me four hours and you said the ways at the second junction were wrong
and I would have to take it apart, and I did, and I did not mind.

"I have minded that eleven hundred times off other people. I have minded
it off Kanzaki and I have minded it off Yagi and I minded it off a
council committee in the sixth month so badly that I wrote three pages
about it that evening.

"I did not mind it off you and I have never worked out why and I have
stopped trying."

---

He stopped and made himself go on, because the next one was the one he
had not planned and he could feel it coming.

"Fourth.

"You have never asked me for anything.

"In four hundred years, by your account, nobody has asked you for
anything either, and then somebody did, and you told me in this yard in
the tenth month that you had not known until that Tuesday morning that
there was anything in you that wanted to be asked.

"And in the three months since then you have not asked me for one
single thing.

"You gave a rose to a woman on the second floor of a tower because you
wanted to see how it came out. You have come eleven Fridays and said yes
eleven times. You have spent nineteen evenings building the only thing
standing between me and a plinth and you did not tell me that was what
you were doing because you thought I could see it.

"And you have never asked me for anything at all, and I have been
turning that over since about half past eight this evening, and here is
the reading and then I'll stop.

"I don't think you know how to.

"And I think somebody ought to have looked at you a long time before I
did, and nobody has, and I'm eighteen and I'm not qualified and I'm
what's here."

---

The yard was quiet.

The lamp went on doing what it did and there was frost on the flags at
the north end and somewhere a long way down the hill a shutter went.

"That is the third attempt," said Katsuyu.

"Yes."

"It is considerably better than the first two."

"I know."

"You used my method."

"Yes," said Sazare. "I'm sorry. I didn't have another one."

"You do not need to be sorry," said Katsuyu. "I want to say something
now and I have thought about it for the last minute and I am going to
say it once.

"I do not know what to do with that."

"That's all right."

"I want to be precise. It is not that I am unmoved. I have been sitting
on this felt attending very carefully to something that is happening in
me and I cannot name it and I have no prior instance of it, and I am
four hundred years old and I have been *four hundred years old* for a
long time.

"I do not think I am going to be able to say anything useful about it
this evening."

"You don't have to say anything at all."

"I would like to say one thing."

"Go on."

"Ask me next Friday," said Katsuyu.

---

He sat back down on the yard stone after a while and got the tin out and
put a grain on the felt, because there was an hour of the evening left
and because neither of them was going to be able to do anything else
with it.

"There's a thing on the twenty-eighth," he said, "and I'm going to ask
you now and I'd like you to think about it for a week and say no if it's
a no."

"Say it."

"There's a man called Jiraiya. He's a toad sage — he's one of Hime's,
he's the other one of the three, and he's fifty-four and he's spent
thirty years being taught by toads and he can't gather and move at the
same time.

"He asked me to teach him and I can't. I haven't got a notation and he
can't match a crystal article and the only thing I could hand him is the
absence of a technique, and you cannot demonstrate a thing that isn't
there.

"So we're going to try to run it along a chain.

"Me to you. You to a toad — a Shikkotsu elder to a Myōboku one, in a
room, in a tower, which as far as anybody knows has never happened. Toad
to him.

"Two of the three links are proven and the middle one is you, and I
can't do any of it and neither can he, and the whole thing is impossible
unless you say yes."

Katsuyu did not answer straight away.

"A toad," she said.

"Yes."

"In a building."

"Yes."

"I will think about it for a week," said Katsuyu, "and I would like it
recorded that I have already decided."
