# Chapter 55, Scene 8 — The Lane

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the lane below the Hokage's house. Shizune, age
thirty-one. POV: Shizune. ~660 words.*

---

Shizune got about forty feet down the lane and stopped and stood
against the wall of somebody's store shed with her arms folded, in the
cold, at ten past eight in the morning.

She was going to have to walk to the tower. There was a delegation at
nine and a temple to move for the third time and eleven weeks of
correspondence in a box behind her desk that she had genuinely, until
about an hour ago, believed she was going to die under.

She stood against the shed.

*He answered it.*

That was the thing. That was the only thing she had, and she had been
turning it over for ninety seconds and had not got past it.

*She put up the armour. She put it up in half a second — I've watched
her do it four thousand times, I could do it myself, it's not even a
sentence, it's a door — and it works because nobody has ever once
answered it. You're supposed to laugh. That's the entire mechanism.
You laugh and she gets away.*

*And he stood there and looked at the table and told her what her own
face does.*

*He told her what her face does, in her own kitchen, in front of me, at
eight in the morning, and he got it right.*

She put her head back against the shed.

*Nobody has ever got it right. I have been beside that woman for twenty
years and I would not have said the price thing. I know it. I've known
it since I was about fifteen and I have never once put it in a sentence
because you cannot say a thing like that to her, you cannot get near it,
she does not permit—*

*He does not know that you cannot.*

*That is the whole of him. That is the entire man. He does not know
that there are things you cannot say, and he never has, and it is going
to get him killed and it has just done in nine seconds what I could not
do in twenty years.*

---

She had told him an hour ago that he could hurt her now.

She had meant it as a warning and she had watched him take it and file
it, seriously, the way he filed everything, and she had been pleased
with herself.

*I gave him half of it.*

*I told him he could hurt her. I did not tell him the other half,
because I did not have it an hour ago and I have got it now, standing
against a shed like an idiot.*

*He can also do the other thing.*

*And he cannot tell those apart either, and he is going to be doing
both of them at that woman, at close range, for years, with no idea
which one is coming out of his mouth.*

She thought about going back in.

She got as far as taking her shoulder off the wall.

*And say what? Ruri, I have revised my advice?*

*He'd ask eleven questions about it.*

She almost laughed and did not quite, and put her shoulder back on the
wall, and stood there for a bit longer in the cold.

---

Somewhere behind and above her a door shut, and she did not turn round
to look, because she had spent three years learning exactly how much
of a thing you were entitled to see.

*Four days.*

*He said four days and he was right, and I am going to spend the whole
of this afternoon inventing a box of ledgers from the sixth month, and
he is going to know, and he is going to do it anyway, and he is going to
put it on my desk on Tuesday morning in order with the arithmetic
checked.*

*And then I will not have a control any more.*

*And neither will she.*

Shizune pushed off the wall and went to work.
