# Chapter 73, Scene 9 — New Study

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the tower, the second floor. Sazare Ruri,
age eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~1130 words.*

---

They came back to the tower on the Sunday evening, by the outside
stair, at an hour chosen for nothing in particular, and the
village they walked through on the way did not look up, because
the village had a story and people with a story do not collect
data.

He was aware, the whole way, of two things running in parallel
and refusing to resolve into a single reading.

The first was operational and he had already logged it: they had
been out of the tower for twenty-six hours, unaccounted, together,
and the only person who knew was Shizune, who had written
*private* in a diary with a flourish. The craft had held because
the craft was Shizune. That would not be true forever. The
treaty in the back of his book said *a bridge, not a house*, and
today it had held one more day's traffic.

The second was not operational at all and had no column.

*I am walking through Konoha at dusk on a Sunday and everything
is exactly where I left it on Friday. The wall. The lamp posts.
The fishmonger's shutter with the bent hinge I have been not
fixing for four months. All identical, and none of it has any
business being identical, because I have been comprehensively
rebuilt since Friday and the world has declined to notice.*

*Finding: the largest events of a life do not disturb the
street.*

*Second finding: I am extremely glad about that. I would not
survive a street that knew.*

---

She went up to be Hokage for an hour, because a country does
not care what its Hokage did on a Saturday. He went to the
upper room and sat down at the desk with the grey notebook and
the manuscript audit he had not read in five days.

He did not open the notebook for a while.

*The rule from last night stands,* he thought. *No entry. The
material does not survive the instrument. Everything I could
write would be smaller than what happened, and I have never
once in my life written something smaller than the thing it
was for.*

*But there is a second category, and I have been ignoring it
since about four this afternoon because I was busy being
happy in a market with a woman who bought four apples and
argued about all four of them, and I am going to attend to
it now, because I am a man who attends to it.*

He opened the book to a clean page.

*NEW STUDY.*

*Subject: us.*

*The previous study, opened in January under the heading of
tolerance, is closed. Its objective was wrong from the first
line — it was never a tolerance problem, it was an inventory
problem, and the inventory has been substantially filled by
both parties working from opposite ends without consultation,
which is the standing method of this arrangement and shows no
sign of changing.*

*The new study is not about surviving her.*

*Baseline, established 14/3: four seconds. Recorded here
because the number is a fact, because she ruled it a baseline
rather than a failure in the exact register a white-haired man
used in a frozen field, and because in about forty years I
intend to look at this line and be extremely fond of the
person who wrote it.*

*Method: graduated. As established, at length, in every other
department of my life. There is no version of me that ever
learned anything on the first attempt and no version of me
that failed to learn it by the fortieth.*

*The floor is not yet known.*

*Nothing else about last night is going in this book. That is
a permanent term. If anybody ever reads this notebook they
will find a gap here, and the gap is deliberate, and the gap
is the entry.*

He ruled a line under it.

Then he wrote, because it belonged to a different file and
that file was the field's:

*Assessment findings for Jiraiya-sama, Monday, delivered as
findings and not as narrative:*

*1. Three to one. She fought at an undeclared fraction. The
touch I scored was purchased with the governor's eleventh
clause, four inches of deliberate bait, and eleven minutes of
prepared ground; it was not speed and it was not skill and I
am reporting it as an engineering result rather than a combat
one, because that is what it was.*

*2. The footwork held on ground I have never trained on. It
found a root before I saw it. That is eight weeks and it is
his, and I would like it recorded that I said so before he
asked.*

*3. THE DECISION. This is the finding and it is large.*

*I found it. Immediately, completely, without hesitation, in
the first ninety seconds — and it was not the decision we
have been hunting since January, because the room I have been
searching does not contain "decide to do harm." It contains
"decide to TAKE."*

*And I could take, instantly, from her, on her ground,
because there was no possibility of damaging her and the
entire brake in me is a brake against damage.*

*Conclusion, offered for his correction: the yes does not
live where he has been digging. It lives inside TRUST. I can
choose, cold and fast and completely, precisely as far as I
trust the ground I am choosing on — and against a stranger on
a road, where I have no trust and no ground, I will still be
standing in a frozen field looking for a room.*

*So the training is not the decision. The training is
building enough certainty about my own limits that a
stranger's ground can be trusted the way I trusted hers.*

*He will say this is obvious and that he has been waiting
eight weeks for me to arrive at it. He will be right. It is
still the largest thing I have ever worked out about myself
and I would not have got there in a field.*

*I got there in a garden, because somebody built the
conditions.*

*She always builds the conditions.*

He capped the pen.

Downstairs, two floors down, a country was being administered
by a woman who had a lamp on a nightstand across the village
and a clean glass beside it, and up here a manuscript audit
sat unread for the sixth day, and the shelf in the corner of
the room held a pinwheel and a hairbrush and a dead
contract and a disreputable orange book, and somewhere in
the drawer there was a piece of paper in his own hand and
another in hers, both folded, both saying the same three
words.

*The shelf will need to be bigger,* he thought, and reached
for the manuscript.

*I have already drawn one.*
