# Chapter 37, Scene 6 — Under Supervision

*Act 4, part D. Konoha, the room over the persimmon tree. Sazare Ruri,
age nineteen. POV: Sazare. ~1,373 words.*

---

He tried it that night, once, badly, exactly as instructed, and got
the nothing.

He wrote the whole of it up because she had told him to and because
writing it up was the only way he had ever got through anything, and
the entry ran to two pages and he read it back in the morning and did
not change a word.

*Attempted as directed. One attempt, deliberate, at the bench, sober,
at approximately the eleventh hour.*

*Method: I have no method. That is the first finding and it took me
about forty minutes to arrive at, which is longer than the rest of the
exercise. I know how to run an assessment on a joint, a wound, a
material, a road, a building, a person's habits and a person's
intentions. I do not have a procedure for the thing I was attempting
and I could not construct one, because every procedure I own begins by
deciding what I am looking for and I do not know what I would be
looking for.*

*What I did instead: I sat here and attempted to think about her in the
way I have watched other people think about other people, and what
happened was that I catalogued her.*

*I got: the haori, the yoke, the obi knot, the callus, the crease in
the hair where the pin had been, the nail she has cut back further
than the others because she caught it on something in the spring, the
way she turns a cup a quarter turn when she is deciding whether to
answer honestly, the eleven-second silence before she told me the
inheritance was a thing she carried, and two bells that do not ring.*

*All of it accurate. None of it what I was trying to do.*

*I made three further attempts of about four minutes each and each one
turned into the same thing inside about nine seconds. I am not able to
look at a person as anything other than a set of facts about a person.
I do not appear to have an off position.*

*Result: nothing. As predicted, precisely, by somebody who has known me
about a year.*

---

He wrote the second half the following evening, after a day of thinking
about it on a ward, and the second half was the part that mattered.

*Amendment.*

*I said above that the result was nothing. That is not accurate and I
knew it was not accurate when I wrote it, which makes it the second
false entry in this book, and I am correcting it inside a day rather
than a fortnight, which is an improvement.*

*The result was not nothing. The result was that I spent four separate
attempts unable to stop cataloguing one specific person, and that when
I gave up and went to bed I lay there for about an hour with two bells
in my head.*

*I have been running the observation ladder on people for three years
since Kakashi taught it to me and I have got steadily better at it and
this is measurable — I can reach intention on a stranger in under
nine minutes and I could not reach rung two in the autumn of the year I
was sixteen.*

*But I have also, in the last eleven weeks, noticed the following about
one person, all of which was in front of me for two years:*

*The two bells.*

*The kanji on the back of the haori. It says* gamble. *In a red circle,
about nine inches, on the back of a garment she wears to Council
meetings and to foreign delegations and to the funeral of a Hokage.
I have stood behind her four times. I have looked at that back. I have
described that coat in this notebook and I wrote* grey-green silk, well
made, relined at the cuff *and I did not write down that there is a
word on it.*

*A woman who wears the name of her own vice across her shoulders in
public, deliberately, at nine inches, and I catalogued the lining.*

*The way she does not touch the necklace. Not that she touches it —
that she does not, and that the not-doing takes her about a quarter of
a second, and that it happens when somebody says something she has
decided not to react to.*

He stopped there, and got up, and put the kettle on and did not make
anything with it, which was a thing he had begun to notice he did.

Then he came back and wrote the part he had been walking around.

*Query.*

*I have two explanations and I cannot distinguish between them and I do
not think I am going to be able to.*

*One: I am simply better at this than I was. Three years of practice,
and the practice is general, and the reason I am noticing more about
her is that I am noticing more about everybody. This is supported by
the fact that I have also, this year, noticed that Emiko carries her
own scissors, that Ochiai's cardigan has been re-elbowed in wool that
does not match, that Kanzaki has known about the second bench for
fourteen months, and that Ashida's boots go at the outside heel on the
right.*

*Two: it is her.*

*Against explanation two: the general improvement is real and
measurable and I have the sheets.*

*For explanation two: I did not notice a nine-inch red circle with a
word in it on a coat I have described in writing. That is not a
failure of a general faculty. A general faculty that catches a
re-stitched belt loop at eleven feet does not miss a nine-inch kanji at
four. Something in me was doing something else while I was looking at
that back and I would very much like to know what.*

*I do not have a way to test this.*

*I noted the same problem in the fourth month about a different
question and arrived at the same place, which was that I was
constructing a mechanism because the alternative was that the variable
was a person. I have now done that twice.*

*I am going to stop constructing mechanisms.*

*I do not know whether it is her. I know that I have been unable to
find out by any method available to me, and that every method available
to me is one I invented for materials.*

---

He read the whole of it back on the Wednesday and did the thing the
book said to do at the end of each chapter, which was to write down
what he would do next.

*Chapter three: ask Yagi about the ear. Ask Ashida his wife's name and
his children's names and their ages, and do it badly, and do not
apologise for the three years.*

*Chapter six: find out eleven birthdays. Start with Handa, because he
will tell me and will not make anything of it.*

*Chapter two: no plan. Cannot do. Will read it again.*

*Chapter five: put a hand on somebody's shoulder before the end of the
month. Any shoulder. Not a patient.*

*Chapter four: unresolved. Eleven weeks, three designs, one burned.
Requirement still not established.*

And underneath, in the second column, where there was still no unit:

*Report the nothing on Thursday, as agreed, in full, including the
amendment.*

*She will have known there would be an amendment. She told me to come
back specifically so that I would have to say it out loud to somebody,
and I worked that out on the walk home and went back to see whether
she had done it deliberately, and she had, and she did not pretend
otherwise.*

*That is the fourth time she has done something for me that I did not
notice at the time and could not have asked for.*

*I have started counting them.*

*I am aware of what it means that I have started counting them and I
am going to leave that here rather than resolve it, because the last
two times I resolved something like this I was wrong in the same
direction, and because a man who cannot stop cataloguing a person
should probably not be trusted to audit why.*
