# Chapter 64, Scene 7 — Something Pushed Back

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the second training ground. Sazare Ruri, age
eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~2280 words.*

---

He closed everything.

He did it from nine feet, on the run, and it was one act on twenty-six
objects and it cost about as much as picking up a cup, and the whole of
the eastern half of ground two stopped at once.

Twenty-five of them stopped.

*One is still going.*

He got to the leg at about a second and a half and went down on his knees
in the dirt beside it and put both hands on it, which was the wrong thing
and which he did because it was what his hands had done four thousand
times on a ward.

*It is at the knee.*

*It went in at the ankle at the point of contact, through the wound
margin — there is a compound at the tibia and there was an opening about
two inches long and it has gone in through the opening because the
opening was the shortest route.*

*It is at the knee and it is not going through the leg. It is going
*along* it, in the compartment, medial, and it is following the vessel.*

*It is following the femoral.*

*Of course it is following the femoral. It goes toward what it can eat
and there is more of what it eats in nine inches of that artery than in
the whole of the rest of the limb.*

---

He closed it.

Not the general close — the specific one, on the one article, with his
hands on it and nine inches between him and the growing edge, the way he
had closed about eleven thousand things since he was seven years old, two
acts, a tenth of a second apart, welded together by eleven years of
habit.

Nothing happened.

He did it again.

*It has not taken.*

*I have closed an article and it has not taken and that has never
happened in my life. Not once. Not on the worst thing I ever made, not on
a bad grain, not at thirty-nine degrees with a fever, not at seventeen
with a hand going to stone.*

*The close has never failed.*

He did it a third time and got about a third of a second of it slowing
and then it went on, and that third of a second was the information.

---

*Right.*

*Say it. Now. In one line, because there is not time for anything else.*

*The close works by shutting the door.*

*That is all it has ever done and it is why everything I make dies —
because a shut article has got what it has got and it spends it and on
the sixth day it runs out, and I have been calling that decay for eleven
years and it is starvation.*

*Starvation only works on a thing that has got nothing else to eat.*

*This one is attached to the supply.*

*I can shut the door on it as many times as I like and it will not
matter, because it is not living off what is inside it, it is living off
what it is standing in, and what it is standing in is a sixteen-year-old
boy.*

---

"*Ruri.*"

Sakura was on the other side of the leg with her hands already going down
and he got hold of her wrist.

"Don't."

"His leg is—"

"Don't put your hands on him."

"Ruri, I am the *medic*—"

"There is a live article inside that limb," said Sazare, "and it is
drawing, and it goes to the largest thing it can reach, and if you put
your hands on him at this range you are a larger thing than the ground
is."

Sakura took her hands back about eleven inches.

"How fast?" she said.

"It was at the knee four seconds ago."

"Where is it now?"

He put his palm flat on the outside of the thigh.

*Mid-femur.*

*It has done about nine inches in four seconds and the last three of
those were faster than the first.*

"It's accelerating," he said.

---

He was aware of about four things happening around him and did not have
any part of himself available for any of them.

He was aware that Tsunade had arrived, because the light changed on his
left. He was aware that Jiraiya had come onto the ground at a speed that
did not belong to a man of fifty-four, and that he had come to the head
and not to the leg. He was aware, distantly, of Katsuyu saying something
at the north post and of one of the toads answering her.

*I cannot close it.*

*It will not take while it is attached to him.*

*So take it off him.*

*I cannot take it off him. It is inside a limb, it is in the medial
compartment, it is following the vessel and it has got about a foot of
itself in there and if I try to withdraw it I will take the femoral with
it and he will be dead in ninety seconds and I have watched that happen
twice.*

*So the answer is not to remove it and it is not to close it.*

*The answer is —*

*The answer is that there isn't one, and it is at mid-femur, and I have
got about eleven seconds before it is at the hip.*

---

It reached the hip in six.

He felt it go past the greater trochanter and into the pelvis and he felt
what happened to its speed when it did, and the speed was the thing that
told him.

*It has got faster again.*

*Four inches a second at the knee. About eleven at the femur. That is not
eleven, that is —*

*It is going up a gradient.*

*It is not moving faster because it is bigger. It is moving faster
because there is *more* the further up it goes, and it is going toward
the concentration the way water goes downhill, and it is not choosing
anything, it is falling.*

*And it is going to keep getting faster.*

*Which means there is something in the middle of him that is—*

He put both hands flat on the boy's abdomen.

---

He had had his hands on about four thousand abdomens.

He had had them on children and on soldiers and on a woman of ninety-one
in the third month and on four people whose insides he had personally
been inside, and he had four years of it and about two thousand hours of
anatomy and he could tell you, blind, in four seconds, whether a spleen
was where it should be.

He put his hands on Uzumaki Naruto's abdomen at nine feet from a ditch on
the twentieth of the twelfth month and there was something in there.

*What is that.*

*That is not an organ.*

*That is not anything. I have got no — there is nothing on any page of
anything I have ever read that is a thing in the abdomen of a healthy
sixteen-year-old male that is*

*it is not tissue*

*it is the size of*

He took his hands off.

He did not decide to do that either.

---

The crystal reached it at about eleven seconds.

And it stopped.

He felt the whole thing stop, all at once, the way a drill stops when it
hits something it was not designed for, and there was about a fifth of a
second of nothing at all.

And then it pushed back.

---

*It is pushing back.*

He was on his knees in the dirt of ground two with both hands off a boy's
body and about four inches of air between him and something he had made,
and it was not obeying him.

*My article is being pushed.*

*Nothing pushes an article. An article is a structure. It sits where it
is put and it holds what it is given and it fails when the load exceeds
it, and it does not* push back*, there is nothing in a structure to push
with, I have made nine hundred of them and not one of them has ever done
anything except be a shape.*

*Something in that boy has put a hand on a thing I made and is holding
it.*

*And it is —*

He got about that far.

*It is enormous.*

*I have got no scale for it and I have got no comparison and I have got
about four hundred pages on things I have no comparison for and every one
of them is a piece of stone.*

*It is enormous and it is not surprised and it has been in there the
entire time.*

*And it is angry.*

---

He closed it.

He did it without any process at all — no decision, no assessment, no
figure and no clause and no four seconds — and it went out of him the way
it had gone out of him at seven years old on the floor of a room in
Kirigakure with a piece of gravel in his hands and nobody watching.

Two acts. A tenth of a second apart.

Everything.

Twenty-six articles, the one in the leg, the four-foot piece at the post,
the eleven hundred pounds of live crystal on the eastern half of a
hundred and twenty feet of packed earth, and the thing in the pelvis of a
sixteen-year-old boy with about nine inches to go.

All of it. At once. From the ground up.

And it took.

---

It took because it was not fighting the supply any more.

He worked that out about four hours later, at a bench, and it was the
only part of the whole day that made him feel anything like himself.

*It stopped moving when it hit that. Whatever that is, it held it, for
about a fifth of a second, and while it was held it was not drawing.*

*And a thing that is not drawing is a thing with a door you can shut.*

*I did not know that. I had no idea. I have never once had an article
stop of its own accord for a fifth of a second in eleven years and if it
had not I would still be kneeling in that dirt.*

*I did not save him.*

*Whatever is in that boy held my work still for a fifth of a second and
that is the only reason the close took, and I have got nowhere to write
that down.*

---

The ground went quiet.

Not silent. There was a great deal of noise on ground two at that moment
and about four people were talking, and he did not hear any of it for
some seconds.

What he had was the bowl going out of the room.

It went the way it had come, which was gradually, over about four
seconds, and the treeline went first and then the ditch and then the far
bank, and at the end of it he was a man kneeling on packed earth with his
hands about nine inches from a boy's hip and his own heart going, and the
world was the ordinary size again and he could not see more than four
feet in any direction that mattered.

*It's off.*

*Whatever that was, it is off.*

He looked at his hands.

They were shaking, which had happened three times in his life and twice
of them had been in a yard.

"Ruri," said Tsunade.

He did not look up.

"Ruri, is it stopped?"

"It's stopped."

"You're sure."

"It's stopped and it's closed and it will not start again," he said, "and
I want to say that I am certain of the first two and that the third one
is an assumption, and it is an assumption I have got about nine hundred
articles behind and one of them has just done something that has never
happened."

He put a hand on the ankle.

"There is about fourteen inches of it inside that limb," he said, "and it
is dead, and it is going to have to come out, and I am the only person
alive who can take it out, and it is going to take about four hours."

---

He looked up then.

Jiraiya was at the head with two fingers on the boy's throat and had not
moved for eleven seconds, and he was not looking at the boy.

He was looking at the abdomen.

And Tsunade, who had knelt down on the other side of the leg and had her
hands out and had already got the whole of the tibia off about a second
and a half of palpation, had gone the colour of the underside of a leaf.

Neither of them said anything.

Neither of them looked at each other, which was the part he came back to.

*They are not going to look at each other.*

*Two people who have known one another for fifty-three years, kneeling on
either side of a boy, and both of them have got something and neither of
them is going to check whether the other one has got it, because
checking would be admitting it in front of me.*

*Right.*

*I do not know what is in that boy.*

*They do.*

*And they have both just found out that I have had my hands on it, and
that is what has happened to their faces, and it is not about Naruto.*

*It is about me.*

He put his hands back on the leg.

"Sakura," he said. "I need the kit, and I need a board, and I need you on
the head, and I am going to be about ninety seconds getting the ankle
free and I want somebody counting out loud the whole time."
