# Chapter 63, Scene 6 — The Only Brake

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the Hokage's tower. Tsunade, age fifty-three.
POV: Tsunade. ~1590 words.*

---

Jiraiya came through the window at about half past eleven, twenty minutes
after the boy had gone down the corridor with four pages and a lamp, and
he did not say anything for long enough that she put the pen down.

"Go on," she said.

"I've been on the bathhouse roof for three days."

"I know you have."

"You do not know I have."

"I have known since Tuesday afternoon," said Tsunade, "because you cannot
sit still and there is a tile on that roof that has been out of line since
the ninth year and it has been in line since Tuesday, and I look out of
that window about four hundred times a day."

Jiraiya sat down on the sill.

"Nine out of nine," he said.

---

"Say your version," said Tsunade. "I've had mine since eleven o'clock
this morning and I have been round it four times and I would like
somebody else's."

"Mine's shorter than yours."

"Everything of yours is shorter than mine."

"There is no incident."

He put his hands on his knees.

"That's it. That is the whole of my version and it took me about eleven
years to be able to say a sentence like that with any confidence about
anybody.

"Four countries have a file on that boy. I have read two of them
properly and I know the shape of the third. Kiri's runs to about ninety
pages because he is a claim and a bloodline and an embarrassment, and
Oto's is an acquisition file and I have not read it and I know what is in
it, and ours is four years of ward notes and a study and a post.

"And there is no incident. In any of them.

"Not a fight. Not a brawl. Not a market row — he was cheated for eleven
weeks and paid it. No disciplinary, no complaint, no altercation, nobody
struck, nobody threatened, and not one line anywhere in ninety pages
about that boy raising a hand to a human being.

"Eleven years, Hime. From seven and a half years old.

"There is not a man in this village with a record like that and I include
the ones who have never left the walls."

---

"So you've got the same finding," said Tsunade.

"I have got the same finding and I have not got the same *worry*," said
Jiraiya, "and that is why I came through the window."

She turned round.

"Go on."

"You are frightened he will not fight."

"Yes."

"That he is going to walk out of that gate in about four months and
somebody is going to come at him who means it, and he is going to do the
same nine out of nine with nobody counting, and it will be a road and not
a yard."

"Yes."

"I know," said Jiraiya. "I have had that one as well and I had it on
Tuesday and it is real and I am not going to argue with it.

"And it is not the one that has had me on a roof for three days."

He looked at the window.

"I am frightened of the other one," he said.

---

Tsunade did not move.

"Say it properly."

"That boy has got jōnin reserves and no seals and no interval," said
Jiraiya. "Nothing. There is not one thousandth of a second between
deciding and the world finding out, and I said that to him myself on that
sill on the eighteenth of last month and I have not stopped thinking about
it since.

"He can put four inches of graded article anywhere inside forty feet in
under a second and he can do it without moving his hands and he does not
need material, and the slug has him at about two hundred and forty of the
small ones on the sheet he brought you tonight at *two-fifths* of his
capacity.

"He has got a scalpel and four years of anatomy and he told you in a yard
this afternoon exactly where the axillary is and how long it takes.

"He has got no face, which means no bluff, which is the only thing on the
list that costs him.

"And there is no governor on any of it. There is a piece of paper with
eleven clauses on it and your name at the bottom, and it is a *docket*,
and everybody in this village including him has known since the eleventh
of last month that it is not holding.

"So what has been holding?"

Tsunade did not answer.

"Nine out of nine," said Jiraiya. "That is what has been holding.

"For eleven years. That is the whole brake. It is not the licence and it
is not the box and it is not the fifteen on that council and it is not
you — it is a reflex that boy built at seven and a half years old going
out through the eastern lines, and it has never once failed, and it is the
only reason that four countries have got a file on a man who has never
hurt anybody."

---

"And you want it left alone," she said.

"I want it *understood*," said Jiraiya. "I have been on a roof for three
days watching a slug and a Hokage try to teach a boy to move toward
somebody, and I have not been able to work out whether I want it to work.

"And I have decided tonight that I do not know, and that not knowing is
the honest position, and that somebody in this building ought to have
said it out loud before Friday morning."

"He's going to lose on Friday."

"He is going to lose on Friday and I have got money on it," said
Jiraiya, "and I want him to lose on Friday, and I have been telling
myself that is about the boy learning something and it is not.

"It is that I would rather find out about all of this at half past nine
in the morning on ground two with Sakura Haruno nine feet away with her
hands free."

"That's my reason."

"I know it's your reason. I have watched you have it for three days and I
have watched you not tell him, and I am not going to tell you that you
have got it wrong, because I have spent thirty-one years not telling
people things for exactly that reason and about a third of the time I was
right."

---

Tsunade stood at the window for a while.

"There's a third thing," she said, "and you have not said it and I do not
think it is because you have not got it."

"No."

"Say it, then."

Jiraiya rubbed the back of his neck.

"If it ever goes," he said, "it will not go because somebody came at
*him*."

The room was quiet.

"He has had eleven years of things coming at him and he has gone round
every single one," said Jiraiya. "The market, the licence, the council,
the woman who took his figure off him in front of eight people, Kiri, all
of it. He has been walked at his whole life and he has never once walked
back.

"So it is not going to be about him. That reflex has been tested on him
about four hundred times and it holds.

"It is going to be about somebody else."

He looked at her, once, and then away.

"And I am not going to say the next part of that sentence in this room,"
he said, "and you have already got it, and we are both going to go to bed
now."

---

He went out of the window at about midnight and she stood at it for some
time afterwards.

*He is right and he has been right since Tuesday and he has sat on a
bathhouse roof for three days rather than come up here and say it, which
is the most Jiraiya thing that has ever happened.*

*And I have got the next part of the sentence.*

*I have had it since about half past four this afternoon, sitting on a
step in a yard, watching a boy write* not to be used *under a line and
put the date on it because I asked him to.*

*He did that in about four seconds. No argument, no hesitation, and he
did not ask why and I gave him the reason anyway and it was a good one
and it was not the real one.*

*I did not ask him to write that down because of what might happen to
him.*

She put the lamp out.

*Down the corridor there is a boy who has not raised a hand to a human
being in eleven years and who wrote a sentence with my own death in it in
a chair in this room four weeks ago and did not change his voice.*

*And on Tuesday I put money on him in front of witnesses because I could
not say his name in a bet against him.*

*So I am not going to pretend I do not know which way this goes.*

*And I have got about nine hours before I have to stand on the edge of a
training ground and watch him lose to a sixteen-year-old, and I want him
to lose, and I have got money on him to win, and I am going to have to be
in exactly one of those states on my face and I have not decided which.*
