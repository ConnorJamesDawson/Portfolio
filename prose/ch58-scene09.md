# Chapter 58, Scene 9 — Mine

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the Hokage's tower. Tsunade, age fifty-three.
POV: Tsunade. ~1050 words.*

---

She had got up somewhere around *if it's less than the ordinary
version*.

She could not have said afterwards what her intention had been. There
had not been one. She had been sitting at that desk for three minutes
and fifty seconds listening to an eighteen-year-old lay out a case
about her with nine numbered observations in it, four of which she had
not known she had done, and she had been perfectly able to sit still
for all of that.

And then he had started dismantling himself, politely, in clauses, to
make sure it did not weigh on her.

*He built the escape hatch.*

*He sat there at four in the morning and he built me a way out and he
put it at the end on purpose, and he explained why it went at the end,
and the reason is that if he had put it first the rest would have been
pressure.*

*He has done a security assessment on his own declaration.*

She had got about halfway round the desk on that one.

*And then he offered to be less than the ordinary version. For as long
as it was on offer. And said he would not ask for a single thing beyond
it and would like it written down somewhere.*

That was where she had taken hold of his coat.

---

She looked at him now.

He had not moved his hands off his knees. That was the first thing and
it went into her and did not come out again for a very long time: that
a young man had been kissed on the mouth by a woman he had been in love
with for however long, and had sat there with his hands on his knees,
because moving them had not crossed his mind.

Then he had laughed, and it had come apart in the middle, and he had
put a hand over his mouth like a child at a funeral.

And he was still doing it.

He was sitting in her chair with his eyes shut and his face completely
undefended and about four separate things happening on it at once, and
not one of them was being managed, and he was not aware that any of it
was showing.

*Look at it.*

*Go on. You've got a list. You've had it forty years and you gave it to
him at volume on Wednesday night with an item four you have never
explained to anybody, and every single thing on it is a* performance*.*

*The name is a performance. The story is a performance. The henge is a
performance and it is mine. The insurance policy, the mothering, the
post — all of it is a man doing something at me and calling it wanting
me.*

*Forty years of watching men present.*

She stood there with her hand on the arm of the chair.

*You cannot fake that.*

*There is no amount of acting in the world. I have been lied to by
professionals — I have been lied to by* Orochimaru*, in a field, with a
straight face, about something that mattered — and I have never once
been fooled and I have never once let it show that I was not fooled.*

*And a boy who has not got a face has just gone to pieces in a chair in
front of me and could not have stopped one second of it if his life had
been on the table.*

*It isn't a technique. He hasn't got one. That's the whole of him.*

*He couldn't perform if you paid him. He has never been able to. It is
the thing that will get him killed and it is the reason four different
people in this village have decided to stand between him and a
procedure in the last nine days without once discussing it with each
other.*

---

*Right.*

*So here it is, and it's five past four in the morning, and you're going
to have to actually decide something for once in your life instead of
doing what you have done since the eleventh, which is let it happen and
tell yourself you haven't agreed to anything.*

She ran it properly, the way she made her students do it, in four
sentences.

*He is eighteen and I am fifty-three.*

*It lands on me and it lands on him and it lands on this post, and there
is a version where the council has it in a fortnight and a version where
it never comes out at all, and I cannot control which.*

*Jiraiya sat on that windowsill on Monday and told me the boy is a lever
on me and I am the thing being levered, and he was right, and he is
never wrong about that class of thing, and I have not slept properly
since.*

*And I have got nothing to put against any of it except that he cannot
lie and he came up my stairs at four in the morning to hand me an
escape hatch.*

She looked down at him.

He had got the hand off his mouth and was trying to straighten his face
out and failing completely, and he said something small and idiotic and
entirely sincere about wanting another one, and she heard herself make
a noise she had not made in about thirty years.

*Sod it.*

*I've done the arithmetic four hundred times in ten days and I get the
same answer every time and I have not once been able to act on it, and
the reason I have not been able to act on it is that every version has
me deciding for him.*

*He's just decided. He came up here and did it in four minutes with
clauses and gave me the way out at the end.*

*So that's that.*

*He's mine.*

*And if the council, or the fifteen, or Kiri, or the daimyō, or any
other bastard on this continent takes a view about it — they can come up
this hill and put it to me in person, and I will be extremely
interested to hear it, and then I will do exactly as I like.*

---

"Ruri," she said. "Shut up."

And she kissed him again, and this time she did it properly, and she did
not think about the door once.
