# Chapter 64, Scene 5 — The Ground Is Mine

*Act 5, part A. Konoha, the second training ground. Sazare Ruri, age
eighteen. POV: Sazare. ~2050 words.*

---

He had been holding for about nine seconds when the bowl came into the
room.

He knew what it was because it had happened before, once, on the
twenty-sixth of the eleventh month in a quartermaster's yard at half past
nine at night, alone, against every piece of advice he had been given in
writing.

*The yard was in the room with me.*

*The flags and the frost at the north end and the bench and the sacking
and four hundred grams of reject in a tin, and the aggregate in the far
bay at thirty-one per cent, and the wall, and the lamp, and the hill
under all of it.*

*And it was not information. There was nothing in it I could have used.*

This was not that.

This was a hundred and twenty feet of packed earth in a bowl and it came
in the same way and it did not stop where the yard had stopped, and it
kept coming for about four seconds, and at the end of the four seconds
he had the whole of it and there was no edge on it anywhere.

*The ditch. Four inches of water and the lip and about eleven feet of
sedge on the far bank.*

*The three posts, and the middle one is rotten eleven inches down from
the top on the north face and has been for about four years.*

*The treeline. All of it. Two hundred and some, and the roots, and the
one on the western edge with a man standing four feet clear of it.*

*The bowl itself. Packed earth over about nine inches of made ground over
gravel over clay, and there is a drain under the north end that nobody
has opened since the ninth year.*

*And it is not information.*

*It is that all of it is in here with me and I am not looking out at any
of it.*

---

And then, underneath the bowl, he found his own work.

*Nine hundred pounds.*

He had it all at once, in a way that was not counting and that he had
never once been able to do at a bench with a lamp and a felt.

*Two walls, down. The bar, four pieces. Four spikes standing and nine
reinforced ones standing. The box, eleven pieces, twelve-foot circle.*

*Twenty-six articles by the piece. Every single one of them closed.*

*Every single one of them is dying.*

---

*Right.*

*I know that. I have known that since I was nine. Everything I make is
dead by Friday and I have got a controlled pair from the twenty-second
and the twenty-seventh of the eleventh month and the finding is that the
close is the mechanism and order is not.*

*And I have got the word wrong for eleven years.*

He stood in a ditch on the eastern edge of ground two with his shoulders
down and his hands at his sides and had it, complete, in about a second
and a half, in the way that findings came.

*It is not decay.*

*Decay is a thing coming apart. Decay is the order going out of a
structure because structures come apart, and I have written it up that
way about four hundred times and every one of those entries is wrong.*

*They are starving.*

*I lay a structure and I close it, and what is in there is what it has
got, and it spends it — holding itself together spends it, being a
structure spends it, it has been spending it since the moment I shut the
door — and on the sixth day it runs out and it stops being able to afford
to be a shape.*

*It is not dying. It is being starved by me and it has been starved by me
nine hundred times.*

---

*And the ground is full.*

That was the second half and it came about a second after the first, and
it came because the whole of the bowl was in the room and he could feel
the difference between two things he had never had in the same place
before.

*The earth is full. The clay is full. The trees are full and the sedge on
the far bank is full and the water in that ditch is full and the man
against the tree on the western edge is so full that I have got to be
careful about looking at him.*

*It is in everything. It has always been in everything, and Katsuyu told
me so in the eighth month and I wrote it down as a property of the
world.*

*It is four inches away from every article I have ever made.*

*And they cannot reach it, because I shut them.*

---

*The rose kept drinking.*

*Nine days. It was open and it was saturated and it was still going on
the fifteenth of the eighth month, and I put a bare palm on it and it
came through me and took my hand to the carpus in eighteen minutes.*

*And I have spent four months treating that as the thing to be avoided.*

*It is not the hazard. It is the answer, and the hazard is that it went
through a human being, and the reason it went through a human being is
that I was standing in the circuit and the hilt did not exist yet.*

*Nothing is standing in the circuit here.*

*There is nine hundred pounds of my own work lying on a hundred and
twenty feet of ground with nobody touching any of it.*

He did not decide.

He had no capacity to decide; he was holding, and holding took the whole
of him, and there was nothing left over anywhere for a decision — and
afterwards, when he wrote it up, that was the only part of the entry he
could not make honest, because *I did not decide* was true and was also
the sentence every man writes at the front of an accident report.

He opened them.

---

All of them.

That was the part he came back to for months, at the bench, at two in the
morning, with the sheet in front of him and the figure at the bottom of
it.

*I cannot make two things at once.*

*That is not a limitation I have got, it is what I am. I hold the entire
shape by hand, every time, from nothing, and there is one of me, and a
hinge is two objects and a relationship and I have got no operation for a
relationship and I told two mountains so in a long room on the
twenty-eighth of the eleventh month.*

*I opened twenty-six articles simultaneously.*

*And it cost me almost nothing.*

*Because I was not building.*

*Building is holding a shape. Opening is not a shape — it is taking the
close off a thing that already has one, and the close is one act, and one
act on twenty-six objects at once is one act.*

*I have had that available since I was nine years old.*

---

The ground began to eat.

He felt the first one go at about eleven seconds — the largest piece of
the box, twelve feet away, where it lay half on packed earth and half on
the made ground — and it went the way a splint goes when the load finally
comes onto it, which was with a settling.

Then it drew.

*It is taking the ground.*

*Not the chakra in the ground. The ground. It is taking the fill, and it
is putting the fill into itself as structure, and it is not stopping when
it has enough because there is no* enough*, there is no specification and
nobody has told it how big to be —*

The piece grew.

It went from about four inches to about eleven in something under two
seconds and it did not grow the way a thing grows, it grew the way frost
goes across a window, out from the edges, in the direction of whatever
was nearest.

And when it reached the next piece it took that one too.

---

*It is eating my own work.*

*The pieces are feedstock. They are closed and they are full of order and
they are the richest thing on this ground apart from the trees, and a
growing one has just consumed a dead one and got bigger for it.*

*And the dead one had about four days left in it and it has been spent in
a second and a half and there is more crystal now than there was.*

He stood in the ditch and did not move.

*Twenty-six pieces.*

*Six of them have gone into three. Three into two. The two are about four
feet across and they are still going and they are going toward each
other, and when they touch —*

*Right.*

*Say the whole of it and say it now, because I am going to need it later
and I am not going to be able to reconstruct this.*

*I have made a thing that grows.*

*It draws, it maintains itself, it grows, and it grows toward what it can
eat, and it does not have a size and it does not have an end and there is
nothing anywhere in it that says stop.*

*I wrote a line in the eleventh month about a completely different
problem and I underlined it, which I do not do.*

A thing that reproduces cannot be recalled.

---

He was still holding.

That was the thing he had not got yet and would not get for another four
seconds, and it was the only thing on the ground that actually mattered.

He was holding because he had run out of directions and because holding
was the one thing anybody had ever taught him, and he had been holding
for about twenty-two seconds, and in that time he had done nothing at all
except take the close off twenty-six pieces of his own broken work.

He had not spent a second of capacity.

He had not built one article.

And there was, on the eastern half of a hundred and twenty feet of packed
earth, about eleven hundred pounds of live crystal where there had been
nine hundred pounds of dead crystal, and it was growing at something over
a foot every four seconds, and every foot of it made the next foot
cheaper.

*The longer I stand here.*

He got that at twenty-six seconds.

*That is the whole of it and it is not a tactic and I did not build it.*

*The longer I stand in one place, the more of this ground is mine, and it
costs me nothing, and it accelerates, and I do not have to be near any of
it because moving a placed article is a fortieth of the price and does
not care about distance.*

*Range is a price. Placed articles are not.*

*I wrote that in capitals on a step on Thursday afternoon and I thought I
had found a tactic.*

*I had found what I am.*

---

And then Naruto came.

He came from twenty feet, from a standing start, and Sazare had the whole
bowl in the room and had him for the entire distance in a way he had not
had anything all morning — the weight going forward, the shoulder, the
line of it, the exact place the lead foot was going to come down at the
fourth step and the fifth and the sixth.

And he had something in his right hand.

*That is not a fist.*

*There is something in his hand and it is turning and it is — I have got
no reading for it. I have got about four hundred pages on how things
behave under load and I have got nothing at all for whatever that is, and
it is about four inches across and it is the densest thing on this
ground apart from him.*

*He has stopped being careful.*

*Nine feet.*

*Seven.*

And for the first time in five minutes and forty seconds, Uzumaki Naruto
was doing something entirely predictable.

*Both feet committed. No options. He cannot turn and he cannot stop and
he has one line and it ends at me.*

*Fourth step.*

*There.*
