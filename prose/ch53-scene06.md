# Chapter 53, Scene 6 — Why Me

*Act 4, part D. Konoha, the Hokage's office. Sazare Ruri, age eighteen.
POV: Sazare. ~210 words.*

---

She drew breath to say something.

He saw that, because he was four feet away and had been watching her
hands for three years, and he had time — a quarter of a second, perhaps
less — to construct what it was going to be, and he got as far as
assuming it would be *go home* in a different voice.

It was not that.

What he saw, and could not afterwards describe to himself in any
notation he owned, was something arriving ahead of her.

She was a woman who had spent forty years being extremely good at
this. He had watched her refuse a slug in a cold yard with no edge on
it anywhere. He had watched her decline to answer a question and let
the not-answering be the answer, and stop a sentence in a scrubbed room
with a single word, and take twenty seconds over a *no* and get it out
gently at the end of them.

And this got past her.

It came up out of somewhere and went through the whole of that
apparatus without touching a single part of it, and she heard it at the
same moment he did, and by then it was in the room.

Two words.

"Why me?"
