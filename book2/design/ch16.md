## 2. ⚠ THE MIDDLE CONTACT — Sarugaya, the sixth
## And it is the chapter's hinge

**The register is the trade's and it is the one Tadayoshi is worst at.**
*Nobody says a noun. Nothing operational is stated. The whole brief is
delivered as ordinary local information by a man who is doing something
else while he says it.*

### What is actually said, and none of it is a lie

> ***"There's a drover takes about forty head over the second ford below
> Hane. Ninth, about an hour after light. It's a bad crossing while he's
> in it."***
>
> ***"Water's low on the near side. You'd want to be past the willows and
> not in the open, if you were minded to wait for it to clear."***
>
> ***"And when the cattle are in, nobody hears anything and nobody looks
> at anything, and that lasts about four minutes."***

**Every sentence is true, checkable, and about cattle.**

### What Tadayoshi takes out of it, and he takes all of it

*He has a ford — **the second**, of four. He has an hour. He has a
position — **past the willows, off the open**. He has a window — **four
minutes**. And he has a signal — **the cattle going into the water**.*

> ***That is a brief. That is the best brief I have ever been given and it
> has not got one word in it that anybody could repeat to a magistrate.***

**⚠ AND HE ADMIRES IT.** *Fourth time* (`ch12` Enkai, `ch14` Deguchi,
`ch15` the boilerplate, **this**) — **and this one is his own side, to his
face, and he still does not ask what it is for.**

### What Uzuki takes out of it, and it is the other half

**She is not listening to the words. She has been reading people since the
sixth month and there is a man in front of her doing a thing with his
hands.**

- *He says **not goods** once, about nothing, and moves off it.*
- *He does not ask them a single question, and a man arranging a job asks
  questions.*
- *He is done in about six minutes and it takes him eleven, and the extra
  five are him not leaving.*

> ***He does not want to have said this to us.***
>
> ***And he is going to say it anyway, and he has said it, and he is still
> standing here.***

**She does not know what it is either.** *She knows there is one, and that
is the whole of her advantage and it is worth nothing on the day.*

> ## ***If I don't call your name, don't fight beside me.***

**It is written here, in scene 10, on the evening of the ninth, by a boy

### ⚠ AND HE WRITES IT WITHOUT THE REASON, WHICH IS THE WHOLE POINT

**He has no data on the state and never will** *(§9: he can measure
everything about it except its arrival)*, **so he cannot write down a
mechanism.** *What he can specify is behaviour, so what goes in the book is

the creed (`ch11`) and the ringing (`ch15` s3).*

> **⚠ AND HE PUTS THE MADE-AND-NOT-COUNTED MARK BESIDE IT**, *which by now
> he has used on four things in two months that are not numbers, and has
> stopped seeing.*

- **It is written out of care and it is written in anger and he would deny
  the second.**
- **It is phrased generically, for anybody**, *because that is how he
  writes everything* — **and she reads it and works out in about two

- **⚠ AND IT IS ALREADY BROKEN BEFORE THE INK IS DRY**, *because what she

> **AND NOBODY EXPLAINS IT TO HER FOR YEARS.** *The reason is Uzuki's and
> it arrives late* (§9) — **so between here and there she obeys a rule
> nobody has justified, from a boy who cannot justify it, which is the

**Four rules, and they are the hardest thing in the chapter.**

## 10. THE SIX HARD PROBLEMS

**1. ⚠ THE BRIEF MUST CONTAIN NO MESSAGE.** *§1's protection.* **The
contact does not know what is in the crate**, *so there is nothing for
Tadayoshi to fail to decode.* **He gets a ford, an hour, a position, a
window and a signal, and he gets all five, because they are geometry.**
*What he misses is a man's discomfort, which is not information and which
Uzuki gets and also cannot use.*

**2. THE OP MUST NOT BE HIS.** *He does not plan it, lead it, or win it.*
**Two of the escort come at him because he looks cheap**, *and that is the
whole of his part in a two-minute operation.* **If the prose lets him be
the reason it succeeds, `ch17` has nothing to be angry about.**

**3. ⚠ §2m IS ON TRIAL.** *Positions before anything happens; actor, limb
and direction; **consequence in the other body**; a chain and not a list;
a POV limit changes which details and never how many.* **Could a reader
draw it.** *And the short-exchange arithmetic runs the right way round.*

**4. ⚠ THE FETCHING MUST BE SLOW.** *Every instinct will compress forty
yards of walking into a sentence.* **Refuse.** *It is the most frightening
thing in the chapter and it contains no violence at all* — **a girl of
eleven crossing an open field in a hat, counting the people who do not
look at her**, *and the count is the scene.*

**5. NOBODY IS A VILLAIN AND NOBODY MAKES A SPEECH.** *Cruelty two is one
man not walking within eight feet of a crate*, **and no line of dialogue
may explain it.** *`ch17` explains it.*

**6. ⚠ THE THRESHOLD IS NOT SPENT**, *and neither is the stab* (§7).

cattle.** *He takes all five — ford, hour, position, window, signal — inside

**And what §2 could not have predicted is what he does with the hour
afterwards:** *he works out that a position is not a task,* **that he is on
the side she comes back to**, *and that he has been given a corner to stand
in while somebody else does a job* — **and it arrives forty minutes too
late to be an argument.**

### ⚠ Kimimaro's clock is his own rule paid back

**§3 gave him the seams.** *The prose makes him refuse to use them until
they have held four cycles, and the reason is his own rule from `ch15`:*
***I decided what somebody else was going to do. I am not going to do that
again.*** **The instrument he gains is gated by the lesson he learned
losing the last one.**

**And the 903 gets a new unit and a horrifying use.** *It is dead as bowls;
the shape survives; he cannot do the sixth in days because he has the end
and not the start;* **so it is the seventh, and he wants it to start** —
**and never once, in ten minutes of correct arithmetic, has the thought
that what he is building is a date.**

### And the sky is the last item in a place that was empty

**§3 asked for it. What the prose adds is the drawer:** *the place where
things go that he cannot use, which had never had anything in it before.*
**It is the first item and it is the sky.**
