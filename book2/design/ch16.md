## 1. THE THREE CRUELTIES ARE STAGED, NOT ARGUED

**`structure.md` §4 lists them and the chapter's only job is to make all
three free** — *no scene may argue for any of them.*

1. **THE CARGO IS THE CHILD, AND TAKING HIM IS OBEDIENCE.** *He is given a
   field order by a man with standing to give it, he asks one question, he
   is answered, and he does it.* **So `ch17` cannot be about
   insubordination** — *which forces it to be about what everybody
   actually objects to.*
2. **EVERYBODY ELSE SEES ANOTHER MONSTER**, *and the chapter plants it in
   one gesture and no dialogue:* **one of the team will not go near the
   crate.**
3. **AND NOBODY IS A VILLAIN.** *The team is competent, tired, and
   ordinary. One of them is decent to him. One of them is decent to him
   **and** will not go near the crate, and it is the same person.*

## 2. ⚠ UZUKI FINDS HIM — and it is the chapter's best structural move

**The reveal does not belong to Tadayoshi.** *He gets the order. She gets
the finding.*

### Why it has to be her, in three facts already on the page

- **Nobody counts her** (`act-2.md` §11). *In a fight at a crossing, a
  girl with a pack is scenery, and she can walk to a cart nobody is
  guarding any more.*
- **She reads roping and loads** (`ch14`, the mule; `ch15`, the pile).
  **A crate roped for a live load is roped differently and she has watched
  a man do it** — *and the tell is not the sound. It is that the lashings
  go over and not round, because a thing that can shift gets held down and
  a thing that cannot gets held in.*
- **And she says everything, immediately** (`portraits.md`). *So there is
  no scene in which she decides whether to tell him.*

> **⚠ AND IT SEEDS §6.1 OF `team.md` TWO YEARS EARLY.** *The unit that
> actually works is Uzuki and Kimimaro, and it works because she tells him
> things out loud.* **The first thing she ever does about him is find
> him**, *and neither of them ever knows that, because he was inside a box
> for the whole of it.*

### ⚠ AND THE AUTHOR'S STAB IS NOT SPENT HERE

**`act-2.md` §11 is AUTHOR and it is fully specified** — *the arity
problem, the pig knife, the back of the leg, Tadayoshi finishes him, and
the horror is in the afternoon.* **Its stated context is hunters** — ***a
fight that is taking too long, which is the same thing with hunters*** —
*and these are a buyer's escort, not the curve.*

> **So it stays outstanding, and `ch16` must not spend it.** *She acts,
> and what she does is the finding, and she does not put a knife in
> anybody.* **The first one is still the first one.**

## 3. ⚠ KIMIMARO GETS AN INSTRUMENT BACK, AND IT IS THE FIRST ONE NOBODY TAKES

**Three have gone in three chapters** — *the bowls (`ch13`), the screaming
(`ch14`), the room (`ch15`)* — **and the pattern is now legible enough
that a fourth loss would be a tic.**

### What the box gives him

**The seams.** *Light along the lid edge, four of them, and they go grey
and then not.* **He has day and night for the first time in two years and
he did not have to build it** — *it was simply there when they shut the
lid, and he had it inside one cycle and spent the second one checking it.*

> **⚠ AND HE IS SUSPICIOUS OF IT, WHICH IS THE WHOLE OF HIM.** *He has
> been given a clock by accident three times and lost it three times, and
> the light in the room failed for a reason he wrote down* — **so he
> tests the seams against the only other thing he has, which is the
> openings of the lid, and they agree, and he still marks it *made and
> not counted* in his own way, which is that he will not use it for
> anything that matters until it has held for four.**

**It holds for four. He gets eleven days and he is right about all of
them.**

### And at the end of the chapter it is taken by being replaced

**The lid comes off for good and he is on a road at night, and what is
above him is the sky.**

> ***That is an instrument and it is too big and I have not got a unit for
> it.***
>
> **The first thing that has ever been given to him that he could not
> use** — *and it is not cruel, and nobody did it to him, and it is the
> worst of the four.*

## 4. THE TWO ON THE ROAD — the canon impression, and it is a refusal

**`canon-cast.md` §3.3, spent here.** *Itachi and Kisame, seen and not
named, on the road between Ochiai and Sarugaya, going the other way.*

- **He reads them in about forty seconds** — *one very large, with

## 6. ⚠ THE CROSSING — and §2m is on trial

**This is the first real fight since `method-delta.md` §2m and the plan
must say so.** *Five rules, and the diagnostic is **could a reader draw
it**.*

### The staging, and it is written before anything happens

- **The crossing below Hane is a ford, not a bridge**, *and there are four
  of them below Hane and this is the second.*
- **The handover completes first.** *A man produces the counterpart. The
  case goes off the frame onto a hand-cart. The counterpart is signed. **He
  is paid three thousand and he is finished**, and he is putting the paper
  away when it starts.*
- **So he has no obligation left at the moment it goes off**, *which is
  the arrangement that makes everything afterwards his own decision and
  not his job.*

### The fight itself — and he is not the point of it

- **The op is the team's.** *They are there for the cart on the far side
  and they are competent and it takes about two minutes.*
- **⚠ WHAT COMES AT HIM IS THE SOFT-TARGET DECISION.** *Two of the escort
  see a boy, a girl, a mule and a case and go for the easy end of the
  field, because that is what you do when the hard end is already
  happening* — **and it is `combat.md` §10b arity from the other side: he
  is one object and they have decided he is the cheap one.**
- **Full choreography, positions first, consequences in the other body**
  (§2m). **Short exchange, higher detail budget per second, not lower.**
- **⚠ AND THE THRESHOLD DOES NOT FIRE.** *`combat.md` §9 is not spent
  here.* **The tone climbs and falls and Uzuki hears both**, *and that is
  the whole of it, and it is the second time she has heard the fall.*

## 7. THE ORDER — and it is nine words and a question

**A man with standing** — *the handler on the ground, not Ōtsuka, and not
anybody Tadayoshi has met* — **gives him the brief the docket did not
contain.**

> ***"Cargo's in the crate. Take it and go north and don't stop."***

**And Tadayoshi asks one question**, *because he asks one question:*

> ***"Whose is it?"*** *— and the answer is:* ***"Ours now."***

*Which is an answer, and it is true, and it is complete, and it is the
last honest sentence anybody says to him in this book about that boy.*

- **He does not ask what is in the crate.** *She has already told him and
  he has already believed her.*
- **⚠ AND HE DOES IT.** *He is not choosing. He is executing.* **The
  choosing is `ch17` and the argument in `ch17` cannot be about the
  order, because he obeyed it.**
- **One of the team will not come near the crate while he opens it**, *and
  says nothing about it, and helps with the mule instead* — **and that is
  cruelty two, planted, with no dialogue attached.**

## 8. ⚠ THE LID — the last scene, and it must not be a rescue

**Four rules for it, and they are the hardest thing in the chapter.**

1. **NO RELIEF.** *He is moved from a box to a road. That is the category
   of the event and it is the same category as everything else that has
   happened to him.* **He does not feel saved, because he has no model in
   which anybody saves anybody.**
2. **HE MEASURES THE BOY WHO OPENED IT.** *Height against the lid, hands,
   the way he stands, how long he takes to say the first thing* — **and it
   is the second person he has ever had data about, and the first one had
   a book.**
3. **⚠ HE IS ASKED SOMETHING, AND IT IS THE THIRD TIME.**
   > *The first was* ***cold enough for you?*** *(`ch13` s8) — asked, and
   > he was not ready, and built the answer over two days and carried it
   > eleven and nobody asked again.*
   >
   > *The second was the man with the book (`ch15` s6), who asked him
   > nothing at all.* ***That is twice.***
   >
   > **And the third is Tadayoshi, who asks an operational question
   > because it is the only kind he has** — ***"How long have you been in
   > there?"*** — **and Kimimaro has the answer, exactly, in his own
   > units, and gives the whole of it.**
   >
   > **⚠ It is the longest thing he has said out loud in two years and it
   > is a measurement**, *and the boy standing over him writes it down.*
4. **AND HE DOES WHAT HE IS TOLD**, *because that is what he does, and
   nobody in the scene notices that it is not consent* — **including the
   narration, which must not say so.** *The reader does the whole of that
   work or the scene has told them what to think.*

## 10. THE SIX HARD PROBLEMS

**1. ⚠ THE READER IS AHEAD AND THE CHAPTER MUST NOT PRETEND OTHERWISE.**
*Since `ch15` s2 the reader has known there is a boy in a room and a boy
with a docket.* **So the tension is not *what is in the crate*. It is
*what does he do when he is told*** — *and every scene must be built for
the second question, which means no coy staging, no withheld noun, and no
scene that would be pointless if the reader already knew.*

**2. THE OP MUST NOT BE HIS.** *He does not plan it, lead it, or win it.*
**Two of the escort come at him because he looks cheap**, *and that is the
whole of his part in a two-minute operation* — **and if the prose lets him
be the reason it succeeds, `ch17` has nothing to be angry about.**

**3. ⚠ §2m IS ON TRIAL.** *Positions before anything happens; actor, limb
and direction; **consequence in the other body**; a chain and not a list;
a POV limit changes which details and never how many.* **Diagnostic: could
a reader draw it.** *And the short-exchange arithmetic — he is fast, so
the budget per second goes **up**.*

**4. NOBODY IS A VILLAIN AND NOBODY MAKES A SPEECH.** *The team is tired
and competent and one of them is kind to him.* **Cruelty two is one man
not walking within eight feet of a crate**, *and no line of dialogue may
explain it.* **`ch17` explains it. This chapter stages it.**

**5. ⚠ THE THRESHOLD IS NOT SPENT.** *`combat.md` §9.* **The tone climbs
and falls and she hears it and neither of them says anything** — *second
time, and the first was the monastery.*

**6. AND THE LAST SCENE MUST NOT BE WARM.** *§8 above.* **A boy is moved
from one container to another by people who have not told him anything**,
*and he cooperates instantly and completely,* **and the only thing that
has changed in his life is that somebody asked him a question and wrote
the answer down.**
