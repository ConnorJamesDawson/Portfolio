# Money — the ryō, stapled down

> **⚠ THIS FILE SUPERSEDES EVERY FIGURE WRITTEN BEFORE IT.** Chapters 1–10
> were drafted on a currency that was wrong twice over, and the retrofit is
> recorded in §5. **`threads.md` carries the corrected figures.**

---

## 1. WHAT WAS WRONG, AND IT WAS WRONG TWICE

**The ryō was built as an Edo-period gold coin** — a large unit, worth
months of subsistence, with a copper sub-unit called the **mon** beneath
it. *That is historically accurate for 1700s Japan and has nothing to do
with this setting.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **⚠ `mon` does not exist** | **Not canon. Invented, from the wrong century.** *Deleted entirely. There is no sub-unit.* |
| **⚠ The ryō is small** | Narutopedia: **1 ryō ≈ 10 yen.** *It behaves like a yen, not like a gold piece* |
| **Scale of the error** | **about three orders of magnitude**, and consistently in one direction |

**Everything below is derived to sit correctly against that table**, so
that a reader who knows what a C-rank costs can price this book's world
without being told.

## 4. ⚠ THE ONE FIGURE THAT IS NOT MINE TO SET

**What is a two-course drop worth?**

*It sets the family's income, the tin, what Mikage's prison output is
worth to Kiri, and what Tadayoshi's craft earns him for three books.* **It
is the most load-bearing number in the world-bible and it should be
ruled rather than derived.**

**PROPOSED, worked from the household:**

- **Eight people at the compound, on degraded output** (`spec.md`), poor
  but not starving. **Subsistence for eight ≈ 12,000 ryō a month.**
- **Nine pieces a day** on the page (C1), thirty days = **270 pieces a
  month.**
- **So the trade must gross 15,000–20,000 to keep them**, which puts a
  **two-course drop at 60–70 ryō** and a **one-course at 15–20** — *the
  cheap grade, loud, and priced like it.*

> **Which makes a day of his work worth about six labourer-days**, and a
> family of eight living on a craft only one and a half of them can still
> do. **That is the Ruri position, in one line, and it is why the tin
> matters.**

**⚠ NEEDS RULING.** *If the drop is worth more, the family were
comfortable and the decline is steeper. If less, they were already at the
edge before the warrant.*

## 5. THE RETROFIT — the conversion actually applied to chapters 1–10

> **1 ryō (as written) → 1,000 ryō. 1 mon (as written) → 10 ryō.**

**Every internal ratio is preserved**, which is why the rescale is safe:
the economy on the page was self-consistent, it was simply denominated in
the wrong unit.

denomination.

## 6. STANDING RULES

1. **Ryō is the only unit. There is no smaller one.** *If a thing is
   cheap, it is a small number of ryō.*
2. **Prices are written as numbers, not as fractions of a coin.** *A third
   of a ryō was vague; 330 is exact* — **and exactness is this book's
   register.** Both children count. Give them something to count.
3. **Nothing gets a price it does not need.** *The ledger discipline is
   unchanged: a figure goes on the page when somebody in the scene would
   have computed it.*
4. **And check every new figure against §2 before writing it.** *A number
   that cannot be placed against the mission table is a number that will
   be wrong for three books.*
