## 2. TADAYOSHI — the event, the write-up, the docket

### 2.1 The job that did not matter (scene 1, 12/2–12/3)

**It is not his job.** He is the carry. Two others run it — *he has been on
freight since the eleventh and nobody has revisited it* (`ch10.md` §3.3).

- **The ordinariness is the whole build.** A relay, a crate, a night's
  walk, and a fallback that has been used eleven times before.
- **⚠ AND IT GOES LONG.** Not badly at first — **long.** The pickup is two
  hours late; the fallback is watched; the second fallback needs the
  first one abandoned; **and by the time anybody is fighting, he has been
  awake for thirty-one hours and has done four separate things that each
  cost him something.**
- **`combat.md` §9 discipline 2 — PROPOSED and load-bearing here: it
  fires on load, not on stakes.** *So the reader must be able to see the
  load accumulating and must not be able to see a reason.* **Nothing
  about this job is important. That is the horror of it.**
- **He is winning when the scene breaks.** *That is mandatory.* **He is
  not cornered, not desperate, and not afraid** — **and the break comes
  mid-sentence, on an ordinary line, in the middle of a competent
  night.**

> **§9's standing rule holds: write up to it, break, resume after.**

### 2.2 ⚠ THE RECONSTRUCTION — and the object is an absence (scene 2)

**Bodies as evidence. Sequence inferred from wounds. The thing he cannot
determine.** *Ch5's method exactly, and he has done it before, which makes
it worse: he knows the procedure.*

**What the count comes to:** **the two he was carrying for are dead**, and
he cannot establish whether that happened before or after. ***That is the
question the chapter never answers and he never stops asking.***

> ## ⚠ AND THE OBJECT THAT STOPPED IT — PROPOSED, and it uses only what
> ## the book already owns
>
> **He finds a stand of crystal he did not lay on purpose.** *Open,
> drawing, still growing* — `combat.md` §3b constraint 4: **the state in
> which he has intent and no specification is the state most likely to
> make more.** Second instance; ch5's *two hundred together, like a thing
> growing*, is the first.
>
> **AND IT HAS NO NOTE.**
>
> **The tone is the oldest instrument in the book** — his mother taught
> it, he fixed it at eight, he found her alive by it at ten. *`act-1.md`
> §6 delivers it to act two as **an instrument, trusted completely.***
> **An open article does not ring.** *He put a hand on something of his
> own and the sound that should have been there was not.*
>
> **HE DOES NOT KNOW THAT IS WHAT STOPPED HIM.** He works it out
> afterwards, from where the stand is and where he was, **and enters it
> as an inference with a question mark on it**, which is the only honest
> way to write it and the first time his book has ever carried one.

- **And §9 is not violated.** *Nothing is written from inside.* **The
  object's operation is reconstructed, like everything else, and could be
  wrong.**
- **He takes a piece of it with him.** *Not sentiment* — **material**, and
  he does not have a word for what it is.

### 2.3 The write-up, and the man who writes it (scene 3, 12/4–12/6)

**⚠ HŌKI, and the continuity is free.** *`ch09.md`, 18/9, four minutes on
a bank:* ***"Then there's four minutes of you on a sheet in Kiri by the end
of the month, and it'll be right, because she was there."*** **The man who
told him he would end up on a sheet is the man who now has to write one.**

- **He is not accused of anything.** *Two of theirs are dead and the boy
  who came back is ten and gave a complete account.* **The account is the
  problem** — it is complete, it is accurate, and it has a hole in the
  middle of it that he flags himself.
- **Hōki asks two questions and stops.** *(Ch9: he asked one and did not
  ask the second. Here he asks the second one, and does not ask the
  third.)*
- **⚠ AND NOBODY SAYS THE WORD.** `act-1.md`: *the threshold happens in
  ch5 and again in ch11 and is never named.* **There is no word for it in
  this organisation.** They have a shape for *a boy who came back alone*
  and they use that.
- **The decision is made in a room he is not in**, and the reader gets it
  through its output: **a docket.**

### 2.4 The docket (scene 5, 12/8)

**A punishment posting, not a promotion** (`structure.md`, `timeline.md`
§2). **And this one is somebody's decision** — *freight was drift; this is
a choice* — **and he can tell the difference and cannot prove it.**

- **Cargo. A mountain temple, four days north-east of Ōgi.** Collect and
  go. **Nine days out.**
- **⚠ THE HAND ON THE DOCKET** (`ch10.md` §8): *he has seen it before.*
  **He does not place it and enters that he could not.**
- **The client is Enkai** — *and the book does not tell him and does not
  tell the reader in this scene either.*
- **He argues.** *Correctly, to the wrong person, at the wrong volume*
  (`ch08.md`), **and it makes it worse and he can see it making it worse
  and does it anyway.**
- **The last beat, and it is `combat.md` §10c:** ***half seals, a blade, a
  kit, and he has spent none of them.*** **He packs all of it. He has
  never used any of it in front of anyone who lived.**

### 2.5 The road, and the shape of the curve (scene 7, 12/9–12/13)

**`act-1.md` §4: by ch11 there are three towns he cannot use and he can
name them.** *The curve is felt as shrinking options.*

- **He routes around all three, and the detour costs him a day and a
  half**, and he writes the day and a half down. **The file is two lines
  and the cost of it is a day and a half.**
- **The hand in the cold** — *he cannot do a seal in a glove; his hands
  are cold from the tenth month to the fourth* (C1). **Month 12, going
  north-east, uphill.**
- **He practises the half seals on the road and fails twice in eleven**,
  which is better than one in three and he does not trust the sample.
- **⚠ AND HE IS BORED, INSULTED AND RESENTFUL** (`timeline.md` §2, and it
  is the required temperature): **which is exactly the state in which a
  boy walks out on a job.**

### 2.6 The Kadono-dera (scene 9, 12/16)

**Arrival, and the reader knows what this building is and he does not.**

- **It is a real monastery that occasionally sells a child** (`ch10.md`
  §2.2). **Eleven men, a vegetable ground, a bell.** *Tokuji is on the
  ground and is kind to him and gives him food.*
- **Enkai produces the cargo and the paper.** *Two crates. Correct
  weights. A clean receipt.* **The job is nine minutes of work.**
- **⚠ AND THE JOB IS FINE.** *He checks the manifest, signs, and is
  offered a bed.* **He nearly leaves.** **That must be on the page: the
  version where he walks out with the crates and act one ends without
  her is available until the last possible moment.**
- **What stops him is bookkeeping.** *The weights do not agree with the
  distance.* **Same operation as the provisioning book in ch6: a boat
  nobody may board still has to be fed.** *He is not being noble. He has
  found a discrepancy and he cannot leave a question standing.*
- **The scene ends on a door he was not shown.**

### 2.7 ⚠ THE FOUR MINUTES (scene 11 — the last scene of act one)

**He is awake for all of it. That is the ruling** (`falsified.md` #16).

- **Enkai explains.** *Reasonably, unhurriedly, and with the arithmetic
  visible* — **and it is the same arithmetic Tadayoshi does about
  everything**, which is the worst part and neither of them notices.
- **The kill is not a fight.** *Enkai is sixty and clerical.* **It takes
  no time at all and there is nothing impressive about it**, and the
  narration must refuse to make it satisfying.
- **⚠ AND THEN HE TAKES HER, AND HE CANNOT SAY WHY.**
  > **He has a reason for everything he has ever done.** *He can produce
  > the working on demand; it is the whole of him.* **And he stands in a
  > cellar doorway with a child in front of him and the reason does not
  > arrive.**
- **He does not decide to save her. He fails to leave**, and the
  distinction should be visible and never stated.
- **THE LAST PAGE, and it is the debt** (§1):
  > ***"I'll get you to a door. That's all this is."***
  >
  > **He says it plainly and she takes it on its stated terms**
  > (`team.md` §10.1), **and he writes it down that night, and the entry
  > is false, and he knows it is false, and it is the only way the four
  > minutes will go in the book.**
- **Close on the smallest true image:** *the receipt, signed, in his coat,
  for a job that no longer has a client.*

## 3. UZUKI — the dark, and the number that arrives

**Three scenes, and nothing happens in two of them, which is the point**
(`structure.md`, act one: *nothing happens to her for eight weeks — that

### 3.1 The date that passed (scene 4, month 12, the first fortnight)

**Ch10 ended on a date arriving and nothing happening on it.** *Now she has
to work out what that means, and she does, and she is wrong.*

- **Her finding: *nobody is coming, therefore I was not sold.*** **The
  correct reading is *the buyer is slow*, and she has no way to reach
  it**, and the reader has it and she does not.
- **⚠ THE COUNT CROSSES 1,500** — *the days until she is fifteen*
  (`ch06.md` §4). **A round number arrives in the dark and there is
  nobody to say it to**, and she says it out loud anyway, once, and the
  sound of her own voice in a root cellar is the worst thing in the
  chapter.
- **The ninety** — *stopped since 26/8* (C9). **She does not restart it.**
  She has nothing wrong with her and nobody to work on.
- **And she keeps the bag.** *That is the thing that has not stopped.*

### 3.2 The inspection (scene 8, 12/14)

**Somebody comes to look at her, and it is not the buyer.** *It is a man
checking that the goods are as described* — **and she has held a halter in
a yard and knows exactly what this is** (`ch10.md` §2.5).

- **He is not cruel and does not touch her.** *He looks at her teeth, her
  hands and her hair, in that order, and writes three things down.*
- **⚠ AND SHE HELPS HIM.** *Stands where she is put, turns when asked* —
  **because eight weeks of a bowl at a fixed amount have taught her that
  cooperation is how the bowl keeps coming**, and she notices herself
  doing it afterwards.
- **THE FINDING, and it is the darkest thing she has written:**
  > ***"I am better at this than I was."***
- **And the dye is long gone** (`ch10.md` §2.4 — *the rain took it*).
  **The hair is why the price is what it is** and she has worked that out
  and cannot do anything with it.

### 3.3 ⚠ *HELP.* (scene 10, 12/16)

**Her scene, and the word is hers.**

- **The building is loud in a way it has never been.** *She has been
  reading that building by sound for eight weeks* — bowls, the bell,
  eleven pairs of feet — **and she knows something is wrong before she
  knows anything.**
- **She does not know he is there. She does not know anybody is there.**
  **She has one word and no information**, and she says it into a door
  she has never once shouted at, **because the sound stopped and the
  stopping was worse than the noise.**
- ***"Help."*** **Once. Not loudly.** *The design has carried this word
  for four chapters and it must arrive small.*
- **AND THE PAGE ENDS THERE.** The door opens in his scene, not hers.

## 7. THE FIVE HARD PROBLEMS

**1. The event must not be exciting.** *Ch5 was a bloodbath with a wall
and stakes.* **This one is a long night on a job nobody cared about**, and
if the reader gets a thrill out of it, §9 discipline 1 is broken — *the
outcome is never net-positive.* **He wins the fight and loses two people
he was supposed to be carrying for, and the arithmetic is worse than
losing would have been.**

**2. The rescue must stay available to be refused, until the last page.**
*He nearly leaves twice: once with the crates signed for, once at the
door.* **If the reader ever feels the rescue is inevitable, the four
minutes stop meaning anything.**

**3. Her word must not be a cue.** *She does not know he is there.* **It is
not a call for rescue. It is a girl saying the only word she has into a
door because a noise stopped**, and the fact that it works is an accident
the book must not dress up.

**4. The debt must read as kindness and be a lie.** *Both, at once, on the
page.* **He says it to give her stated terms she can hold** — *and he
writes it to give himself a column he can fill.* **The reader should not
be sure which came first, and neither is he.**

**5. Kimimaro's relief.** *One scene, and it has to land the worst
inversion in the act:* **the only good news he has had in two years is
that somebody has finally decided what to do with him.**

## 9. REVISED ON THE PAGE
