## 2. ⚠ THE BOILERPLATE — and it is found by his method, not by luck

**`structure.md` §3.** *A posting written to be forgettable is **a document
that is entirely parts doing no work**, and he is constitutionally the one
person in this country who reads a job sheet the way he reads a kata.*

### The finding, and it is his eight-year-old operation run on paper

> ***Every docket I have ever had has one thing in it that had to be
> there.*** *A name, a weight, a date, a place — take it out and the job
> cannot be done.*
>
> ***This one has not got one. I could do the whole of this off nine words
> and the other forty are furniture.***
>
> ***And that is not a badly written docket. A badly written one leaves
> the thing out by accident and you have to go back and ask. I do not have
> to go back and ask. It is complete.***
>
> **⚠ *So somebody has written a complete instruction with nothing
> memorable in it, on purpose. And that is a skill and it took work.***

### ⚠ AND HE ADMIRES IT, WHICH IS THE THIRD TIME

| Chapter | Whose work | What he saw |
|---|---|---|
| **ch12** | **Enkai routing a child** | *he is doing what I do* |
| **ch14** | **Deguchi doing the speech** | *this is a man who knows a thing and is describing it accurately* |
| **ch15** | ⚠ **the boilerplate** | ***that is good work*** |

> **He recognises competence and cannot see what it is for, every single
> time, and the pattern is now three deep and nobody has said it.**

### And it hides behind the rebellion's own security culture

**Nobody uses a real name.** *Handlers and brokers go by trade names,
because a real name is a family somebody can visit.* **So a world where
every document is deliberately featureless is a world where a featureless
document is invisible** — *and he has been reading those documents for
fifteen months and has never once found this one strange.*

### ⚠ AND THE SECOND SHEET IS A NEAR MISS, ON PURPOSE

**`structure.md` §3 keeps *the second sheet is one of his own* for the
pipeline.** *It does not land here.*

- **He gets his own file out** — *he has been in a records phase since
  ch14: a page called ROADS FOR AN ANIMAL, a ledger totalled for the first
  time, a girl's arithmetic audited* — **and looks for it.**
- **⚠ AND LOOKS IN THE WRONG MONTHS.** *He goes at the contracts, because
  a thing that rings must be a thing that mattered, and the one he wants is
  in the freight.* **Three checked, none of them it, and he concludes he is
  wrong.**
- **The reader knows exactly which pile he did not open.**

## 3. ⚠ THE SAME PILE OF PAPER, TWO READINGS — the chapter's structural join

**He gets every docket he has ever had out onto the boards to hunt a
wording. She reads them.**

> **He is looking at the words. She is looking at the money.** *And in
> about an hour she finds the thing he has been wrong about for fifteen
> months.*

### Her finding, and it is correct

> ***You get paid for who the man was. You told me that and it's the only
> rule you've got.***
>
> ***And Kadoya was pleased.*** *3,600, and he was **delighted**, and he
> told somebody about it afterwards for the pleasure of telling.*
>
> ***A man who is pleased has paid less than he would have.***
>
> ⚠ ***So the fee is not for who the man was. The fee is for what the
> buyer has got, and you have never once looked at the buyer, and you have
> told me you did not know who they were nine times out of eleven.***

### ⚠ AND IT IS INAPPLICABLE, WHICH IS THE POINT

**He does not disagree.** *He goes at it for a day and cannot break it and
says so, flatly, which is the largest thing he has ever conceded to
anybody.*

> **And then:** ***"I don't set the fee. It's on the docket."***
>
> **She has just solved a problem he is not allowed to have.**

- **He is an employee.** *He has never negotiated a fee in his life. The
  number arrives on a piece of paper and he does the work.*
- **⚠ SO SHE HAS THE RIGHT ANSWER FOUR CHAPTERS BEFORE THERE IS A QUESTION
  FOR IT**, *and has to sit on it,* **and is the first person in the book
  to see the shape of what they would be if there were nobody above them.**
- *`ch14.md` §7 planted it:* **she found out five months later that he had
  taken it away to work on.** *Month 3 is where he takes it. Month 6 is
  where it comes back.*

## 4. KIMIMARO — three scenes, and he is being prepared

**⚠ THE READER MUST KNOW HE IS THE CARGO AND TADAYOSHI MUST NOT.** *That
is the whole of ch16 and it is loaded here.*

### 4.1 They start doing things to him (scene 2)

**The bowl went up in ch12 and he proved it off the rim with a piece of
his own finger.** *Now the rest of it arrives, and he registers every
item and can interpret none of them.*

- **He is washed.** *Twice in nine days, by somebody, with water that is
  warm, which has not happened since he was eleven.*
- **The room changes.** *A second blanket. The gap high on the west wall
  is boarded, and he measures the boards.*
- **⚠ AND HE IS LOOKED AT.** *Somebody stands in the doorway for about
  forty seconds and does not come in and does not say anything, twice, on
  two different days.*
- **His reading:** ***something is going to happen. I had that in the
  twelfth month off the bowl and I was right, and it has taken this
  long.*** **And he cannot get one step further, because every one of the
  four readings is about somebody upstairs** *(ch12).*

### 4.2 ⚠ THE EXAMINATION (scene 6) — and it is Uzuki's, and nobody says so

**A man with a book, on a stool, for eleven minutes.** *Teeth. Hands, both,
palm and back. The place on the back of the left hand where the sixth one
is coming.*

> **He cooperates perfectly and instantly and does not need to be told
> anything twice**, *because he has been doing what he is told since he was
> eleven and has never once been offered a second option.*
>
> **⚠ AND HE DOES NOT KNOW IT IS AN EXAMINATION.** *He thinks it is the
> thing that happens.* **Uzuki, on 15/12, knew exactly what it was inside
> the first minute and got it off the book** — *he opened the book on a
> page that had writing on it already* — **and Kimimaro has no book, no
> comparison, and no model, and gets nothing at all.**

- **⚠ AND HE MEASURES BACK.** *That is the scene's one piece of agency and
  it is worth everything:* **he times the man, counts his questions, and
  notes that the man wrote after the hands and not after the teeth**, *and
  files it, and it is the first data he has ever had about a person.*
- **The one thing he wants and does not get:** *the man does not ask him
  anything.* **He had an answer ready, once, in the second month** *(ch14 —
  **cold enough for you?**)*, **and he still has it.**

### 4.3 He is moved, in something (scene 9)

**⚠ AND THE INSTRUMENT GOES A THIRD TIME.** *Ch13 broke the bowls; ch14
broke the screaming; and this one takes the room itself* — *eleven paces
by seven, measured four times over, drain in the north corner* — **which
was the last thing he had that held still.**

- **He is put in something with a lid.** *He does not resist and does not
  ask.* **He measures it, because measuring is what he has:** *by feel, in
  the dark, in thumbs* — **and it is the smallest space he has ever been
  in and he gets its dimensions to a thumb inside two minutes.**
- **⚠ AND THE FIVE PIECES ARE INSIDE HIS SHIRT AND THE SIXTH IS ON THE
  BACK OF HIS HAND AT SEVENTY-AND-SOMETHING**, *and he cannot lay them out
  because there is no floor to lay them on.*
- **Last beat, flat:** *he works out that a thing with a lid is a thing
  that is going somewhere*, **and that is the most hopeful thought he has
  had in two years, and it is not hope, it is a deduction about
  containers.**

## 7. THE FIVE HARD PROBLEMS

**1. ⚠ THE DOCKET MUST NOT BE SINISTER ON THE PAGE.** *Every instinct will
want one wrong detail in it to tip the reader.* **Refuse.** *It has to be
a boring, competent, complete piece of freight paperwork* — **and the only
thing wrong with it is a negative: there is nothing in it you would
remember.** *If the reader spots it before he does, the chapter is about
the reader being clever.*

**2. He must not get the second sheet.** *§2.* **The near miss is the
whole design and it runs for three years** — *and he must fail for a
reason that is in character and creditable, which is that he looked in the
pile where the important things are.*

**3. Her finding must be delivered as arithmetic and refused as
procedure.** *He does not argue and he does not thank her.* **He concedes,
which he has never done, and then produces the one fact that makes it
useless** — *and neither of them says the thing that is standing in the
room, which is that it stops being useless the day nobody hands him a
docket.*

**4. ⚠ KIMIMARO'S EXAMINATION MUST NOT BE FRAMED.** *`method-delta.md`
§2i and §8.* **He does not know what it is, and the prose does not know
either.** *The reader has ch12's teeth-hands-hair scene and does the whole
of the work, and no sentence in ch15 may point at it.*

**5. And the chapter must move.** *`method-delta.md` §2l — ch14 was the
substance chapter and this is not one.* **Three threads converge on one
sheet of paper and the last line is an instruction**, *and if it reads as
another twenty-five days of two children on a road, the gear change has
failed.*

## 9. REVISED ON THE PAGE

**Ten scenes, 17,490 words, T×5 · U×2 · K×3, as planned.** *What follows
is what the prose changed, and it is `method-delta.md` §2j — the prose is
the canon, this file is not.*

### The docket grew a clause, and it paid for two things

**PAGE: *4,000, of which 1,000 on acceptance.*** *Not in §5. It is what
makes* **WHAT IS LEFT** *go up once* — the plan said it should and did not
say how — **and it produced a new column, ruled three inches in and headed
*AGAINST***, because a thousand he has not earned is not his: ***if I do
not deliver the case I give it back or I am a thief.*** *Both totals stay
on the page, 6,988 and 5,988, because both are true and he was not going
to pick.*

### The ringing is not localisable, and that is the point

**§5's scene 3 had a wording that rings.** *The prose refuses to let him
get down to a phrase at all.* **Four days of exclusions, all negative:**
covered a line at a time, read aloud, read against two other dockets in
four orders — **and, the new one, copied out in his own hand, where the
copy did not ring at all.**

> ***That is a fact about me and not about the sheet.***
>
> **He writes it down and it stays exactly that useful for three years.**

*This is better than a phrase, because a phrase is a lead and a lead can
be followed. What he has is recognition without retrieval, which is the
one thing his method has no procedure for* — **and it is why §7's near
miss is not a failure of effort.**

### ⚠ The four days cost her, and that is new

**The frame was promised for the sixth and came out on the tenth.** *She
asks twice —* ***is it the sixth***, *and then* ***are you stuck on
something*** *— and is told* ***no***, *and stops asking.*

> **It is the second one.** *(The first is the sparring page, 5/2.)*
>
> **And the prose keeps it in reported narration and out of her interior
> grammar**, because it is his scene — *the ch14 s8 device, not a POV
> break.*

### Her instrument is the question, and the plan did not have it

**§3 had her finding it in the paper. She does not.** *The sheets carry a
place, a person, a number and a date; **what was he like** is not on any
of them, and the only copy in the world is six feet away.*

> ***"What was he like?" — "No — what was he **like**." — "He laughed."***

**And the objection-killer is one question, correctly aimed, on the 17th:**
***"When he laughed. Had he paid you?" — "Four days before. On
delivery."*** *A man does not go and tell somebody about a fair price.*

*This is `team.md` §10 material and it arrived here on its own: her method
is acquisitive, not exclusionary, and it runs on asking.*

### ⚠ She pre-concedes the weakness, and that is why he concedes

**§7.3 asked for arithmetic delivered and procedure refused. The prose
adds the step that makes it land:** *she has one buyer out of eleven, she
knows one is not a line, and she says it **first**.*

> ***Because he is going to say it in about ninety seconds, and if he says
> it first then it is his objection and I am answering it.***
>
> **She had never in her life planned an argument.**

*And what he concedes to is not the finding. It is being met at his own
standard by somebody who got there without being taught.*

### The briefing gained a second unqueried line, and it is the better one

**§5 had *no weighing required at transfer*, and it is on the page and it
is queried in his own head and answered correctly** — *flat fee, no rate,
nothing to weigh against, said on the front in advance.* **The answer is
right. That is the whole trick.**

**PAGE, and new:** ***"What's in it." — "Dye. Says on the sheet."***

> **It does not say on the sheet.** *He has it word for word and knows it
> does not, and does not say so, and never afterwards found a version
> where he had a reason.* **It took about a second and a half.**

*Two failures, two different kinds: one he reasoned through and got right,
and one he simply let past. The second is the one that will not forgive
him.*

### And the near-miss sheet is dyestuff, which makes the cover a system

**§2 said only that it is one of his own.** *The prose makes it dye,
Ōtoma to a yard south of Kanda, 12/8 of the year before last, aged ten,
forty-one words* — **so the same operation has run the same cover through
the same town before**, and the echo is not a coincidence for the reader
to spot, it is a pattern for the reader to hold. *And it does not indict
him further, because dye is ordinary freight and that is exactly why it
was chosen.*

### Kimimaro: three changes, and two of them are the established method

1. **He cannot reach the gap** *(`ch13` s8: **I cannot reach it and I could
   if I stood on something and there is nothing**)*, **so he does not
   measure the boards — he throws pieces of the floor at them and
   listens**, which is how he did the ceiling. *Two days, about sixty
   goes: board is a hollow and stone is a nothing.* **Three boards, not
   four**, and they stop where the stone starts.
2. **⚠ THE MAN DOES NOT COME BACK.** *§4.2 gave him the second reading as
   a hope. The prose takes it, and takes it before the box:*
   > ***I decided what somebody else was going to do, and I have never
   > done that before, and I am not going to do it again.***
   > **Kept for the rest of his life.**
3. **⚠ AND THE BOX COSTS HIM THE FLOOR, not the room.** *§4.3 had the room
   as the instrument. The prose finds the sharper loss:* **the five pieces
   are read by being laid in a row with the bases lined up, and there is
   nowhere to lay them**, and chest and knee-fold both fail because the
   bases carry the reading.
   > ***So I do not need to lay them out. I want to lay them out.***
   >
   > **⚠ *I have not had a thing I wanted that was not a thing I needed.***
   > *It sits in him about a minute and goes, and he does not go looking
   > for it again for two years.*

*The room's dimensions are corrected to the prose throughout* — **four
thumbs by eleven; the door one and a half paces from the east corner,
opening inward, hinge away from the drain; the floor down a thumb toward
the north corner** *(`ch13` s8)* — **and §2j is the reason: I had the
plan's numbers in hand and the prose's were different.**
