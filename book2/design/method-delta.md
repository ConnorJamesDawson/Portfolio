# Method — what book two adds

**`design/method.md` carries in full, unchanged.** All 57 prompt lines:
scene-not-summary, the ban on montage transitions, one POV per scene with
its own interior grammar, equipment as load path, *say the large thing
through equipment*, cost every emotional statement physically, number
things, withhold one clause, prefer the object to the statement, pay debts
quietly, close on the smallest true image.

**Chapters 14k–20k, 8–11 scenes. Lines wrap at 78 characters. Summary where
a scene belongs is the failure mode that matters most.**

This file is the delta — what this book adds on top, and why each addition
exists.

---

## 1. THE ANTI-WIDENING RULE

> **No rule may be falsified without a replacement constraint named in the
> same entry.**

Book one killed twelve rules and widened twelve times. This is the fix, and
it lives in `falsified.md` as two columns.

**At the scene level it reads:** if a chapter would be easier because the
protagonist turned out to be able to do something new, **that chapter is
wrong.**

## 2. THE AGE ANCHOR

> **The age ledger is an anchor, not an output.** If a scene needs more time
> than `timeline.md` has, the scene is wrong.

Book one drifted a boy into a man across seventy chapters, one reasonable
addition at a time. **Ten at the warrant, thirteen at the door.**

## 3. THE THRESHOLD IS NEVER WRITTEN FROM INSIDE

> Write up to it. **Break.** Resume after, and let the **reconstruction** be
> the scene — the bodies read as evidence, the sequence inferred from
> wounds, the thing he cannot determine.

A man who cannot account for what he did is more frightening than one who
narrates it, and the reader never gets handed the power fantasy. **If it is
ever broken, that is an event.**

## 4. THE LIVED-IN RULE

> **Every canon figure gets a first contact in which nothing happens.**

Waiting on a payment. Sheltering out of rain. Two people at a counter. No
recognition, no significance, no music.

Book one's diagnosis was that its protagonist felt *slotted into the gaps
between canon events*. The fix is not to write around canon. It is to
**stop treating canon as events and start treating it as population.**

**Texture rules, so it does not become cameo tourism:**

1. **Nothing happens.** If a first contact advances a plot, it is not a
   first contact — it is a scene wearing a hat.
2. **He does not know who they are**, and the book does not tell the reader
   either. No aside, no narrator's wink.
3. **One per stretch.** Rationed, or the world stops being large.
4. **The payment is structural, not sentimental.** Tsunade is a door.
   Zabuza is a prior. Neither is a callback.

## 5. HE ACTS INSTEAD OF SAYING

Book one's engine was *can this person say a true thing out loud, and what
does it cost.* Keep the engine, reverse the polarity:

> **He initiates constantly and it is all doing — so the true sentence never
> gets said, not because he cannot say it, but because he keeps making it
> unnecessary by fixing the problem first.**

**So the book must repeatedly hand him problems that cannot be got by going
and getting them.** You cannot make people want to survive. You cannot
teach what somebody else should become. **A disease cannot be fabricated
around.**

**And his failures are things he DID.** Sazare's failures were failures to
move. For a protagonist who changes events, that is the correct shape: a
man who changes events is a man whose mistakes change events.

## 6. CRUELTY IS THE CARRIER

Book one: *do not walk up to him with a feeling — walk up with a task that
contains the feeling.* Same technology, different vehicle.

> **Kindness has to be aimed. Contempt needs nothing.**

When he has something enormous and kind to say, he finds a **true unkind
thing that does the same job** and says that instead. It is not a lie — that
is why he can say it. And it is indistinguishable from what he actually
thinks.

## 7. THE SIGNATURE OPERATION IS THE PROSE'S SHAPE

*Learn it whole. Find the part doing no work. Remove it.*

Kata, tone, job sheets, people. **It should be visible in how the sentences
move**, not just in what he does — the same instinct that finds the dead
part of a form finds the dead clause in a paragraph.

## 8. THE UNSTATED FACT

**Some facts are conveyed only by their effects, and the narration never
states them.**

The standing case is Uzuki's beauty: **no POV describes it, ever** — she is
ten, and then thirteen, and a book that looks at her is a book that cannot
be read. It arrives through **how rooms change, how prices change, how long
a man takes to answer**, and through Tadayoshi filing the effect as a
security finding without ever locating the cause.

**The general rule this instantiates:** where a fact would be cheapened or
poisoned by narration, give the reader the *behaviour it causes* and let
them assemble it. **The prose earns more by refusing to say it**, and this
project's whole method — *prefer the object to the statement* — already
says so.

## 9. THE SERIES MUST BE MAINTAINED

Four running quantities (`threads.md`): the mother's tone, Kimimaro's
decline, the hunter curve, the trafficking count. **A series that stops
being maintained is a series the reader stops believing.** If one has not
been touched in six chapters, the book has quietly dropped an instrument.

## 10. NO SILENT CAPS

If a chapter skips something the ledgers say should have happened —
a hunter contact, a kit expiry, a reading — **say so on the page.** A
skipped cost reads as no cost.
