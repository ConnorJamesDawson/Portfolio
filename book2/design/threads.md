# Threads — authoritative per-chapter state

**Status: skeleton. No prose written.**

This file is the state of record once writing begins. Book one's discipline:
after every chapter, the fixed facts of that chapter are entered here, and
**this file wins** over memory, over the spec, and over any earlier draft.

---

## How an entry is made

Per chapter, in this order:

1. **Date and clock.** Story date, elapsed time since the last chapter, and
   the ages of everyone on the page — checked against `timeline.md`, which
   is an anchor and cannot be moved by the entry.
2. **Fixed facts.** Anything a later chapter is now obliged to honour:
   names, numbers, objects, injuries, promises, undertakings, who was told
   what.
3. **The kit.** What he is carrying, what was made, what was spent, what
   expired. `combat.md` §3 — every article has a date on it, and this file
   is where the dates live.
4. **The ledgers he keeps.** The hunter file, the tone readings off his
   mother's work, Uzuki's readings on Kimimaro, the blade's length. Each is
   a **series**, and a series that stops being maintained is a series the
   reader stops believing.
5. **Open loops.** What was raised and not closed.
6. **Rules killed.** Anything that contradicts a design file goes to
   `falsified.md` **with its replacement constraint**, in the same pass.

### ⚠ THE TONE SERIES IS CORRUPTED FROM YEAR 2, AND HE IS NOT TOLD

**Mikage dies in year 2.** The series does not stop. It is corrupted twice
over and neither corruption announces itself:

1. **Lag.** Prison crystal circulates for years, so her hand keeps arriving
   after she is gone.
2. **Forgery.** Kongō lays her courses on purpose, to keep a boy hunting a
   living mother.

**So from year 2 the entry in this file is not a reading. It is a
transcription of somebody else's handwriting**, and the state file must
record the true value and the value Tadayoshi holds **as separate columns**,
because they diverge and the prose depends on the gap.

**He catches it as an anomaly, not as news:** the chart **stops flattening**
— it plateaus — and a decline that stops declining is a decline that is no
longer being generated by a dying person.

- **ONE ROOM WAS SEARCHED: Meno's.** Bed pulled out a foot and pushed back
  eight inches; rug kicked up at the corner. **Shelf empty, box gone.**
- **He walked the path at their pace: four minutes ten.** House eight,
  boat six. **Eighteen. Call it twenty.**
  > ***"About twenty minutes, and there was no shouting."*** — **his
  > mother's exact sentence about Kongō, arrived at independently.**
  > **And he cannot tell whether he measured a procedure or measured
  > her**, and there is no way to find out, and *"the person who could
  > tell me is on a boat."* **First time his own instrument fails him.**
- **He shut all thirty-one doors**, finishing at about three in the
  morning. **Not Take's.**

**THE LEDGER FAILED — the third, night, Take's house**

- **Third from the end on the sixth**, chosen off one remark of Meno's two
  years old: *"They took the shutters off it for the sheds and they never
  took the bed, because it wouldn't go through the door."* **The roof is
  sound. The sixth was shut before the fifth and shut properly.**
- **He could not write.** Four minutes with the pencil on the page.
  > *Because a finding is for somebody… I have been writing a report for
  > three weeks. And the person a report is for is not there.*
- **What he wrote instead:** ***"The bed is six foot four and the door is
  five foot ten."*** *"It is the only measurement I have taken today that
  I took with my hands."*
- **In the small hours:** *Somebody will have to go down and shut the
  doors.*

**THE FIRE — the fifth, morning**

- **Four feet across, in the middle of the yard flags, away from the
  buildings.** Built properly, given air, burned to a depth. A day and a
  half of rain on it.
- **Two boards, ten and a quarter by seven and a half** — the register's,
  which had come away from the block at the top and was never mended.
- **Three corners of paper. One had a ruled line and a third of the next
  and part of a character.** A spot of rain took some of the ink out
  sideways while it lay in his hand. **He kept it.**
- **THE CRYSTAL ALL SURVIVED.** Fourteen pieces, undamaged, laid out in a
  row on a wet flag: the comb-piece, the mount with three empty settings,
  the wandering first attempt, eleven more.
  > **"They have burned the only thing on this hill that could not be
  > replaced and left everything that could be bought."** *And it was not
  > malice — somebody had an instruction about a household's papers and
  > followed it.*
- **HE TOOK ONE: Kongō's drop**, note a third of the way up, the break
  clean. *"They are answers. All fourteen of them are answers to questions
  I have already asked. This one is not."*
  > *"I am about to do a thing I cannot cost, for a reason I cannot write
  > down… It is simply the one I am taking."*
- **He buried the other thirteen** in the ash, six inches down, and raked
  the burn back to looking like a burn. **It took twenty minutes.**
- **It is inside his shirt with the ledger and the corner of paper.** He
  checks it through his coat with a flat hand — *"about four hundred times
  in the following year without once noticing he was doing it."*
- **Last line of the act's first movement:** he opens Take's door and does
  not shut it after him, **because there is nobody left on the hill to
  shut it against.**

**UZUKI — days two to four**

- **Three men sitting on the bank forty yards into the wood.** No cart, no
  packs, no tools, doing nothing. One looked at her and went on looking.
  **The road was dry, so the condition was not met, and she waited anyway**
  — two hours forty — having argued it out in four steps.
- **She practised the third form eleven times while waiting** and was
  rubbish at it, *"aware, in a distant and unwelcome way, that she was
  practising in order to have somewhere to put her hands."*
- **Ine**, slate cart, and her son **Tarō**. Went through in forty minutes.
  One of the men said *"Morning."* **Nothing happened.**
  > **Ine:** *"You never find out… You do the careful thing and nothing
  > happens and you spend the rest of the day feeling daft… So you do the
  > careful thing. And you keep doing it. And you never get to know, and
  > that's the price, and it's not a big one."*
- **Ine's method, written on the back of the mill's chit:** ***"Water
  queue, 6, ask 3, take the one that comes twice."*** **It worked in nine
  seconds.**
- **KAYANO.** The parcels delivered first. **Anzai, the medic. Bottom of
  Kanmuri lane, green door that was blue, a step down you go over.**
- **He has not been home since Tuesday. He is at the tannery — a pit went
  in.** His girl has been up and down twice for things.
- **She did not go to the tannery**, and reasoned it: *"'Not now' is a
  thing you can't argue with and it's a thing you can't come back from…
  I get one go at it."*
- **She slept in the doorway** with the cart on its side across the front
  of it and the bokken along her right side. **The hundred is unspent.**

**KIMIMARO — the twelve days after the inspection**

- **Warm food on the ninth day. More in the bowl.** He ate at the same
  speed: *"if you eat more the same way you ate less, nobody has to think
  about you."*
- **A blanket on the fourth day of warm — folded.** *"Folding is a thing
  you do to a thing that is going to be somewhere."*
- **Washed on the sixth day** by two who were not the keeper. *"The left
  one was worse."* **They did not speak to him.** He folded the cloth
  because it had come in folded.
- **His conclusion: they are going to use me.** *"If I am used, then there
  is a thing I can be good at. And if I am good at it they keep using
  me."*
- **HE HAS STARTED TRAINING FOR THE MEASUREMENT.** Four a day in the long
  stretch between bowl and bucket, on the right forearm. **Forty-one to
  fifty-two in six days.** He times the ache so it is gone before anybody
  comes in.
- **He does not have the word for what he wants the man with the book to
  be.** *"For the first time in about two years, looking forward to
  something."*
