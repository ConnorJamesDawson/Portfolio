# Threads — authoritative per-chapter state

**Status: skeleton. No prose written.**

This file is the state of record once writing begins. Book one's discipline:
after every chapter, the fixed facts of that chapter are entered here, and
**this file wins** over memory, over the spec, and over any earlier draft.

---

## How an entry is made

Per chapter, in this order:

1. **Date and clock.** Story date, elapsed time since the last chapter, and
   the ages of everyone on the page — checked against `timeline.md`, which
   is an anchor and cannot be moved by the entry.
2. **Fixed facts.** Anything a later chapter is now obliged to honour:
   names, numbers, objects, injuries, promises, undertakings, who was told
   what.
3. **The kit.** What he is carrying, what was made, what was spent, what
   expired. `combat.md` §3 — every article has a date on it, and this file
   is where the dates live.
4. **The ledgers he keeps.** The hunter file, the tone readings off his
   mother's work, Uzuki's readings on Kimimaro, the blade's length. Each is
   a **series**, and a series that stops being maintained is a series the
   reader stops believing.
5. **Open loops.** What was raised and not closed.
6. **Rules killed.** Anything that contradicts a design file goes to
   `falsified.md` **with its replacement constraint**, in the same pass.

## The four series to maintain from chapter one

These are the book's instrument panel. Each is a number or a reading that
changes across the book, and three of the four are unobtainable or
unreadable to somebody.

| Series | Kept by | Reads | Cannot be read by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The mother's tone** | Tadayoshi | **flattening — then lying.** See below. | Uzuki |
| **Kimimaro's decline** | Uzuki | **flattening** — weekly, in a book | Tadayoshi |
| **The hunter curve** | Tadayoshi | **rising** — and it yields a date at which it exceeds him | — |
| **The trafficking count** | Tadayoshi | **unobtainable, by design** — the boilerplate exists so the carrier cannot know | everyone |

> **Two dying people in one house. He can read his and not hers; she can
> read hers and not his.** Two flattening series, facing pages, in a book
> about withholding.

### ⚠ THE TONE SERIES IS CORRUPTED FROM YEAR 2, AND HE IS NOT TOLD

**Mikage dies in year 2.** The series does not stop. It is corrupted twice
over and neither corruption announces itself:

1. **Lag.** Prison crystal circulates for years, so her hand keeps arriving
   after she is gone.
2. **Forgery.** Kongō lays her courses on purpose, to keep a boy hunting a
   living mother.

**So from year 2 the entry in this file is not a reading. It is a
transcription of somebody else's handwriting**, and the state file must
record the true value and the value Tadayoshi holds **as separate columns**,
because they diverge and the prose depends on the gap.

**He catches it as an anomaly, not as news:** the chart **stops flattening**
— it plateaus — and a decline that stops declining is a decline that is no
longer being generated by a dying person.

## C — per-chapter state

### C1 — Chapter 1, "Two Courses". Month 1 to the last week of month 2.
### 10 scenes, 16,900 words. T×5 (57%) · U×4 (36%) · K×1 (8%).

**THE CRAFT, as now established on the page**

- A drop is laid in **courses**. **One course is one note** and shatters.
  **Two courses is the family grade** and is what Kanaya buys.
- **The note lives where the courses end** — a thinness under the thumb at
  the fat end of a two-course drop. **Always. Without exception.**
- **The grain is a fault.** *"It had always been a fault. He had worked
  that out when he was eight and it had taken him about six weeks, and the
  six weeks had mostly been spent not believing it."*
- **The frame is copper, shaped like an open hand, and the clan calls it
  the lattice.** Meno can recite eleven lines about it that nobody can
  explain.
- **Seals: four, held about a second and a quarter**, timed against the
  eave drip, *"which came at eleven to the minute in steady rain."*
- **Grit is free** — off the third bar down at low water, sieved twice,
  dried on the stove. **The only reason the Ruri are at Shioiri.**
- **You cannot do a seal in a glove.** His hands are cold from the tenth
  month to the fourth.

**TADAYOSHI — fixed**

- **Ten. Lays two courses on purpose, nine pieces a day.** He can lay six.
- **He works from a sample of his mother**, kept on top of the tin, put
  there every morning. Hers goes on the left.
- **He can forge her hand and does, daily** — and puts in a deliberate
  tiredness: *"She pushes with the heel of the hand at the start and gets
  tired of pushing. I do not get tired. So I have to."*
- **The tic: thumb up and back along the taper.** He got it from her and
  learned that in scene 1, watching her do it to his own forgery.
- **The instruction, verbatim, eighth month two years ago:** *"Nobody sees
  this. Not your father. Not Meno. Nobody."* Reason given, entire:
  *"Because of my uncle."*
- **He has started a ledger** (sc6) — a discarded Kanaya day-book, and
  **he ruled a fourth column down it, narrow, because that is how it is
  done.** First entry: *"Nineteen went. Nobody says the names."*
- **Academy: four seals, not five.** *"Dog is a bracing seal… It is part
  of the hand."* Demonstrated in front of 25 people. **Aoyagi is putting a
  note on his record**, and a note moved him last year.

**THE COMPOUND — fixed**

- **Six courses. Eight people. Thirty-one roofs.** Above the fourth course
  nothing is lived in; the fifth dropped an eave two winters ago and
  nobody said a word on the way down. **About nine hundred tiles.**
- **The month's sale: four dozen and one drops, grade two and a low three,
  fourteen and a half.** Seventeen in the eighth month, because of a
  wedding at Sunaba. **Money sits out one evening so everybody has looked
  at it.**
- **Kanaya's man at Ōura is called Kenji** and made a face at the eleventh
  drop of the fourth dozen. Iwao did the impression.
- **KOISHI'S QUESTION, and nobody could answer it:** *"Who wears them?"*
  Tadaharu: *"I take them to a counter in Ōura, and a man behind the
  counter gives me money, and what he does with them after that is his
  business, and if I started asking he'd start wondering why I wanted to
  know."*
- **Hisui's ninth attempt.** A house in Ōura, two rooms and a yard, seen
  twice. *"This is where thirty-two people were from."* **Ended by Iwao
  making everybody laugh. Hisui did not laugh.**
- **Tadaharu, spending his one outsider's opinion a year:** *"You don't
  stay small because it's safe… now you've got a family that makes a worse
  piece than it can, sells it for less than it's worth, to a man who makes
  a face at it, and calls that surviving."*
- **Mikage's answer:** *"People who are worth something get collected."*
- **Iwao puts a hand on the back of his neck about four times a day.**
  Tadayoshi has never liked it and has never said so.

**THE REGISTER — fixed**

- **Four ruled columns: name, born, died, and one more.** The fourth is
  **narrower than the others**, because whoever ruled the page thought it
  would need less room.
- **Three words go in it: *died here*, *taken*, and *went*.**
- **Meno kept it from thirty-one, because that is when the woman before her
  stopped.** She skipped **Ruri Manzō** and put her hand flat on the page.
- **The rule:** *"We don't say the names."*
- **THE COUNT: nineteen went. Thirteen died or were taken.** Counted three
  times. **Hisui is the last one making an argument nineteen people won.**
- **The box under the shelf.** In it: a mount with three empty settings; a
  comb-piece; a wandering first attempt; **and a plain drop with the note a
  third of the way up.** *"A hand that goes wrong does not go wrong
  tidily."* **Meno: "Kongō's."** Then: *"Put it back, Tadayoshi."*

**UZUKI — fixed**

- **Ten. Nakadō, Land of Hot Water. Nozaki Kiyoshi and Sae.**
- **The dye is done at a bowl, about every two months**, by her father,
  badly. Reason given: *"Because red's a rich colour and we're not rich
  people."* Her answer: **"Hair is hair."**
- **Her grandfather did the same thing — the hand on the head, the look at
  the door** — and gave her less than a wrong reason.
- **Nine forms, numbered to fourteen. No four, six, ten, eleven, thirteen.**
  Kiyoshi asked twice; the second time his father *"looked at the door for
  a bit and said something about how it wasn't a good time for it."*
- **The third form's eighth count.** *"You put it in late… You're paying
  before you've decided."* **She got it on the eighteenth try**, and it
  felt like less than the wrong way.
- **She has about a thimble of chakra** and will have a cup by twenty.
- **The slate went from eleven jobs to four.** Iwasa's boy has the mill.
  Sae: *"Nobody's taken it off him… They just stopped putting them in his
  hand, and that's how it's done."*
- **THE HUNDRED.** She went to Ōgi unsent, collected three things, and took
  **a hundred on account against a debt of two hundred and forty owed since
  the eighth of the previous month.** Sae counted it, said nothing, and put
  it **on the shelf above the stove, at the back, behind the tin.**
  **Uzuki thinks it was put high to keep it safe.**
- **Tazu's finding:** deep part of the left, *"it's got a shape to it,"*
  present since **the harvest before last**. He can name six things and
  this is not one of them.
- **A medic at Kayano. Three days if the road's dry.** The going-to-them
  figure is **the tin and a bit.**
- **AND THE LOOSE END HE LEFT ON HIS WAY OUT:** *"Ask about the teaching…
  a medic worth the name will sit somebody down for an afternoon and give
  them the bones of it… You'd have it in the house."*
- **Her sum, in the dark:** *"Three days there. Three days back. And an
  afternoon in the middle. That's seven days. And seven days is not a sum.
  Seven days is a walk."*

**THE BOY IN THE ROOM — fixed**

- **Twelve. Unnamed on the page. No date, no place-name in his scenes.**
- **The door opens three times on an ordinary day.** Water and bowl;
  bucket; bowl going back.
- **His count is broken.** He marked the wall past forty, was sick for an
  unknown length of time, and rubbed it off. **He now only counts forward
  from things.**
- **He was inspected: two hundred and six days since the last one.** The
  keeper limps on the left and has never been unkind and has never been
  anything else.
- **He is measured with a marked strip of wood laid against a bone he puts
  out of his right forearm.** *"Whatever the wood said was the thing he
  was."* **He has never been told what the number means.**
- **He worked out, unaided, that *being difficult made the visit longer*,
  and stopped flinching**, and the visits got shorter.
- **There was a door along the passage with a nine-count gap after his.**
  It stopped. He started counting the days since and then stopped that too.
- **Verdict: *"He's fine."*** He was relieved.

**THE CLOSE**

- **Akagane made him a frame.** Nine weeks. Tapered fingers, a fillet at
  each root, **the palm dished four in a hundred**, and a rolled edge at
  the wrist because he had watched the boy rub the web of his thumb since
  he was seven.
- **Akagane has independently discovered why Ruri work has grain in it:**
  *"It's the fingers running ahead."* He does not know what a course is
  and never will.
- **He is making one for Mikage next.** A quarter-inch out of the palm.
- **THE WITHHELD SENTENCE.** Tadayoshi got as far as the name. What he said
  instead: **"Akagane, it's the best thing anybody in this family has made
  in my lifetime."** *"He had not lied. He had looked for the truest
  available sentence that was not the sentence."*
- **Cost of the gift: four days**, relearning the distance from his
  under-hand to the grit. Entered in the narrow column.
- **The old frame is in the bench drawer, at the back, under the rag.**
