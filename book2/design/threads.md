# Threads — authoritative per-chapter state

**Status: skeleton. No prose written.**

This file is the state of record once writing begins. Book one's discipline:
after every chapter, the fixed facts of that chapter are entered here, and
**this file wins** over memory, over the spec, and over any earlier draft.

> ## PROVENANCE — swept after ch10, and this file is exempt.
>
> **Everything in the C entries is PAGE** (`spec.md` §0). It is not
> design and not inference: it is a transcription of what is on a page
> the author has read and accepted, much of it quoted verbatim. **There
> is nothing here to mark**, and a sweep that marked it would be marking
> the book itself.
>
> **The one place to watch is *open loops*** — a loop is a question, and
> a question that gets answered in a later entry without the author
> ruling on it is an inference entering through the back door. **If an
> entry closes a loop the author never closed, that entry is PROPOSED
> and says so.**

## How an entry is made

Per chapter, in this order:

1. **Date and clock.** Story date, elapsed time since the last chapter, and
   the ages of everyone on the page — checked against `timeline.md`, which
   is an anchor and cannot be moved by the entry.
2. **Fixed facts.** Anything a later chapter is now obliged to honour:
   names, numbers, objects, injuries, promises, undertakings, who was told
   what.
3. **The kit.** What he is carrying, what was made, what was spent, what
   expired. `combat.md` §3 — every article has a date on it, and this file
   is where the dates live.
4. **The ledgers he keeps.** The hunter file, the tone readings off his
   mother's work, Uzuki's readings on Kimimaro, the blade's length. Each is
   a **series**, and a series that stops being maintained is a series the
   reader stops believing.
5. **Open loops.** What was raised and not closed.
6. **Rules killed.** Anything that contradicts a design file goes to
   `falsified.md` **with its replacement constraint**, in the same pass.

### ⚠ THE TONE SERIES IS CORRUPTED FROM YEAR 2, AND HE IS NOT TOLD

**Mikage dies in year 2.** The series does not stop. It is corrupted twice
over and neither corruption announces itself:

1. **Lag.** Prison crystal circulates for years, so her hand keeps arriving
   after she is gone.
2. **Forgery.** Kongō lays her courses on purpose, to keep a boy hunting a
   living mother.

**So from year 2 the entry in this file is not a reading. It is a
transcription of somebody else's handwriting**, and the state file must
record the true value and the value Tadayoshi holds **as separate columns**,
because they diverge and the prose depends on the gap.

**He catches it as an anomaly, not as news:** the chart **stops flattening**
— it plateaus — and a decline that stops declining is a decline that is no
longer being generated by a dying person.

**HANASE — 7/2 and 7/3. FIRST HUNTER CONTACT, and no enemy on the page.**

**UZUKI — THE WEDDING, 6/29**

- **Nine people, counting the priest and counting her.** Iwasa and his

**THE BRICK HOUSE — 7/2**

- **THE FLOUR BAG — and there is no stone by the step.** Four candidates

**THE GOOD WEEKS — 7/3 to 7/19, and they are genuinely good**

- **Meat twice in a fortnight.** The roof. Oil on a shelf with no line

**⚠ THE SIXTEENTH — SIX SECONDS, AND SHE TAKES HIM OFF THE LIST**

- **She came in from the water with the bucket, sleeves up, hair wet at
  the front. He looked up the way anybody looks up when a door goes.**
  **And then he did not stop.**
  > **Six.** *Tazu is four and Tazu is the worst there is.*
- **Four seconds later: *"Is that the good bucket or the one that
  leaks,"* and he got up and went out to the shed. That was the whole of

- **THAT NIGHT SHE PUT IT ON TRIAL PROPERLY AND ACQUITTED HIM.**
  - **For:** the door is behind her and the line off that table goes
    through where she stood; he had been at a notched stick for an hour;
    **he looked away before she did**; the bucket.
  - **Against:** eleven weeks; a fortieth given straight in his own yard;
    six for the mule when he could have said eight; the top of the door

  > **It's a habit. It isn't evidence.** *"And a girl who puts her
  > mother's husband on that list is a girl who has decided to be unhappy

  > **She had had that list since she was seven. She had never once, in

**⚠ THE TWENTIETH — THROUGH A BRICK WALL**

- **A completely ordinary day**, gone over four hundred times afterwards

  clock stopping: not the sound, the absence of the other half of it."*

- **THE CHAIR WENT OVER. And somebody stood it back up.** The grate, the
  three-inch scrape, the knock of the legs coming down.
  > **You don't pick a chair up in the middle of an argument. You pick a

  >
  > *(She carried that one detail for thirty years and could produce it

  wasn't going, and I did the other eighty-four anyway."***

- **⚠ AND SHE HAS THE INSTRUMENT.** *Hand on. What's different. Where does

  >
  > *"She has never once in my life moved her hands away from me."*
  >
  > ***Then I know.***

     >
     > **And the other one I could have used, she used up before six

**THE CARRYING WAS AN EXAM — 8/2, a hay barn above Ōtoma**

- **The summons came as a docket with the parcel line missing:** *2nd of
  the 8th. Ōtoma. The barn on the hill, any time after dark.* ***No
  parcel.***
  > *"He has told me in two words rather than leave me working it out for
  > three days. That is the third time he has done that, and I have not
  > once seen him do it."*
- **Kajiya, on the good end of a bale, able to see both doorways and
  framed in neither.** *"He has not got worse and he has not got better
  and he has not got any older."*
- **FOUR JOBS: Fukae, Hanase, and two in the seventh month — Nunobiki
  and back. He opened none of them.** Asked how near he came, he gave the
  right answer: **he learned the knot on a milestone outside Sakabe on
  the second of the sixth month, in about four minutes.**
- ***"You've been on trial since the first of the sixth month. That's
  what carrying is. It isn't a job, it's an exam, and everybody starts
  on it and most of them are still on it a year later and don't know."***
  - ***"You thought it was a job." — "Yes." — "Good. That's the point of
    it."***
  - **The distinction, and it is given as a warning:** *"Somebody was
    checking. That's not the same as watching and don't confuse them,
    it'll get you killed. Nobody stood behind you on a road for nine
    days. Somebody at the far end had a look at a knot."*
- **⚠ ONE OF THE FOUR HAD NOTHING IN IT.** Rags, about the weight of a
  book, tied and waxed and sealed like the rest. **Hanase.** *A man took
  it off him and said thank you and put it under a bench and it is still

  - **Nine days of walking, a sixteenth for a floor, a coat on a nail,
    and the whole of the second book — for rags.**
  - **And it was not a bad feeling**, because he got there in eleven
    seconds:
    > *"The parcel was not the product… Somebody wanted to know what a
    > boy of ten does over four days when there is nobody behind him, and
    > the only way to find that out is to send him four days with
    > something, and the something has to be real enough that he does not
    > know it is not."*
    >
    > **Nothing I did was wasted. It was all of it the point.**
    >
    > *(And the coat went on the same road either way, because the man at
    > two in the morning was not there about a parcel.)*

**THE HEAT SINK, SAID OUT LOUD — and he is pleased**

- **Kajiya says the worst thing at the front, as he did in the yard**, and
  Tadayoshi notices that he has now done it twice and that both times it
  is what made him believe the rest.
- ***"Every team they've got looking at you is a team that isn't looking
  at somebody else. That's the whole of it and there isn't another one
  underneath."***
  - **Eight weeks from riverbed to a man asking a keeper for ages, and
    that date is on a sheet.** *"Eight weeks is very fast."*
  - ***"An untrained one lasts about eight months. Then he's dead in a
    ditch and we've got nothing and they've got their attention back. A
    trained one goes on drawing for years."***
  - ***"In about four years somebody will tell you it was because we
    thought you were special, and they'll believe it, and it will be a
    lie. It's because you're a thing they can't stop looking at."***
- **AND HE IS PLEASED, AND IT IS PLAYED WITHOUT COMMENT.**
  > *"He has taken a boy of ten and put him against eleven hundred people
  > and a number of years and worked out what the trade is, and he has
  > done the whole sum out loud in front of me, including the part where I
  > am a thing being spent, and he has not put one single word on it to
  > make it go down easier. It is correct. I have checked it."*
  >
  > **Nobody has ever told me what I am for.**
  >
  > *"My mother gave me instructions and never once gave me a reason. Meno
  > told me what a thing was. Aoyagi told me where to stand… That is the
  > first honest thing anybody has said to me about myself since the fifth
  > of the fourth month."*
- **The carrying stops in one line with no ceremony.** *"You'll not carry
  another parcel for me as long as you live."*
- **And Kajiya's last word, from the doorway, which he does not stand

**HŌKI — 8/9 to 8/20**

- **About fifty, a bad knee he does not favour, hands broken once and set

**UZUKI — FOUR MARKS AND NO RULE, 8/8**
