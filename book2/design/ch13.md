## 1. ⚠ THE SPINE — he has an open entry and he cannot close it

**`act-1.md` §6 hands act two *a debt: a girl on a road and an undertaking
to get her to a door.*** **He is a boy who closes entries.** *So month 1
is him trying to discharge it, properly, with verification, the way he
does everything.*

### And it fails for the exact opposite reason the boy failed

| | Ch11, the boy | ⚠ Ch13, her |
|---|---|---|
| **The problem** | **there was nowhere** — no place, no rate, no list, and fourteen thousand ryō bought none of it | **there is somewhere, and it is good, and he checked it** |
| **The failure** | the world had no column | ⚠ **she will not stay in it** |
| **What it costs him** | a boy on a step and no name in a ledger | **an entry that cannot be closed, and a household he did not choose** |

> **`spec.md`'s line arriving on the page:** *the difference is not his
> intention, which was identical, but which of them latched.* **He does
> the same operation twice in three months and the variable is not him.**

### ⚠ AND HER REASON IS NOT AFFECTION — that is the whole trick

**It must not be *because she loves him*.** *She is eleven, she has known
him five weeks, and the book has not earned that and does not need it.*

> **She does the arithmetic, because that is what she does.**
>
> **She has been through four doors** — *her mother's house after the
> remarriage, Shimoda's yard, the almshouse behind the shrine at Ōgi, the
> Kadono-dera* — **and every single one of them ended in a number.**
>
> **⚠ AND HE HAS NEVER ONCE TOLD HER WHAT SHE IS WORTH.** *Not in the
> hall, not on the road, not when he bought her a coat, not when she
> offered to be useful and he did not take her up on any of it.* **It is
> the first time in her life and she cannot say it out loud and does not
> try.**

**Which is also her act-two arc loaded in one scene** — *the girl running
from valuations becomes the person who does the pricing* (`act-2.md`
§4.12).

## 2. TADAYOSHI — five scenes

### 2.1 The kit, and the year turns (scene 1, 1–3)

**It is the first month, and he is eleven, and he notes it the way he
notes weather.**

- **§2k:** *boots that are a pair, a pack that fits a child, a blanket, a
  knife she may carry where people can see it.* **An afternoon, no
  hesitation, and he replaces nothing of his own** *(`act-2.md` §4.7 —
  flat and unremarked the first time).*
- **⚠ AND HE BUYS HER A HAT WITHOUT KNOWING WHY** *(§4.15).* **She asked
  for it. He costed it and bought it and did not ask what it was for**,
  *and she did not tell him, and the reader has known since the tenth
  month.*
- **The first docket of the year is nine hundred ryō and he does not
  think anything of it yet.**

### 2.2 The series says something (scene 4, 4–11)

**Two jobs, both small, and he does what he always does, which is put
them in the line.**

> *3,600 · 2,900 · 4,100 · 14,000 · 2,200 · 4,800 — and then 900 · 1,100.*
>
> **And the gaps: eleven days, nine, four, six — and then nineteen.**

- **He asks Ōtsuka and gets nothing.** *Not hostility. Nothing.* **Which
  he cannot use, because nothing has no shape.**
- **⚠ AND HE MISREADS IT**, *and the misreading is in character and is the
  chapter's second engine:* **he takes it as a fact about the market and
  not a fact about him.** *There is a war on. Winters are thin. Clients
  are careful.* **Every one of those is true and none of them is why.**

### 2.3 ⚠ THE DOOR (scene 6, 12–17) — and he does it properly

**He finds one. It is real and he does not take the first one he hears
about.**

- **Three candidates, priced and rejected** — *§2k applied to
  institutions, and it is the ch11 sequence run again with money and a
  different answer.*
- **The one he takes: a household, not a house.** *A widow with a trade
  and two of her own, four days out, who wants a pair of hands and will
  feed them.* **Not charity — an arrangement, which is the only kind of
  thing he trusts.**
- **He verifies it.** *Two days at four hundred yards, and then a day in
  the town asking three people what she pays and what she is like, which
  is his own rule from the sixth month.* **It is sound. It is genuinely
  sound and the book must not cheat about that.**
- **And he pays a year in advance**, *because paying in advance is how you
  make a thing not depend on you.*

### 2.4 She is behind him (scene 9, 21–24)

**Four days later she is on the road at about forty yards and neither of
them says anything about it for six miles.**

- **He does not ask why.** *`method-delta.md` §5 — he acts instead of
  saying — and there is no action available, so the scene is six miles of
  two people walking.*
- **⚠ AND HE DOES THE ONE THING HE CAN DO, WHICH IS ARITHMETIC.** *He goes
  back over the money and works out that the year in advance is spent and
  not returnable and that he has bought a stranger a year of a child's
  keep and has no child to put in it.* **And enters it.** *And the column
  is the fourth one.*
- **The last beat is not about her.** *It is the road, or the light, or a
  number* — **and the reader supplies everything else.**

### 2.5 The ford (scene 10, 26–30) — ⚠ first impression, and it has a handle

**`canon-cast.md` §3.2 and §1.6.** *A hunter at four hundred yards on the
far side of a ford, standing where you stand, and there is something wrong
about the right eye and Tadayoshi cannot say what.*

- **Nothing happens.** *He goes round, three hours, and pays for it in
  daylight.*
- **⚠ AND IT GOES ON THE SHEETS AS THE THIRD LINE**, *seventy-one days
  after the second* — **and it is the first entry that has a person in it
  who did not come for him.** *He was standing there. That is all.*
- **⚠ AND THE HANDLE IS MUTUAL** *(§1.6)*: **a man who can one day say
  *you were the boy at the Kanō ford* has been made, and Tadayoshi does
  not know that he has made one**, *and the price of every future cameo is
  the same price and the book should start charging it here.*

## 3. UZUKI — three scenes

### 3.1 Nobody said what it cost (scene 2, 1–3)

**Her side of the same afternoon**, *and the interior grammar is the
whole point:* **he bought her four things and did not once say a number in
front of her.**

- ***Every single thing I have ever been given came with what it cost
  attached to it, and he has not done it, and I do not think he is doing
  it on purpose.***
- **She asks for the hat and does not say why**, *and he does not ask*,
  **and she puts it down as the second time in five weeks that a question
  did not arrive.**
- **And she counts.** *1,528 or thereabouts.* **Still in her head, still
  no charcoal.**

### 3.2 The docket over his shoulder (scene 5, 4–11) — the seed

**She reads one and says a number back**, *and it is the wrong number, and
she is wrong in the direction that will make her right in six months.*

> **He is charging off the man. She looks at the client's gate.**
> *(`act-2.md` §4.12 — the seed only. The finding is later.)*

- **What she actually does on the jobs is nothing**, *and it is a real
  problem and the chapter should say so:* **she waits somewhere for eleven
  hours and it is boring and she is not useful and she knows it.**

### 3.3 ⚠ INSIDE THE DOOR (scene 7, 17–21) — the chapter's centre

**It is good.** *A trade, a bed, food twice, two children who are not
unkind, a woman who explains things once and expects you to have it.*
**She is there four days and every one of them is better than any day of
the tenth month.**

- **She works.** *Well, fast, on purpose, the way she did at the
  Kadono-dera* — **and catches herself doing it and stops in the middle of
  a yard.**
- **⚠ AND THAT IS WHERE SHE GETS IT.** ***I am doing the thing I did last
  time. And last time it did not come off the number.***
- **Then the four doors, in order, and every one ends in a figure.**
  > **And then the one that does not**, *and she goes at it for a night
  > and cannot find where the number is, and gets up on the fifth morning
  > and leaves.*
- **She does not tell the woman she is going.** *She leaves in the
  ordinary way at the ordinary time and does not look back at the house*,
  **and she is not sorry and does not pretend to be**, *and that is the
  first properly cold thing she does on her own account.*

## 4. KIMIMARO — two scenes, and the second one is the cruel one

### 4.1 ⚠ The door opens at the wrong time (scene 3)

**Two years of a bowl at three times a day, and then the door opens
between bowls.**

- **He does not resist, because resisting requires wanting a different
  outcome and he has never had one.**
- **⚠ AND HE GOES OUTSIDE.** *First time in two years.* **The sky is not a
  feeling. It is a fact he has no column for**, *and what he actually does
  with it is count* — **how far he can see, and how many things are
  moving, and neither number is any use.**
- **The four pieces are in his hand and the fifth is on the back of his
  hand at about ninety-five bowls**, *and there is nothing to lay them
  against.*

### 4.2 ⚠ AND THE MOVE DESTROYS HIS ONLY INSTRUMENT (scene 8)

**The new place has bowls and they do not come at times.**

> **This is the scene.** *He built everything on a rhythm — 178, 151, 129,
> ninety-five and thirty-four to go — and a bowl that comes when somebody
> remembers is not a clock.*
>
> ***I cannot count it. I have not got anything to count it with.***

- **He tries three replacements and all three fail** — *the light, which
  is not the same underground; the noise, which is people and people are
  not regular; and his own sleeping, which he has never been able to
  measure from inside.*
- **⚠ AND HE ARRIVES WHEREVER HE HAS ARRIVED WITH NINE HUNDRED AND THREE
  AND NO WAY TO SPEND IT.** *The number survives and the counter does
  not.*
- **He does not despair, because despair needs a model of the future and
  he has one number and no calendar.** *He puts the four pieces in a row
  by feel, in a room he has not seen the shape of, and waits.*

## 6. THE FIVE HARD PROBLEMS

**1. ⚠ THE DOOR MUST BE GENUINELY GOOD.** *Every instinct will want to put
a flaw in it so that leaving is justified.* **Refuse.** *The woman is
decent, the arrangement is fair, the food is real, and he checked it three
ways.* **If the reader can point at a reason, the scene is about the
reason and not about her.**

**2. Her leaving must not be affection.** *§1.* **If one line reads as *I
want to be with him*, the whole act-two arc goes soft.** *It is
arithmetic, it is about numbers not arriving, and she cannot articulate
it and does not try.*

**3. Thin ice must be invisible to him and legible to the reader.** *He
has the data and the wrong conclusion.* **The reader has ch12's silence
and can see the shape.** *Nobody in the book says it out loud in this
chapter or in the next two.*

**4. Kimimaro's scenes must not become sad.** *`act-1.md` §5a — counted,
not dated.* **He loses his clock and does not grieve, because grief needs
a picture of what was lost and he has never had one.** *The reader
grieves. He counts.*

**5. ⚠ AND THE CHAPTER MUST NOT BE A SUBSTANCE CHAPTER.** *`method-delta.md`
§2l says one in three, and this is not it — **ch14 is**.* **Ch13 has a
spine and it should move**, *and the temptation after that rule went in
will be to slow everything down at once.*

## 8. REVISED ON THE PAGE

**1. ⚠ THE OPEN ENTRY IS MUTUAL, AND THAT WAS NOT PLANNED.** *The plan had
his open ledger line and her four-doors arithmetic as separate things.*
**Written, they are the same object from two sides:**

> **He has *Open: get her to a door* in the fourth column and cannot close
> it.** **She has thirty-six days she cannot price and it is the only
> transaction of her life she has failed to read.**
>
> **Neither knows about the other's, and the book does not say so.**

**2. ⚠ AND SHE DOES NOT COME BACK FOR HIM. SHE COMES BACK TO GET MORE
OBSERVATIONS.** *Which is the coldest available version and it holds §1's
line without any effort:* ***I am not going back to tell him anything. I
am going back to watch him for longer… the only way I have ever found out
what a thing is worth is to be where it is and count.***

**3. The rejections carry the method and one of them has no case under
it.** *The religious house is struck out because it is the same shape as
the Kadono-dera, and the entry is two words —* ***Same shape. Not
going.*** — *and he knows it is not an argument and leaves it standing
anyway.* **Third time in his life. And he does not know why he cannot go
back and the reader does.**

**4. ⚠ AND HE NEVER ASKS HER.** *Nine days, four candidates, two days on a
rise, three people asked, eight thousand ryō* — **and the last line of the
scene is that it did not occur to him**, *because a list is a set of
things that occur to you.* **The ch11 failure repeated exactly, and
nobody in the book notices.**

**5. He overpays by two thousand and finds the honest reason.** ***The
extra is not for her. It is so that I can stop looking.***

**6. ⚠ HE ASKS ŌTSUKA AND GETS NOTHING, AND NOTHING HAS NO EDGE ON IT.**
*Not planned in this form.* ***If he had lied I would have him. A lie has
an edge on it.*** **And he works out that asking a third time tells a man
who will not answer that it matters** — *so he stops, correctly, and then
does the wrong thing, which is to pick one of three true reasons.*

**7. ⚠ KIMIMARO INVENTS A UNIT AND IT IS WRONG BY A FACTOR.** *Not
planned.* **He looks up, finds no wall, and turns the largest thing he has
ever seen into a number in three minutes with a thumb** — ***twenty-five
yards, where a yard is the yard*** — **and is pleased about it and has no
word for that either.** *It is wrong and he does not find out for two
years.*

**8. ⚠ AND SOMEBODY SPEAKS TO HIM.** *Also not planned, and it is the seed
of his whole act-two function.* ***"Cold enough for you?"*** **He takes it
as a question, spends two days building a true and checked answer, carries
it for eleven days, and is never asked again.** *He answers rhetorical
remarks literally because nobody has ever taught him not to* — **which is
the mechanism under `team.md` §6.2's dropped plate.**

**9. And he measures the room, precisely, to a thumb, and has no use for
any of it.** ***I have got the exact size of a room and I do not know what
day it is*** — *and he has the nearest thing to a joke that has ever
happened in his head, and does not recognise it.*

**10. ⚠ UZUKI GIVES HIM THE READING HE COULD NOT GET.** *Not planned this
early.* **He had *something wrong about the right one* and could not close
it. She had *he wasn't cold* — dried above the water line, an hour, and
standing straight** — *and she demonstrates the shape of a person in cold
water in the road, for a second and a half.* ***"You were working."***
**First line on the sheets that is not his, and he writes that it is
hers.**

**11. ⚠ AND THE CHAPTER ENDS ON THE PRICE OF §1.6, WHICH HE DOES NOT PAY
AND DOES NOT SEE.** *His own rule from the eighth month —* ***a boy
walking is nobody*** — **stopped being true at twenty past eleven on the
seventeenth of the twelfth month.** *Two children on a road are not
nobody. There is exactly one of that in this country.* **The reader has
it; he does not; and `canon-cast.md` §1.6's mutual handle is now on the
page as a cost rather than a note in a design file.**
