# Falsified — the ledger of dead rules

> ## ⚠ THE RULE THIS FILE EXISTS FOR
> Book one's `falsified.md` killed **twelve** design rules. **Every single
> replacement made the protagonist stronger. Not one ever added a
> constraint.** That is where the suspense went, and it happened one
> reasonable entry at a time.
>
> **No rule may be falsified without a replacement constraint named in the
> same entry.** If an entry cannot name the new constraint, **the old rule
> stands.**

**Every entry gets both columns.** *What died* and *what was installed in
its place.* An entry with an empty right-hand column is not an entry; it is
a widening with paperwork.

### #12 — THE THREE YEARS ARE COURIER WORK

| | |
|---|---|
| **Killed** | The postings were carrying — six ports, nine inland places, four days to a fortnight, and fences on a road when required (`ch06.md` §3.3). **For three years.** |
| **By** | Author ruling: **a rebellion holding a child who killed four genin does not spend him as a parcel runner.** Carrying is the probation. Then they train him — fieldcraft, sparring, and **one element** — **because he is a heat sink**, and every team hunting him is a team not hunting a cell. |
| **Replacement constraint** | **`combat.md` §10b — and it is not a cap, it is a cost that rises with him. He is one object.** *(A first attempt at this entry capped him at one element. That was an author's power budget wearing a world's clothes — **a losing organisation does not ration the one asset that works** — and it died before it reached a page.)* (1) **Arity does not move**: a dozen hunter-nin is not a harder fight, it is **twelve fights**, and no technique makes him two places. The only thing that solves it is people, **and people are the one thing he will not take.** (2) **The field has no judgement** — more reach is more unattended lethal ground, and a fence does not know who walked into it, **so every increase in his power increases the number of innocents his own work can kill.** The creed is not paranoia about the threshold; **it is arithmetic about a field.** (3) **The threshold spends whatever he has got**, so what he cannot account for afterwards is larger every year. (4) **More medium makes him louder**, along his own vulnerability axis. Plus: **the men who train him die**, and the catalogue only ever gets shorter. |
| **Net** | **Constraint added, and a better one than the rule it killed.** The old version capped him. This one lets him have everything and makes the having worse: **the training confirms the creed**, the creed keeps him alone, and alone is the exact condition the curve is built to beat — four teams, four objectives, one day. **There is still no version of this book in which he gets stronger and the danger goes down**, and the mechanism is now his own belief rather than an author's budget. |

### #13 — UZUKI IS NOT A FIGHTER

| | |
|---|---|
| **Killed** | `team.md` §4.4, published as a ⚠ HARD RULE: *her escalation is capacity and diagnosis, never damage. She cannot fight anybody at ten feet, at any age.* |
| **By** | Author ruling: **she is a glass cannon.** Katana, straight in, no guard and no disengage, **because she has given up caring** — and the emotional development is **a sadistic streak routed through his pleasure**: he enjoys the mission completed, so she enjoys *going and causing a scene.* |
| **Whose rule it was** | **Mine, and it was an inference presented as design.** Extrapolated from §11b's stamina limit plus the ch9 knife — *medic, therefore not a fighter* — and then written into a file with a warning glyph on it. **Nothing in `spec.md`, `portraits.md` or `combat.md` ever said it.** Logged because a rule invented by the assistant and stamped as authoritative is the most dangerous kind this project can accumulate. |
| **Replacement constraint** | **Four, and they are harder than the rule they replaced.** (1) **It is a valuation, not a death wish** — *she is not trying to die, she is refusing to spend anything on not dying* — and the policy is statable, consistent, and wrong in a way she cannot see. (2) **It makes arity WORSE**: he now has somebody who will absolutely be in the other place **and will not come back**, so his refusal moves from *she cannot* to ***she will***, which is worse for both of them. (3) ***You cannot tell during*** — **the one person who can diagnose anybody cannot diagnose herself while her hands are on, and she does not stop.** (4) ***It buys hours, not cures*** — **so everything she survives is borrowed and comes back on a delay she will not track.** |
| **Net** | **Constraint added, and a character arrived.** The dead rule made her safe and small. This one makes her the most capable and least survivable person in the book, **and hands the fourth reason the team never assembles to her willingness rather than her weakness.** |

## Watch list — rules under pressure, not yet dead

Recorded so that when one of them goes, it goes with a replacement.

1. **"The sword cannot be wielded by anyone who is not a crystal user."**
   Absolute until Guren. When it falls, the replacement is already
   specified: the blade **is** inheritable, therefore by his own reasoning
   **capable of rot**, and his last-of-the-line identity is false.
2. **"The theory stays private."** Marked to break in act three as the price
   of Kimimaro's diagnosis. **The replacement constraint must be named
   before it breaks**, not after.
3. **"The threshold is never written from inside."** If it is ever broken,
   that is an event and it gets an entry.
4. **"Nobody within the stated distance."** The household's only law, void
   from the moment he comes back. It does not need an entry — it needs a
   catastrophe.
