## 1. ⚠ THE MECHANISM — and §3.9 supplied it four days ago

**`structure.md` §4 requires three cruelties and forbids the easy one:**
*the argument cannot be about insubordination, because he obeyed.* **So
what ends the arrangement has to come from somewhere else, and it does.**

> **`team.md` §3.9, AUTHOR-derived:** *he is decent in a room, he listens
> better than anybody, and* **⚠ he cannot decline a question.**

### The chapter in one sentence

> ## **The household is destroyed by an honest answer to a friendly question.**

**Somebody in a corridor is kind to a boy of thirteen** — *not clever, not
hostile; kind* — **and asks him what he is.** *And he tells them. All of
it. What his clan did, what he can do, and how long he has got*, **because
somebody asked and he has the measurements and it did not occur to him
that an answer could be a thing you withhold.**

- **Nobody lied.** *The room decided correctly on excellent information.*
- **Nobody was cruel.** *The information was supplied voluntarily by its
  subject.*
- ⚠ **AND TADAYOSHI COULD HAVE ARGUED THE CLAN NAME.** *He could not argue
  a boy's own accurate account of himself.*

> **`structure.md` §4's *nobody is a villain* is not achieved by softening
> anybody. It is achieved by everybody doing their job properly.**

## 9. THE SIX HARD PROBLEMS

**1. ⚠ NOBODY IS ANGRY AND NOBODY IS RELUCTANT.** *Reluctance is a request
for the reader's forgiveness.* **Everybody in this chapter does their job
properly and the job is correct.**

**2. THE ORDER IS NEVER RAISED.** *He has the airtight defence and rehearsed
it and it never comes up.* **A defence nobody attacks is worse than losing
an argument**, *and he cannot even deploy it.*

**3. ⚠ KIMIMARO'S ANSWER MUST BE COMPLETE, ACCURATE AND UNFORCED.** *No
trick, no pressure, no interrogation.* **A kind person, a plain question,
and a boy who does not know that an answer is a thing you can decline** —
*and the prose must not flag it as a mistake while it happens.*

**4. THE ARITHMETIC MUST BE SHOWN AND MUST BE RIGHT.** *If a reader can
find the flaw in the resistance's sum, the chapter is about people being
stupid.* **It has to be a sum this book's own protagonist would sign.**

**5. ⚠ AND HE MUST NOT BE GIVEN A SPEECH.** *He is a boy who acts instead
of saying* (`character.md`). **His entire response to the largest event of
his life is that he asks one operational question, is answered, says
*right*, and leaves** — *and does the book that night, and the entries are
where the chapter lands.*

**6. AND THE LAST LINE BELONGS TO HER**, *because `act-2.md` §5's ch18 is
**the road, and no organisation**, and this is the sentence that starts
it.*

## 11. REVISED ON THE PAGE

**Eleven scenes, 16,900 words, T×4 · K×3 · U×3 · one-off ×1.**
*`method-delta.md` §2j — the prose is canon and this file is not.*

### ⚠ THE SCENE ORDER CHANGED, AND IT HAD TO

**§8 had the one-off POV at scene 8 and the corridor at scene 10.** *That
cannot work: the arithmetic needs the boy's account in front of it, so the
corridor has to happen first.*

**Corridor is now scene 8, the one-off is scene 9, the room is scene 10.**
*The reader watches the round get loaded, then fired, then land* — **and
the room becomes the only scene in the chapter where nobody learns
anything.**

### ⚠ AND THE CORRIDOR FOUND ITS OWN MECHANISM, WHICH THE PLAN HAD NOT

**§1 said he answers a friendly question. He does not.** *`ch17` s7 gave
Uzuki a fourth instruction off her own bat —* ***and don't answer
anything*** *— and he takes it, and keeps it.*

**So the man asks one question — *that your animal?* — and gets nothing.**

> ## **AND THEN THE MAN SAYS THREE WRONG THINGS, AND KIMIMARO CORRECTS ALL THREE.**
>
> ***A wrong thing is not a question and correcting one is not answering
> one, and I have never let a wrong measurement stand in my life, and
> nobody has ever told me to.***

**This is enormously better than the planned version and it is the same
finding harder:** *he obeys the letter of the instruction exactly — which is
the only way he has ever obeyed anything — and it delivers everything.*
**Both children's competence is complete, correct, and useless**, *and
nobody disobeys anybody.*

- *And the third wrong thing is not wrong* — ***you're a bit thin for a lad
  your age*** — **it is a fact with no cause under it**, *and a fact without
  a cause is going to be wrong the next time somebody uses it,* **so he
  supplies the cause.**

### The one-off gained a morning, and it is what makes her un-villainous

**§3 said busy and not troubled. The prose makes it the fourth of nine**,
*and names the second — a man of six years taking money from a shipping
house, done at half past seven in nine minutes* — **and she ate afterwards,
because not eating afterwards helps nobody and is a kind of vanity.**

> **⚠ AND *NO PREJUDICE* IS HERS AND IS THE ONLY CHOICE SHE HAS.** *It
> costs nothing, does nothing, and she knows both* — **and a boy of eleven
> who has worked fifteen months without a loss should not have a mark on
> his sheet, and she is not going to be the person who puts one there.**

### ⚠ AND THE ROOM GAINED ELEVEN WORDS THE PLAN DID NOT HAVE

**§4 asked the man from the crate to say why in eleven words and left the
words open.**

> ***"I was fourteen at Sasago. There were two of them. Two."***

**And nobody in the room reacts, because they all knew.** *He did not say it
to persuade anybody — there is nobody in there to persuade.* **He said it
because Tadayoshi did not know and he thought he should**, *and it is the
only thing from that room Tadayoshi never worked out.*

### The defence was built and never used, on the page

**§9.2 required a defence nobody attacks.** *The prose builds it in full —
five parts, checked against both counterparts, rehearsed three times* —
**and then:** ***it holds. There is not a hole in it and I am good at
looking for holes.*** *About a question nobody in that building had any
intention of asking.*

### ⚠ AND THE FAILURE OF HER PLAN IS ARCHITECTURAL, NOT PERSONAL

**§7 said nobody thought about corridors. The prose gives the reason and it
is not carelessness:** *eleven years of a village, a monastery, a stable and
a road* — **and in every one of those, outside is a yard.**

> *She has been asked twice whether she blames herself and both times said
> no and both times it was true.* **What she has never said is that she also
> cannot walk past a bench in a corridor, and has known those are the same
> fact since about fifteen.**

### What the wait grew that the plan did not ask for

- **⚠ THE BODY.** *Nine miles on the ninth after two years of eleven paces,
  paid for over four days* — **and the left foot is worse because the room
  fell a thumb toward the drain.** ***So a room gets into you.***
- **⚠ *THE GROUND* — a third page, headed in one second**, *against two he
  cannot head.* **And saying it out loud is what costs him**, *because he
  has made it forty times and never once had to describe it.*
- **⚠ AND HE GETS ONE STEP FROM §5a AND STOPS**, *which is the pattern:*
  ***there is no version of this where I am putting anybody on that ground
  on purpose.***
- **She learns it as a route and not a list** — ***she is learning where she
  can be*** — **and he watches from the trees and does not tell her.**

### And the last beat is bigger than §5 planned

**§5 wanted *there is nobody above you now* and got it.** *What the prose
adds is his answer, which he has been carrying since 6/2:*

> ***"I don't know how to ask for money… I have watched you do it. In a
> yard, in the second month, with a mule."***

**So her finding does not just become usable. It becomes *hers to do*** —
*and* ***"Well. That's a thing I can do"*** **is the household's first
division of labour and nobody names it**, *which is `team.md` §6.1's rule
arriving on a different pair.*

### What did NOT change

**§9.1 held** — *nobody is angry and nobody is reluctant, at any point, in
any scene.* **§9.3 held** — *no trick, no pressure, and the prose does not
flag it as a mistake while it happens.* **§9.4 held** — *the arithmetic is

**And §9.6 held: the last line is hers.**
