## 1. THE ARGUMENT — three correct answers, and none of them help

**Ch7 was two children opening records. Ch8 is what the records say.**

| | Is right about | And it costs |
|---|---|---|
| **Tadayoshi** | **a posting.** The road is bad, the objection is sound, and he refuses it | **they send somebody else and that person does not come back.** *Being right did not remove the job. It only removed me from it.* |
| **Uzuki** | **the sixteenth.** She was right about six seconds, and the acquittal was wrong | **she cannot put him back on the list**, because putting him back convicts her of a month |
| **Kimimaro** | **his own body.** The count finished; he has the number the man wanted | **the number is bad**, and it is correct, and being correct is what makes him disposable |

> **Nobody in this chapter is wrong. That is the chapter.**

### The rhyme cuts

- **Scene 3 → 4.** A boy hears another child's door stop opening. **A man
  finds a hole in Tadayoshi's method that Tadayoshi had not found.** Two
  people discovering they are worse than they thought, twenty minutes
  apart on the page.
- **Scene 6 → 7.** He is correct out loud and it makes him a problem.
  **Kimimaro gets a bad number off his own hand and decides to train.**
  Two children found wanting: **one argues and one breaks a bone off
  himself.**
- **Scene 9 → 10.** A man dies because Tadayoshi was right and refused.
  **She cannot reinstate a man on a list because reinstating him means
  she has sat in a room with him for a month.** *Both are children
  finding out that being right is not a lever.*
- **And the chapter's last two objects are a knife going under a pillow
  and a column that stops.**

## 2. TADAYOSHI — the carrying stops

> `combat.md` §10b. **Ch7's contract was the last of its kind and nothing
> in ch7 said so.** This is where the reader finds out.

### 2.1 The heat sink, said out loud (scene 1, month 8, the 2nd)

**Kajiya tells him the ugly half first, because that is what Kajiya
does** (ch6: *I'm not here because of what was done to your people*).

**The decision was made off Hanase.** Eight weeks after a riverbed,
before he had finished two postings, **a man was asking a lodging keeper
for ages at two in the morning.** That date is on a sheet.

**The argument, delivered flat and complete:**

> *"Every team that's looking at you is a team that isn't looking at
> somebody else. That's the whole of it. You'll be hunted whether we do
> anything or not, so the only question we've got is whether the thing
> being hunted is any good at it."*
>
> *"An untrained one lasts about eight months and then we've got nothing
> and they've got their attention back. A trained one goes on drawing for
> years."*

**AND TADAYOSHI IS PLEASED.**

> **That is the horror of the scene and it must be played without
> comment.** Being valuable in a way somebody has costed is the only kind
> of value he can perceive at all. **He is told, to his face, that he is
> being spent as an attention sink, and he checks the arithmetic, and it
> holds, and something in him settles.**
>
> *"Nobody has ever told me what I am for before."*

**And the carrying stops in one sentence with no ceremony in it**, and
the reader realises retroactively what ch7 was.

### 2.2 Hōki, and the first hole anybody else has found (scene 4)

**HŌKI — *the broom.*** Trade name, no real name, per `portraits.md`.
Field operator, fifty-ish, **not kind and not unkind**, and the man who
teaches him not to leave anything.

**He is spent, like all of them** (`combat.md` §10b) — and the reader
should like him, because `act-1.md` §5.1's rule is running a second time
on a second family: **each death removes a named capability, and the
catalogue only ever gets shorter.**

**THE BEAT THE SCENE EXISTS FOR:**

> **Somebody out-analyses him. For the first time in the book.**

He is shown ground-breaking — **medium generation, `combat.md` §7b's
sibling: stone and packed earth into workable grit** — and he takes the
mechanism fast, because he is what he is, **and Hōki names the price
before he has finished being pleased about it:**

> *"So now you need ground you can break."*
>
> *"You've been picking your ground on cover and footing. Now you'll pick
> it on what's under it, and you'll do it without noticing, and in about
> a year somebody will write down where you stand."*

**And it is the exact constraint from §10b, arriving in a man's mouth
rather than as authorial bookkeeping** — and it is a flaw in his own
method that he had not found, **and he is thrilled**, and that is the
most likeable he has been since ch1.

**What else he is taught, and none of it is a technique:** breaking
contact; using a broker; reading a briefing for what it does not say;
leaving a town; **and pricing a job** — *the four things he cannot do,
taught partially and badly by people who do them for a living.*

### 2.3 He is correct, out loud, at the wrong moment (scene 6)

> **He is not difficult because he is defiant. He is difficult because he
> is right, out loud, in front of people who cannot afford to be
> corrected.**

**He is given a briefing and he audits it**, because auditing is the only
way he knows how to read anything, and **he finds a real error** — a
date, a distance, a name that appears twice as two people — and he says
so, flatly, at the wrong volume, in a room.

**And he is right, and the room knows he is right, and the man who wrote
it is standing there.**

- **Nobody thanks him.** The error gets fixed and something in the room
  closes.
- **`character.md`, the missing organ, first light again** — but this
  time it is not a front room and a stranger. **It is his own side**, and
  the thing he cannot do is the thing that would have let him say it in
  a way that cost nobody anything.
- **He does not understand what has happened**, files it as an anomaly,
  **and does it again eleven days later.**

### 2.4 The refusal, and the man they sent (scene 9)

**He refuses a posting and the objection is sound.**

**The route puts him back on the east road out of Hanase** — the road he
ran eleven miles down at three in the morning five weeks ago, in the
district where a man was asking a keeper for ages. **He has it in the
file. He can produce the date.**

**And the counter-argument is also correct**, which is what makes it a
scene and not a lesson:

> *"Five weeks. Nobody's still standing on a road for a boy who went out
> of a window five weeks ago. And the job is on that road, because the
> thing that needs carrying is at one end of it and the man who wants it
> is at the other."*

**Two correct arguments, one road, one job. He says no.**

**They send somebody else.** *(Trade-name rule, biting: he never learns
what the other one was called.)* **A boy of about sixteen with a bad
ear**, seen twice, in passing, and not spoken to.

**And Fuda tells him, in nine words, without emphasis, three weeks
later.**

> **THE FINDING, and it drives the rest of the act:**
>
> ***Being right did not remove the job. It only removed me from it.***

**Which is the causal chain into ch11** — this is why he takes the
punishment cargo run, and why he stops refusing anything, and the book
should never say so.

## 3. UZUKI — the month it becomes a pattern

### 3.1 Four marks, and no rule (scene 2, the 8th)

**The fourth column has four marks in it and she does what she does with
numbers: she looks for the rule.**

- **She has the dates.** She has the ninety column and the money column
  beside them, so she can put the marks against everything else that
  happened in those months.
- **She tests: days of the week. Money. Drink. Weather. What her mother
  did. What her mother said. What she herself did.**

**AND THERE IS NO RULE THAT LIVES IN THIS HOUSE.**

> **Everything that correlates is on the outside of it.** A price at
> market. A thing that broke. A day that went badly two fields away.
>
> **Which means her mother is not the cause and therefore cannot be the
> fix**, and Uzuki, who is ten and who fixes things with numbers,
> **has found a variable she cannot reach.**

**And a partial pattern would have been a mercy.** A rule is a thing you
can manage. **Four marks and no rule is a thing you can only wait for.**

### 3.2 She discovers she is a deterrent (scene 5, the 14th)

**Her action, and it works, which is the trap.**

She starts finding reasons to be in the kitchen late. Mending. The box.
A thing that needs doing to the range. **Nothing sophisticated — she is
simply there, and awake, and visible.**

**And nothing happens on the nights she is there.** Four for four.

> **So she is a deterrent.**
>
> **Which means every night she goes to bed is a decision**, and she
> works that out on about the fifth night, lying down, **and gets back
> up.**

**And she cannot sustain it.** She is ten, she is doing Kuriya twice a
week, and there is a night in the third week when she cannot keep her
eyes open and goes to bed at nine.

**And it happens that night.**

> **The escalation of ch7 scene 8, exactly:** *every gap was a door and
> she walked past one every three seconds.* **Now every night is a
> door.**

### 3.3 Twenty-five minutes with the one man who does not talk (scene 8)

**The conditions are perfect and she does not say it.**

Kuriya, twice a week, in daylight, doing the step. **Twenty-five minutes
in which nobody speaks by convention.** He is the one man in Nakadō who
does not talk and he said so himself at his own gate. **She has never had
a better opportunity and will not get one.**

**Three reasons, worked out in order while her hands are on his knee:**

1. **She would have to explain how she knows**, and how she knows is a
   chair, a lie about a bucket, and a pair of hands moving away from
   hers. **She has already tested that on herself and it does not
   survive being said out loud.**
2. **He would have to do something.** And what a decent man does is **go
   and speak to Shimoda** — and then Shimoda knows that she knows.
   > **Telling anybody makes it worse for her mother that night.**
3. **And she is being paid.** Half a ryō a month and one relationship
   outside that house, **and she is not going to spend either.**

**And the fourth, which she does not put in the list:** at the sixtieth
she wants to say it and does not push, **and the not-pushing is the same
motion as the technique**, and she notices that and does not like it.

> **She has been taught, by a good man, in a kitchen, that stopping is a
> separate action that costs as much as the going.** *And she is now
> using it on herself, to not say a thing.*

### 3.4 The second look — and she cannot put him back (scene 10, the 26th)

**It happens again. Longer.** And this time there is nowhere to file it,
**because there is no list.**

> **She struck him off on the sixteenth of the seventh month, deliberately,
> as an act, and felt grown-up about it.**

**And she cannot reinstate him**, and the reason is not pride:

> **Putting him back on convicts her of the month.**
>
> **If he goes back on the list, then the acquittal was wrong, and if the
> acquittal was wrong then she has been sitting in a room with a person
> she had already correctly identified, every evening, for six weeks,
> and calling it being fair.**

**So she does not put him on the list. She does something else instead,
and it is the same judgement expressed as an object.**

### 3.5 The knife, and the column that stops (scene 11, the 26th, night)

**THE KNIFE.**

- **It is a kitchen knife and she takes it out of the kitchen**, which
  means the kitchen is short a knife, which means somebody will notice,
  **and she has costed that and taken it anyway.**
- **She does not know what it is for.** She would tell you she does. She
  would say *in case*, and she cannot finish the sentence, **and she
  tries to, twice, lying down, and cannot.**
- **⚠ It goes under because of the fourth column, not instead of it.**
  The record made it a pattern; the pattern made it a thing that is going
  to happen again; **and a thing that is going to happen again is a thing
  you prepare for.**

**AND THEN THE NINETY STOPS.**

> She goes to the brick, as she has every night since the second of the
> seventh month, **and puts her hands on it, and nothing happens.**
>
> **Not *won't*. Nothing happens.** She holds it for a while and takes
> her hands off and does not write a number.

**And the column stops there, on the twenty-sixth of the eighth month,
and no character and no line of narration ever remarks on it again.**

> `team.md` §4.4 and `ch07.md` §3.4: **from here she can only do it on a
> person.** The empty ninety — 20, 22, 19, 24, 26 … 31 good … 29, 28 —
> **just ends.**
>
> **Three chapters of a rising number and then nothing.**

## 4. KIMIMARO — the second sequence, and he is in it

**Rules held:** his two scenes are **the two shortest in the chapter**,
and **neither opens with a time or a place.**

### 4.1 He has two sequences now, and he knows which one he is in (scene 3)

**This is the payoff of ch3 scene 4 and ch6 scene 8 together, and it
costs nothing because both sequences are already on the page.**

| | **Sequence one — *they are going to use you*** | **Sequence two — *they are not*** |
|---|---|---|
| | inspection | viewing |
| | warm food, on the ninth day | nothing, for ten |
| | more in the bowl | **the blanket taken, folded, going out** |
| | a blanket, folded | **cold food on the eleventh** |
| | washed, by two who are not the keeper | **and less in it** |

**He has run sequence one once and he is thirty-eight days into sequence
two.**

**AND THEN HE HEARS SOMEBODY ELSE RUN IT.**

- **He knows the passage by gaps.** C1: *there was a door along the
  passage with a nine-count gap after his; it stopped.* **There is
  another one, with a different gap, and he has been counting it for two
  years without ever having decided to.**
- **An inspection has a rhythm** — more feet, a different weight, a
  pause. **He can hear one happen four doors away.**
- **Then the intervals change.** He does not see a bowl or a blanket.
  **He hears the door open fewer times.**
- **And then the door with that gap stops.**

> **He knew it was going to happen before it did, because he could count
> it against himself.**
>
> **He is behind that child by a number of days, and he knows the
> number.**

**THE FINDING, and it is the coldest thing in the chapter:**

> **He has a timetable for his own disposal and he built it by
> listening.**

**And he never learns the child's name.** `act-1.md`: *he counts them
instead.* **He does not know whether it was a boy or a girl or how old.
He knows the gap was six.**

### 4.2 The count finished, and the answer is bad (scene 7)

**He started it in ch6 scene 8**, in the dark, on the mat, untaught: one
out of the back of the left hand to be broken, **one out of the right to
be left alone, for comparing.** Then bowls, three to a day.

**It is finished. He has the number the man wanted.**

> ***How long does it take to come back?***

**AND IT IS THE WRONG ANSWER.**

- **It came back.** It took a long time and he has the figure exactly,
  in bowls, and can convert it if anybody ever gives him a day.
- **And it came back smaller, and not the same shape**, and the one on
  the right hand — never broken, put out and left — **is fine, and is the
  proof.**

> **So the answer to the question the man came four hundred counts to ask
> is: *slowly, and worse.***
>
> **He is a poorer thing than the man was hoping for. And the evidence is
> his own hands. And he worked it out himself.**

**AND THEN HE WORKS HARDER** (`act-1.md` §5, verbatim requirement):

> **He does not decide to lie.** He has almost no idea what lying is and
> would not be able to work out how. **He decides to make the number
> better.**
>
> **He will break one, and count, and break the next one, and count, and
> go on until the number comes down and the shape comes back right.**
>
> **So that when he is asked, the answer will be a good one.**

**Nobody taught him to. He worked it out.** **The one initiative of his
life, spent on making himself better property**, running for the second
time, on a deadline he inferred from a door four along the passage.

**And the second count starts on the last line of the scene.**

## 8. THE FIVE HARD PROBLEMS

**1. Tadayoshi must be pleased in scene 1.** If he is troubled by being
called an attention sink, the character is wrong. **Being valuable in a
way somebody has costed is the only kind of value he can perceive**, and
the scene should be played straight and without comment and the reader
should feel sick on his behalf.

**2. The refusal must not make him a victim.** He is right, he refuses,
somebody dies. **The book must not let him — or the reader — conclude
that he should not have refused.** The finding is narrower and worse:
*being right did not remove the job.* **A correct objection relocated a
death. It did not prevent one**, and there was never a version available
where it did.

**3. Uzuki's month must escalate without repeating.** Four distinct
movements — **the rule that isn't there, the deterrent that works, the
confession that doesn't happen, and the reinstatement she cannot
afford** — and each is a different *kind* of failure. **None of them is
her being frightened.** She acts four times.

**4. Shimoda still does not become a monster on the page.** No scene is
written from inside the kitchen. **Everything is inference from a
record**, and the record is a girl's marks on a flour bag. **The reader
convicts him on the same evidence she has, which is not enough for
anybody else and is plenty.**

**5. Kimimaro's scene 3 must be dread, not information.** The two
sequences are already on the page from ch3 and ch6; **the scene's job is
to let him lay them side by side and find himself in the wrong one.**
It must not explain itself. **The table in §4.1 is for the author, not
the page.**
