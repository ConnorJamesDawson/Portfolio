## 1. ⚠ THE CHAPTER'S ENGINE — the creed does not work when he is choosing

**This is the thesis of the whole series and act one ends on it.**

### 1.1 He does not consult the page

**The creed is twenty-five days old on the seventeenth.** *He wrote it,
tested it backwards over five jobs, found the load-bearing stroke in it,
and put **no exceptions** on the same sheet as the first exception.*

**And when Enkai explains what the consignment is, he does not get the
page out.**

> **Not because he decides not to. Because it does not fire.** *He wrote
> **not the ones who did not choose** about a boy he might have killed,
> and he has read it ever since as a rule about killing.*
>
> **Carrying is not killing.** *One line of interior, no more:* ***It is
> not a killing.*** **And on he goes.**

**⚠ SO THE FIRST WIDENING HAPPENS IN THE SAME ACT AS THE WRITING**, *on a
technicality, in its author's own head, and he does not notice it at the
time.* **He notices on the road down** — §4.5 — *and the correction he
writes is procedural, and he knows it is the wrong shape of correction,
and writes it anyway because it is the only shape he has.*

### 1.2 And then it fires when he is not there

**Step 7 of the author's staging.** *He looks over her and does nothing.*

> **The rule he refused to apply consciously works in the four minutes he
> cannot account for.**
>
> **Which is `spec.md` §2g arriving whole:** *a governor written by a man
> who knows there are minutes of his life he does not attend.* **He built
> it for the state he is not in, and it only works in the state he is not
> in, and neither he nor she can see that it worked.**

**Nobody in that building knows a rule held.** *She sees a pause. He has
no account of any of it. And the reader has both halves and neither
character has one.*

## 2. THE ARITHMETIC ENKAI GIVES HIM, AND IT IS AIRTIGHT

**From the archived draft, and it survives whole, because it is the best
thing in it.** *He explains the way a man explains a routing decision to
somebody who will be executing it.*

- **A cart is watched and a man is not.**
- **The twelfth month is the best month because the roads are empty and
  nobody is counting.**
- **A boy is better than a man** — *two people on a road in winter are a
  father and a child, and nobody has ever asked a father anything.*
- **The buyer's man walked four days on the fifteenth to confirm the item
  was as described, and confirmed it.**
- **It was priced on the twenty-eighth of the tenth month and held since,
  and holding costs, and the holding is why the schedule is what it is.**

> ***He is doing what I do.*** *The road, the cover, what the weather
> buys, what the waiting costs.* **I have got four pages of that in my
> coat.**

### And the offer has no third thing in it

> ***"You can take the consignment… or you can decline it and go down that
> road empty, and I'll write to the man who sent you and say the courier
> declined, and he'll send another one in the second month and it will
> cost me nine weeks."***
>
> ***"That's the whole of it. There's no third thing. You'd have done the
> same arithmetic."***

**And he would have. And he does.** *Declining does not save her; it costs
her nine more weeks in a cellar.* **`method-delta.md` §1a: the trap is a
price, not a wall, and every exit from it is worse.**

## 3. AND THE CRATES ARE THE POSTING, MADE LEGIBLE

**Also from the archived draft, and it is the thing that tells him what
he is.**

- **Eight hundred and seventeen pounds, nine crates, four days to Ōgi and
  nine to Ōtoma, and he is one man.** *He raises it at loading in Ōtoma
  and is told to sign, because the courier signs.*
- ***"The crates go in the spring. There's a cart comes up from Ōgi when
  the pass takes one. You'd not get them down this mountain in the twelfth
  month with nine men."***
- **⚠ THE WEIGHTS ARE ON THE PAPER SO THAT THERE IS SOMETHING ON THE
  PAPER.** *And the thing he is actually carrying is a thing you send one
  boy on foot into the twelfth month with, and no such thing weighs
  anything, and no such thing goes in a crate.*

> **He walked four hundred miles up a mountain in winter carrying freight
> that was never going anywhere**, *and the nine days were the posting,
> and the posting was the point, and he worked all of that out standing on
> a hall floor in about two seconds.*

**And he never opens a crate and never finds out what is in them**, *which
is the courier's discipline and is also `combat.md` §10's rule about
things that stay open.* **Carried into act two as *the unexplained*.**

## 4. TADAYOSHI — seven scenes

### 4.1 The loading (scene 1, 12/1–5)

**Nobody says it is a punishment and he knows.** *Ōtsuka is correct with
him and nothing else.* **The weight raised, and the answer is *sign*.**

- **§2k:** *the new boots go on the road for the first time, and the coat
  he did not buy is the coat he is wearing, and nine days uphill in the
  twelfth month is what that decision costs him.*
- **Last beat: he prices the trip and it does not price**, and he goes.

### 4.2 The road (scene 3, 12/5–15)

**Nine days, uphill, in winter, alone, with freight.** *This is where §2k
lives at length: what he eats, what he burns, what he does about the
water, and what eight hundred and seventeen pounds does to a boy of ten
across nine days.*

- **PROPOSED, and it pays a debt:** *the hunter sheets come out on the
  first of the twelfth month, because he wrote himself that instruction,*
  **and this time there is nothing to add and he writes the nothing.**
  *So the instrument is not dead — it was the twenty-first of the tenth
  that was the anomaly, and now he has proof and can no longer tell
  himself otherwise.*

### 4.3 The arrival (scene 6, 12/16) — *archived ch12-sc9, reusable*

**He reads the ground and the ground says nothing at all.** *Eleven men,
twelve; somebody in the hall and somebody behind it; and nine or ten in
the long building who are not men.* **He puts it down and does not do
anything with it**, *because a working temple in the twelfth month is a
temple with winter people in it and that is what they are for.*

> **⚠ AND TOKUJI SAYS *"Have you eaten?"*** — **the same building, the
> same man, the same question, sixty-four days after he said it to her**
> *(ch10 sc8).* **Neither of them knows and the book does not say so.**

### 4.4 ⚠ THE CONSIGNMENT (scene 7, 12/17, morning) — the chapter's centre

**§2 and §3 in one scene, and it is bigger than the massacre.** *The
crates, the spring, **what am I carrying**, the explanation, the offer,
and he takes it.*

- **Tokuji, afterwards, in a doorway, at a volume that has not been used
  in that building in nine weeks:** ***"She's been down there since the
  second of the eleventh."***
- **Forty-five days.** *And he came up that road at eleven the previous
  morning and read the ground and counted the ways out of five buildings
  and ate a bowl on a step and has been within thirty feet of it twice.*
- **⚠ AND HE STILL TAKES IT**, *because Tokuji's sentence changes the
  particulars and not one line of the arithmetic.* **That is the scene's
  last move and it must be flat.**

### 4.5 ⚠ THE WALK TO THE HALL (scene 8, 12/17, ten past ten) — short

**Eight hundred words, and the reader spends every one of them ahead of
him.**

> **He is going to the hall to ask an operational question about
> restraining her on the road**, *because a child who runs is a problem
> and he has not priced it.* **That is the worst available reason and it
> is the only one in character.**

- **The handover is at noon. They have her up at ten. He does not know
  that.**
- **He does not knock — AUTHOR:** *"he just enters instead of knocking;
  knocking would've made them straighten up."* **So the men are mid-
  sentence, which is why he hears the line at all.**
- **⚠ THE POV BREAKS HERE.** *Write up to it and stop* (`combat.md` §9 as
  amended, and `method-delta.md` §3).

### 4.6 The coming back (scene 10, 12/17) — the reconstruction

**He is standing in a hall and does not know what happened**, *and the
reader does, because they have just watched it from four feet away.*

- **He counts them.** *That is the first thing he does, because counting
  is what he has.* **Fifteen.**
- **He does the reconstruction discipline and gets almost nothing** — *a
  door, the start of a sentence, and then the floor* — **and he can
  account for the crossing of no distance at all.**
- **The receipt is on the table, signed, with his own mark on it.**
- **⚠ AND TOKUJI IS AMONG THEM.** See §5.3.
- **And she is standing there, and she does the thing she knows works.**

### 4.7 The road down (scene 11, 12/18–19) — ⚠ ACT ONE'S LAST PAGE

- **No fee, a dead client, and a failure to report he has no account
  for.**
- **⚠ HE REALISES HE DID NOT CONSULT THE PAGE.** *Twenty-five days old, and
  he had it in his coat, and it did not occur to him.* **The correction he
  writes is procedural** — *and he knows it is the wrong shape and writes
  it anyway because it is the only shape he has.*
- **⚠ AND THEN HE ASKS HER NAME.**

> **Flat, operational, and not kind.** *He asks because a person you are
> carrying has to go in a column.*
>
> **It is the one thing he did not do in the eleventh month.** *Neither of
> them knows that. The reader has been waiting a whole chapter for it.*
>
> **She gives it. He writes it at the top of a blank page two leaves on
> from the creed, and there is nothing else on the page, and he cannot
> think of a heading for that one either.**

**And that is the end of act one.**

## 6. KIMIMARO — one scene, and it is the last before he is moved

**Scene 4.** *`act-2.md` §5 opens act two with him being moved, so this
scene is the last ordinary day of two years of them and neither he nor the
reader is told that.*

- **He is counting toward nine hundred and three** *(ch11)*, **and by the
  twelfth month he has spent about ninety of them and it is going
  exactly as he calculated**, *which he finds satisfying and has no word
  for.*
- **⚠ AND THEN THE BOWL CHANGES.** *Not the time. The amount.* **And he
  has no way to read it**, because a change is a thing somebody decided
  and he has never had access to anybody's decisions.
- **He does the only work available**, which is to prove it — *and he
  proves it the hard way, off the rim, over four days* — **and then has a
  proved fact and nothing to do with it.**
- **⚠ THE RHYME, AND IT MUST NOT BE POINTED AT:** *Uzuki proved the same
  fact about the same object in the same month in a cellar four hundred
  miles away, and drew the opposite conclusion, because hers did not
  change and his did.*
- **Last beat: he does not conclude anything**, *because concluding
  requires a model of the people upstairs and he has never been given
  one.* **He puts it with the three pieces and waits for the next bowl.**

## 8. THE FIVE HARD PROBLEMS

**1. ⚠ SCENE 9 IS THE HARDEST THING IN THE BOOK AND THE LINE IS THIN.**
*She may enjoy it; the prose may not* (`method-delta.md` §2i). **The test:
every sentence must be legible as her attention, not as the narration's
appetite.** *Her register is a craftsman watching a craftsman — she notes
economy, order, what he does not waste — and the horror is entirely in the
gap between that register and what is being described.*

**2. Step 7 must read as nothing.** *A pause. A man looking at her and
moving on.* **If the prose flags it, the whole engine dies**, because the
point is that nobody in the building can see the rule work. *One sentence,
no emphasis, and the reader supplies it.*

**3. Scene 7 must not become a trial.** *Enkai is not defending himself
and Tadayoshi is not prosecuting.* **Two people who route things,
comparing routes**, and the horror is the professional courtesy. **Nobody
raises their voice in this chapter until step 5.**

**4. Scene 10 must not give him an insight.** *He has no account of it and
that is permanent.* **He counts, he finds the receipt, he cannot cross the
four feet in memory** — and the temptation is to let him understand
something on that floor. **He does not. He gets that on the road, and what
he gets is procedural.**

**5. Nine days on a mountain must not be a montage.** *`method-delta.md`
§2k exists for exactly this scene.* **Scene 3 is the road written as
operations** — *weight, fuel, water, feet, boots* — **and if it compresses,
§2k was never a rule.**
