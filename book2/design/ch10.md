## 1. THE ARGUMENT — a losing rebellion sells reliability

**AUTHOR: he takes solo jobs to take the pressure off other operatives**,
which means high-profile work, **including clients who have to be kept
sweet so they back the rebellion instead of selling it out.**

> **So the product is not a dead man. The product is: the thing gets done
> and nothing else happens.**
>
> **That is the standard, it is commercial, and it is the one he will fail
> — twice.** *Month 12 at the monastery is strike one. The Kaguya is the
> final straw.* **Neither is a moral judgement and nobody accuses him of
> anything.**

### And the chapter's engine is a mismatch, not a moral

| He is | He is not |
|---|---|
| **superb at the execution** — the route, the ground, the timing, the leaving | **capable of a room with a client in it** |
| **exactly what they need on the night** | **anything they can put in front of a man who is deciding whether to fund them** |

> **He is the best thing they have and the worst thing to be seen with**,
> and both facts arrive in the same fortnight, **and he can only see the
> first one.**

## 2. TADAYOSHI — the graduation

### 2.1 They stop giving him parcels (scene 1, 9/20–22)

**No ceremony and no conversation about it**, because that is how this
organisation works and he already knows it (C9): *things get decided once,
by accident or otherwise, and then they are what there is.*

- **What he notices is the sheet.** *A docket with no cargo line on it.*
- **And a fee that is nine times the last one.** *He does the division
  before he does anything else and enters it.*
- **⚠ AND HŌKI TELLS HIM WHAT HE IS FOR**, plainly, in a yard, because
  Kajiya is gone and somebody has to:
  > **Every week a boy carries a box is a week four other people are on a
  > road being looked at.** *One of you being looked at very hard is
  > cheaper than five of you being looked at a bit.*
  >
  > **He is not being promoted. He is being spent, and told so, and he is
  > pleased about it**, which the reader should find frightening and he
  > does not.

### 2.2 The first one (scene 3, 9/26)

**A killing, for money, done properly, and the chapter refuses to make it
an ordeal.**

- **The target is not sympathetic and not a monster.** *A man who has been
  passing names.* **He is forty, he has a routine, and the routine is the
  whole of the job.**
- **HARD RULE: no threshold, no rage, no hesitation, and no relish.**
  *Eleven minutes of work, and eight of them are waiting.* **He does it
  with his hands and a knife because a seal leaves an article and an
  article is a disclosure** (`combat.md` §4.3).
- **⚠ AND IT IS WRITTEN, IN FULL, AT THE BRUTALIST REGISTER**
  (`method-delta.md` §2i). *This is the first killing for money in the
  book and it sets the calibration for three books.*
  > **Name the anatomy. Let it take the time it takes.** *A man does not
  > stop because the sentence has.* **And the POV leaves before he is
  > finished** — the reader stays with him and Tadayoshi does not, **and
  > that gap is the whole characterisation.**
  >
  > **Indifference is staged and never stated.** *There is no line about
  > what he felt.* **There is what he does next, and what is on his
  > hands, and the fact that he checks the window on the way past.**
- **AND THEN THE LEAVING**, which is four minutes of route, a decision
  about a lamp, and a dog — **because that is what he is good at and what
  he is being paid for**, *and putting it directly after the body is what
  makes the chapter's point without an adjective.*
- **The entry afterwards is two lines and a number**, and there is
  **nothing** in it about how it felt, **and the absence is the point and
  is never remarked on.**

### 2.3 ⚠ THE CLIENT (scene 5, 9/29 — the chapter's real subject)

**A man with money who is deciding whether to keep funding this.**

- **Somebody senior takes Tadayoshi to a house to be looked at**, because
  the client wants to see what he is buying, **and this is the part
  nobody briefed him on.**
- **He answers every question accurately.** *That is the disaster.* When
  asked whether it was clean he says **it was not clean, it was
  adequate**, and explains the four things that were wrong with it and
  what he will do differently, **in front of a man who wanted to be told
  it was clean.**
- ***"He asked me a question."*** — **and he cannot be made to see it**,
  then or ever.
- **AND THE CLIENT STAYS BOUGHT ANYWAY**, for a reason Tadayoshi
  misreads: *the man is not reassured; he is **impressed**, in the way
  people are impressed by a machine*, **and what he has actually bought is
  a story to tell.**
  > **⚠ WHICH IS THE FIRST TIME ANYBODY OUTSIDE THE REBELLION HAS
  > DESCRIBED HIM TO SOMEBODY ELSE FOR PLEASURE**, and it goes on the
  > eleven-town page under a heading that did not exist before.

### 2.4 The second and the third (scene 7, 10/2–10/9)

**Write two more at a fraction of the length**, because ch7 wrote one
contract whole so that the rest can be compressed (`ch07.md` §2) — *and
compression here is not montage: it is his ledger, and the ledger is the
scene.*

- **Both go well. He is getting faster and he can see it in the numbers.**
- **⚠ AND ON THE THIRD HE IS ASKED TO WAIT.** *The client wants it on a
  particular day for a reason that is not operational.* **He waits four
  days and does it on the day, and enters the four days as a cost, and
  does not ask what the reason was.**
  > **He has just been used as a message and does not know it.**

### 2.5 What it is doing to him (scene 9, 10/10–12)

**Not guilt. Fluency.**

- **He is better at this after three than he was after one**, and he can
  prove it, and the proving is the thing the scene is made of.
- **THE FINDING, and it should be the coldest line in act one so far:**
  > **The part that got easier was not the killing. It was the
  > afternoon.**
- **And he notices that he has stopped writing anything in the second
  book** — *the one with the column called **what it cost*** (`ch07.md`,
  7/23) — **and has not written in it since the twenty-sixth of the ninth
  month.**
  > **He does not know what that means. He writes down that he does not
  > know.**

## 6. THE FIVE HARD PROBLEMS

**1. ⚠ THE FIRST KILLING MUST NOT BE AN EVENT.** *If it is a crisis the
book has told the reader he is not this.* **He is. It is eleven minutes
and eight of them are waiting, and the scene is about the route out.**

**2. Graphic, and not cool.** *`method-delta.md` §2i: detail is not
savour.* **Name the anatomy and let the dying take its time — and give the
prose no admiration at all.** *No craft-pride in the wound, no line about
how quiet he is.* **The pride is in the leaving, which is the honest place
for it and the more damning one.**

**3. The client scene is the chapter and must not be played for comedy.**
*He is not socially awkward. He is accurate at a man who asked a question
he did not mean.* **`combat.md` §11d — he offends by accuracy, and this
is the first time it costs somebody other than him.**

**4. Compression is the ledger, never montage.** *Ch7 bought this by
writing one contract whole.* **Scene 7 is entries, dates and margins, and
the reader does the arithmetic.**

**5. The hunter sheets must stay empty and unremarked.** *The column
called **what it cost** is not written in on 26/9 and nobody says so.*
**He notices in scene 9 and cannot interpret it, and the reader can.**

## 8. REVISED ON THE PAGE

**Five changes, and one of them hands ch11 a much better job than the plan
had.**

1. **⚠ SCENE 10 IS THE MERCHANT CONTRACT, ARRIVING EARLY.** *The plan had
   scene 10 as a bigger docket and a name.* **On the page it is the
   Terasaka job — eleven acres above the second weir, a man in Ōgi who
   has wanted them for two years and pays four thousand a quarter, and a
   cargo line that says *the ground*.** *So ch11's contract is set up
   before ch11 starts, and the reader gets the shape of it a chapter
   early.*
2. **⚠ AND HE ASKS WHO IS IN THE HOUSE.** *"Him. His wife. And there's a
   boy." — "How old?" — "I don't know how old." — "Find out."* **Which
   means he does not discover the boy afterwards. He is told, before, and
   takes it anyway.**
   > **THE CH11 SHAPE THIS FORCES, and it is better than the plan's:**
   > *he prices the boy.* **He is told the boy will be at a relative's on
   > the night, and he checks it, and it is true, and he does the job on
   > the strength of it** — **and the boy comes home on the wrong day.**
   >
   > *Which is the creed's exact shape:* ***he did everything correctly
   > and it did not work.***
3. **The three-question architecture is the chapter's real find.** *Is it
   operational, does it matter, can I do it* — **three doors, and every
   job he has ever taken has answered itself on them.** *Terasaka passes
   all three and there is still something standing in the road, and he has
   no fourth question and no column for it.*
4. **The hunter file was corrected against the prose, not the plan.**
   *`ch07.md` had it started on 7/23 with a fourth invented column; the
   prose put it at Hanase on **the third of the seventh month**, on eleven
   sheets of spoiled harbour paper, with **five** columns and the **fifth**
   invented.* **And it has exactly two lines on it** — the blank one, and
   **the left-hander, 17/9** — *which is the best page he has ever made and
   the last thing on the sheets.* **Twenty-four days by scene 9, and the
   docket came three days after it**, and he writes himself an instruction
   he never obeys: *check these on the first of every month; if there is
   nothing to add, that is the entry.*
5. **PEOPLE WHO WILL TELL SOMEBODY** — *a second list, started on the
   first of the tenth month, and the first entry on it is not a place.*
   **The eleven towns are people who could describe him. This is people
   who want to.**
