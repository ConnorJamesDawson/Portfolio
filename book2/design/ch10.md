# Chapter 10 — "Enough To Live On"

**Act one. Month 10, the 1st – month 11, the end.**
**Tadayoshi 10 · Uzuki 10 · Kimimaro 12**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Scenes** | **11** |
| **Target** | ~18,400 words |
| **POV split** | **U 6 / T 3 / K 2** |
| **Weight** | **U ~56% · T ~28% · K ~15%** — `act-1.md` says U 55 / T 30 / K 15 |

**The chapter's job:** feed a starving child properly for six weeks, and
then price her twice in one room in a language she already knows, and put
her in a cellar — **and then cut to a boy who has been in one for two
years.**

---

## 1. THE ARGUMENT — somebody worked out the amount

**`act-1.md`: the act's best structural move.**

> *The snake pays well for children like her*, and she goes into a cellar
> to wait for a buyer — **and the chapter cuts to a boy who has been
> waiting for one for two years. Two children in boxes, in two countries,
> for the same customer, and neither knows the other exists.**

**And the title is the thing they have in common and neither of them can
see.**

> **Somebody, at some point, worked out how much food keeps a child alive
> without improving them, and wrote it down.**
>
> **Kimimaro works that out in scene 11 after two years. Uzuki has it
> within a week.**

### The rhyme cuts

- **Scene 8 → 9.** A man values her out loud, item by item, in front of
  her. **And Tadayoshi is moved to a class of work nobody tells him he
  has been moved to.** Two people being repriced by other people's
  arithmetic in the same week.
- **⚠ SCENE 10 → 11. THE CUT THE ACT IS BUILT ON.** She goes into a
  cellar with a date in her head. **He has been in one for two years and
  has just worked out that his bowl is a decision.**
- **And the chapter ends on him, not on her**, so the reader holds both.

## 2. UZUKI — priced twice in a language she already knows

### 2.1 The road out of Ōgi (scene 1, month 10, 1st–9th)

**Hunger, properly, for the first time.** `timeline.md`: *the road.
Hunger.*

- **She has Kiku's two openings and nothing else:** the almshouse behind
  the shrine **until the tenth month**, and a fortnight at the Kanaya
  **in the eleventh** when the road trade comes through.
- **The almshouse fails first**, and it fails administratively, which is
  the book's register: **it takes people until the tenth month and it is
  the tenth month.** Nobody is cruel. There is a date and the date has
  passed.
- **So there is a five-week hole between the two things she has**, and
  she has about a third of a ryō, and she has costed the fortnight at the
  Kanaya and she cannot reach it.
- **Write two days whole and let the rest be numbers.** Same discipline
  as ch9 scene 9.
- **And the feet get better**, which is the only good thing, **and she
  gets shoes** — badly, off a heap, wrong size, for a twentieth — and it
  is a genuinely enormous day.

### 2.2 The monastery takes her in (scene 3, month 10, ~the 10th)

**The Kadono-dera**, four days north-east of Ōgi. A working mountain
temple: eleven men, a vegetable ground, a bell, and a long low building
where people who have nowhere sleep in the winter.

> ## ⚠ IT IS A REAL MONASTERY THAT OCCASIONALLY SELLS A CHILD.
>
> **Not a front. Not a trap.** The charity and the trade live in the same
> building and do not contradict each other, **which is much worse than a
> false one**, and it is the same rule as Shimoda in ch7: **the reader
> must be relieved when she gets there.**

- **They take her in the ordinary way**, without ceremony, and the man
  who does it is not Enkai and is genuinely kind and is called **Tokuji**
  and is about sixty and does the vegetable ground.
- **They feed her and it is enough and it is regular**, and she has not
  had that since the seventeenth of the ninth month, **and she cries
  about it, once, out of sight, and is furious about that.**

### 2.3 The good weeks (scene 5, month 10, 11th–25th)

**And they are genuinely good, and the reader takes them too.**

- **Work: water, the ground, the floors, and the wood.** She is good at
  all of it and she is fast and she does not stop, **and for the first
  time since the fourth month somebody wants what she can do.**
- **⚠ AND SHE USES HER HANDS.** Tokuji's back, twice, in the dark, on
  the condition that nobody is told — **and the one genuinely valuable
  thing about her is the one thing nobody in that building ever prices**,
  because nobody sees it and she makes sure of it.
- **She starts a new bag.** A piece of sacking, four columns again, and
  the third one carries forward: **1,620 → 1,584.**

### 2.4 The rain (scene 7, month 10, the 26th)

`portraits.md`, the hair sequence: **the rain takes it out — nobody, and
an elder does the arithmetic.**

- **Two days of it, hard, and she is out in the whole of the second
  day**, because the wood has to come in and she is the one who does the
  wood.
- **It does not come out all at once and it does not run.** It stops
  being there, from the roots out, and forty hours of soaking finishes
  eleven days of growing-out, **and by the evening of the twenty-sixth
  there is about two inches of it that is not the colour it was.**
- **She knows before anybody else does**, and does nothing, because there
  is nothing to do and no bowl and no one to do it.

**⚠ ENKAI'S FIRST LINE — and it is the only thing he says in this
scene:**

> ***"How long has your family had that?"***

**A question about provenance, not about her.** The exact question a man
does an inventory with.

> **And she cannot answer it.** She has spent her whole life unable to
> say what her family was called, **and the first person who ever asks
> her about it is a man doing arithmetic.**
>
> *(`combat.md` §7b: her grandfather took Nozaki on arrival and never
> gave the old one, and nobody wrote it down.)*

### 2.5 Priced twice, in one room (scene 8, month 10, the 28th)

> ## ⚠ THE RHYME THE CHAPTER IS BUILT ON, planted in ch7 and never
> ## mentioned since.
>
> **Shimoda valued a mule out loud, item by item, in a yard, fairly, in
> front of a ten-year-old holding the halter.**
>
> **And now a man does exactly that to the ten-year-old, and she
> recognises the form before she recognises what is happening, because
> she has stood in a yard and heard this speech before.**

**The man is not Enkai and is not named** and is the kind of man who
comes to look at stock: **unhurried, courteous, a book, and he writes
after some questions and not others.**

> **The reader may notice that this is the same kind of man who came into
> a cell in ch6 and asked eleven questions.** *He is not the same man.*
> **Neither Uzuki nor the reader is ever told either way**, and the book
> does not help.

**THE TWO PRICES, and neither of them is anything she earned:**

1. **The blood.** Off the hair, off Enkai's question on the twenty-sixth,
   and off two more questions she cannot answer.
2. **And then, separately, the rest.**

> **HARD RULE** (`portraits.md`): **the narration never describes her.
> Not once.** It is conveyed **entirely through the men's behaviour and
> their language** — the order they take things in, what gets written
> down, how long a question takes to answer, and **the fact that the
> second valuation is done in the same voice as the first.**

- **`portraits.md`: one of the men in that room says it out loud.** It is
  the visitor, not Enkai:
  > ***"The snake pays well for children like her."***
  >
  > **And it means nothing to her at all.** No emphasis, no reaction, no
  > line of narration marking it. **A trade name in a book full of trade
  > names.**

**⚠ ENKAI'S SECOND LINE — and it is logistics, and it hands her the
clock:**

> ***"Not before the eleventh month. There's the road trade and I'll not
> have people through."***

**Two scenes, one line each** (`portraits.md`), **and he does more damage
than anybody in the book.**

### 2.6 The cellar (scene 10, month 11)

`timeline.md`: **Dark, and enough to live on, while somebody
negotiates.**

- **It is a root cellar under the long low building** and it is not a
  punishment and nobody is unkind. **She is fed. She is warm enough. The
  door is not always locked.**
- **And the door not always being locked is the worst detail available**,
  because it means the arrangement does not require a lock: **there is
  nowhere to go, she has no shoes that fit, it is the eleventh month, and
  she has about a third of a ryō.**
- **She counts.** Bowls, the light under the door, the bell. **And she
  works out inside a week that the amount in the bowl is the same amount
  every time**, and that somebody decided it.
- **AND SHE HAS A DATE**, which Kimimaro never gets: *not before the
  eleventh month.* **So she counts toward it and it arrives and nothing
  happens on it**, and then she has to work out what a date that passes
  means.
- **The bag, and the third column: 1,584 → 1,559.** *(And she keeps it.
  That is the thing that has not stopped.)*

## 3. TADAYOSHI — six weeks of being nothing

### 3.1 No hands (scene 2, month 10, 1st–20th)

**He said it himself on the Aku bank: *that is everything I can do.***

- **Idle for the first time in his life and it is unbearable**, and he
  cannot name what is wrong, and describes it three times as being
  *ill*, which he is not.
- **Hōki keeps him working at the only things left**: walking, reading
  ground, and being made to talk through jobs he cannot do — **and it is
  the most he has ever been taught and he cannot enjoy any of it.**
- **⚠ HE TRIES TO SEAL LEFT-HANDED**, for eleven days, in secret, badly.
  - **He can make the shapes.** What he cannot do is the placement — the
    under-hand distance is a right-handed habit two years deep.
  - **He gets it to about a fifth**: shorter, thicker, wrong grain,
    slower by half a second, **and it never gets better than that in his
    life.**
  - **And he enters it, with the number**, as a capability, **because a
    bad capability with a number on it is worth more than a good one
    without.**

### 3.2 The paper (scene 6, month 10, 21st–29th)

**A boy who cannot fight and cannot work goes back through paper**, and
what he is doing is the hunter file, not the pipeline.

> `act-1.md` §4 is explicit: **the boilerplate as a pattern, and the
> snake under any name, are not in act one at all.** So the *activity* is
> established, **every docket he has ever been given passes through his
> hands**, and **the finding is about where he has been seen.**

- **He builds a list: every place where a person alive could describe
  him**, with the date and what they would say.
- **And it is longer than he expected.** Shioiri. Ōura. Kanaya. Sunaba —
  *Ide, Nunome, and a staffing return.* Nezu — *Kihara, and a market.*
  Sakabe. Fukae — *a woman on the middle street, an innkeeper's wife, two
  carters.* Hanase — *Torii, and a keeper.* Iwae. Nunobiki. Ōtoma.
- **THE FINDING:**
  > **A description does not expire.** *"I can take a town off this list
  > when the last person in it dies. That is the only way one comes off."*
  >
  > **Eleven places, and the number only goes up**, and `combat.md` §10
  > discipline 1 — *the curve is felt as shrinking options* — **is now an
  > object he can hold.**
- **⚠ AND FUDA'S FIRST DOCKET IS IN HIS HANDS AND NOTHING IS MADE OF
  IT.** *12th. Fukae. The Kiribata, front room, before noon… No return
  required.* **He reads it, files it under Fukae, and moves on.**

### 3.3 Freight (scene 9, month 11)

**The hand comes back and it is not the same hand.**

- **The splint off; six weeks of it being no good; and a thing across the
  back that tells him when it is going to rain.** *(Hōki, 18/9: about a
  year.)*
- **The first seal after six weeks takes him four goes** and he does it
  alone and does not tell anybody.
- **AND THE WORK HE IS GIVEN IS CARRYING.**
  - **Nobody decided it.** He could not seal, so they used him for the
    thing a boy who cannot seal can do, **and then the hand came back and
    nobody revisited it**, because nobody revisits anything.
  - **He notices inside two dockets and says so, out loud, correctly, to
    the wrong person, at the wrong volume** — *`ch08.md`: he is not
    difficult because he is defiant, he is difficult because he is right*
    — **and saying so is what makes it permanent.**
- **⚠ AND KAJIYA HAS STOPPED COMING.**
  > **The man who told him what he was for has not been seen since the
  > second of the eighth month**, and Tadayoshi has no way to ask about
  > him and nobody to ask, **and he does not find out anything at all**,
  > and the reader should feel the shape of that and get nothing either.
- **The last beat:** he works out what class of work he is in and cannot
  work out how he got into it, **and enters it as an anomaly, under
  review**, which is the fourth one this year and the first he does not
  expect to close.

## 4. KIMIMARO — and the amount in the bowl

**Rules held:** the two shortest scenes in the chapter; **neither opens
with a time or a place.**

### 4.1 Three in a line (scene 4)

**The second count, running since the eighth month, and there are three
pieces on the mat now.**

- **He puts all three in a line and looks at them together**, which he
  has been waiting to do since ch8.
- **AND THE LINE SAYS THE WRONG THING.** Each one is shorter than the
  one before it and the turn near the top is worse each time. **It is not
  getting better. It is getting worse at a steady rate he can measure.**
- **So he changes the experiment**, untaught, because the first one has
  produced a bad answer and a bad answer that is *consistent* is a thing
  you test:
  > **He breaks the next one before the last has finished coming back**,
  > to find out whether the interval is the variable.
- **He does not have the word for any of this** and is doing it correctly.
  **He is twelve, in a room, with a bolt on the door, running a series.**

### 4.2 ⚠ TWO YEARS (scene 11 — THE LAST SCENE OF THE CHAPTER)

**This is the cut the act is built on and it must land on the reader with
no help from the book.**

- **It follows immediately on Uzuki going into the cellar with a date in
  her head.**
- **And what he has is a bowl.**

> **He works out — after two years, in the eleventh month, sitting on a
> mat — that the amount in the bowl is always the same amount.**
>
> Not roughly. **The same.** And a thing that is always the same is a
> thing somebody decided, **and somebody worked out, at some point, how
> much keeps a boy alive without improving him, and wrote it down, and it
> has been that number for two years.**
>
> ***Enough to live on.***

- **And the second half, which is his and not hers:**
  > **A number that has not changed in two years is a number nobody has
  > had a reason to change.**
  >
  > **Which means nobody has decided anything about him in two years.**
  >
  > **Which means he is not waiting for a buyer. He is waiting for
  > somebody to think about him.**
- **HE HAS NO DATE.** She has one and it is wrong; **he has none at all**,
  and the difference between them is the whole of the two scenes and
  neither of them will ever know the other existed until they are in the
  same house.
- **Last line: he counts the bowl.**

## 7. THE FIVE HARD PROBLEMS

**1. The monastery must be good, and stay good.** Not a front, not a
trap, not a place with a wrongness the reader can feel. **Tokuji is
kind, the food is regular, the work is real, and the charity is genuine
— and the same building sells a child.** If the reader is not relieved
when she gets there, the chapter has failed before it starts.

**2. The pricing scene must convey what it conveys without one word of
description.** `portraits.md`'s hard rule. **It is done in the men's
grammar** — the order of the questions, what gets written down, what does
not, and the fact that the second valuation is delivered in the same
voice as the first. **And Uzuki's recognition of the FORM (a mule, in a
yard, in the fifth month) is the reader's instrument, not the
narration's.**

**3. Enkai gets two lines and no explanation.** `portraits.md`. **No
speech, no justification, no moment of being human, and no cruelty
either.** *"How long has your family had that?"* and *"Not before the
eleventh month."* **That is the whole of him and it is enough.**

**4. The snake line must not be underlined.** *The snake pays well for
children like her* is said by a visitor, in passing, and means nothing to
Uzuki, and **no line of narration marks it.** `act-1.md`: the boilerplate
and the snake as a pattern are not in act one. **It is a trade name in a
book full of trade names.**

**5. Scene 11 must not explain the cut.** No line of narration links the
two cellars. **The reader does it or nobody does.** Kimimaro does not
know she exists, she does not know he exists, and the chapter ends on a
boy counting a bowl.
