## 2. HER PLAN HAS A HOLE, AND IT IS THE POINT

Tazu's argument was built for a local: *teach me and you get a chest off
your list permanently.*

**Anzai has never had her father on his list. She is four days away.**

> **She is not taking a chest off his list. She is asking a stranger for a
> present, and she has walked four days to do it, and she has been telling
> herself for eleven days that she is not.**

**He finds the hole in about four seconds and says so.** He is not unkind
about it; he is tired past unkindness. **And then he does it anyway**, for
a reason that has nothing to do with her and which she never learns:

> **He has spent six days at a tannery losing people, and he needs one
> thing this week to have been worth doing.**

**So she gets it as charity, having intended to sell it**, and she knows
the difference, and that is the wound she takes out of Kayano along with
the technique.

*(Method 46 — the secondary character gets the perception the protagonist
lacks, and it is correct.)*

---

## 3. AND SHE ALREADY HALF HAD IT

**The best thing in the chapter, and it costs one line of dialogue.**

The palm work is control: *a small amount of something exactly where you
meant it, and stopping when you said you would.* **Which is the eighth
count of the third form.**

> *"You put it in late. Not at the set. At the going. You're paying before
> you've decided."*

**Anzai notices inside ten minutes** — *somebody has taught you to hold
back* — and she says *my father*, and it goes past both of them.

**She gets it on the road home, on the second day, walking.**

> **The thing she walked four days to fetch, she was given most of by the
> man she is fetching it for, on a flat bit of ground by a barrow, a year
> ago, and neither of them knew.**
>
> **And he is dead by the time she works it out.**

## 5. KENJI — scene 4, and it is the chapter's coldest

**Kenji has already been visited.**

A minor was struck from a household and the record names **Amagiri Kenji**
as the father. So somebody came to a counter three doors up from the
bridge and asked a man of forty-five whether he had fathered a child on a
Ruri woman thirteen years ago.

**He has had the worst week of his working life for no reason he can
identify**, and he is never going to find out, and it happened because a
frightened boy looked at a wall and reached.

**And now the woman's son is standing in his shop.**

| Holds | Cannot see |
|---|---|
| **Tadayoshi** | that his brother is alive, and named this man, and why |
| **Kenji** | that the boy in front of him is the reason |

**What Kenji does: his job.**

He buys the eleven and the rest of the tin, **at a fair price, quickly**,
and does not report him, and does not help him. **Not kind, not cruel —
frightened, and correct.** He tells him to go. He locks the door after
him.

**And he says one thing that Tadayoshi files and cannot use:**

> *"I don't know what your family did."*

**Which is true, and is the whole of what the state left him with.**

*(Third instance of the standing rule: nobody in this scene does anything
wrong.)*

## 6. THE HUNTER CURVE HAS NOT STARTED — ruling, recorded here

`act-1.md` puts first hunter contact at month 7. **That stands, and here
is why it is not a hole:**

> **Nothing is hunting him in month 4.** A ten-year-old struck from a
> household is a **description on a form** — constables, a name at the
> ports, somebody asked at Ōura. **The corps does not send hunter-nin
> after a missing child.**

**The curve begins when he first makes it necessary — the genin team, in
month 5.** *(`combat.md` §10: every hunter he kills is a specification
handed to the state. The first specification does not exist yet.)*

**And he does not know that.** He behaves for a month as though he is
being pursued by people who are not looking for him, **and that is more
frightening than being hunted**, because he cannot tell the difference and
neither can the reader.

## 7. THE SEARCH BEGINS — scene 9

He has money and nowhere to be, and the only action available to him is
the only one he takes:

> **A boat with a house on it went north past the bar on the third at
> about twenty past five, and boats stop somewhere.**

**Asking at ports is a thing a boy can do.** It will get him nothing for
months. It is also the origin of everything:

> **The household that forms next year will never be described as a search
> party, and that is exactly what it will be, and it starts here, alone,
> in month 4, with one boat and a direction.** *(`structure.md`, act
> three.)*

## 8. HOME — scene 10, and the rules for it

**She comes in talking.**

Three days of rehearsing on a road, and she is through the door and four
or five sentences in — the palm, the afternoon, *watch, sit down, let me
show you* — **before she reads the room.**

**Let her get further than is comfortable.**

**Rules:**

1. **No melodrama and no speeches.** Cost it physically, per method 39.
2. **Sae does not comfort her**, because Sae has had two days and has
   nothing left, **and because Sae has to say the thing about the
   eleventh day and cannot.**
3. **The hundred comes back.** She brings it out and puts it on the table,
   unspent, exactly as instructed — *if nothing happens you bring it back
   and put it where it was* — **and it is the worst object in the room.**
4. **Nobody mentions Kayano.**

**Closing image: the rack by the back door.**

She puts her bokken back on the bottom peg. **His is still on the top
one.**

> **There are two on the rack again, and there is nobody in the house who
> is going to take either of them down.**

## 9. What must not be in chapter 4

**Any word of Tadayoshi's family reaching him.** **Any hunter.** **The
genin team.** **The creed.** **Kimimaro** — he sits this one out and
returns in ch6.

**And Uzuki must not be told about her father before she gets in the
door.** No neighbour on the road, no black cloth, no face at a window.
**She walks eleven days and finds out inside her own kitchen, mid-
sentence.**
