## 1. THE ARGUMENT — one child becomes an entry, the other stops being one

**The book's spine has been records since page one.** This is the chapter
where the two threads split on it.

> **Scene 8.** He acquires a person and files her. No name, so he gives
> her one out of the only system he has: ***the left-hander.*** **The
> hunter file gets its second line and its first human being.**
>
> **Scene 9.** She goes off every record there is. **Nobody in Nakadō
> writes her down.** Kuriya will wonder. Ine will ask. And there is no
> register anywhere in the world with her in it, and there will not be
> one for a year.

### The rhyme cuts

- **Scene 5 → 6.** He is fought, for the first time, by somebody who
  leaves. **She is come for.** *Same night, four hundred miles apart, and
  the dates are in the headers and the book does not point at it.*
- **Scene 7 → 8.** She is on a road with a knife and a flour bag and
  about half a ryō. **He sits down and works out what to call somebody.**
- **Scene 8 → 9.** ***The left-hander*** goes into a book. **And Uzuki
  goes out of all of them.**

## 2. UZUKI — the month it turns

### 2.1 The deterrent has stopped working (scene 1, month 9, 2nd–9th)

**Month 8's arithmetic is dead.** She sat up eleven nights and bent the
interval by nine days. **This month she sits up and it happens anyway.**

- She tries three nights. **It happens on the second one, with her awake
  and visible on a stool in the kitchen and the door shut behind them.**
- > **So the last variable she had is gone.** *"I was a thing that
  > slowed it down. I'm not any more, and I did not do anything
  > differently, so it isn't me that changed."*
- **And she does the sum and the sum has no use in it**: the intervals
  since the twenty-second are short and getting shorter and **the
  deterrent line in the middle of the data is now a lump she cannot
  explain**, which is what real data looks like and is no comfort at all.

**Her action:** she takes the knife out from under the mat and looks at
it **in daylight, for the first time**, and puts it back.

> **Which is the whole scene: she has stopped trying to prevent it and
> started keeping a thing under a mat.**

### 2.2 The dye, and the sentence she does not finish (scene 3, the 11th)

> **Established in ch1: the dye is done at a bowl, about every two
> months, by her father, who never said why.** `portraits.md` — *cheaply,
> at home, and it goes wrong at the roots and it goes wrong in rain.*

**Since the fourth month her mother has done it.** Which makes it **the
last physical intimacy left in that house** and the one occasion on
which neither of them can walk out of the room.

**And Sae starts a sentence and stops.**

- **It is not a confession and it is not a warning.** It is four or five
  words, and it is going somewhere, and it does not get there.
- **AND UZUKI DOES NOT FINISH IT FOR HER** — the girl who cannot stop
  saying things says nothing at all, and knows she is doing it, **and the
  reason is that if her mother says it, then it is said, and Uzuki will
  have to do something about it, and she does not know what.**
- **Both of them know what has just not happened**, and the dye goes on
  being done, and it takes about forty minutes.

> **⚠ AND IT IS THE LAST ONE.** *Nobody says so.* **From here the roots
> go, and in the tenth month it rains** (`portraits.md`, the hair
> sequence).

### 2.3 The last Kuriya, and he hands her the destination (scene 4, 14th)

**She does not know it is the last one. Neither does he.**

- **The knee. Twenty-five minutes. Three times a week since the eighth
  month, and it is the best it has been.**
- **She asks about Ine's sister** — the woman at Ōgi who has held a house
  on her own for eleven years, mentioned once, in passing, in month 8,
  *it's not the woman, it's the trade.*
- **And Kuriya gives her the name and the street**, and thinks nothing at
  all of it, and goes back to what he was doing.

> **The one adult who has helped her most hands her the place she is
> going to run to, casually, three days before she runs, in answer to a
> question about houses.**
>
> **And he will never know he did it.**

- **He pays at month end and it is the fourteenth**, so she leaves with
  nothing owing to her and nothing paid, **and about half a ryō of her
  own from the eighth month, which is the whole of the estate.**

### 2.4 THE NIGHT (scene 6, the 17th)

#### The staging, and it is the geometry of the whole act

**She has spent two months on one side of a wall, listening.**

> **Tonight the door is open and she can see.**

- **Her mother is in the kitchen. Awake. Sitting down. At the table, with
  a lamp.**
- **Not frozen mid-motion. Sitting.** Which means she got up and came out
  and sat down, **which means she knew.**
- **Uzuki looks at her, and the book gives the number, because that is
  what the book does with looking:**

> ## ⚠ FOUR SECONDS. And her mother does not move.
>
> **The reader has been trained for eight chapters that a number of
> seconds means somebody looking at Uzuki. This time the number is how
> long her mother sat still.**

#### The knife

- **It is under the mat at the top, where her hand goes when she lies on
  her right side.** Ten weeks of lying on it.
- **She never once decided what it was for and she does not decide now.**
  *(ch8: she tried to finish the sentence twice and could not.)*
- **One movement. About a second. Short, physical prose, and then it is
  over and the scene does not linger on it.**
- **The face. And the eye.**

#### ⚠ SHE DOES NOT KILL HIM — and she does not know that

> **He lives.** *(Required: `structure.md` §4b — Nakadō in act two needs
> him alive, and needs a door for her to knock on.)*
>
> **And she does not find out.** She runs, and there is no letter and no
> register and nobody to ask, **and she spends two years not knowing
> whether she killed a man**, and that is one of the reasons she goes
> back.
>
> **When she knocks on that door at eleven, she does not know who is
> going to open it.**

#### And what makes her run is not him

**She is not frightened of the man on the floor.** He is on the floor and
he is making a noise and he is not going to do anything.

> **She looks at the doorway. Her mother is still sitting down.**
>
> **That is what makes her run.** Not the four seconds — **the ones
> after.**

**And she is neither triumphant nor horrified. She is ten and she is
finished** — she has done the one thing and there was never anything
after it, because there was never a plan.

### 2.5 Seven from the mat (scene 7, the 17th–18th)

**The payoff of `ch07.md` scene 4, planted two chapters ago and never
mentioned since.**

> **Down on one knee at the corner. Two fingers. The brick out. The bag.
> Two and a half.**
>
> **The window catch is stiff and she timed that too. Four.**
>
> ***Seven from the mat.***
>
> **She timed it eleven times in the seventh month, on the second day in
> a house that was better than the one she left, and she wrote none of it
> down, and she told herself she was not planning anything and that
> everybody should know where their things are.**

**⚠ AND THE SWORD STAYS ON THE WALL.**

> It is above the door **in the back room, through the kitchen, past her
> mother, past him.** *(`combat.md` §7b — Shimoda carried it up the lane
> and hung it there himself on two nails in month 7.)*
>
> **She goes out of a window with a flour bag and a knife and leaves the
> only object in the world that says the Nozakis were ever anything.**
>
> **Which is the entire reason for Nakadō in act two**, and this chapter
> does not say so.

**What she has, counted, because she counts:** the bag with four
columns; the pig knife; about half a ryō; what she is wearing; and no
shoes on for the first two hundred yards.

**The first hours:** not the road — the road is the first place anybody
looks. **She goes the way she went to Kayano in the third month**, which
is the only route out of that valley she has ever walked alone, **and it
is the wrong way for Ōgi and she does it anyway because it is the only
one she knows in the dark.**

### 2.6 The first days (scene 9, the 18th–24th)

**Where the act's last movement starts.**

- **Ōgi, and Ine's sister, and the name Kuriya gave her.** She has four
  days of walking and about half a ryō and she has done the sums.
- **And the thing she has not costed is that she is ten.** Month 8
  established it — *it isn't the hands, it's that I'm ten* — and the road
  runs it again with no Kuriya in it to be decent.
- **Hunger starts here** (`timeline.md` month 10), and the dye starts
  going at the roots, and **she still has a knife she does not know what
  is for.**
- **And the fourth column ends**, because the thing it was counting is
  four hundred yards behind her, **and she does not rule a fifth. She
  writes on the back.**

> **THE CLOSE:** she works out, on about the fourth day, that **there is
> nobody in the world who knows where she is**, and that this is the
> first time that has ever been true, **and that it is both of the things
> it is** — and she can name both, and she writes neither down.

## 3. TADAYOSHI — the file gets a person in it

### 3.1 He takes the job he would have refused (scene 2, the 4th)

**The consequence of the twenty-ninth of the eighth month, and the book
never says so.**

- **A posting with the same shape as the Hanase one** — a route through a
  district where he is on somebody's paper. **He reads it, does the same
  cross-reference, gets the same answer, and takes it.**
- **He does not argue with himself about it.** That is the point: **there
  is no scene of him deciding.** He reads the sheet and says *right* and
  the reader has to supply the twenty-ninth.
- **And it goes perfectly.** Four days, nothing happens, the thing is
  delivered.
  > **Which is worse than it going wrong**, because now he has one data
  > point on each side and no way to weigh them, **and the only thing
  > that has actually changed is that he has stopped asking.**
- **Hōki notices and says one thing about it** and does not follow it up.

### 3.2 SAGARA NAGI (scene 5, the 17th)

**She has no name in this chapter. She is not introduced. She arrives.**

**And the design's whole requirement is that she leaves alive**
(`portraits.md`: *she survives an early encounter and comes back, and
back, better each time, because every fight she survives is a report she
wrote herself*).

**Four things must be true of the fight:**

1. **She does not beat him.** He wins the exchange.
2. **She works out, DURING, that she cannot win** — and, more
   importantly, **what to go for.** `combat.md` §4.1: *the hands. Bare
   permanently, because armoured hands cannot seal. Everybody who fights
   him twice learns to go for them.* **She learns it in the first
   fight**, in about ninety seconds, off nothing but watching.
3. **SHE BREAKS OFF.** She does enough to make pursuit expensive and then
   leaves, deliberately, while she is losing. **Nobody has ever done that
   to him.** Everyone else in that file is dead or never arrived.
   > **Which is `team.md` §11's endpoint, seeded three years early: she
   > does not get better at fighting him. She gets better at not having
   > to.**
4. **He loses something** (`combat.md` §10 discipline 3): **the use of
   his right hand for about six weeks.** No seals. **Which is the entire
   craft, gone, on the first cold night of the ninth month.**

**And the thing she takes away is not a wound. It is where he stood.**

> Hōki said it on the twentieth of the eighth month: *in about a year
> somebody will write down where you stand.*
>
> **It is twenty-eight days.**
>
> **She is on the ground he broke, and she looks at it, and she looks at
> the bank he chose, and she is still looking at it when she goes.**

### 3.3 The left-hander (scene 8, the 18th)

**He cannot use his hand, so he writes with the other one and it is bad,
and he writes it anyway.**

- **Five columns and this time four of them have something in them.**
  > *17/9. — . Came off the west side into ground I had opened. Left
  > hand. Went for my hands inside two minutes and had it right by the
  > third. Broke off at about four minutes with the exchange going her
  > way, on purpose, and went north.*
  >
  > ***What it cost: the right hand, about six weeks.***
- **AND THE NAMING**, which is the scene's real business:
  > **He has no name for her and no system for naming people**, and the
  > column will not take a blank twice, **so he writes what he has.**
  >
  > ***The left-hander.***
  >
  > `portraits.md`: *he names her before he knows her name… and stays
  > that for two years. Then he learns her name, and something is spoiled
  > that he cannot put back — because a designation can be filed and a
  > person cannot.*
- **And the finding**, which he does not enjoy having:
  > **She left. Which means she is coming back. Which means the next one
  > is her, and the next one after that, and she will know more each
  > time, and every single thing she knows she will have got off me.**
  >
  > **I am the syllabus.**

## 7. THE FIVE HARD PROBLEMS

**1. The night must be written from where she is and no further.** Same
rule as ch7 scene 8 and for the same reason: **the limit is her position,
not authorial flinching.** She is in her own room; the door is open; she
can see the kitchen. **What is on the page is what a girl on a mat can
see and hear, and it is not softened, and it is not extended.**

**2. The knife takes one second and the prose does not admire it.** No
choreography, no reversal, no beat where she is capable. **She is
frightened, fast, close, and has been lying on it for ten weeks.**
`team.md` §4.4's hard rule: *this is not a combat feat and must never be
retconned into one.*

**3. Sae must be culpable here and must not become a cartoon.** She is
sitting down at a table with a lamp because she got up and came out and
sat down. **That is the whole of the case and it is enough.** No speech,
no plea, no explanation — **and no cruelty either. She does not say
anything at all**, and the absence is the evidence.

**4. Nagi must not be a rival.** No banter, no mutual respect on the
page, no name. **She is a professional doing a job who works out
something true in ninety seconds and then declines to die for it.** The
respect is entirely on his side, in a book, afterwards.

**5. Scene 9 must not be a montage.** Six days on a road is the shape
that kills chapters. **Write two of them whole and let the rest be the
numbers she keeps** — and the numbers are the point, because the last
thing she does in this chapter is find out that nobody has one for her.

## 7b. REVISED ON THE PAGE

**1. The deterrent was never a deterrent.** Planned as *it stops
working.* Written: **they go into their own room and shut the door with
her four feet away on a stool with a lamp on** — so the eighth month's
finding is retrospectively demolished.
> **I was not a reason. I was a nuisance. And a nuisance is a thing you
> go round.**

**2. Oiling the knife is the moment it becomes hers.** Not planned. She
takes an oiled knife out of a drawer, lets it go for a fortnight, and
then has to decide: *if I oil it, then it's mine, and a thing you look
after is a thing you're going to have for a while, and a fortnight ago I
could still tell myself I was carrying it.*

**3. His change shows as a blank in a ledger.** The plan said *no scene
of him deciding.* The page found the better version: **he opens the book
to do the day and there is nothing to write**, and he has the sentence —
*I did not object this time* — and shuts the book. **A moral change
rendered as a missing entry, in a book of findings, by a boy who has
never had trouble writing one down.**

**4. Three endings, not one.** The plan had Sae's unfinished sentence
land. Written: **Uzuki gets three candidate endings inside four seconds
and cannot choose**, and that is why she says nothing — *"whichever one
it is, once it is said, it is said, and then I have got to do something
about it, and I do not know what I would do."* **And half of not making
her finish it is for herself, and she says so.**

**5. The name went the wrong way.** The biggest addition. Kuriya's gift
turns out to be worthless at the door: ***"I don't know him."*** **Ine
told Kuriya about Kiku; Kuriya told Uzuki; Kiku has never heard of
Kuriya.** *One person says your name to somebody else — and what she has
is somebody saying HER name to ME.* **She walked five days on a piece of
gossip going backwards.**

**6. Ōgi fails inside chapter 9.** The plan left her still walking. **She
gets there, is refused correctly and kindly, and is given the only two
openings she will have** — the almshouse until the tenth month and a
fortnight at the Kanaya in the eleventh. **Which hands ch10 a girl who
arrives at a monastery because everything else is exhausted**, rather
than one who wanders into it.

**7. Scenes 6 and 7 redistributed.** *Seven from the mat* and the window
came inside the night scene, so scene 7 is the road and the inventory
instead. **The payoff lands three lines after her mother does not stand
up, which is where it belongs.**

**8. He reads his own left hand as a stranger's.** Not planned. Forty
minutes to write one entry, and then: *there's no habit in it anywhere…
that's a man who has never done this before and is not going to get
better at it and knows it.* — ***"That's me."***

**9. And Hōki says the thing the whole book has been waiting for.**
*"Then there's four minutes of you on a sheet in Kiri by the end of the
month, and it'll be right, because she was there."* Which detonates ch6's
market — *the wrong description was protective and it had a date on it* —
**and the date was four months.**
