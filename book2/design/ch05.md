# Chapter 5 — "Alive If Possible"

**Act one. Month 4, 9th – month 5, end.**
**Tadayoshi 10 · Uzuki 10**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Scenes** | 10 |
| **Target** | ~16,700 words |
| **POV split** | **T 8 / U 2** |
| **Weight** | **T ~84% · U ~16%** |

**The chapter's job:** make his own method the thing that finds him, put
four children in front of him under an order that kills them, **break the
page where the threshold starts**, and give him his mother back off a body.

---

## 1. THE STRUCTURAL SIGNATURE — the gap is a scene

The standing rule (`method-delta.md` §3): **write up to it, break, resume
after, and let the reconstruction be the scene.**

> **Scene 7 ends mid-action. Scene 8 is Uzuki, in Nakadō, doing sums.
> Scene 9 opens afterwards, with four bodies.**

**The reader is thrown out of the fight into a different life entirely and
comes back to the end of it.** The gap is not a line break and not a row
of asterisks — **it is 1,350 words of somebody else's month**, and the
reader experiences the absence as an absence.

**This is the chapter's whole formal argument and it should not be
repeated later in the book.** It is the first time; it earns being
conspicuous exactly once.

---

## 2. HIS METHOD IS WHAT FINDS HIM

The anti-widening rule, arriving a chapter before the hunter curve does:
**he gets better and the danger goes up, mechanically, with no thumb on
the scale.**

### The book at Sunaba is a DUES book, and what he wants is exempt

His first real application of *find where it is written down* fails for a
structural reason he could not have predicted and would not have been
able to guess:

> **State movements do not pay dues.**

The harbour ledger at Sunaba records craft, master, cargo and dues — and a
government charter goes in as **exempt**, with no master, no cargo, no
destination. **A tally mark and a date.**

**What he gets, entire: three exemptions that month, one of them on the
third.**

That is all. It confirms the boat existed and went somewhere and stops.

> **And it is the most valuable thing he learns all year**, because it
> tells him that the category of thing he is hunting is **the category
> that is not written down in the books he can reach** — and he will spend
> the next four years learning which books have which holes, and that is
> the career.

### He gets into the building by working in it

**A boy who wants a book gets a job in the room the book is in.** Two and
a half weeks sweeping and running messages at the Sunaba harbour office
for board and about nine a week.

**Which is a scene, not a montage** — see §4, scene 3.

### And a records man notices a records habit

**Somebody notices which pages he has been in.**

Not what he asked. **Which pages.** A clerk who has kept a ledger for
eleven years knows the exact feel of his own book, and a boy who keeps
going back to the exempt column is a boy about whom a sentence gets said
to somebody.

**It rhymes with Sakuma and it is meant to** — the same competence, in a
smaller room, pointed at him instead of at his family.

**He runs. There is a description within four days.**

---

## 3. THE ORDER — and it is the indictment

**He is not a fugitive. He is an ASSET.**

`falsified.md` #8 and `ch02.md`: he is an **undeclared producer** on a
schedule, which is the whole reason the household was taken. **The state
does not want him dead. It wants him at a bench.**

So the tasking is **retrieval**:

> **A ten-year-old. Non-combatant. No training on file. Take him alive if
> possible.**

**Every word of it is accurate.** And the four children sent to do it are
freshly graduated, have never taken a life, and are working under a
restriction he does not have.

> **The order is correct about who he is and wrong about what he is, and
> that is what kills them.**
>
> **Whoever wrote *alive if possible* killed that team.** Tadayoshi is the
> instrument.

**And it is not spite. It is a correct assessment of a boy who has never
been in a fight, made from a file, by somebody doing their job.** Fourth
instance of the standing rule.

## 5. THE FIGHT — what he actually does, and the discipline

`combat.md` §11 is binding: **his ceiling is technique, his floor is a
child's body, and any fight he wins by being stronger is written wrong.**

**He has never been in a fight in his life.** He is a maker.

### What he has

- **Four seals, about a second and a quarter**, and two years of laying
  nine a day.
- **A drop is a tapered point.** That is the whole of his armoury: the
  object he has made four hundred of, which comes out of grit in about the
  time it takes to think about it, **and which he can put where he likes
  because he has spent two years putting them exactly where he liked.**
- **Grit, in his mother's tin.** *(The tin is empty since Ōura. This is
  what it is for now, and he filled it without deciding to.)*
- **And the fact that they are being careful with him.**

### The first half — he does not fight them, he makes the ground wrong

**Crowd control in its most primitive possible form**, arrived at by a
child with no doctrine: **obstruct, separate, and make the footing
unusable.** He is not trying to hurt anybody. He is trying to make four
people into one person at a time, which is the only arithmetic that gives
him anything.

**And the reason it works for six minutes is the order.** They will not
commit. They keep leaving themselves an exit because they are trying to
close a hand on a child.

### THE TURN — the restriction is his whole advantage and it ends

> **The moment they stop trying to take him alive is the moment he is
> losing.**

One of them is hurt badly enough that the calculus changes — **and it
should be an accident of his making rather than an intention**, because he
does not have intentions in a fight yet.

**And then he is a ten-year-old against three trained children, and it
goes exactly the way that goes, for about forty seconds.**

**He is losing. He is on the ground. He is going to be taken.**

**Then scene 7, and then the break.**

## 6. WHAT COMES OUT OF THE GAP — the mandatory shape

`combat.md` §9 and `falsified.md` #1:

1. **The rage does not save him.** *(He was going to be taken alive, not
   killed. So the threshold does not rescue him from death — it converts
   a capture into four corpses. **The outcome is strictly worse than
   losing.**)*
2. **It does not stop when the fight does.** The one who has dropped the
   weapon. The one crawling. The one calling a name.
3. **He cannot reconstruct which of them he killed while he was still
   thinking**, and there is one he cannot place at all.
4. **It is never written from inside.**

### The reconstruction is the scene, and he does it with his own method

He reads the ground the way he read Shioiri: positions, distances, what
happened in what order, timed off his own body.

**And it fails him in a new way.** At Shioiri the problem was that he could
not tell whether he had measured a procedure or his mother. **Here the
problem is that the evidence is his own hands** — and he gets a clean
sequence out of it for three of them, and the fourth will not sit down.

> **He can prove he killed three of them after the fight was over.**
>
> **He cannot prove anything about the first one, and the first one is the
> only one that would tell him whether he meant it.**

**That is the question the whole book asks, arriving at ten, unanswerable
by the only instrument he owns.**

## 7. THE CLASP — and the mechanism is airtight

### How it reaches him: the TIC

`portraits.md`: *he tests edges — a thumb along a rim, constantly, without
noticing. His hands check by habit.*

> **Mid-threshold, his hand closes on a fitting and his thumb goes up it,
> because his hands do that without him.**
>
> **The tic is what stops it.** Not exhaustion, not conscience — **an
> involuntary habit of the hands, which are the only part of him still
> working.**

*(And it is the same behaviour that is his unreliable threshold tell.
The gesture that predicts it badly is the gesture that ends it.)*

### How he knows it is hers

**He knows her hand better than anyone alive, because he spent two years
counterfeiting it.** Ch1: *in the lamp they were the same object twice,
and he could not afterwards have said which was which.*

### How he knows she is alive NOW — and this is two chapters of groundwork

> **The family has never sold to Kiri. They sell to a shop.**
> *(ch1, Tadaharu; ch2, the trade chain; `world.md`.)*

**A Kiri-issue fitting with his mother's hand in it cannot have come
through Kanaya.** So she did not make it at Shioiri, and it did not travel
by the only road her work has ever travelled.

**She made it somewhere else.**

**And the kit is new**, because fresh graduates get new kit. **So she has
been working for weeks.**

> **He learns his mother is alive from her own work, on the body of a
> child he has just killed** — and he learns it by the one method nobody
> could have taken from him.

**He takes the clasp.**

---

## 8. AND THE LEDGER STARTS WORKING AGAIN

Ch3's failure, resolved, and it is the chapter's last beat.

> *Because a finding is for somebody… I have been writing a report for
> three weeks. And the person a report is for is not there.*

**She is there.**

**The ledger works again the moment he has somebody to hand it to** — and
he will never say so, and it explains the rest of his life:

> **The books are for her.** Every column, every narrow fourth, every
> dated finding, for the next three years — **a report being compiled for
> a woman in a room with a bench, against the day he gets to give it to
> her.**

**Closing image: the first entry he has been able to write since the third
of the fourth month.** It should be short, it should be dated, and it
should not mention the four children at all.

## 9. UZUKI — two scenes, and the second one is the gap

### Scene 2 — *ask me tomorrow*

She asks. **And the honest answer is worse than a lie.**

**Sae was never going to come.** Not on the eleventh day, not on the
twelfth. She had worked it out before the death: **there was no money to
go and fetch her with**, and a woman who walks to Ōgi and stands in the
road has walked to Ōgi and stood in a road.

> **She said it in front of him because it was the kind of thing you say
> in front of a man who is being told he can let his daughter go.**

**And she does not apologise for it**, because Sae does not, and because
she is right, and because she has known for eleven days that she was going
to have to say it.

### Scene 8 — the sums, and ninety a day on nobody

**The gap scene.** It must be quiet, domestic, and completely absorbing,
because it is doing structural work the reader must not notice.

- **The debt, and the arithmetic** — a civilian widow with no income,
  doing it at a table, and now Uzuki *can* read it, because she has been
  doing sums for a month. **That is the growth and it is not a comfort.**
- **The mill's work is gone.** Iwasa's boy has it. Nobody is going to give
  a carrying round to a widow and a girl of ten.
- **And the ninety.** Every day, on her own forearm, at the same time,
  written down: *90. Own left arm. 31 good.*

> **She learned it for one man and he is dead, and she does it anyway,
> every day, on nobody.**

**Closing image: the number, and what it has climbed to, and what it is
for.**

## 11. Open before drafting

1. **How many of the four die?** **Recommend all four**, per
   `structure.md` (*four dead*), and recommend that **one of them is hurt
   and not killed in the first half** so the reader knows exactly what
   changed.
2. **Does he bury them?** **Recommend no** — he takes the clasp and goes,
   and it costs him for years, and *somebody will have to go down and shut
   the doors* is the line that comes back at him and does not get obeyed
   this time.
3. **Where is the fight?** It needs ground with mineral in it — a road, a
   shore, a quarry cut. **Recommend a dry riverbed**, which gives him grit
   in unlimited quantity and gives them clean sightlines, so the terrain
   favours them and the material favours him.
