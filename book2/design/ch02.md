# Chapter 2 — "One Declared"

**Act one, month 3. Year 0, ~3 pre-P1.**
**Tadayoshi 10 · Uzuki 10 · Iwao 13**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Scenes** | 9 — **WRITTEN, 15,230 words** |
| **Target** | ~16,400 words |
| **POV split** | T 4 / U 4 / **Sakuma 1** |
| **Weight** | T ~47% · U ~43% · S ~10% |

**The chapter's job:** two children make a proposal in the same month.
**One of them is refused and stays. One is permitted and goes.** And in
the middle of it, in an office neither of them will ever see, a rule
fires.

---

## 1. The decision this chapter turns on

The author has ruled that **the reader witnesses the collection order.** So
the question is whose eyes, and the answer produces the best structural
move available in act one.

> **Give it to a records officer. And give him Tadayoshi's grammar.**

Ruled columns. Dated findings. Numbers, checked twice, entered narrow.

**The reader has spent an entire chapter learning to love that voice** —
learning it off an old woman with ruined hands and a boy who ruled a
fourth column because that is how it is done — **and chapter two hands it
to the man who kills the family.**

He is not cruel. **He is good at his job.** He finds a discrepancy and
closes it, and is quietly pleased, and goes home and eats.

**And he never finds out what happened**, because it went up and came back
signed and the next file was underneath it.

---

## 2. WHAT THE ORDER ACTUALLY SAYS — and it changes the whole tragedy

The finding is not *this family is producing more.*

> **The finding is: ONE DECLARED PRACTITIONER. TWO HANDS IN THE WORK.**

**Standing return on registered bloodline households:** declared
practitioners, and output, annually. The rule fires when output moves more
than a third against an unchanged head count.

- **The Ruri declare one.** Mikage. It has said one for eleven years.
- **Output is up about two thirds over two years.**
- **Bloodline children are required to be presented for assessment.**
  Tadayoshi was not. He has been working for two years.

**So the crime is not the gift. The crime is the concealment.**

> **If they had declared him, he would have been taken and the rest of
> them would have lived.**
>
> **Hiding him is what turned a collection into a raid.**

**Which means his mother's instruction — given out of love, on the day he
was proudest of anything — is the thing that killed her family.** She will
never know. He will read the order in the last act and understand exactly
what the word *undeclared* is doing in it.

*(And it keeps §3f ruling 3 intact and makes it richer: the improved yield
did put them on the list, and Tadayoshi is right about that, and he is
never going to learn that **Akagane's frame is about to add the second
half** — a grade uptick arriving a few months later that accelerates the
timetable. Both cousins contributed. Neither knew. One of them is dead
before it lands.)*

- **4 → 5.** A child's costed proposal refused at a kitchen table, then a

- **8 → 9.** **He is refused and stays in the house. She is permitted and

## 4. The scenes

### 1 · T — Three of them and a tin

**Want:** to be told what the new grain does at the counter.
**Obstacle:** it is a day and a half of walking with his family.
**Turn:** he watches Iwao work on their father for two hours and get
something out of him, and cannot identify a single move that was made.

The road puts the trade chain on the page as a place rather than a
sentence: the mule, the tin, the ledger of what is in it, and **a father
who has walked this road four hundred times and has never once asked what
happens at the far end of it.**

**And Iwao is good company.** That is the scene's real job. Fifteen miles
of a thirteen-year-old being funny, and everybody liking it, including the
reader.

**Closing image:** Ōura, at the bottom of the last hill, from above.

---

### 2 · U — What exactly can be taught

**Want:** to find out whether Tazu meant it.
**Obstacle:** he did mean it, and it was a throwaway line, and he has not
thought about it since.
**Turn:** he thinks about it properly, because a ten-year-old asked him a
precise question, and gives her more than he gave the adults.

**She asks what the adults did not:** not *can it be taught*, but **how
long, by whom, and what exactly would I be able to do afterwards.**

Tazu is honest, which is his whole function: the palm work is control and
practice, an afternoon gets you the bones, and **you would be able to do
the nights and not the thing.** He also says the part that matters —
**that a medic who is busy will teach faster than one who is not**, because
teaching is quicker than treating and they know it.

**This is where the plan becomes a plan.** She goes in with a question and
comes out with a schedule.

**Closing image:** something in his hand that he does not give her.

---

### 3 · T — The counter

**Want:** to see what Kanaya's man does with the new grain.
**Obstacle:** Kenji has decided to grade the whole tin down, for reasons
that have nothing to do with the tin.
**Turn:** **Iwao gets it back up** — not by arguing, by making Kenji like
him — and the family goes home with more money than the work was worth.

**Kenji on the page, properly, so that ch3's name is a person and not a
joke.** Middle-aged, careful, professionally sour, and vain in one small
identifiable way that Iwao locates in about ninety seconds and uses.

**And this is the scene that earns the tribunal.** The reader must watch
Iwao read a room and win it, and enjoy watching it, so that when a
frightened boy of thirteen talks his way out of a room a month later it is
not a plot convenience — **it is the thing he has always been able to
do.**

> **Tadayoshi watches the whole thing and cannot reconstruct it
> afterwards.** He can take a kata apart in forty seconds. He watches his
> brother do this and gets **nothing** — no sequence, no method, no part
> doing no work. It is the first thing in his life he has been unable to
> take apart.

**Closing image:** the money, and what Iwao does with the extra.

---

### 4 · U — The proposal

**Want:** seven days.
**Obstacle:** she is ten, and her mother has a number in her head that
Uzuki does not know about.
**Turn:** refused — and refused so fast that she understands, for the
first time, that the refusal is not about the road.

**She brings a sum.** That is the growth from ch1: she could not read her
mother's arithmetic and now she has done her own, out loud, at the table.
Three days out, an afternoon, three days back. **No fee, because she is
not buying a medic, she is buying an afternoon.** Food for seven days.
What it saves.

**And Sae says no in about four seconds**, which is the tell. *(The reader
knows what is behind the tin. Uzuki does not.)*

**Closing image:** the shelf above the stove.

---

### 5 · S — One declared

**~1,700 words. The only scene in the chapter with no child in it.**

**Want:** to clear the standing return before the quarter closes.
**Obstacle:** one file does not reconcile.
**Turn:** it reconciles perfectly, and the answer is a person.

**His grammar is Tadayoshi's grammar**, and it should be close enough to
be uncomfortable: ruled columns, a narrow one on the right, dated
findings, numbers checked twice because checking twice is what you do.

The mechanics, on the page, without a word of exposition:

- **Declared practitioners: one.** Eleven years of *one*.
- **Output: up about two thirds in two years.**
- **The rule: more than a third against an unchanged head count.**
- **And the presentation requirement**, which he looks up, because he is
  thorough, and which has not been complied with.

**He is pleased.** Not gleeful — *professionally satisfied*, the way a
person is when a number that would not sit down finally sits down. He has
found an undeclared producer in a household that has been declaring one
for eleven years, and that is a good afternoon's work.

**He recommends. He does not sign.** It goes up. It comes back four days
later with a signature that is not his, and a date, and a schedule.

**And then the next file is underneath it.**

**Closing image:** what he has for his supper.

**Not in this scene:** any name the reader recognises except *Ruri*. No
malice. No hesitation. **Nobody in this scene does anything wrong.**

---

### 6 · T — The case

**Want:** to write down something he intends to say.
**Obstacle:** he has never had to make an argument to a person before —
only to a page.
**Turn:** he finishes it, and it is good, and he cannot find the flaw in
it, and that is what frightens him.

**The ledger, four days after the new frame.** Cost: four days, entered.
Benefit: Akagane has evened the feed from the outside; Tadayoshi can even
it from the inside; **put the two together in Mikage's hands and the
family's grade goes from a low three to the top of the scale
permanently.**

He costs it. Fourteen and a half becomes a number he can write down.
Hisui's Ōura rent becomes a thing that could be paid.

**And he writes the objection in himself, in the narrow column**, because
that is what the narrow column is for:

> *If it works, we produce more. She said people who are worth something
> get collected.*

**REVISED ON THE PAGE — and it is better.** He answers it, and **the
answer is right.** He reasons his way to *declare it openly, on a day of
our own choosing* — which is the actual mechanism, arrived at from first
principles, at ten.

**The wrongness is not in the logic. It is in the clock.** He does not
know there was a date, and that it was in the eighth month of the year he
was eight, and that it has passed. **His mother names it in scene 8:
*you're not declaring, you're confessing.*** 

**Closing image:** the page, ruled, finished, and turned face down.

---

### 7 · U — Yes

**Want:** her father on her side.
**Obstacle:** he agrees with her and cannot say so in front of her mother.
**Turn:** he says it anyway, and it costs him — **because saying *let her
go* means saying out loud what he is going to need.**

**This is the scene that makes him a man and not a symptom.** He has spent
two chapters mending things and making jokes. Here he says a true thing at
full price, in front of his wife, about his own body.

**And Sae's objection is the correct one** and she is not the villain of
it: a ten-year-old, seven days, a road, and no money at the end of it
anyway. *(And behind the tin, a hundred where two hundred and forty should
be — which she still does not say.)*

**Give the best line to whoever is right.** They are both right. It costs
them both.

**Closing image:** the rack by the back door, and which peg is empty.

---

### 8 · T — Nobody sees this

**Want:** to be allowed to give the family the thing he has.
**Obstacle:** a rule with no reasoning in it.
**Turn:** he asks properly, with a case, like an adult — **and gets a
fragment more than he got at eight, and the fragment makes it worse.**

He puts it to her the only way he knows: **a proposal, in order, costed,
with the objection already written into it.**

And Mikage — who is not stupid, who has heard him out, who can see the
arithmetic — says no.

**What she gives him this time:** not the whole thing. A piece.
Something on the order of *they came for him in the daytime, and everyone
watched, and nobody in this family has ever been able to say whether we
could have stopped it.* **Enough to be a wound and not enough to be a
reason.**

> **The third unexplained rule of the chapter, from the third person who
> loves him.**

**And the reader is holding scene 5.** They know that the concealment is
the charge; that the order is already signed; that the woman refusing is
refusing on the grounds that producing more gets you collected, and that
she is **completely correct and eleven years too late.**

**The last exchange should be small and should not be about crystal.**

**Closing image:** something of hers he is holding when she says it.

---

### 9 · U — Before light

**Want:** to get out of the house without waking anybody.
**Obstacle:** her mother is already up.
**Turn:** her mother lets her go — and gives her something, and it is the
wrong thing, and it is everything she has.

**No speech.** This scene runs on objects: what goes in the bag, what does
not, what is on the shelf above the stove, and what her mother puts into
her hand at the door without saying where it came from.

**And Uzuki takes it and does not ask.**

**Closing image:** the road, and the count she starts.

---

## 5. What must not be in chapter 2

**The word *warrant*.** **Any Ruri knowing anything.** **Kimimaro** —
per `act-1.md`, he sits out this chapter entirely and returns in ch3.
**Any hint that the order exists, on the Shioiri side, in any scene.**

**And the rule from ch1 still holds: no foreshadowing.** Scene 5 is not a
warning to the characters. **It is a fact given to the reader and to
nobody else**, and every scene after it must be written as though it did
not happen, because for the people in them it has not.
