# The canon cast — who exists in the world, and how they may be used

> **AUTHOR:** *"There is a thing about side characters — there's no mention
> of them. You have an entire anime to choose from: fleeting scenes or
> descriptions, conversations with characters of the anime. Sprinkle them
> through the act. Tadayoshi and Uzuki will be travelling a lot once Kimi
> comes into things."*

**Correct, and it is an omission.** *Twelve chapters of act one contain not
one canon figure and the world reads smaller for it.* **Act two is a year
on a road across three countries and it is where this gets fixed.**

## 1. THE FIVE RULES, and the first one does all the work

### 1.1 ⚠ THEY DO NOT KNOW WHO ANYBODY IS

**Tadayoshi is a warranted eleven-year-old out of a Kiri compound whose
education stopped at ten.** *He has not heard of the Sannin. He does not
know what a red cloud means. He could not name the Five Kage.*

> **So canon figures arrive unnamed, and the reader does the
> recognising.** **That is free dramatic irony and it costs the scene
> nothing** — *and it is the book's own engine, because what he does with
> a stranger is read them off their work.*
>
> **He does not get a name. He gets a stance, a kit, a pair of hands, and
> a price.** *And the reader gets a name and cannot tell him.*

### 1.2 The scene is about the protagonists or it does not happen

**A canon figure may be a mirror, an obstacle, a price, or a piece of
information. Never a guest star.** *If the scene would be diminished by
replacing them with an unnamed stranger, it is fan service and it comes
out.*

### 1.3 No plot debt

**A fleeting encounter must not require the person to come back.** *If it
pays off, it pays off books later and by implication, and the book
survives the payoff never arriving.*

### 1.4 Canon is not bent to fit

**If a person's age or location does not work in this era, they do not
appear.** *There is no version of this where the timeline is adjusted for
a cameo.*

### 1.5 Sparingly, and mostly as description

**At most one per chapter, and most of them are two lines in passing** —
*a figure on a road, a name overheard in a kitchen, a coat with a pattern
on it.* **The rate at which this gets tiresome is much lower than it
feels while writing.**

## 3. THE ROSTER — PROPOSED, and every date in it needs checking (§0)

### 3.1 ⚠ MEI TERUMĪ — not a cameo
*A slot that already exists in the design.*

**`act-2.md` §5's one-off POV slot is currently unallocated and reads:**
*the section of the resistance doing the arithmetic that ends him — one
scene, once, in which nobody is angry.*

> **That slot is hers.** *She leads the rebel faction he has been working
> for since the sixth month of year 0. She is the person the arithmetic
> belongs to. And she is going to win this war and run this country.*
>
> **⚠ AND SHE IS RIGHT**, which is the whole point of the scene: *a losing
> rebellion cannot afford a boy who draws hunters and a Kaguya who draws
> worse, and letting them go is the correct decision, made by a competent
> person, without malice, in about four minutes.*
>
> **He never learns her name.** *He gets a decision, delivered through
> Hōki, and the reader gets the room it was made in.*

### 3.2 Itachi and Kisame, seen and not named

**Two figures on a road, going the other way, and Tadayoshi reads them and
declines** — *the first time in the book he has refused a thing on
assessment alone, without a fee or a docket in it.*

- **It is a character beat and not a sighting.** *He has spent a year
  learning to price people and he has just met something he cannot price,
  and the honest entry is that he went round.*
- **And it feeds the ramp** (`act-2.md` §2d): *he wants to know what that
  was, and knowing costs money he has not got.*

### 3.3 ⚠ UTAKATA — the mirror, and he is the best one available

**A young man travelling alone, hunted by his own village, some years
further into it than Tadayoshi is.** *One fire, one night, nobody named.*

> **The thing that makes it land is that he is worse off.** *Older,
> better, and more alone* — **and Uzuki works out inside ten minutes what
> he is and does not say it, and Tadayoshi does not work it out at all.**
>
> **A substance chapter** (`method-delta.md` §2l) *and a look at the far
> end of the road they are on.*

### 3.4 Jiraiya — and the danger is that he notices the hair

**A traveller who talks to everybody and notices everything, in a country
he has no business being in.** *He knew the Uzumaki. He would know what
five inches of that colour on a child in a hat means.*

- **⚠ KEEP IT FLEETING AND KEEP THE PLOT DEBT AT ZERO** (§1.3). *He sees,
  he asks one question that neither child understands, he pays for a meal,
  and he goes.* **The reader's stomach drops and nothing happens.**
- **Ruling wanted before it is written**, because *a man who knows there is
  a live Uzumaki* is a large object to leave lying about.

### 3.5 Tsunade — a near miss, and it plants the act-three ask

**`structure.md` §5 already has the bet, later.** *So act two must not
meet her.*

> **Instead: Uzuki is trying to buy medical knowledge** (`act-2.md` §2d,
> her side of the ramp) **and is told there is one person alive who could
> teach her that and nobody knows where she is.** *She writes the name
> down.* **That is the whole of it and it costs one exchange.**

### 3.6 Ao, or a hunter with something wrong about one eye

**Seen once, at distance, and entered on the sheets.** *A plant for a
later book at a cost of two lines, and it does not need to be him.*

### 3.7 Places, which are better value than people

- **⚠ YUGAKURE, or a hot-spring village that has given up being shinobi.**
  *Uzuki is from the Land of Hot Water; this is her country.* **A village
  that quit is a mirror for a boy who cannot stop, and Tadayoshi cannot
  understand it at all** — *he goes at it for two days and gets nowhere,
  because there is no arithmetic in which quitting comes out.*
- **The Land of Waves, before it goes bad.** *No bridge started, a town
  that is doing all right,* **and a local man everybody talks about who
  once stood in a river.** *(Kaiza.)* **Tadayoshi files it as *a man who
  did a thing that did not pay*** — **and `structure.md` §6 walks him past
  those graves in act four.**
- **Tanzaku, or a gambling town**, *for one line about a woman who loses
  enormously and keeps playing.*

## 5. ⚠ THE FAILURE MODE, NAMED

**The scene where the canon figure is recognisable, does the thing they
are known for, and leaves.** *It stops the book, it flatters the reader,
and it teaches them that this world is a set of references rather than a
place.*

> **The test:** *cover the name and read the scene.* **If it still works,
> it stays. If it only worked because of who it was, it was never a
> scene.**
