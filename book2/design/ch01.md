# Chapter 1 — "Two Courses"

**Act one, months 1–2. Year 0, ~3 pre-P1.**
**Tadayoshi 10 · Uzuki 10 · Kimimaro 12 · Iwao 13**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Scenes** | 10 |
| **Target** | ~16,800 words |
| **POV split** | T 5 scenes / U 4 / K 1 |
| **Weight** | T ~57% · U ~36% · K ~7% |

**The chapter's job:** make the compound worth losing, put three interior
grammars on the page in the first half, and end on the first withheld
sentence in the book.

---

## Three rulings this chapter needs, made here

1. **He is still at the Academy** in months 1–3. The transfer put him in a
   class a year above, where he is **the youngest and the one nobody sits
   next to**, and both facts are on the same form.
2. **Uzuki is inland, in the Land of Hot Water.** Unremarkable, days from
   the coast, and far enough that the threads cannot cross before month 12.
   A punishment posting *should* be a long walk. *(Swappable — it only has
   to be far.)*
3. **Mikage gets one exchange that is not about hiding**, or she becomes an
   instruction rather than a mother, and three years of reading her tone
   will have nothing to stand on.

## The scenes

### 1 · T — Two courses

**Want:** finish the piece before his mother comes in.
**Obstacle:** it has to be *worse* than he can make it, and worse is harder
than better.
**Turn:** it comes out perfect — indistinguishable from her work — and he is
not pleased.

He lays two courses where he could lay six. **He has to count down to a bad
piece**, and the counting is the reader's first lesson in the craft: one
course is one note, two is commercial grade, and enough of them and a piece
has nothing to give.

**Establishes, all inside the action:** courses and the note; the seals and
their cast time; **the hands**; the cold; and — stated plainly, not revealed
— **that he fixed the tone two years ago and was told to hide it.**

> **THE PLANT, and it is the largest in the chapter.**
>
> **He has taught himself to imitate his mother's hand.** Flat, thin, two
> courses, level. It is how he hides. He is ten and he is good at it.
>
> **At twelve he will be fooled for a year by exactly this trick, performed
> by somebody else — and he was the first person to do it.**

**Closing image:** the finished piece next to one of hers, and he cannot
tell them apart either.

*(Mikage should arrive at the end of this scene or the start of scene 3 and
say something to him that is not an instruction. One exchange. It is the
whole foundation of her.)*

---

### 2 · U — The dye, the cough, and a good evening

**Want:** to be allowed to go with her father on Thursday.
**Obstacle:** the answer is no and she cannot get a reason for that either.
**Turn:** she gets a better evening instead, and takes it, and it is genuinely
good.

**Her father does her hair**, badly, over a bowl, **coughing** — both facts
in one action, and the cough is already old enough that nobody comments.

She asks **why**. He gives her the thing he always says, which is not an
answer. **She accepts it, because she always has**, and there is nothing sad
in the room while it happens.

**Establishes:** the warm family; the dye and the unexplained rule; **that
she is fast**, in action rather than assertion; her mother; and a household
with enough in it to be worth losing.

**Rule for this scene:** *she is not passive, she is unlucky.* **She asks,
she argues, she wins the smaller thing.**

**Closing image:** the water in the bowl, and the colour in it.

---

### 3 · T — The argument

**Want:** to get through dinner without being asked about school.
**Obstacle:** his father is home, which means the argument is on.
**Turn:** Hisui loses again, for the ninth time, and Iwao makes everyone
laugh, and it works, and the thing is not settled.

**The whole household on one page** — Mikage, Tadaharu, Meno, Akagane,
Hisui, Koishi, Iwao, and him. **Eight people in a building that held forty**,
and the reader should feel the empty rooms without being walked through
them.

- **Tadaharu, back from the road**, with the money counted onto the table
  and an opinion. ***Hiding is cowardice.*** Unresolved, at a table, with a
  boy of ten listening — **and it is the boy's entire adult politics,
  arriving two years before he will believe he derived it from the purge.**
- **Hisui makes the case to leave.** Meno will not go; the old will not go;
  **and she will not go alone.**
- **Iwao defuses it.** This is his function and it is visible: **he is the
  one who makes the argument survivable**, and everybody loves him for it,
  and the reader should too.
- **Koishi** is under the table, or on the bench, asking things.

**Closing image:** the money, counted, in stacks.

---

### 4 · K — The count goes wrong

**~1,200 words, and it is the shortest scene in the chapter. It stays the
shortest all act.**

**No date. No place-name.** He cannot supply either.

**Want:** the day to be an ordinary one.
**Obstacle:** the door opens a different number of times.
**Turn:** an inspection — and **he passes**, and is relieved.

**He counts.** Meals, footsteps, door-openings, days-since. **He does not
know what month it is**, so every number is relative and none of them
anchor.

**The inspection is clinical and brief.** A keeper, a lamp, hands, one
question, a note taken. Nobody is cruel. **That is the horror** — it is
maintenance.

> **The reader's first contact with him ends with a child being glad he was
> found satisfactory.**

**And he is not idle in that room.** The reader should be able to tell that
he has *prepared* — that this is a thing he works at — without a single
sentence explaining why.

**Closing image:** the number he starts again from.

**Not in this scene:** the word *Kaguya*. His name. The illness. Any
connection to a cage carried past two years ago.

---

### 5 · U — The katana

**Want:** to get the eighth repetition right.
**Obstacle:** she is close and keeps losing it at the same place.
**Turn:** she gets it — and he has to sit down, and does not get up.

**Grandfather → father → her.** Basics, forms, chakra-assisted movement,
taught by a man who knows a little and has never explained where it came
from because **his own father never told him.**

The unexplained rule, again, in a second register: **she has a technique
without a lineage, exactly as she has a hair colour without a reason.**

**Closing image:** the sword put away by her, not by him.

---

### 6 · T — The register

**Want:** an answer to *why is most of this building shut.*
**Obstacle:** Meno answers the question she prefers.
**Turn:** she shows him the book instead of telling him, which is how he
learns that a record is a way of not saying things.

**The register, open, on a table.** Names, dates, and what happened to
each — and **a column he does not have a word for.**

> **The *left* column.** He asks. She gives him a short answer. **He
> counts** — and the reader sees a page with names in it and cannot read one
> of them.
>
> **Guren is on that page.**

**And this is where his voice comes from.** The dated findings, the numbered
everything, the audit register — **it is hers.** The most characteristic
thing about him is a family artefact, and the reader should watch him copy
it from an old woman with ruined hands, in chapter one, and never be told
that is what happened.

> **THE DEEP PLANT.** In her box of old family work is **a piece with a
> course laid out of sequence.**
>
> **He can tell it is wrong. He cannot tell it is deliberate** — he is ten,
> and he has had the fixed tone for two years, and reading intent takes
> longer than that. **He files it as a bad piece.**
>
> It is fifty years old. It burns in ch3.

**Closing image:** the box shut, with the odd piece back in it.

---

### 7 · U — The sums

**Want:** to be given something to do.
**Obstacle:** the adults have started talking over her.
**Turn:** she takes one of her father's jobs without asking and does it
badly and does it.

**He stops being able to work.** Not a collapse — **an absence from a
rota.**

**Her mother starts doing sums**, on paper, at a table, and stops when
Uzuki comes in. *(Rhymes forward to month 5, when the same woman prices a
house.)*

**Establishes:** a civilian household with no margin, and a child who can
see arithmetic happening and cannot read it.

**And she acts.** Badly, unasked, insufficiently. **That is her spine and it
starts here.**

**Closing image:** what she got wrong, and nobody correcting it.

---

### 8 · T — The Academy

**Want:** to get through the exercise without being looked at.
**Obstacle:** he does it correctly.
**Turn:** the room reacts wrong, and **Aoyagi is kind about it**, and the
kindness is management.

**Seals on the page in a low-stakes room, so ch5 can be brutal:** the
sequence, the cast time, the tell, and what a bad hand costs. **The reader
must be able to see how to beat him by the end of this scene.**

- **A class a year above him.** He is the youngest and the one nobody sits
  next to, and both facts came off the same form.
- **The boy whose arm he broke two years ago is in the room.** Not
  addressed. Present.
- **Aoyagi** — decent, tired, and the author of a kind lie. He says
  something encouraging that is also a decision about where this child is
  put. **He believes he is being kind. He is. It is the institution's
  opening move.**

**Closing image:** the walk out, and who is walking with him.

---

### 9 · U — Three days away

**Want:** for somebody to say what is wrong with him.
**Obstacle:** they do say it, and then say what it would take.
**Turn:** **there is a medic three days away**, and the number after that is
money, and nobody moves.

**The engine of her whole thread starts here:** she knows what could help,
where it is, and how far. **The obstacle is not ignorance. It is a sum.**

**She volunteers in ch2 because she was in the room for this.**

**Closing image:** the distance, said out loud, in days.

---

### 10 · T — The frame

**Want:** to tell his cousin the truth.
**Obstacle:** *keep it to yourself.*
**Turn:** he gets to the edge of the sentence and does not say it.

**Akagane has made a frame and it is beautiful.** Twenty years old, the best
copper-worker in the family, proud of it, and he shows it to the boy because
the boy is the one who notices things.

**And Tadayoshi knows it is unnecessary.** He knows the frame's whole job
is evening the feed and he has been doing that with his hands since he was
eight.

> **He almost says it. He does not.**
>
> **The first withheld sentence in the book, and it is about a frame, and
> nobody in the room notices anything has happened.**

**This is the chapter's thesis, delivered as an object.** Two years later
this man dies still making a thing that stopped being necessary, for a boy
who knew.

**Closing image:** the frame held up to the light, and the boy saying the
true small thing instead of the true large one.

---

## What must not be in chapter 1

**The creed. The word for the threshold. Orochimaru or any buyer. The
boilerplate. Gatō. Guren by name. Kimimaro's name or clan. The collection
order.** *(It is signed in ch2.)*

**And no foreshadowing of the warrant.** No dread, no omens, no adult
looking out of a window. **Months 1–2 are ordinary, and the reader should
have to be told afterwards that they were the last ordinary months of his
life.** The chapter earns its ending by refusing to warn anybody.

## The three grammars, side by side, by scene 6

| POV | Grammar | First scene |
|---|---|---|
| **Tadayoshi** | **dated.** Findings, filed, numbered, under a date. | 1 |
| **Uzuki** | **immediate.** No format at all; the thing, said. | 2 |
| **Kimimaro** | **counted, anchored to nothing.** | 4 |

**All three are on the page inside the first four scenes.** That is the
chapter's structural obligation and it is why the K scene sits at 4 rather
than later.

## Open before drafting

1. **The compound's location and shape.** Forty people's buildings; what
   kind? It has to be describable in half a line and returned to for three
   years afterwards.
2. **What the family actually sells, and to whom.** Tadaharu's routes need
   two named towns and one named buyer, because month 5 depends on the work
   being *in circulation*.
3. **Uzuki's mother and father's names.**
4. **Whether Mikage's one non-instruction exchange lands in scene 1 or 3.**
