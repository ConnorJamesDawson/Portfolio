# Book Two — Tadayoshi Ruri

**Status: spec in progress.** Author-supplied material is marked AUTHOR.
Anything marked PROPOSED is mine and is not canon until ruled on.
Nothing here is settled unless it says FIXED.

---

## 1. Premise — AUTHOR

- Protagonist: **Tadayoshi Ruri.**
- Son of a **dying line of Shōton users**.
- The mother's line: *there were forty of us in the compound once.*
  **Now there are eight.**
- Of those eight, **the only two crystal users are his mother and him.**
- **His brother has no crystal potential at all** — and is a **prodigy with
  a sword**, rumoured to be in succession for one of the **legendary
  blades**.
- **This Ruri is canon-shattering.** He changes events; he is not slotted
  into the gaps between them.

---

## 2. What the premise already buys — PROPOSED reading

Recording this so the spec knows what it must not throw away.

- **A decline the reader watches happen.** Book one opened with a survivor;
  this opens with a survival still in progress. Forty to eight is a number
  that can go to seven on the page.
- **The bloodline is one death from gone — literally.** Two users. That was
  the best unused idea in book one (the paragraph that never left his own
  book) and here it is the premise rather than a finding.
- **The brothers are a structural engine, not a contrast.** The clan's
  *future* sits in the boy with the blood; the clan's *standing* sits in
  the boy with the sword. In a village that kills bloodlines, the brother's
  rise both protects the family and makes it visible. That tension is
  permanent, generates scenes indefinitely, and cuts both ways — neither
  brother is the lucky one.
- **Antagonist and clock come free.** A Shōton user in Kiri during a purge
  is hunted by the state from page one. Rule 4 of the learnings (opposition
  on the page in act one, not in a design file) is solved by the premise.
- **Combat is solved at the level of cast rather than character.** The
  legible, choreographable fighting lives with the brother and the swords.
  It does not have to be retrofitted onto the crystal user.

---

## 3. The one thing that must be settled before anything else

**The power's ceiling, written as costs, before chapter one.**

Book one's `falsified.md` killed twelve rules and every single replacement
made the protagonist *stronger*. Never once did a ruling add a constraint.
That is where the suspense went, and the premise here does not prevent it
from happening again — Shōton is still the power that has no cast time, no
material requirement, and no interruptible step.

**Binding rule for this book:** the Shōton spec carries, from day one,

1. at least **two interruptible steps** an opponent can attack;
2. **one resource** that can be taken, spent, poisoned, or run out; and
3. a cost curve where **the useful applications are the expensive ones**.

And: **no rule may be falsified without a replacement constraint named in
the same entry.** The ledger gets both columns this time.

## 4. OPEN — needed next, in priority order

1. **Era.** Purge-era Kiri under the Fourth Mizukage is the obvious read
   ("dying line", the swords live, bloodline hunting). Confirm or place it
   elsewhere. This dates everything else.
2. **Ages and birth order.** Which brother is older, and by how much. This
   decides who protects whom and who is failing whom.
3. **The clan's trade.** Forty people in a compound were doing something.
   Book one's Ruri worked frames and made jewellery; keeping a real craft at
   the centre is the single most portable asset from book one.
4. **The Shōton spec** — shape, and the three constraints above.
5. **Tadayoshi's lack.** The theme is *agency that was never installed*.
   PROPOSED: he is the clan's succession plan. Every decision about him was
   taken before he could speak, by a committee of eight frightened people,
   and nobody has ever asked him whether he wants to be the reason there is
   a ninth.
6. **The mother.** The only other user; the only possible teacher; the
   previous generation's version of him. Book one had no living parent —
   this is entirely new ground.
7. **POV roster.** Does the brother narrate? (PROPOSED: yes.)
8. **What "canon-shattering" costs.** Which canon events move, and what the
   book is prepared to lose by moving them.
