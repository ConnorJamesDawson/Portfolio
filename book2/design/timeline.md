threads are a woman's value being calculated by somebody else.

**The strain, named and fixed.** Eight months from *kills four genin* to
*trusted with a solo job* is too fast read as promotion. It is not one.
**The cargo job is a punishment posting** — he argues, modifies orders, does
not report cleanly, and twice somebody has had to explain a body. He arrives

temperature for trying to walk out on her.

invoice — AUTHOR.** **He leaves a survivor on ground a client has paid
for, the client complains, and he is ordered back to finish it and refuses
with no reason he can give.** *The probation's whole content was doing a
thing you were told without being given the reason, and he did it four
times, and now he has stopped.* **Three weeks later he is holding a docket
for a mountain temple four hundred miles off. A losing rebellion does not
discipline an asset it cannot replace. It prices it, and puts it where it
cannot cost anything.** *(`falsified.md` #25.)*

## 5. Canon strains bought — and the ones refused

### BOUGHT — the Kaguya assault at ~5 pre-P1

Book one's table has ~8–10. Moving it to ~5 puts Tadayoshi at **eight** for
the cage-sighting and Kimimaro at about **ten** for the assault.

**Cost:** small, on a figure already labelled approximate.
**Bought because** it improves canon plausibility — a Kimimaro of ten as a
combatant is easier to credit than one of five — and because it buys:

> **The year he fixed the tone and was told to hide it is the same year a
> boy his own age went past in a cage. The instruction and the evidence
> arrived within months, and he still did not understand what he was being
> told.**

### ACCEPTED, not strained — Kimimaro older

Roughly **thirteen at the extraction, fifteen at Wave**.

**Kept because** it makes a better household — the eldest, the most
physically dangerous thing in the building, the only one who has never made
a decision, being decided for by a boy two years younger — and because it
hands the reader a countdown the characters do not have. `spec.md` §3f
refuses to give him a date. A reader who knows canon knows he dies at about
fifteen, **and he is fifteen when he walks into Konoha.** The book never
says it.

### REFUSED — book one's coup strain

`world-bible.md` records an accepted strain pushing Zabuza's coup to ~3–4
pre-P1. **That was bought to fit Sazare's age ledger and it dies with him.**
Book two keeps the databook ~6, where the coup is somebody else's history
and Tadayoshi is seven.

### REFUSED — the coup as the Kimimaro extraction

An earlier proposal made the coup the convergence. At ~6 pre-P1 he is seven,
so it is not available — and it was never asked for. **The shipment moves
because the buyer has paid.** He is robbing a delivery, not exploiting a
riot, which wires the extraction directly into the pipeline and sharpens the
snake's motive from *stock went missing* to *a specific, expensive,
already-purchased acquisition disappeared*.

### REFUSED — Gatō dead at twelve

Zabuza dies at Wave as in canon, which requires Gatō alive and solvent to
hire him. **The encounter at twelve cannot end with a corpse.** Zabuza nails
the location down and keeps the proceeds.
