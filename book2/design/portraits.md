# Portraits — look, manner, voice, and what changes each year

**`character.md` is what each person is FOR. This is what they look like,
how they move, and how they sound on the page.**

---

## The five rules

### 1. No feature is decorative

Every physical detail is a **load path**. It shows a cost, a trade, or a
year. If a detail is not doing one of those three jobs it comes out.

### 2. Describe by function and by damage, not by adjective

Not *he was thin*. **The belt has a new hole in it, punched with a knife,
and the old one is still visible.** Not *her hands were careful*. **She
files her nails before she touches him and she does it with her own file.**

### 3. Change is tracked, not narrated

**No montage of growing up.** The reader learns he has grown because
something does not fit, or because a number in a notebook is different. The
prose never announces a year passing by describing a face.

### 4. One anchor image each, allowed to change once per act

Listed per character below. **The anchor recurs. It changes exactly once a
year and the change is the only announcement the book gives.**

### 5. POV characters are described by what does not fit

A POV character does not catalogue himself in a mirror. **He sees his own
hands, his own kit, and the things that have stopped working** — a sleeve
that ends too soon, a glove that goes on harder than it did.

---

## Clan colouring — the Ruri

Canon's only Shōton user is Guren: **dark hair with a violet cast, pale
eyes.** `world-bible.md` already rules Guren is a Ruri, so that is the
clan's colouring and it is canon-sourced rather than invented.

**And it is not correlated with aptitude.** Both brothers have it, plainly,
in the face, to anybody who looks.

> **Which makes *different blood* absurd on its face. Anyone with eyes could
> have refused it, and the tribunal took it anyway.**
>
> **The lie did not have to be believable. That is what the tribunal was
> for.**

## TADAYOSHI — 10 → 13

### The anchor: THE HANDS

The most important object in the book. **Bare permanently, because armoured
hands cannot seal** (`combat.md` §8) — in Kiri, in the wet, in the cold.

- **Cracked across the knuckles and along the finger-pads**, permanently, in
  a country where nothing dries.
- **Nails cut brutally short**, by knife, too often. A torn nail is a lost
  sequence.
- **Scarred across the backs and nowhere else**, because everywhere else has
  a plate on it and they never will.
- They warm slowly and he knows the number. He has timed it.

**The hands get worse every year and that is how the book counts time.**

### The gloves — and this is the safety indicator

He carries gloves he cannot work in. In that climate you need them; with
them on, there is no craft.

> **He puts them on when he believes he is safe and takes them off before
> anything might happen.**

The reader learns this without being told, and then the book can say
everything with a pocket. **A man sitting with his gloves on is a man who
has made an assessment.** He is wrong about it exactly once, and that is a
scene.

### Build

**Small, and he stays small.** Short at ten, still short at thirteen, and it
is not a phase — `combat.md` §11: *his floor is a child's body, and he can
be picked up and carried.* No mass, no reach.

**By thirteen the mismatch is the thing people notice.** The manner of a man
on the body of a child, and adults keep addressing the wrong one.

### The eyes — what Zabuza recognises

Not a colour and not *dead*. **They do not track the way other people's do.**

He looks at a person once, whole, and then at the part that is doing no work
— which is how he looks at a kata, a job sheet, or a wall. **People find
they have been read and cannot say when it happened.** Nobody is looked at
twice.

Zabuza killed his entire graduating class. He knows exactly what it looks
like when nobody is home behind a face, and he says so before Tadayoshi has
a word for it himself.

### Manner and tic — and the tic is the tell

> **He tests edges.** A thumb along a table edge, a coin, a rim, a blade —
> constantly, without noticing. His entire craft is whether a thing has a
> note in it, and his hands check by habit.

He also **sets things down flat.** He cannot leave an object at an angle.

**And when the threshold is close, the hands go still.**

That is the unreliable tell (`combat.md` §9 discipline 3): *he can measure
everything about it except its arrival.* **His hands also go still when he
is concentrating, so it is a poor signal, and he has written down that it is
a poor signal, and it is the only one anybody has.**

> **Uzuki watches his hands. She is wrong most of the time. She reacts
> anyway.**

### Voice on the page

- **He answers the question asked, not the question meant.**
- **Numbers, unprompted.** *Four days. Nine minutes. About a fortieth.*
- **Working vocabulary out loud** — *noted*, *filed*, *finding*, *the
  acceptance test is* — in ordinary conversation, and people react to it.
- **No hedges.** No *I think*, no *maybe*. He says **"I do not know,"** which
  lands entirely differently and is meant to.
- **He asks *why* as a genuine question**, and in every institution he has
  ever been inside it has read as insolence.
- **He cannot deliver comfort, so he delivers accuracy**, which is usually
  worse and is never intended to be.
- **And when he needs to say something enormous and kind, he finds a true
  unkind thing that does the same job.** *(`method-delta.md` §6.)*

### Year by year

| | Look | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| **10** | Academy clothes with the crest picked off, badly. Soft hands. Nothing fits wrong yet. | — |
| **11** | The hands start. First real chapping; the first cracks that do not close. | **The gloves appear.** He buys them for the cold and works out inside a week that he cannot use them. |
| **12** | Plates under the coat that do not match — three makers, none of them good, all of them him. He is carrying more than he can afford. | **He has grown two inches and the coat is the same coat.** |
| **13** | A small adult, and it is wrong. Everyone assumes he is older and then sees the wrists. | **The hands have stopped healing between jobs.** |

## UZUKI — 10 → 13

### The anchor: THE HAIR

**Red, and it is the plot.** Dyed for years — cheaply, at home, by her
father, and it goes wrong at the roots and it goes wrong in rain.

The sequence is the character:

| When | State | Who decided |
|---|---|---|
| Before | dyed | her father, who never said why |
| **The monastery** | **the rain takes it out** | nobody — and an elder does the arithmetic |
| After the rescue | **dyed again** | **Tadayoshi tells her to, and she does** |
| **The door** | **shown** | **Tadayoshi tells her to, because it is worth something** |

> **The first thing she ever gives up for him is the one conviction she
> arrived with** — *hair is hair* — and she does not experience it as a
> loss. **That is the corruption starting, small, in the first week.**

And at the gate he **orders her to reveal the thing he ordered her to
hide**, because he has costed it. She will do it instantly and gladly, and
Kakashi's whole decision turns on a girl showing her hair because a boy told
her to. **Nobody in that scene knows what they are doing.**

### THE THING THE BOOK NEVER SAYS

**She is a beauty.** The Uzumaki equivalent of Hinata — and bound to one
person **by complete choice.**

> ## ⚠ HARD RULE: the narration never describes it. Not once.
>
> **No POV in this book calls her beautiful, or dwells on her face, or
> catalogues her.** She is ten, and then thirteen, and a book that looks at
> her is a book that cannot be read.
>
> **It is conveyed entirely through other people's behaviour, and through
> what it costs.** How a room reorients. How a price changes. How long a man
> takes to answer. What an innkeeper does with a key. **The reader assembles
> it and the prose never states it.**

### And in his POV it is a logistics problem

This is the best version and it is also the most in-character one.

He can read a kata in forty seconds and **he cannot see the most obvious
fact about the person standing next to him** — not out of restraint, out of
the missing organ. What reaches him is the **effect**, never the cause, and
he files the effect:

> *She cannot be sent for anything alone. Not because she is unsafe —
> because the errand takes longer and it is remembered.*

**He writes her beauty into a security log without ever once knowing that is
what he is describing.** And it should be the coldest, most accurate
paragraph in the chapter.

> **She is beautiful, and it is the one thing about her that has never been
> mentioned by the person she has organised her life around.**

### It is not an asset. It is a trigger.

She is not flattered. She has never been flattered.

**Being wanted is how she nearly ended** — the monastery priced her twice,
for the blood and for the rest, and one of the men in that room said so out
loud. So every approach, every compliment, every man who looks a beat too
long arrives at her as **the same event, recurring.**

> **A beautiful girl who reacts to being admired the way other people react
> to being followed.**

Which is what actually arms `character.md`'s *anybody touching him loses the
hand* — because the far more frequent problem is people touching **her**,
and her response is not jealousy and not fear. **It is calm, procedural, and
disproportionate**, under a rule she can state.

### The dye was never protection

Her father hid the blood. **He could only solve one of the two problems, and
he solved the wrong one** — she was six, and it did not occur to him that
there would be another.

**The monastery elder saw both at once and priced both.**

So the hair sequence carries more weight than it looked: dyeing it never
made her un-lookable-at. It only ever hid *which* thing she was worth.

### The Hinata inversion, precisely

| | Hinata | Uzuki |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | admires from years away | never more than a few feet |
| Speech | **cannot say it** | **cannot stop saying it** |
| Effect on her | makes her braver — she grows **toward** him | makes her narrower — she grows **around** him |
| His knowledge | oblivious for years, then learns | he knows, and can do nothing with it |
| Her clan | one that watches everything | one that was hunted to nothing |
| The declaration | the hardest thing she ever does | the easiest; she has never withheld it |

> **Hinata's love is the thing she cannot say. Uzuki's is the thing she
> cannot stop saying** — and book one was about a man who could not say a
> true thing out loud. **This one stands him next to the person who says
> everything, always, and he still cannot.**

**Hinata has a clan telling her she is not enough. Uzuki has no clan at
all.** She has one person, which is why the devotion has nowhere to
distribute and no ceiling.

### "By complete choice" — and the book must hold the line

The reading that ruins her is *trauma-bonded child who cannot help it*. That
is pity, and pity is not a character.

**She chose. She can state why. She would choose again.** And by fifteen she
has had every opportunity to leave and has declined all of them, knowingly,
which is what makes it a choice rather than an imprint.

> **She has re-chosen it several hundred times, and she keeps a record.**

That is `character.md`'s standing rule in its strongest form: **her
reasoning is internally consistent, she can produce it on demand, and it is
wrong in a way she cannot see.**

### Build and hands — the inversion of him

**Uzumaki.** Robust, heals fast, tires slowly. Not small — **by twelve she
is taller than he is**, which is the correct visual for this household: the
one doing the protecting is the bigger one, and the one being protected
gives the orders.

> **Two people whose hands tell opposite stories.** He destroys his because
> they are tools. **She looks after hers because they are instruments** —
> filed, clean, oiled in winter, kept out of fights she could win.

She is the only person in the book who takes care of anything.

### The knife

Cheap, from under a pillow, and it is the object she will not replace.

**He offers to make her a blade and she says no.** She has a rule about an
object and she can state it: **the knife is the thing that worked.**

Which is his vow's specification, in miniature, arrived at independently —
and he will recognise it instantly and never once mention it.

### Manner and tic — she positions

> **Half a step off his weak side, angled outward, hands free, between him
> and the room.** In every room, unconsciously, always.

This is what Haku recognises at the Gatō meeting, and it is why Tadayoshi
can name it: **pointing at a position is an engineering observation, and he
can make those.** He indicates a place on the floor.

**And when he is not there, she does not know where to stand.** In the year
he is gone this is the whole of her on the page.

### Voice on the page

- **No format at all.** Where he says *noted*, she says the thing she feels,
  immediately, and it is alarming.
- **And she is polite.** A civilian mother and three weeks of a monastery
  left her with manners that sit strangely on top of what she is saying.
  *"Please don't touch him."* Said properly. Then the hand.
- **She never lies to him.** Not as a virtue — as an incapacity
  (`character.md`), and it is the thing that saves Kimimaro.
- **She states her reasoning and it is internally consistent** and wrong in
  a way she cannot see. She will have written the rule down.

### Year by year

| | Look | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| **10** | Underfed, and it shows in the wrists and the hair. Somebody else's coat. | — |
| **11** | Eating. It shows within two months, and it is the first thing about her that reads as recovery. | **She starts filing her nails**, and it is the first thing she does for herself. |
| **12** | Taller than him. Competent. Carrying a medical kit she assembled and can inventory from memory. | **The politeness has become procedure**, and procedure is what she does the frightening things inside. |
| **13** | A young woman who stands in one specific place, and every room she walks into does something. | **The hair, shown, at a gate** — and a jōnin who knew Kushina has to decide what to do about it. |

## KIMIMARO — 13 at the extraction, 15 at the door

**White hair. Green eyes. The two red marks above the brows.** Canon, and
they are the reason a room full of people who lost family to the Kaguya
recognise him in under a second.

### Three years in a cell, and a Kaguya body

**Wasted when he comes out** — muscle gone, skin the colour of something
kept indoors, and he cannot walk far. **And it comes back fast**, because he
is what he is.

> **His body recovers in four months. His manner never does.**

### The anchor: HE WAITS TO BE TOLD

**He will stand in a doorway indefinitely.** He does not sit unless invited.
**He asks permission to eat.**

That is the character, and it is what three cages produce. Uzuki works out
inside a fortnight that she has to *tell* him things so that he is allowed
to do them, and it becomes the household's grammar — a girl narrating
permissions all day so a boy can have a life.

**And it is why the refusal detonates.** Four words, quietly, at the village
conversation — **the first no of his life** — and the man who taught him it
was possible walks out of the door on the strength of it.

### The strongest person in the house, and he cannot decide to be

Shikotsumyaku is a widening risk and the constraint is not power, it is
**will**. He does exactly what he is told, perfectly, and then stops. **He
has no initiative whatsoever.**

Terrifying and sad in the same object, and it means every fight he is in is
a fight about whether somebody remembered to tell him something.

### The illness — what Uzuki measures

Canon never names the disease, so **nobody in the book can name it either.**
What exists is a reading, weekly, in a book:

> **She times him.** How long he can hold an extension before it fails.
> **The number goes down.**

Which is combat-relevant, physically observable, and the exact mirror of
Tadayoshi reading his mother's tone off a brooch. `threads.md` — the two
flattening series, facing pages.

The cough comes later and it is not the measurement. **The measurement was
already falling before anybody could see anything.**

### Voice on the page

Almost nothing. When he speaks: **complete sentences, formal, and exactly
responsive.** He does not volunteer. He does not elaborate. He says *yes*
and *I will* and *may I*.

### Year by year

| | Look | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| **13** | Wasted, indoor-coloured, in somebody else's clothes. An object that has been moved. | **Taken.** |
| **14** | Physically restored and completely still. A boy who has learned to ask. | **He asks a question that is not a permission.** |
| **15** | Tall, healthy to look at, and the number in the book is two thirds of what it was. | **He has said no once and is still working out what that means.** |

## THE BROTHER — RURI IWAO, now AMAGIRI KENJI — 13 → 16

### He looks like Tadayoshi, and that is the horror

Same colouring. Same face, three years on. **Anybody who saw them together
would laugh at *different blood*, and the tribunal did not care**, which is
the whole indictment in one look.

### And he got the body

Three years and a sword. **Where Tadayoshi stayed small, Iwao got the
height** — and by sixteen he is a young man where his brother is still a
boy.

> **The one without the blood got the body. The one with the blood got the
> hands.**

### The anchor: HE IS FLUENT

**He has the organ Tadayoshi is missing.**

He can talk to people. He always could. He reads a room, says the right
thing in it, and is liked — and **that is what saved him at thirteen**: a
frightened boy who could say the correct sentence to a tribunal.

> **So the defect is not in the blood. His brother has the faculty and used
> it to sell him, and the one person who could have taught him is the one
> who did that.**

He apologises easily and means it **in the moment**, which is worse than not
meaning it at all.

### Year by year

| | Look | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| **13** | A boy in borrowed kit, holding an issue blade, at a table with a document on it. | **He signs.** |
| **14** | Corps kit that does not fit yet. Visibly being kept. | Attached to hunts. |
| **15** | It fits. | |
| **16** | A young man with a blade that has a name. | **What the curve delivers.** |

## THE MOTHER

**Three scenes, and then she is a tone in a shop window for three years.**
So the look must land fast and then live entirely in her work.

- **A jeweller's hands, worn out at the fingertips** — the opposite ruin
  from her son's. Decades of small precise work; she can still thread a
  setting and she cannot feel a coin.
- **Worn young**, and she was never the strong one. `character.md`: she is
  not unlucky, **she is what survived** a harvest that removed every high
  producer for generations.
- **She gives instructions without reasons**, which reads as coldness and is
  not. *(The ruling: she tells him only partly — enough to frighten, not
  enough to explain.)*

**Her real portrait is her hand on an article**: how she sets a stone, how
many courses she can lay, what the piece sounds like. **He can read her out
of a brooch in a shop window and the reader learns her that way too.**

## The pairings — every physical fact is somebody's inverse

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **Hands** | he destroys his, they are tools | **she maintains hers, they are instruments** |
| **Build** | he stays small and gives the orders | **she outgrows him and does the protecting** |
| **Blood vs body** | he has the blood and the hands | **his brother has the body and the fluency** |
| **The organ** | he cannot read a room | **his brother can, and sold him with it** |
| **Speech** | he says only what is accurate | **she says everything, immediately** |
| **Decisions** | he cannot stop making them | **Kimimaro has never made one** |
| **Ruin** | his hands are cracked open | **his mother's are worn smooth** |
| **Being seen** | nobody looks at him twice | **every room looks at her, and he is the only one who does not** |
| **The reading** | he reads his mother off crystal | **she reads Kimimaro off a stopwatch** |

## The standing check

Before any scene with two or more of them in it:

1. **Where is Uzuki standing?** She always has an answer and it is always
   the same answer.
2. **Are his gloves on or off?** It is never neutral.
3. **Has anybody told Kimimaro he may?**
4. **What does not fit today?** That is where the year is.
5. **Who in this room has looked at her too long, and has she counted
   them?** She has. She always has.
