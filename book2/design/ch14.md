## 0. ⚠ THIS IS THE SUBSTANCE CHAPTER, AND THE RULE IS THE POINT

> **AUTHOR:** *"The thing I want to have is substance. There should be
> chapters where the characters don't advance the plot very much — have
> another courier mission where he talks to Uzuki and have her thoughts on
> things."*

**`method-delta.md` §2l, and it is being spent here.** *At least one
chapter in three goes on the life rather than the plot, and a quiet
chapter is not a chapter where nothing happens — it is one where what
happens does not change what happens next.*

### ⚠ SO THE PLOT DOES NOT MOVE, AND THAT IS A DESIGN DECISION, NOT A GAP

| | |
|---|---|
| **The job is freight** | *a courier run, nine days out and nine back, and nothing goes wrong with it* |

## 1. ⚠ THE MULE IS THE CHAPTER

**`act-2.md` §2c: it is the act's best object and it is not about
transport.**

### The operational cause is real and boring, which is what makes it work

**There are two of them now and the job is freight.** *He carried
fifty-one pounds nine days uphill in the twelfth month on his own and it
was fine. Two people and a consignment is a different sum, and he does it
on paper in about four minutes and it comes out at **buy an animal**.*

**And a horse is a description** *(§2c)* — **three children and a horse is
a thing people tell hunters about** — *so it is a mule, and he prices it,
and the price is six thousand-ish and he has eighteen.*

### ⚠ AND THEN HE CANNOT DO IT

**The seller does the speech.**

> *Item by item, out loud, in a yard, unhurried, courteous: the age, the
> feet, what the near hind was like when he got him, what he is worth in
> the spring against what he is worth now.* **The same speech Shimoda did
> at Nakadō in the sixth month with a halter in a girl's hand.**

**Tadayoshi is not troubled by it and does not recognise it**, *because he
has never heard it before and it is a man describing an animal.* **What
happens to him is what happened over a pair of boots on 28/10:** *he puts
his hand on the mule's shoulder before he has said a number.*

> ***Do not touch the thing first.***

### ⚠ SO SHE DOES IT

**And she is very good at it.**

- **She knows the speech from the inside**, *and she is the only person in
  this book who does* — **so she can hear the two places in it where the
  man is lying, because she has heard the true version done by a man who
  was not.**
- **She does not touch the animal once.**
- **Asked 7,500. She pays 5,200.** *And gets a headcollar thrown in
  because she asked for it last, after the price was agreed, which she
  learned from watching Kuriya do it to her mother.*

**And afterwards she has to sit down**, *behind a wall, out of the yard,
for about a quarter of an hour* — **and nothing arrives, again, the way
nothing arrived behind the wood store on 28/10, and what arrives instead
is the list.**

### ⚠ AND NEITHER OF THEM NAMES IT — and the reasons are different and unsaid

| | Why not | |
|---|---|---|
| **Him** | **naming is not an operation.** *It is an animal and it has a load and a rate* | *and he does not notice that he has not* |
| **Her** | ⚠ **she knows exactly what a named mule costs** | *her father called his **Genzō**, after the man he bought it off, and she held its halter in a yard while it was priced, and she is not doing that twice* |

> **So the mule has no name for six chapters and both of them have a
> reason and neither says it** — **and `act-2.md` §6.1 is the payoff:
> Kimimaro asks for it to have one, and it is the first thing he has ever
> asked for, and neither of them has an answer ready.**

## 2. TADAYOSHI — four scenes

### 2.1 The docket, and the weight (scene 1, 2/3–5)

**A courier run: freight, nine days out to Kanaya and nine back, 1,400
ryō.** *Which is more than the two contracts in the first month, and he
notices that and does not like what it implies and puts it away.*

- **She asks to come and he says yes in about two seconds**, *because the
  alternative is a room she cannot leave and he has already written that
  down* **(ch13: *a room she cannot leave is a room she has been in
  before*).**
- **And then does the sum and finds it does not close.** *Two people, a
  consignment, eighteen days, in the second month.*

### 2.2 ⚠ THE YARD (scene 4, 2/6) — and he loses before he opens his mouth

**§1.** *He puts his hand on the shoulder. The seller's voice changes by
about a quarter of a tone and Tadayoshi hears it and cannot do anything
with it, and knows in that second that he is going to pay whatever is
asked.*

> **And he does the thing he has never done in his life, which is to
> stop.** *He steps back and does not say a number at all.*
>
> **He does not hand it to her.** *She is standing four feet away and says
> **can I** and he says **yes**, and the whole handover is three words,
> and he thinks about those three words on and off for two years.*

- **He watches her do it and does not understand what he is watching**,
  *and writes down four things afterwards and gets three of them right.*
- **⚠ AND THE ONE HE GETS WRONG IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS:** *he puts her
  down as **good with people** — **which is the flattest and most useless
  reading available and he is a boy who reads people off their work**, and
  he has just watched her do it and has come away with a compliment.

### 2.3 The road (scene 6, 2/7–14) — and one thing he will not answer

**Eight days out with an animal, and it is the easiest travelling he has
ever done, and he says so out loud, which is rare.**

- **§2k: what a mule actually costs.** *Feed, water, twenty minutes a day
  on the feet, a headcollar that rubs and has to be packed with rag, and
  **it cannot go where a person can**, which costs them four miles on the
  second day and he enters it.*
- **⚠ AND SHE SEES HIM DO THE TONE READING.** *A thing out of a tin, on a
  fifth morning, four minutes, at a distance from her.* **She asks what it
  is.**
  > **And he does not answer.** *Not a refusal — he says **it's a
  > reading** and then does not say anything else and puts it away, and it
  > is the first time in fifty days he has not given her a complete answer
  > to an operational question.*
  >
  > **She notices it exactly.** *And so has she got one, and neither of
  > them knows about the other's, and the book does not say so* —
  > **§4.15's hat and §9's tin, four feet apart on a road for eight days.**

### 2.4 Home, and nothing has changed (scene 10, 2/24–27)

**They come back, the freight is signed off, the fee is paid, and there is
nothing on the boards.**

- **Four lines in the ordinary column and the second column empty.**
- **⚠ AND THE THING HE DOES NOT WRITE DOWN**: *that the eighteen days cost
  him 5,200 for the mule and about 400 in feed and returned 1,400, and
  that he is now a boy who runs at a loss and has decided it is the
  season.*
- **The mark is still beside the sentence from 11/1** *(**expect recovery
  in the 2nd month**)*, **and the second month is over.** *He looks at it.
  He does not take the mark off. He does not add a new line either.*
- **Last beat: he counts what he has left and it is a number he has not
  had to think about before**, *and it is going down, and it is the first
  time.*

## 3. UZUKI — four scenes

### 3.1 She asks to be taught, and is (scene 2, 2/3–5)

**She asks on the first evening out and she has it prepared** — *she has
had it prepared since a hall floor on 17/12.*

> ***"Will you teach me to fight?"***
>
> **"No."**
>
> *And then, about four seconds later:* **"Stand up."**

**`combat.md` §11d.** *He cannot teach it and says so accurately —* ***I
haven't got a thing to teach. I've got a lot of other people's and it
doesn't come apart*** — **and then puts her on the ground eleven times in
about four minutes**, *and does not go easy and does not know how to.*

- **⚠ AND THE TWO COUNTS START HERE.** *He counts how long she lasts, in
  seconds, and tells her the number, because a number is the only
  encouragement he owns.* **And she counts something else and does not
  tell him.**
  > ***He did the same thing eleven times.***
  >
  > **One.**
- **And she treats herself afterwards**, *in the dark, out of sight,
  because §11b is hers and nobody knows she has it.* **The person who
  repairs the damage he does to her is her, and neither of them counts
  that.**

### 3.2 ⚠ THE SPEECH (scene 5, 2/6) — the chapter's centre

**§1.** *Her POV, the whole yard, and the register is precision and not
distress.*

- **She hears the two lies before the man has finished the first
  sentence** — *one about the age off the teeth, and one about what he has
  been doing for the last two years off the shoulder* — **and she has both
  of them because Shimoda did the same speech honestly in front of her and
  she has had two years to go over it.**
- **⚠ AND THE THING THAT HAPPENS IN THE MIDDLE OF IT:** *she opens her
  mouth to do the second half of the speech and finds she has it whole,
  word for word, in Shimoda's rhythm, with the pauses in the same places.*
  **She uses it. It works. It is the most effective thing she has ever
  done and it is a dead man's speech in her mouth.**
- **She does not touch the animal.** *And she does not know why not until
  she is behind the wall afterwards.*
- **And behind the wall, nothing arrives, and the list does:**
  > *I have just done the thing that was done to me and I was good at it
  > and I liked it.*
  >
  > *And the mule did not mind. And that is the whole of the difference
  > and it is not a small one and it is not enough.*

### 3.3 ⚠ THE COUNT, AND HE CHECKS IT (scene 7, 2/14–18)

**She tells him the number.** *She has not told anybody a number since her
father, and she does it on the fifth evening on the way back, for no
reason she can afterwards identify, in about nine words.*

> ***"There's one thousand four hundred and seventy-five days until I'm
> fifteen."***
>
> *(Checked: 1,518 on 1/3 — ch13 — less 43 days to 2/16. And it is one
> low, because she took the low one on 8/10 and has carried it since.)*

**And he does the thing nobody in her life has ever done.**

> **He asks her how she got it.**
>
> *And she tells him — 26/3, a hundred thousand ryō and a cart and four
> days; 1,620 under a bridge at Ōgi on 22/9; the nine days she lost in the
> tenth month and the number she made and did not count; the charcoal
> running out on 9/12; and every day since in her head.*
>
> **And he checks it. On paper. In about six minutes.**
>
> ***"You're one out. You lost a day in the tenth month and you took the
> low one on purpose, and you've carried it ever since."***

- **⚠ IT IS THE KINDEST THING THAT HAS HAPPENED TO HER SINCE HER FATHER
  DIED AND IT IS DELIVERED AS AN AUDIT**, *and she has no idea what to do
  with her face, and he has gone back to the fire.*
- **She does not correct the number.** *She keeps the one that is one out,
  on purpose, and cannot explain it, and it is the first thing she has
  ever kept that was wrong.*
- **⚠ AND IT PREFIGURES `act-2.md` §6.3 EXACTLY:** *the one useful thing
  he can do for a person is audit their arithmetic, and he will do it
  again in month 10 to a boy with a number in a dead unit, and it will
  read as cold both times.*
- **The other thing that gets said on that road, and it is hers:** *what
  she would eat if she could have anything.* **He has never once thought
  about this and cannot produce an answer**, *and goes at it seriously for
  a mile and a half and comes back with a thing his mother made and does
  not say whose it was.*

### 3.4 ⚠ THE FIRE (scene 9, 2/22) — and the canon impression is the mirror

**`canon-cast.md` §3.3 — Utakata, unnamed.** *One fire, one night, on the
road back.*

- **A young man travelling alone who asks for nothing and shares what he
  has**, *and blows a bubble off the end of a pipe while he is thinking,
  and does it three times, and does not explain it and nobody asks.*
  **§1.6's handle, and it is the only unusual thing about him.**
- **⚠ HE TALKS TO HER AND NOT TO HIM**, *because he sees a boy who is
  dangerous and a girl who is not, and he is wrong about exactly one of
  those.*
- **She works out what he is inside ten minutes** — *the way he sits
  relative to the road; that he has not put anything down; that he asked
  where they were going and not where they were from; and that he is doing
  the thing Tadayoshi does with a fire, which is to be on the side of it
  the smoke is not.* **She says nothing.**
- **⚠ AND THEN HE ASKS TADAYOSHI ONE QUESTION AND UNDERSTANDS WHAT HE HAS
  DONE.**
  > ***"How long have you been out here?"***
  >
  > **And Tadayoshi does not answer.**
  >
  > *And the man looks at him for about two seconds and then says:*
  > ***"No. Don't answer that. I'm sorry."***
  >
  > **And that is the whole of it. He is gone before light.**

> **He knows exactly what kind of boy does not answer that question,
> because he is one, and he is ashamed of having asked** — *and Uzuki gets
> every part of it and says nothing, and Tadayoshi gets none of it and
> writes the man up as **no threat, going north**.*

**PROPOSED and it costs nothing:** *nine years on, in a different book,
somebody says **there was a man on the Kanaya road who blew bubbles** and
she stops what she is doing.*

## 4. KIMIMARO — two scenes, and they are the darkest in the book

### 4.1 ⚠ HE FINDS A CLOCK (scene 3)

**Ch13 broke his instrument** — *the bowls come when somebody remembers,
and a thing somebody remembers is not a clock.* **In month 2 he gets a new
one and it works and he is pleased with it.**

> **Somebody screams at regular intervals.**
>
> *Not constantly. Not randomly. **Regularly** — and he establishes that
> over eleven of them before he will use it, because he is careful, and he
> times the gaps against nothing but each other and finds they hold.*

- **⚠ AND HE DOES NOT THINK ABOUT WHAT IT IS**, *and the prose must not
  make him.* **He has no model of anybody upstairs** (ch12) — *he has
  never been told one thing about one person in two years* — **and a
  sound that repeats is a sound that repeats.**
- **He rebuilds the whole count on it in four days.** *Nine hundred and
  three, converted; the fifth piece re-anchored; the thirty-four
  recovered.* **He is more pleased than he has been since the sixth
  month.**
- **⚠ THE PROSE SAYS NONE OF IT.** *No adjective, no shudder, no line that
  tells the reader how to feel.* **The reader supplies the whole of it,
  which is `method-delta.md` §8 at its most extreme, and this is the
  scene that proves the technique or breaks it.**

### 4.2 ⚠ AND IT STOPS (scene 8)

**On a day he cannot date, it does not happen.**

- **He waits.** *Two intervals, then four, then eleven.* **And does the
  work, properly, the way he does everything:** *is it the same time and I
  have miscounted; is it further away and I cannot hear it; has it moved.*
- **⚠ AND HE ARRIVES AT THE ONE ANSWER HE CAN CHECK, WHICH IS THE WRONG
  ONE:** ***the clock has stopped.*** **And the count is broken again and
  he has lost four days of it and cannot recover them.**
- **He does not grieve and does not wonder** — *wondering requires a model
  and he has none* — **and what he actually feels is the flat
  unpleasantness of an instrument breaking in the hand, for the third time
  in his life.**
- **⚠ AND THE LAST LINE IS THE ONE THE CHAPTER EXISTS FOR**, *and it must
  be flat, and it must be short, and nobody may comment on it:*
  > ***It was going well.***

## 6. THE FIVE HARD PROBLEMS

**1. ⚠ THE PLOT MUST NOT MOVE, AND I WILL WANT TO MOVE IT.** *Every
instinct built over thirteen chapters will try to put a hunter on that
road, make the freight matter, or have the stranger be somebody.* **Refuse
all of it.** *The freight is freight, the road is a road, and the man goes
north.*

**2. Kimimaro's clock must be reported and never framed.** *§4.1.* **No
adjective, no shudder, no sentence that tells the reader what it is.** *He
establishes a regular sound and uses it and is pleased.* **If one word of
the prose flinches, the scene becomes about the author's discomfort and
stops being about him.**

**3. The speech must be precision and not distress.** *She is not
re-traumatised in that yard; she is **excellent** in it, and the cost
arrives fifteen minutes later behind a wall and is a finding rather than a
feeling* — **which is the shape her scenes have had since ch11 and must
not change now that it is about her enjoying something.**

**4. The audit must not read as tenderness.** *He checks her arithmetic
because a number that is wrong is a number that is wrong.* **The warmth is
entirely the reader's, and every word of his must be technical**, *and he
must go back to the fire immediately.*

**5. ⚠ AND THE CHAPTER MUST STILL HAVE A SPINE.** *Substance is not
shapelessness.* **The mule is the spine** — *needed on scene 1, failed on
scene 4, got on scene 5, paid for across scenes 6 and 10* — **and if a
reader cannot say what the chapter was about, §2l has been used as an
excuse rather than a rule.**

## 8. REVISED ON THE PAGE

**1. ⚠ AN ELEVENTH SCENE WENT IN, AND IT WAS A REAL GAP.** *The plan had
the sparring start in scene 2 and then vanish for twenty days, in a
chapter that covers twenty-two days of road with a session every night.*
**Scene 8 is his side of it, and it makes the chapter's thesis land a
third time:** *he keeps a page — 2, 9, 9, 11, 10, 13… 21 in seventeen days
— and the page is accurate and every number on it is true and he is
measuring the thing that can be measured rather than the thing that is
moving.*

> **And she asks for the same one four times and he reads it as
> drilling.** *She was checking whether the fourth would be the same as
> the first, because if it was the same it was still one thing.* **It was
> the same all four times.** *So her number has stayed at one for sixteen
> sessions and she goes to sleep knowing something about him he does not
> know about himself.*
>
> ***"How many have you got?" — "Ways of doing that?" — "I don't know."***
> **He counts eleven he can name and stops because the rest have no
> names, and tells her, and gives her another one, and it is the same
> one.**

**2. ⚠ DEGUCHI SAYS SHIMODA'S SENTENCE, AND THAT IS THE TRIGGER.** *Not
planned.* **The eight words are a stock line of the trade —** ***he was
bred for more than he's ever done*** — **and it is the same eight words in
the same place, at the shoulder, after the feet and before the price.**

> *So it is not his and it is not Shimoda's.* **It is the speech, and
> everybody who does this has got it, and Shimoda did it honestly and this
> one is doing it to her.** *And that is the moment she has the whole
> thing, word for word, with the pauses in the same places.*

**3. And Tadayoshi thinks the speech is good work.** *Which is the only
possible reading available to him and is the horror of the scene:* ***this
is a man who knows a thing and is describing it accurately to somebody who
does not, and he is not lying to me anywhere I can find.*** **He has never
heard it before and nobody has ever done it to him.**

**4. ⚠ HE STOPS, AND HE HAS NEVER STOPPED.** *He puts his hand on the
shoulder, hears the seller's voice move by a quarter of a tone on the
fourth word of a sentence about hay, knows exactly what it means, and can
do nothing* — **so he shuts his mouth and says no number at all**, *which
she notices before Deguchi does:* ***he does not have a stopped.***

**5. She does not touch the animal, and the third reason is the one she
finds behind the wall.** *Because he has done it and it cost two thousand;
because going over would be telling him she did not already know;* **and
because she is not putting her hand on it while a man is saying what it is
worth.**

**6. ⚠ AND THE FOURTH THING HE WRITES IS THE FLATTEST SENTENCE HE HAS EVER
PUT IN THAT BOOK.** *Three correct and useful observations, and then:*
***4. She is good with people.*** **Seven months of a method whose entire
content is that you do not describe a person, and he has just watched
somebody take a man apart and come away with a compliment.**

**7. ⚠ TWO OBJECTS ELEVEN INCHES APART ON A BANK.** *Not planned.* **He
does the tone reading with the sword across his back**, *and it is making
the note it has always made, and he has four words about it in the book
from 17/12 and has not gone back to them.* **One of them tells him what he
wants to know; the other has been telling him something else for fourteen
months.**

**8. And the reason he will not answer her about the tin is the true one
and it is not danger.** ***The day I say it out loud is the day it becomes
a thing that is happening to me instead of a thing I am measuring.***

**9. ⚠ THE AUDIT LANDED HARDER THAN PLANNED BECAUSE OF WHAT CAME BEFORE
IT.** *Nobody has ever asked her how she got a number* — **her father said
*fancy* and meant it kindly; her mother said she was a clever girl and put
a hand on her head** — *and he rules a page, does the gaps twice, finds
the fault at 8/10, tells her the rest is clean, and puts a stick on the
fire.*

> **And she keeps the wrong number, and gets as close as she ever gets to
> why:** ***the one is nine days in Ōgi being hungry and making a number
> up and being honest about making it up. And I am not taking that out.***

**10. He reciprocates, late and sideways and about barley.** *A mile and a
half of work on a question with no answer in it, and he comes back with a
thing done in an unwashed pan with a green thing in it that goes almost to
nothing.* ***"It takes about an hour and it's not worth an hour." — "It
sounds worth an hour." — "It's barley."*** *Eight paces.* ***"It was."***
**He does not say whose pan.**

**11. ⚠ AND UTAKATA UNDOES A FINDING SHE HAS HAD FOR TWO YEARS.** *Not
planned.* **She cannot price him either** — *second in seven weeks* — **so
*everybody has a number* has failed twice and she will not keep a finding
out of habit.** *And the replacement is better:*

> ***He had nothing. A man with nothing has got nothing he wants from you,
