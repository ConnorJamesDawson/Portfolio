# The team — what the other two are for

> **Governs:** Uzuki's and Kimimaro's capability, how each of them fits
> the shape Tadayoshi leaves, and the rule that stops the three of them
> ever assembling into the thing that would solve his problem.
>
> Sits under `combat.md` §10b (arity), `character.md` (the missing
> organ), and `portraits.md` (the two portraits).

---

## 1. THE THESIS

> **The three of them are a complete unit, and he is the reason it never
> assembles.**

`combat.md` §10b: *a dozen hunter-nin is not a harder fight, it is twelve
fights, and no technique makes him two places. The only thing that solves
arity is people, and people are the one thing he will not take.*

**The answer to his problem is standing in his house.**

| | Is | Covers the gap that |
|---|---|---|
| **Uzuki** | **the noise, and the tactician** | he cannot read people, and he cannot draw fire off himself while doing anything else |
| **Kimimaro** | **the close-range monster with no plan** | he is pre-emptive; *you cast before contact, because during contact you cannot* |
| **Tadayoshi** | **the ground** | neither of them can do anything with a field |

> ## ⚠ AND THE UNIT DOES ASSEMBLE. IT JUST DOES NOT ASSEMBLE AROUND HIM.
>
> **Uzuki and Kimimaro work.** She has tactical sense and cannot stop
> telling people things; he is a devastating fighter with no tactical
> sense at all and can only act on an instruction. **She issues them. He
> takes them. It functions immediately and neither of them ever calls it
> anything.**
>
> **Tadayoshi is not the leader of a three-person team. He is a
> specialist who goes off and does the job alone**, while behind him two
> people he has assessed as next to non-combatants operate perfectly
> together, **and he does not notice, because noticing would require the
> organ he has not got.**

---

## 2. THE AXIS HE GRADES THEM ON — and it is the wrong one

**His standing assessment, and it is his own words:** *Uzuki and Kimimaro
are next to non-combatants.*

**He is not being cruel and he is not being wrong on his own terms.** He
grades one thing, because it is the only thing he has ever had to be:

> **Can you hold a piece of ground on your own, indefinitely, against
> people who are trying to kill you?**

**On that axis they score zero, and so does almost everybody alive.** It
is the axis of a boy who has never once had anybody to hand a thing to,
and it measures **self-sufficiency**, not capability.

> **He is not misjudging their power. He is measuring the wrong quantity,
> with an instrument he built to measure himself.**

---

## 3. KIMIMARO — the best fighter in the book, and no plan whatever

### 3.1 What he actually is

**Straight, with no hedging: at full health he is the strongest of the
three by a distance, and he is never at full health again after the cell.**
Three years indoors, wasted, and **the illness has already started**
(`act-1.md` §5). Body back in four months; **manner never.**

> **Call it: the strongest fighter in this book, running at something
> under half, and falling.** And nobody — including him — knows the
> denominator.

### 3.2 The economy, and it is the exact inverse of Tadayoshi's

| | Tadayoshi | Kimimaro |
|---|---|---|
| **Material** | **external** — grit, bought or broken out of ground | **internal** — himself |
| **Article** | **dated. Starves. Degrades before it dies** (`combat.md` §3) | **alive, permanent, and does not starve** |
| **Cost** | **medium and time** | **his own body** |
| **Left behind** | **a thread — every article is a disclosure** (§4.3) | **nothing that is not him** |
| **Range** | **pre-emptive, before contact** | **contact, and only contact** |
| **Deliberation** | constant, and it is his whole method | **none. He does not decide.** |

> **Tadayoshi spends material. Kimimaro spends himself.**
>
> **And he is dying, so every article costs him days.** Nobody has told
> him that. **He started taking pieces off himself at twelve to have an
> answer ready for a man who was never coming back** (`ch06.md` scene 8),
> and he has never stopped.

### 3.3 The two properties that make him the only usable body on a field
### Tadayoshi has laid

1. **He does exactly what he was told and then stops.** No improvisation,
   no initiative, no drift. `portraits.md`: *appallingly clean — no rage,
   no appetite, no satisfaction. He is told, he executes exactly, he
   stops, he waits.*
2. **Which means he is the only person alive who can stand inside
   Tadayoshi's own area denial and not die in it**, because the thing
   that kills people in that field is **moving in a way nobody
   specified.**

> **The field has no judgement** (`combat.md` §10b) — *and Kimimaro does
> not require it to have any.* **He is the one person the tripping
> hazards cannot catch**, and Tadayoshi will take years to work that out,
> because working it out requires him to think about a person as a piece
> of a system, and that is the missing organ.

### 3.4 HE HAS NO TACTICAL SENSE AT ALL — and the trigger exists

**He is not a weapon nobody can fire. He is a weapon Tadayoshi cannot
fire.**

> **He will not act without an instruction**, and he has no capacity
> whatever to generate one. Told *hold this door*, he holds that door
> while the building burns down behind him and does it perfectly.

**And Tadayoshi cannot issue one.** Not "will not" — **cannot**.
`character.md`, the missing organ, third light: **he cannot teach.** He
can name a fault in a form in four words. He cannot say *what to do*,
because that requires knowing what the other person is **for**, and he has
never once been able to tell anybody that.

> **Kimimaro is perfectly usable — by anybody except him.**
>
> **Orochimaru would have had him running inside a week.** Not a slur on
> Tadayoshi and not a compliment to the snake. **It is the book's cruellest
> single fact**, and `portraits.md` already states it: *Orochimaru would
> have given him meaning. Tadayoshi gives him a room.*

**AND UZUKI CAN DO IT.** (§6.1.) **She is the trigger**, and she is not
being generous or clever — **she simply cannot stop telling people
things**, which is the one trait of hers Tadayoshi finds hardest to be
near, **and it is the exact thing that makes Kimimaro operable.**

**The eventual document is still the horror.** *Tell me what I am for* is
answered with **a specification** — purpose, scope, acceptance criteria,
review date — because **Tadayoshi builds a document when he cannot build a
relationship.** Kimimaro accepts it gratefully; it is the best thing
anybody has ever given him. **Uzuki says it is obscene, and she is right,
and she has been doing the same job out loud for two years for free
without either of them noticing.**

### 3.5 The one thing he fixes, and it is the riverbed

**Ch5's lesson, in a person:** *a fence stops a foot that is walking. A
foot at a run arrives.*

> **Kimimaro is the answer to the thing that beat him at ten**, and he
> will be standing in the same house as it for two years without it being
> used, **because using it requires an order.**

## 4. UZUKI — the glass cannon

### 4.1 What she is

**She goes in first, katana in hand, and does not come back out until it
is finished or she is.**

**No guard. No disengage. No retreat.** She does not fight a duel and she
does not manage a fight; **she arrives in the middle of one and makes it
about her.**

> **He is a monster of holding ground. She is the opposite object
> entirely:** everything spent immediately, nothing conserved, **and no
> plan for afterwards because afterwards is not a thing she budgets for.**

### 4.2 WHERE IT COMES FROM — and it is a valuation, not a death wish

> ## ⚠ THE FLOOR, and it is not optional
>
> **She is not trying to die. She is refusing to spend anything on not
> dying.** Those are different things and the difference is the whole of
> her dignity.

`portraits.md`: *the reading that ruins her is trauma-bonded child who
cannot help it. That is pity, and pity is not a character.* **The same
rule governs this.**

**She has done what everybody in this book does. She put a number on
herself.**

By thirteen she has been valued by a monastery, priced twice in one room,
listened to a wall for two months, watched her mother watch, and been
taken out by a boy **who did it as a debt and said so**. And she has drawn
the obvious conclusion:

> **Conserving herself buys nothing she wants. Spending herself buys the
> thing she wants.**

**It is a policy. She can state it. It is internally consistent and she
would produce it on demand** — `character.md`'s standing rule for her —
**and it is wrong in a way she cannot see.**

**And it makes the arity problem worse, not better**, which is the test of
whether this is a real constraint:

> **He now has a partner who will absolutely be in the other place — and
> who will not come back from it.** His refusal is no longer *she cannot*.
> **It is *she will*.**
>
> **The distance rule was never about her competence. It is about her
> willingness**, and he worked that out early and has never said it to
> her, because saying it would require telling her what she is worth,
> **and he cannot price anything.**

### 4.3 THE SADISM — and the mechanism is what matters

> **Her enjoyment is routed through his.**

**He does not enjoy violence.** What he enjoys is **the thing completed
correctly** — the arithmetic coming out, the mission fulfilled, the entry
that does not fight him. That is the whole of his pleasure and it is
bookkeeping.

**So hers is derived, and it is derived into the one job that is left
over:**

> ***You go and do it. I'm going to go and cause a scene.***

**She is the noise.** Drawing attention off him is the most useful thing
she can be, it is the thing he needs and cannot do while working, **and
somewhere around thirteen she finds out that she likes it.**

**AND SHE DOES NOT EXPERIENCE IT AS HER OWN APPETITE. She experiences it
as service.**

That is the same corruption `portraits.md` already names, escalated:
*the first thing she ever gives up for him is the one conviction she
arrived with — and she does not experience it as a loss.* **Here she gives
up a moral limit and experiences it as usefulness.**

> **She learned to enjoy hurting people by routing it through somebody
> who does not.**

**AND HE CANNOT SEE IT.** Of course he cannot. **He files the effect and
never the cause** (`portraits.md`, and it is the same machinery as her
beauty):

> *Diversion held eleven minutes. Two down, one walking. Cost: nothing.
> Do not send her for anything where the object is to be quiet.*
>
> **He writes her sadism into a mission log without once knowing that is
> what he is describing**, exactly as he writes her beauty into a security
> log. **It should be the coldest paragraph in the chapter.**

**THE COST, and it is hers alone:** at some point she works out that **she
is only able to be this because he does not look.** Nobody tells her. She
never says it. **It is the one thing in her life she does not narrate.**

### 4.4 What makes it work — three instruments, all already in the files

1. **THE EIGHTH COUNT.** *Late; not at the set, at the going; stop on
   purpose* (`combat.md` §11b). **A swordswoman built entirely on
   timing** — her father's actual bequest, and the reason she took
   Isaki's trick in one afternoon: *it's not where you put the effort in,
   it's where you take it OUT.*
   > **Tadayoshi's craft is placement. Kimimaro's is execution. Hers is
   > TIMING**, and she is the only one of the three who improves by
   > understanding rather than repetition.
2. **UZUMAKI VOLUME.** Not a power — **fuel.** `combat.md` §11b: *a
   thimble at ten and a cup by twenty.* **She can go very hard for a
   short time and the window widens every year.** An Uzumaki with a
   thimble is a person standing on a well with a teaspoon; the arc is the
   rope getting longer. **And nobody else alive could run ninety a day on
   a dying boy for three years.**
3. **DIAGNOSIS, USED OFFENSIVELY.** §11b step 2 — *hand on, what's
   different, where does it hurt, and do those two agree; they disagree
   about half the time.* **On a field that is: which leg is taking
   weight, what is being protected, what is a feint and what is a
   limitation, and which of two men is finished and does not know it.**
   > **She does not use it to support somebody. She uses it to pick.**
   > And **it is exactly the organ Tadayoshi lacks** — *he can read a kata
   > in forty seconds and cannot see the most obvious fact about the
   > person standing next to him.*

**RULED OUT: fūinjutsu.** The classic Uzumaki widening, refused, in the
book's own grammar: **the capability exists and the transmission chain is
broken.** Her clan was hunted to nothing, there is nobody alive to teach
her, **and her father could hide the blood and could not pass anything
on.** *A loss, not a gift* — beside Kongō's mark, the household's degraded
output, and a boy who has to build a reason from nothing.

### 4.5 THE PRICE — and it comes out of the medical hands

**Both of the palm's stated limits become weaknesses the moment she turns
them on herself:**

1. ***You cannot tell during*** (`combat.md` §11b). **The one person in
   this book who can diagnose anybody cannot diagnose herself while her
   hands are on.** She does not know how badly she is hurt until forty
   seconds after she stops, **and she does not stop.**
2. ***It buys hours, not cures.*** **So every fight she walks out of is
   borrowed**, and it comes back, and it comes back on a delay she does
   not track because tracking it would mean admitting the loan.
   > **Her body accumulates on credit.** That is the glass cannon's honest
   > price and it is derived entirely from machinery already in the file.

**And the third: she cannot be told to stop.** Not by him — a stop order
is an instruction about what she is for. **Kimimaro can be told to stop
and cannot start. She cannot be stopped and does not need starting.
Between them they are one functional soldier and Tadayoshi is a third
thing entirely.**

## 6. THE THREE PAIRS

### 6.1 Uzuki + Kimimaro — THE UNIT THAT ACTUALLY WORKS

> **This is the important entry in this file.**

**He is a devastating fighter with no tactical sense and no capacity to
act unbidden. She has tactical sense and constitutionally cannot stop
telling people things.**

> **She tells him what to do. He does it exactly. It works from the first
> day and neither of them ever gives it a name.**

**And she is not being kind, or clever, or generous.** `portraits.md`:
*Hinata's love is the thing she cannot say. Uzuki's is the thing she
cannot stop saying.* **The trait that makes her exhausting is the trait
that makes him operable**, and Tadayoshi — who finds it hardest to be
near — has the one disposition in the world that cannot supply it.

**The pairing is complete in both directions:**

| | She gives him | He gives her |
|---|---|---|
| **On a field** | **an instruction, out loud, immediately** — the one input he requires and nobody else issues | **the close-range monster she does not have to be**, which is the only thing that has ever kept her alive past a bad decision |
| **Off it** | **a purpose, informally, daily, without either of them noticing it is happening** | **a body to practise on, forever** — which is what takes her from a thimble to a cup |
| **The cost** | **she is building her craft on his decline** and knows it | **he watches her have a reason and cannot work out how she got one** — *and it is not jealousy in any register the book will name* |

> **She reads him off a stopwatch** (`portraits.md`) — weekly, in a book,
> flattening. **And she cannot fix him**, and the whole of her arc is
> finding that out slowly enough to be unbearable.

**THE THING THAT MAKES IT A TRAGEDY:**

> **The two people Tadayoshi cannot use are, between themselves, a
> working soldier.**
>
> **He goes off and does the job alone because he believes he has to, and
> behind him a girl he has assessed as next to a non-combatant is running
> the best fighter in the book, competently, out loud, for two years.**
>
> **Nobody tells him. There would be no way to.**

### 6.2 Kimimaro + Tadayoshi — the only person with standing

- **The only other person alive who has been made valuable by an
  institution.** *His clan caged him; Tadayoshi's brother sold him.* Same
  story, two mechanisms.
- **So he can say things nobody else has standing to say**, and he says
  almost nothing, **so when he does it lands like a dropped plate.**
- **He agrees with the doctrine.** *Stand alone* is obviously correct to
  him: every single person who has ever held him has hurt him.
  > **The doctrine's most sincere adherent and its living refutation, and
  > he asks permission to eat.**

### 6.3 Uzuki + Tadayoshi — the one who can read the field is the one who
### will not respect it

- **There is a written household rule with a stated distance in it**
  (`combat.md` §9). His only law, and it is a law about how far away from
  him the people who love him must stand. **He wrote it out of care.**
- **She breaks it. Not from stupidity — from doctrine** (`combat.md` §9).
- **So the only person who could tell him what is actually wrong with the
  man in front of him is the person who cannot be on the field**, and she
  is on it anyway, **and when it goes wrong it is nobody's fault and
  everybody's.**

> **This is the catastrophe, pre-loaded, from the day she arrives.**

## 7. WHY IT NEVER ASSEMBLES AROUND HIM — four reasons, all facts about him

1. **The field has no judgement.** `combat.md` §10b — *a fence does not
   know who walked into it.* **Two children who cannot read a field they
   did not lay are two casualties standing in it.**
2. **The threshold.** He cannot predict its arrival (§9 discipline 3), and
   they are the nearest bodies. **He has a number and anybody standing
   next to him on that date dies too.**
3. **She will go, and she will not come back.** Not incompetence —
   **willingness** (§4.2). He has a partner who will absolutely be in the
   other place and who has decided her own survival is not worth
   spending on. **Sending her solves arity and costs her**, and he has
   done that sum and will not sign it.
4. **He cannot teach, and cannot lead.** Kimimaro needs an instruction he
   cannot issue; Uzuki needs a place to stand that he cannot specify
   without specifying what she is *for*.

> **He is right about the first three and wrong about the fourth**, and
> the book must never refute the first three in order to get at the
> fourth (`combat.md` §6). **The answer to a liability is not distance —
> and distance is the only tool he owns.**

> ## ⚠ AND THE FOURTH IS THE ONE THAT IS ALREADY SOLVED AND HE DOES NOT
> ## KNOW IT.
>
> **Uzuki has been issuing the instructions for two years.** The unit
> functions. **It just functions in the room he has left.**

## 8. WHAT EACH IS ALLOWED TO GAIN, AND WHAT IT COSTS

**Per `combat.md` §12 — no capability without a constraint named beside
it.**

| | Gains | Costs, named |
|---|---|---|
| **Kimimaro** | **his body back, in four months.** Then range, volume, and everything canon says the bloodline does | **every article is a piece of himself and he is dying**, so the better he gets the faster it goes — **and he does not know, and would not stop if he did** |
| **Uzuki** | **capacity** — thimble → cup over ten years, which is fuel and window. **Timing, which sharpens on its own. Diagnosis, which sharpens with every body she touches.** And she gets very good with the sword | **she cannot tell how hurt she is until forty seconds after she stops, and she does not stop.** Everything she survives is **borrowed and comes back**. And the ceiling on the healing is real at every level of the arc: **a cup is more hours. It is never a cure.** |
| **Tadayoshi** | **everything the rebellion has** (§10b) | **the four costs of §10b, all of which rise with him** |

**And the shared rule, which is the book's spine:**

> **Nobody in this book gets better at the thing they actually need.**
> He needs to be in two places. She needs to cure somebody. He needs to
> be told what he is for. **All three improve enormously and none of them
> move an inch on that.**

## 9. WHERE IT PAYS

- **Wave.** He is on a bridge over water — **ground he cannot break** —
  with two people on it he made himself responsible for. `combat.md`
  §10b: *the problem is not Zabuza's strength; it is that there are
  things to protect and he is one object.* **The unit that would solve it
  is standing right there.**
- **The taking-stock, after Gatō.** He looks at Zabuza and Haku — **a man
  who could teach and a child who was taught** — and reads it correctly
  and draws the wrong conclusion. *He can point at holes in a form. What
  he cannot do is teach what is best for them, only for himself.*
- **The village decision.** Not fear. **Arithmetic** (`combat.md` §10):
  *he is not making a judgement about the children, he is reading a graph
  about himself.* **And they say no.** And *that's a shame, I'm going.*
- **The scene where he finds out.** At some point — and it should be
  late, and off-hand, and not a revelation scene — **he watches them
  work.** She calls it and he goes and it is clean and it took four
  words. **And Tadayoshi has to stand there holding the fact that the
  unit exists and he is not in it**, and files it, and does nothing,
  because the only tool he owns is distance.
- **And the refutation, which is the end of the arc:** the reason he
  breaks his own rule at last is **Uzuki telling him Kimimaro is dying** —
  **her diagnosis, turned on the one person she cannot fix, delivered to
  the one person who cannot hear it**, and she cannot not say it.
  > **The organ he is missing arrives in the house on the day she does,
  > and it takes three years and a dying boy for him to be made to use
  > it.**
