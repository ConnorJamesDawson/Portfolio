# Beyond the door — the Konoha book, and after

> **Shape, not plan.** Written to `method-delta.md` §2c: **every forecast
> gets its interval before it gets its beat.** Three columns — *what must
> be true at T, what is true now, what happens in between* — and the third
> is the book.

> **⚠ NUMBERING.** The repo calls the current work `book2/` because book
> one was Sazare's. **The author numbers the Tadayoshi sequence from one**,
> so *"book 1"* in a ruling means **the current book**, ending at Wave, and
> *"book 2"* means **the Konoha book below.** Files stay as they are.

**And the split is institutional, not emotional.** Nobody leaves. **The
village simply files them in three different places**, and it is the first
time in their lives that anything has been decided about them by somebody
who meant well.

> **Which is the joke, and nobody in the book says it.** *Tadayoshi spent
> three years keeping people at a stated distance to protect them. The
> village does it in a week, on a form, for scheduling reasons.*

## 2. THE THREE POSTINGS — forecast, then the interval

### 2.1 UZUKI → THE HOSPITAL

| | |
|---|---|
| **Must be true at T** | **AUTHOR: she works in the hospital. An aspiring healer, and the medic corps takes all volunteers.** |
| **True now** | **thirteen. No papers, no village, no training and no theory.** Superb diagnosis off six years of reps on one patient; **no supervision ever** (`combat.md` §11b, §11c) |
| **In between** | **⚠ PROPOSED, and it is a scene** |

**She has to be assessed, and the assessment goes two ways at once.**

- **She reads a body better than people twice her age**, because *ninety a
  day on a boy who never gets better* is more clinical hours than the
  corps can give anybody, **and she does it by hand and cannot say why.**
- **And she does not know a single word of the vocabulary.** No anatomy,
  no names, no theory — **the holes a teacher would have caught, all of
  them, at once, in front of people whose job is catching them.**

> **So she half-passes spectacularly and half-fails embarrassingly**, and
> both halves are §11b's curve arriving exactly on schedule. **The corps
> takes her because the half they cannot teach is the half she has.**

**AND SHE FINALLY GETS SUPERVISION**, which is the thing she has never
had in her life — *not in medicine, not in sealing, not in anything.*
**Somebody stands over her and tells her she is wrong**, and it is the
single largest change to her since she was ten, **and she has no idea how
to be corrected by anybody but him.**

### 2.2 KIMIMARO → THE WARD

| | |
|---|---|
| **Must be true at T** | **AUTHOR: confined to the hospital for research into his disease. He will be cured. It puts him out of action for a while.** |
| **True now** | **dying, on a curve nobody has plotted.** Three years in a cell, three on a road, and the illness started before either |
| **In between** | **⚠ PROPOSED** |

> ## ⚠ THE CURE LOOKS EXACTLY LIKE THE CAGE, AND THAT IS THE POINT
>
> **A boy whose whole life has been rooms is put in a room, for his own
> good, by people who mean it** — **and he says yes immediately, because
> he was told to**, and nobody in the building understands what they have
> just done or that he would have said yes to anything.

- **He is stock again**, and this time the inspection is a kindness, **and
  he cannot tell the difference and does not try.** `act-1.md` §5's
  machine, running a third time, benignly.
- **And an institution owns his day.** *He has never once decided what to
  do with an hour.* **The one thing Konoha will not give him is the thing
  he actually needs**, which is somebody to say what he is for — **and a
  hospital is very good at schedules and has no opinion on that at all.**
- **⚠ PROPOSED — what the research costs him.** *It needs samples, and
  his samples are bone.* **Giving pieces of himself to people who ask is
  the one transaction he has always understood**, and here it is finally
  for his own benefit and it feels identical. **Needs ruling.**

> ### AND THE CURE IS THE REFUTATION OF TADAYOSHI'S ENTIRE CREED
>
> **Tsunade's diagnosis comes true** (`structure.md` §5): ***he needs a
> village, not a medic.***
>
> **Uzuki gave him three years of hands, every day, and could not reach
> it. The place Tadayoshi despises cures him.** *Needing a village's
> protection makes people complacent, and complacent people rot* — **said
> at ten, disproved at fourteen, on the one person he would have paid
> anything for.**
>
> **And he will not change his mind.** *He will file it.* **Which is what
> makes the eyes, two books later, a decision he had the evidence to
> avoid.**

### 2.3 TADAYOSHI → THE MISSION ROSTER

| | |
|---|---|
| **Must be true at T** | **AUTHOR: thrown at the missions, because it is what he is good at** — and he visits Kimimaro where he can |
| **True now** | **a foreign asset with an amnesty, a threshold, and a file three countries thick** |
| **In between** | **⚠ PROPOSED** |

- **Konoha uses him and watches him**, and both are correct, **and he
  would do the same.**
- **Missions are the one thing that has never required the organ he has
  not got.** *Go there. Do that. Come back.* **He is, for the first time
  in his life, in a role that fits him exactly**, and it is the loneliest
  he has ever been.
- **⚠ AND THE VILLAGE SOLVES ARITY FOR HIM** (`combat.md` §10b) — teams,
  cover, relief, somebody else holding the other place. **He experiences
  it as being used rather than as being helped**, and he is not entirely
  wrong, **and he cannot tell which part of it is which.**

> **The village does to him, voluntarily and on a form, what he did to
> himself by policy for three years** — puts distance between him and the
> people who love him. **And because it looks like his own rule, he does
> not resist it.**

## 3. UZUKI IS THE CENTRE, AND IT IS STRUCTURAL RATHER THAN THEMATIC

**She is the only one with a reason to be in every room.** *The ward is her
work. The ward is also Kimimaro. And Tadayoshi comes back to where she is,
because she is where the house is.*

> **Nobody made her the centre. She is simply the only character who is in
> the building with everybody**, and that is what a protagonist is.

**And it is the first time she has ever had somewhere to be that is not
him.** *Her arc has been: attach out of necessity, feed on him, narrow
around him* (`team.md` §10). **A job is the first thing she has ever had
that he did not hand her**, and the book has to decide whether that widens
her or whether she routes it through him too.

> **⚠ It routes.** *She is good at medicine and she is better at it
> because being good at medicine is useful to him*, and §12's machinery is
> already built for exactly this. **The job does not save her. It gives
> her reach.**

## 4. ⚠ THE MANIPULATION — AUTHOR, and it is the book's real subject

> **AUTHOR: she leans on the Uzumaki name Kushina left, and uses the thing
> that saved her life in book one — manipulation.**

**Everything in `team.md` §10 was survival. Here it becomes method.**

### 4.1 HINATA, and why her

**AUTHOR: Hinata takes one look at Uzuki and admires her** — *a girl of ten
put onto the street who survived* — **and Uzuki's surplus is precisely
Hinata's gap: conviction in love, said out loud.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hinata's defining trait** (`portraits.md`) | **her love is the thing she cannot say** |
| **Uzuki's** | **hers is the thing she cannot stop saying** |

> **So Uzuki has the exact medicine for Hinata's exact disease, and the
> dose is poison**, and she does not know that because it is the dose she
> took.

### 4.2 THE TURN — when admiration becomes acquisition

**⚠ PROPOSED, and it needs a specific moment with a specific reason**,
because *she starts the indoctrination* has to begin somewhere:

> **The trigger is that Hinata can see.** **The Byakugan reads a field and
> reads people** — ***which is the organ Tadayoshi has not got***
> (`character.md`), **stated as a bloodline and standing in a corridor.**
>
> **Uzuki routes everything through his usefulness** (`team.md` §12), so
> she does not need a plan. **She needs one afternoon of watching a shy
> girl describe a room, and the sum does itself.**

**AND IT RHYMES FORWARD, AND THE RHYME IS FREE:**

> **This book: she tries to acquire a girl whose eyes see what he cannot.**
>
> **The book after: she cuts a dead boy's eyes out and puts them in his
> head** (`structure.md` §7b).
>
> **Same instinct. Two magnitudes. And nobody, including her, ever
> connects them.**

### 4.3 HOW IT RUNS — and she never lies, which is worse

**`portraits.md`: she never lies to him. Not as a virtue — as an
incapacity.** **She does not lie to Hinata either, and she does not need
to.**

**It starts small and every single step is true:**

1. **Admiration, given freely and meant.** *Uzuki genuinely likes her.*
   **That never stops being true and it is the load-bearing part.**
2. **A true question.** ***"Have you told him?"*** — asked kindly, and
   accurately, and it lands like a dropped plate.
3. **The escalation, still true.** ***If you have never said it, is it
   real?*** *A person who has organised her entire life around saying it
   is asking in good faith, out of her own experience, and it is the most
   destabilising thing anybody has ever said to Hinata.*
4. **AUTHOR: *"You'd make a great sister-wife!"*** — **said brightly,
   sincerely, as a compliment**, and it is the first moment Hinata
   understands that this has been going somewhere.

> ## ⚠ AND IT IS THE MONASTERY, RUN BY THE CHILD WHO SURVIVED IT
>
> **Uzuki was priced twice in one room by a man who valued her for what
> somebody else would pay** (`portraits.md`, Enkai). **She is now doing
> the arithmetic on a girl in a corridor, for what Tadayoshi would get out
> of her.**
>
> **She does not notice. She would be appalled if it were put to her.**
> *And it would have to be put to her by somebody, out loud, and the book
> should make the reader wait a long time for that.*
>
> **Nakadō closed the violence loop. This closes the pricing one.**

### 4.4 AND SHE TELLS HIM EVERYTHING, IMMEDIATELY, AND HE FILES IT

**`team.md` §12's machinery, unchanged:** *he writes her sadism into a
mission log without once knowing that is what he is doing.* **Here he
does it to a recruitment.** *Hyūga girl, useful, working on it* — **entered
under a date, in a book, by a man who cannot price anything.**

### 4.5 THE OPEN QUESTIONS — and they decide what kind of book it is

1. **⚠ DOES IT WORK?** *AUTHOR said **starts** the indoctrination.*
   **Whether Hinata bends, resists, or bends and then comes back is the
   spine of the whole thread and nobody has ruled it.**
2. **⚠ NARUTO.** *Canon's endgame is Hinata and Naruto.* **Peeling her is
   a canon-shatter with a bill** — and it wants pricing in
   `falsified.md`'s strains table **before** it is written, not after.
3. **⚠ WHO CATCHES HER.** **Somebody has to see what Uzuki is doing and
   name it** — and it cannot be Tadayoshi, who files effects and never
   causes. *Kimimaro has standing (`team.md` §6.2) and says almost
   nothing, which is why it lands when he does.*
