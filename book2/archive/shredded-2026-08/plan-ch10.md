## 1. THE ARGUMENT — he does not break. He specifies.

**AUTHOR, session one:** *when he is shoved into the world to make money
during the rebellion he has no creed, he takes any contracts he can to
survive, **until he sees he's made another him** — a family murdered
because the merchant wanted the house, a little boy coming back from a
relative's to find them dead.*

> **The recognition is the whole event and it must not be stated.**
> *Month 4: five people killed in a morning and he was not there for any
> of it.* **Month 9: a family killed in a morning and a boy who was not
> there for any of it.** *Same shape, and the book must let the reader
> put them together before he does — and he does, and it takes him nine
> days.*

### And the creed is not a moral awakening. It is an entry.

**He has answered every problem of his life with a specification.** *The
frame at eight. The seals at ten. The doctrine in month 10.* **So when he
is handed a thing his procedures cannot price, he does not weep and he
does not change.** He writes a line.

> ***Stay your hand from the blood of the innocent.*** **AUTHOR, verbatim,
> unchanged, and it is what he writes.**

**And the tension the chapter is built on:** *it is the only line in that
book with no number in it, no date beside it, no closing condition, and
nothing to test it against.* **He knows that while he is writing it.**

### ⚠ THE THING THAT MAKES IT WORK, AND IT IS THREE MONTHS AWAY

**`spec.md`: *a creed a man cannot keep in a state where he is not
choosing.*** **So it is not a guarantee, and the book must plant that
here** — *he writes it and can see, on the page, that it does not cover
the one state that matters.*

> **And then it holds at the monastery. Once. And he cannot explain why.**
> `ch12.md`. *That single demonstration is the whole reason he trusts a
> rule he already knew was unenforceable, for three years, past every
> piece of evidence.*

## 2. TADAYOSHI — the contract, and what it made

### 2.1 The job, taken clean (scene 1, 9/20–22)

**He is not tricked and he does not cut a corner.** *This is mandatory. If
there is a mistake in the contract the chapter becomes a story about a
mistake.*

- **The docket is ordinary and the fee is good.** *A merchant named — see
  `world.md` — wants a family off a piece of ground.* **The brief is not
  a killing.** It is pressure: be seen, be known to have been seen, make
  the ground untenable.
- **He costs it, plans it, and does it well.** *Two days, four visits,
  nothing touched.* **He is professionally pleased with it** and enters it
  as a clean job with a good margin.
- **⚠ AND HE ASKS NO QUESTIONS ABOUT THE GROUND**, because asking is not
  in the brief and he has learned in the eighth month what asking costs.

### 2.2 The gap (scene 3, 9/23–29)

**Six days of nothing, and that is where the chapter lives.** *He does two
other small things, gets paid, and the reader waits.*

- **The pleased entry stays in the book, unamended**, and the reader
  carries it.

### 2.3 What the merchant did with it (scene 5, 9/30)

**He finds out sideways and not from a client**, because a man who is
told is a man being managed and this has to arrive as information.

- **The family is dead.** *Not by him and not by his brief* — **the
  merchant sent somebody else four days after the ground went untenable,
  and the second man was cheaper and did it the fast way.**
- **⚠ AND TADAYOSHI'S WORK IS WHY THE SECOND MAN WORKED.** *They were
  frightened, they were watched, they had stopped going out, and a family
  that has stopped going out is a family in one room.* **He made the
  room.**
- **He goes and looks**, which nobody asked him to do and which is the
  first thing in the chapter he does for no reason he can enter.

### 2.4 ⚠ THE BOY ON THE STEP (scene 6, 9/30 — the chapter's hinge)

**AUTHOR: a little boy coming back from a relative's to find them dead.**

- **He is about seven.** *He has a bag. He has been away four days and
  nobody has told him.* **He is sitting on the step because the door is
  open and he has been inside once.**
- **HARD RULE: Tadayoshi does not speak to him and the boy does not
  speak.** *Nothing is exchanged.* **The whole event is a boy on a step
  and a boy in a lane, forty feet apart, for about eleven minutes.**
- **AND THE BOY LOOKS AT HIM.** *Once. Not with accusation — with the
  blank attention of a child looking at the only other person in a
  street.* **He does not know what Tadayoshi is.** *That is the worst
  available version and it is the one to write.*
- **⚠ AND THE RECOGNITION DOES NOT ARRIVE HERE.** *He walks away and it
  takes nine days.* **The scene must end with him having felt nothing he
  can name.**

### 2.5 The nine days (scene 8, month 10, 9/30–10/1)

**He tries to price it, four ways, and every one of them works.**

| He tries | And it comes out |
|---|---|
| **Was the job wrong?** | **No.** The brief was pressure and he applied pressure |
| **Was he lied to?** | **No.** Nobody said the family would be safe and he did not ask |
| **Could he have refused?** | **Yes — and he would have starved**, and he has the arithmetic for that from the seventh month |
| **Is it his?** | ***He cannot get an answer and the procedure returns nothing*** |

> **Every instrument he owns says he is fine, and he is not fine, and the
> instruments are not broken.** *That is the crisis and it is entirely
> procedural, which is why it lands on this boy and not on a different
> one.*

### 2.6 ⚠ THE ENTRY (scene 10 — THE LAST SCENE OF THE CHAPTER)

**Month 10, the 1st. He writes it.**

- **He does not decide to become a better person.** *He arrives at the
  only place his method can go, which is that the specification is
  missing a term.*
- **The working, shown, because he always shows the working:**
  > *A brief says what to do. It does not say who it is done to.*
  >
  > *So I have been pricing the action and not the object, and the object
  > is the part that comes back.*
- **AND THEN THE LINE, and it is not in his register at all:**
  > ***Stay your hand from the blood of the innocent.***
- **⚠ AND HE SEES WHAT IS WRONG WITH IT WHILE HE IS WRITING IT.**
  > **No number. No date. No closing condition. No way to test it.** *And
  > it does not say who is innocent — he does.*
  >
  > **And, ruled off, in the same hand, the thing that makes the whole
  > book:** ***and it does not cover the minutes I am not in.***
- **He writes it anyway.** *Close on the smallest true image: the line, in
  a book of numbers, with nothing in the column beside it.*

## 6. THE FIVE HARD PROBLEMS

**1. He must be guilty and not culpable.** *If he did anything wrong the
chapter is about a mistake and the creed is a correction.* **He did the
job as briefed, well, and the job was the cause.**

**2. The boy must not accuse.** *One look, no words, no knowledge.* **A
boy who blamed him would let him argue.**

**3. The recognition must be late and must not be stated.** *Nine days,
and the book never says **another him**.* **The reader has it from month
4 and he arrives after them.**

**4. ⚠ THE CREED MUST ARRIVE AS A PROCEDURE AND READ AS A SURRENDER.**
*He gets there by finding a missing term in a specification.* **And what
comes out is a sentence with no number in it, which is the first thing he
has ever written that he cannot check.**

**5. And its defect goes in on the same page.** *`spec.md`: a creed a man
cannot keep in a state where he is not choosing.* **He writes the hole in
under the line, in his own hand, in month 9** — **so that when it holds at
the monastery in month 12 the reader knows exactly how little it should
have been able to do.**
