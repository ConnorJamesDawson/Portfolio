# Chapter 9, Scene 5 — She Went North

*Act 1, month 9, the seventeenth. The Aku bank, two days east of Ōtoma.
Tadayoshi Ruri, age ten. POV: Tadayoshi. ~2,170 words.*

---

He had eleven seconds and he used all of them.

That was the whole of the difference and he knew it afterwards: eleven
seconds, because he had come up onto the Aku bank at about four in the
afternoon and had done the thing he now did without deciding to, which was
to look at what was under him before he looked at anything else.

*Gravel over a cut face. Old road spoil, about two feet of it, then
shale.*

*Good ground.*

He had been on it about forty minutes when a bird went up out of the
scrub on the west side, at the wrong distance and in the wrong direction
for a fox.

*Eleven seconds.*

Ram, Boar, Ox, Snake — and again — and again, and the first three stands
were in before she came out of the scrub, and they were where he wanted
them and not where he was.

---

*Left.*

That was the first thing he had about her and he had it before he had
anything else, because it was the thing his eye did.

*The blade's in the left and the left foot's forward and she came out of
that scrub on the left side of the gap, which is what you do if you want
your working hand inboard.*

*So she is left-handed and it is not a habit, it is how she was taught,
because the feet are wrong for a person who has swapped over.*

*Twenty-eight. Thirty. Not more than that.*

*No mask.*

He did not know what that meant. He put it down whole, the way he put down
things he could not lift, and made the fourth stand while he was doing it.

---

The first minute went entirely his way.

That was not modesty and it was not confidence; it was what happened. She
came at him twice off the west side and both times she went into ground he
had already made, and both times she had to give up the line and go round,
and going round put her further from him than she had been when she
started.

*Good.*

*She is doing what everybody does. She is looking at the field.*

*Which is correct and it is what I would do and it is why the field is
there, because a person looking at the field is a person not looking at
anything else, and the whole of my trade is that I get to choose what the
other person is looking at.*

He laid to seaward — there was no sea; he thought the word anyway, the way
he had thought it since the fifth month, because a bed is a bed — and he
laid behind, and he left the north end open, because an open end is an
offer and an offer is a thing you can predict.

She did not take it.

*She did not take it.*

*Everybody takes it. Four of them took it in a riverbed.*

---

The tin went low at about a minute forty and he did the thing he had been
taught on the twentieth of the eighth month.

He went down on both knees on the bank with his hands flat and did six
seals slowly and correctly and held for two seconds, and about a square
yard of the gravel in front of him came apart with a long low crack, and
he filled the tin out of it in four handfuls and was up again.

It took him eleven seconds altogether and he had practised it about forty
times and it was clean.

And she stopped moving.

*She's stopped.*

He had about a second and a half to be pleased about that before he
understood what had happened, and understanding it was the worst thing
that happened to him that month.

*She is not looking at me.*

*She is looking at the ground I have just opened.*

*She has been at this four minutes and she has stopped, in the middle of
it, in the open, at thirty yards, to look at a hole in a bank.*

---

Then she came in on the second minute and she came in differently.

He did not understand it at first. She stopped going round the stands and
started going *along* them — close, on the outside edge, where the points
were thin and a person can be hurt and can also be quick — and she did not
once look down.

*She is not avoiding the field. She is using it as a wall.*

*She is putting my own work on her right side so that I have to come round
it to reach her, and she is doing that because her blade is on the left
and mine is —*

He got it at about a minute fifty and it went through him cold.

*She is looking at my hands.*

---

She went for them three times.

The first one was at about two minutes and it was not really an attempt;
it was a question, and it came at him low and across and it made him take
the right hand out of the shape it was in and put it somewhere else, and he
lost a stand doing it and did not mind, and she went back out.

*That was to see what I do.*

The second one was at two forty and it was better.

She came in on the same line and then did not, and the thing that came at
his hands came from a direction his eye had not been in, and he got the
hand back and the seal did not finish and about a third of what he was
making came out as gravel and fell on his own boots.

*Second time and she has already moved where it comes from.*

The third one was at about three ten and it very nearly worked, and
afterwards, for two years, he could not remember what she had actually
done.

---

*She has had it in ninety seconds.*

He was giving ground now, which he had not done at any point in the fifth
month or the eighth, and he was giving it in the direction he had chosen
to give it in, which was the whole of what he had left.

*Everybody who fights me twice goes for the hands. Aoyagi said that when I
was eight. He said it about a boy in a yard.*

*She has done it in one.*

*Nobody has told her. There is not a sheet in this country with that on
it, because there has not been a second time with anybody, because nobody
has had a second time.*

*She watched me for four minutes and she worked out the thing it takes
people two fights to learn, and she did it while I was winning.*

---

He went down at about three fifty and he went down on his own ground.

That was the part he made himself write properly afterwards, because there
was a version of it in which he had been put down and there was a version
in which he had slipped, and the true one was neither: he had been moved
onto four feet of the gravel he had opened at a minute forty, and gravel
that has just come out of a bank is not gravel that has been sitting in a
road for eleven years, and it went out from under him, and he put his
right hand down flat to stop himself going all the way over.

Which was correct. That is what you do.

And she stepped on it.

---

She put her heel on the back of his right hand and put her weight through
it, once, and it was not a stamp and there was nothing in it that was
angry, and he felt two things go under it that were not the same as each
other.

Then she took her foot off.

And then she did not do anything else.

---

He was on the gravel on his side with his right hand in front of him and
she was standing about four feet away with a blade in her left hand and a
boy of ten on the ground, and the whole of the field was behind her,
because she had come through the thin edge of it and it was all on the
wrong side.

*Now.*

*Do it now. This is the whole of the thing, this is what four minutes was
for, do it.*

She looked at him for about two seconds.

Then she went.

---

She went at a run and she went north, which was not the way she had come
in and was not the shortest way off that bank, and he got himself up onto
his knees with his left hand and watched her go and could not do one single
thing about it, and he was not going to be able to do anything about it
for six weeks.

She stopped once, at about forty yards, at the top of the cut.

She did not look back at him.

She looked at the square yard of gravel where the bank had come apart, and
she looked at it for about three seconds, and then she went over the top
and was gone.

---

He knelt on the Aku bank for a while with his right hand held in his left
and did not get up.

*Right.*

*Do it in order. Do not do the other thing yet.*

*The hand.*

He looked at it. It was already going and there was a bad shape across the
back of it that had not been there four minutes ago, and when he tried the
first seal the fingers went about a third of the way and stopped, and it
did a thing he had not felt before and did not want to feel again.

*Two of them. Maybe three.*

*Six weeks. That is what Meno said about Akagane's when the press went
over. Six weeks and then another six before it is any good.*

*I cannot seal.*

He sat back on his heels.

*I cannot seal for six weeks.*

*That is the whole of it. That is everything I can do. There is nothing
underneath it — there is no second thing I am good at, there is a boy of
ten with a knife, and I have spent five months finding out that a boy of
ten with a knife is nothing at all.*

---

He made himself do the other thing then, because it was worse and because
leaving it was how you got it wrong.

*She had me.*

*She had me on the ground at four feet with her blade in the hand it lives
in and she had four minutes of knowing exactly what I do, and she was not
tired and she was not hurt anywhere that mattered, and she looked at me
for two seconds and she went north.*

*So it was not that she could not.*

He turned it over three times and it came out the same way each time and
he did not like any of them.

*One. She was sent to look and not to take, and she has done what she was
sent to do, and the thing she was sent for is on the top of that bank in a
hole.*

*Two. She costed the last four feet and it came out too dear, which it
would have done, because I was on my own ground and I had a hand and a
knife and she would have had to come to me.*

*Three.*

*She had enough.*

He knelt on the gravel with his hand in his lap.

*She had enough, and she knew she had enough at about three minutes, and
everything after that was her collecting the rest of it, and stepping on
my hand was not her hurting me, it was her making sure she could walk away
slowly.*

*And then she stopped at forty yards and looked at the ground.*

**Not at me. At the ground.**

*Twenty-eight days.*

*Hōki said in about a year somebody would write down where I stand, and
it is the seventeenth of the ninth month, and it is twenty-eight days, and
she has done it standing up in the open in the middle of leaving.*

---

He got himself down off the Aku bank on the east side, which took about
forty minutes because he could not use a hand, and he did not recall any
of the stands, because recall is close and kneeling and slow and he had
not got the hands for it.

So he left eleven stands of two-course points standing on a road bank two
days east of Ōtoma, in the open, with a square yard of freshly opened
gravel four feet from the middle of them.

He knew he was doing it while he did it.
