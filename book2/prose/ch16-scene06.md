# Chapter 16, Scene 6 — Whatever's Small Enough

*Act 2, year 1, month 4, the sixth to the eighth. The water track and the
road above Hane. Nozaki Uzuki, age eleven. POV: Uzuki. ~1,900 words.*

---

She had not listened to a word of it.

That is not quite true and she was careful about it afterwards, because she
was not going to let herself have a better version than the one that
happened. She heard all of it. She could have said it back. What she means
is that the words were not the thing she was doing, and she had known that
inside about fifteen seconds, because there was a man in front of her doing
something with his hands.

---

She got four things off him and she had three of them before he had
finished about the drover.

**One.** *He is not mending that trap.*

*He is cutting withies out and putting withies in and he has done eleven of
them and not one of them has gone in against the ribs, and if you do not
tie it in against the ribs the whole side goes when you lift it. My
grandmother did those. I have held one open while she did it.*

*So the trap is a thing to be doing.*

**Two.** *He said **it's not goods**.*

She went over that one three times on the shingle and four more on the
track and never got any further with it, and it stayed with her for
eleven years.

*He said it in the middle. Not at the start where you say what a thing is
and not at the end where you say the important part. He said it in the
middle of the near bank and the willows, and then he said **anyway**, and
went straight back to the willows.*

*Nobody says **anyway** unless they have gone somewhere.*

**Three.** *He did not ask us one question.*

*Not one. Eleven minutes and he did not ask a boy of eleven with a mule
whether he had done this before.*

*And I have watched about forty men give somebody a job and every single
one of them asked something, and half of them did not want the answer,
they wanted to hear the voice it came in.*

*He did not want to hear anything from us.*

**And four**, and the fourth one is the one she actually had, and it did
not come out of any of the other three.

*He does not want to have said this to us.*

*And he said it.*

*And he is still sitting there.*

---

She turned that one over for two days and it did not improve and it did not
go away.

*A man who wants to be somewhere else does not stay for five extra minutes
on a trap he cannot mend.*

*A man who is ashamed of a thing leaves as soon as it is out of his mouth.
I have watched that. Kuriya did it. My mother did it. It takes about four
seconds and then they are doing something on the other side of the room.*

*This one stayed.*

She could not get it, and she made herself stop at about the fourth mile on
the seventh, and put it down properly rather than carry it half-done.

*So: there is a thing he did not say.*

*I have not got what it is and I am not going to get it, and that is the
whole of the finding.*

*And a finding that is only **there is one** is worth about as much as
somebody telling you a house has a room in it.*

---

Then the other part, and she came at it sideways because she came at it
sideways for two days.

*I have got a job.*

She would not let herself have that at the fire on the sixth. She had said
it out loud once, which was the whole of what she was going to allow, and
he had said *it is*, and she had put a stick on the fire and shut up.

On the seventh, walking, about four feet behind him and on the left, she
let herself have it.

*Somebody said my name.*

*He did not say my name. He said **you**, and he turned round, which he had
not done in eleven minutes, and he said it to me and not to him, and then
when Tadayoshi answered for me he said it again and he still did not say it
to Tadayoshi.*

She walked.

*And it is not a favour and it is not being looked after and it is not
somebody being decent. It is a job, and there is a reason I am the one
doing it, and the reason is that nobody looks at me.*

*Which is a thing about me that has been true my whole life and has never
once been worth anything.*

She put her hand up and touched the brim of the hat, which she had been
wearing in the evenings since the second month for no reason he had asked
about.

*And now it is worth something.*

---

She prepared.

Nobody had told her to and nobody had told her how and she did it anyway,
for two days, in her head, because that is what she does with everything
and because she had nothing to write on.

*Whatever's small enough.*

*Small enough for what. For me. So it is: what can I pick up and carry
forty yards at a walk.*

She had the number by the middle of the seventh, and she got it by
carrying the pack and then carrying the pack with the water in it and
then carrying that and a rolled blanket, and she did it on a bit of road
where he was ahead of her and did not see.

*Eleven kan and I can walk with it. Fourteen and I can walk with it badly
and everybody watches a person walking badly.*

*So: eleven.*

*And it has got to be carried in front, because a thing in front is a girl
holding a thing and a thing on your back is a girl who has been given
something, and those are two different pictures.*

She had never in her life thought about what a picture of her looked like
from outside. She found she could do it. She was not pleased about that
and did not examine why.

*And I am not going to run.*

*Nobody runs at a ford. If you run you are the only thing running, and I
have watched a yard full of people and the one who is running is the one
you look at.*

*So I walk, and I walk the way somebody walks who is allowed to be there,
and I have never been allowed to be anywhere.*

*I will have to make it up.*

---

There is one more thing on the seventh and it is about him and she did not
raise it.

*He said the thing about the pack.*

*The thing about the pack is true. If I am fetching, the pack is on the
ground and somebody is with it, and that is a real problem and I would
have said it myself.*

*And it is not what he was doing.*

She walked about eleven paces with that.

*He was doing the other thing and it came out as the pack, and he knew it
was coming out as the pack, and I watched him hear himself say it.*

*He has never done that. He says the true thing and it is always the thing
he means, and this is the first time in a hundred and eleven days that he
has said a true thing that was not the one.*

She did not know what to do with it.

*So he did not want me to do it.*

*And he did not say why, and he could not, and the man said it twice and he
said right.*

She got to the fourth of the four ways she went round it and stopped there,
because it was the only one that was any use and she did not like it.

*If he had had the words he would have used them, and the man would have
said it twice anyway.*

*So it would not have mattered.*

*And I would have known.*

---

On the eighth she tried to price the job and could not, and it is the first
time her own instrument has ever failed her instead of him.

She had had it since the tenth of the third month and it had held for
twenty-nine days. *You get paid for what the buyer got.* She had taken his
whole working life apart on a floor with it and it had come out clean and
he had not been able to break it.

*So: four thousand. What did they get.*

She stood on a road above Hane with the mule behind her.

*They got a case moved eleven days. Any carrier in the country moves a case
eleven days for fourteen hundred, because I have read sixty-one of them and
none of them was over fifteen.*

*So they paid four for a fourteen-hundred job.*

*So they bought something else. That is not a guess, that is the arithmetic
and the arithmetic has been right every time.*

*And now: what.*

She waited for it.

Nothing came.

She went at it for about an hour and got exactly one step and the step was
a wall.

*I cannot do it, because I do not know what is in the box.*

*And he does not know what is in the box. He was told **dye** by a man
reading a sheet that does not say dye, and I watched him be told, and he
took it, and he has not been back to it since.*

*So the buyer is the only person who knows what he bought, and the whole of
my finding is that you have to look at the buyer, and I cannot get near
him, and neither can the person carrying it.*

She stood in the road.

*Then the finding does not work.*

She made herself go all the way to the bottom of that, because she was not
going to keep a thing out of habit and she had said so on a road in the
second month about a different one.

*No. It works. It is right and it was right about Kadoya and it is right
about this.*

*It works and it cannot be used, because using it needs a thing nobody in
this arrangement is allowed to have.*

She started walking again.

*Which is the second time.*

*It was inapplicable because he does not set the fee, and now it is
inapplicable because nobody tells him what he is carrying, and both of
those are the same sentence with different words in it, and I have not got
the sentence.*

She got about a mile with that and then, at the top of the rise where you
first see the water below Hane, she got it, and she did not say it, and she
carried it for four months.

*They are both about him not being told things.*
