# Chapter 17, Scene 10 — Nobody Is Angry

*Act 2, year 1, month 4, the twenty-fourth. A room behind a rope walk at
Kadono. Tadayoshi Ruri, age eleven. POV: Tadayoshi. ~2,000 words.*

---

He had it all with him and he had had it all with him since the
twenty-second.

The Sarugaya sheet. Both counterparts, the Ochiai one and the Hane one,
folded flat in the inside pocket with the seal reads on the back of each in
his own hand. The dates. The wording of the brief as the man under the
bridge had given it, which he could produce word for word and had gone
through four times on the road.

He had rehearsed it and he does not rehearse things.

*If they say I exceeded the brief, the brief said get the cargo and go and
I got the cargo and went.*

*If they say I should not have taken a person, nobody told me it was a
person and the man who gave me the order said **cargo's in the crate** and
I have got that in a book with the date on it.*

*If they say I should have handed him on, they said **north and wait till
somebody comes** and I went north and I waited and nobody came for
fourteen days.*

*It is airtight. I have been over it eleven times and there is nothing in
it.*

---

**Nobody raised it.**

Not once. Not at any point in twenty-two minutes did any person in that
room suggest that he had done a thing wrong, and by about the ninth minute
he understood that they were not going to, and there was nowhere to put
that, and he sat with the counterparts in his coat and did not take them
out.

---

There were six of them and a table.

A man of about fifty chairing, who was tired and courteous and who called
him *Ruri* twice and did not use his own name at any point. Two who did not
speak. A woman with a ledger who wrote and did not look up. A man he had
never seen who came in at about the fourth minute with two sheets and put
them down and went out again.

And the sixth.

He knew him at four feet. Bad left knee, no pipe in his hand now, and he
had spent the whole of the ninth of the fourth month eight feet from a
crate holding an animal that was already tied.

---

The chairing man went through it and it was flat and it was accurate and it
took about six minutes.

The Hane operation. The consignment. The recovery, per instruction. The
fourteen days north. And Tadayoshi listened to a version of his own last
month said out loud by a stranger and it was correct in every particular
including two he had not expected them to have.

*They have got the pallet.*

*Somebody has been to Ochiai and asked Ide about the pallet and Ide told
them it had been standing since the twenty-fourth, and that is on a sheet
in front of a man in Kadono, and I put it in my own book on the
twenty-ninth of the third month and told nobody.*

*So they are careful.*

He was, for about two seconds and against everything, impressed.

---

**And then the man with the bad knee said his piece and it was eleven words
and it is the only thing anybody said in that room that Tadayoshi could not
have predicted.**

The chairing man had come to the boy, and had said *the Kaguya*, twice, in
the flat way he said everything, and the man with the bad knee put one hand
flat on the table and said:

"I was fourteen at Sasago. There were two of them. Two."

Then he took his hand off the table.

---

Nobody in that room reacted.

That is the thing. Not one of the other five looked at him, or looked away,
or said anything, and the woman with the ledger did not stop writing, and
the chairing man waited about a second and then went on.

*They all knew.*

*Every one of them already knew that about him and they have known it for
years and it is not news in this room, it is only news to me.*

*And he did not say it to persuade anybody. There is nobody in here to
persuade. He said it because I am here and I did not know it and he thought
I should.*

He looked at the man with the bad knee, who was not looking at him, and he
had no idea at all what to do with any of it and put it in the operational
column that night in one line with no mark and it is the only thing from
that room he never worked out.

---

Then they did the arithmetic and it took about four minutes and it was
read off a sheet.

Hunter contacts, four in fifteen months, and the rate.

A team that comes here is a team that is not in Kirigakure — and the man
said that one in the way you say a thing you have said many times and are
about to say the other half of.

And then the other half.

The Kaguya. What it is, what it draws, and how fast, and the number of
people in three provinces, and the number of months of money.

---

**He checked it.**

He could not not. It was arithmetic in front of him and it was about him
and he has never in his life let a number go past unchecked, and he went
after it while the man was still talking and he was still going after it
four minutes later when the man had stopped.

*The four is right. It is four and I have got five in my file, and the
fifth is the left-hander who lived, and they have not got him, so they are
one under and it does not move it.*

*The rate is right.*

*The nine months is not mine and I cannot check it and I have no reason to
think it is wrong.*

*And the last one —*

He stopped there and made himself do it properly, because it was the one he
wanted to break and wanting to break a thing is the fastest way to break
yourself instead.

*The last one is: he cannot keep his own name in his mouth.*

*Is that true.*

He sat in a room in Kadono with a piece of paper he had not read in front
of a man he did not know.

*I have known that boy for fifteen days and I have asked him four questions
and got four complete answers and one of them was about a shirt.*

*And she found it on the sixteenth and told me on the twenty-third and I
said **that's a real thing** in under six seconds because it was.*

He put his hands on his knees.

> ## *It is true.*
>
> *I knew it before I came in this room and I made a plan about it on a
> road on Tuesday, and the plan was to keep him out of the room.*

---

**And that is where the thing broke, and it did not break with a noise.**

*They are not wrong.*

*I have been in here twenty minutes waiting for the part where somebody
says a thing I can answer and there is not going to be one, because nobody
in this room has said a single thing that is not true.*

*The sum is right. I have checked the parts I can check and they are right,
and the parts I cannot check are the kind I would take off a man who had
been right about the others.*

*And it comes out that keeping me costs more than I make.*

He sat very still for about four seconds.

*Which is a sentence I have said about other people. I said it about a mule
in the second month. I have got a page for it.*

---

The chairing man read the last line off the sheet and it was one line.

> *Not to be retained. Pay to the ninth and clear. No prejudice, no notice
> to the boards, no follow-up.*

And then he said, "That's not mine, that came down this morning," which was
true and which was the only defensive thing anybody said in that room, and
he said it to be accurate and not to be excused.

---

**Tadayoshi asked one question.**

He has thought about the fact that he only asked one. He had eleven
available. He had the counterparts in his coat and a fifteen-month record
and two documented refusals and a man in the room who had been eight feet
from a crate, and he asked one, and it was the only one that was going to
be any use tomorrow.

"Is he ours or is he mine?"

The chairing man looked at the sheet, and then at the second sheet, and
then said:

"He isn't anybody's. He's not on a sheet. He was never on a sheet."

"Right," said Tadayoshi.

And that was the whole of it.

---

They paid him to the ninth, which was four hundred, counted out on the
table by the woman with the ledger, and he counted it again himself because
you always do, and it was right.

Nobody shook his hand. Nobody in that trade ever had.

The man with the bad knee got up when he got up and went out the other door
and did not look at him and Tadayoshi has never been able to decide whether
that was a discourtesy.

He was in the passage at about twenty-five past ten, and the passage had a
bench down one side of it with three people sitting on it waiting to go in,
and he went past them and out into the yard and the sun was very bright.

---

She was four paces behind him and had not said anything since the room.

The boy was against the wall by the gate with his hands in front of him,
where they had put him, and he was looking straight at the two of them
coming across the yard, and he did not ask anything, and he did not stand
up straighter, and he did not do anything at all.

*He knows.*

*I have not said one word and he knows, and I do not know how.*

Tadayoshi stopped in front of him.

"We're done here," he said. "You're coming with us."

And the boy said:

"Yes."
