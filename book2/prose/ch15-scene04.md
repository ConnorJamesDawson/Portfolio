# Chapter 15, Scene 4 — Everything He Has Ever Done

*Act 2, year 1, month 3, the tenth. Ōtoma. Nozaki Uzuki, age eleven. POV:
Uzuki. ~2,000 words.*

---

He got the frame out on the tenth and it took him forty minutes and she
watched every one of them.

He did not say anything about the four days. He came in in the morning and
moved the tin and lifted two of the boards and got down and brought it up
and put it on the floor and said, "Right," and undid it, and the whole of
the apology was that he did it before he ate.

She noticed that and did not say anything about it either.

---

There were more than she had expected.

That is the first thing and it is worth putting down properly, because she
had a picture of it and the picture was wrong, and she had built the
picture off four dockets she had seen and a boy who talked about work the
way other people talk about weather.

She had thought: a dozen.

What came out of the frame was a stack about two fingers thick, tied in
four bundles with the tie going round the short way, and every one of them
flat, and none of them creased at the corner.

"How many is that?"

"Sixty-one freight," he said. "Nine of the other. Two I didn't take."

"You keep the ones you didn't take?"

He looked at her as if she had asked why he kept his boots.

"They're the same information."

---

She sat down on the floor with the stack in front of her and he went and
sat six feet off with the Sarugaya sheet, and that was the arrangement for
the whole day and neither of them proposed it.

She started at the bottom, which was the oldest, and she went up.

And she did it wrong on purpose, or what he would have called wrong, and
she knew inside about four sheets that she was doing it wrong and did not
stop, because it was working.

*He reads the job.*

*I am not going to read the job. I have got sixty-one jobs and I have not
been on any of them and reading them is going to tell me what a road is.*

*I am going to read the money.*

---

She had no paper.

She did it in her head the way she did everything now, in a line, oldest
first, and she said each one twice before she put it down because that was
the rule she had made under a bridge at Ōgi and it had never once let her
down.

By the middle of the afternoon she had it and it went like this.

*Freight: nine hundred, nine hundred, twelve hundred, nine hundred, eight
hundred, fourteen hundred. And forty more between eight and fifteen
hundred and not one of them over.*

*And the nine.*

She went at the nine differently, because the nine were different, and she
had known they were different before she read one of them, because he kept
them in their own bundle with the tie done twice.

*Three six. Two nine. Four one. Fourteen thousand. Two two. Four eight.
Nine hundred. And two he did not take.*

She sat with her back against the boards and looked at the ceiling.

*So there are two kinds of work and one of them pays about a thousand and
one of them pays about three and a half, except once when it paid
fourteen, and once when it paid nine hundred, and he has told me about
the fourteen and he has never once told me about the nine hundred.*

---

Then she started asking him things, and this is the part that mattered and
she did not know it was the part that mattered until about the fifth
question.

She asked him because the sheets did not have it. The sheets had a place
and a person and a number and a date, and she wanted the thing that was
not on them, and the only copy of it in the world was six feet away with a
road sheet on his knee.

"The shutter-maker."

"Sakuta."

"Who paid for it?"

"Man called Kadoya. Timber, above Ōtoma. He was funding the other side and
Sakuta was passing names."

"What was he like?"

He did not look up.

"About fifty-five. Big room. Kept a man behind him."

"No — what was he *like*."

Two seconds.

"He laughed," he said.

She stopped with her hand on the stack.

"When?"

"When I told him the arithmetic didn't work. I said the reassurance he
wanted was worth more than the thing he'd bought and he'd bought the
cheaper one." He turned the road sheet over. "He laughed. Once. Not
unkindly. Then he asked me three more that weren't questions and told me
he'd have work."

"Did he?"

"Twice."

She sat very still for about four seconds and then asked the next one in
exactly the same voice, which took some doing.

"Did you like him?"

"He was straight with me."

*That is not an answer to that question and he thinks it is.*

She let it go, because she had what she came for and she was not going to
spend it.

---

She did seven more the same way.

Some of them he had almost nothing for. Two of them he had a name and a
door and a night and no man at all, because the man who briefed him had
been a voice through a screen and he had never seen a face.

*Nine out of eleven, he said once. He said he did not know who they were
nine times out of eleven and he said it the way you say the price of
bread.*

And one of them he had a great deal for, and it came out flat and in order
and took about a minute and a half, and she listened to the whole of it
and did not interrupt, and at the end of it she said, "Right," and he went
back to the sheet.

---

She got it at about six in the evening and she got it in three steps and
she can name all three, which is not true of most of the things she got in
her life.

**The first step was his own sentence and she had it word for word.**

He had said it on a wall above the Sakabe wharf in the first month, not to
her, out loud, in the middle of doing something else, and she had put it
straight into the bag because it was the only rule about the work she had
ever heard him state.

> *"You get paid for who the man was."*

*That is the whole of it. That is his line and he has never once looked
sideways at it. The fee is the man. A big man is a big number. Sakuta was
eleven years of passing names and he was three six.*

**The second step was Kadoya, and it took about a minute.**

*Kadoya paid three thousand six hundred and Kadoya laughed.*

*He was pleased. He was so pleased he asked three more questions he did
not need the answers to, and then he went and told somebody about it
afterwards, because a man who does that tells somebody, that is what the
pleasure is for.*

She sat forward.

*And I have watched a pleased man. I have watched two.*

*I watched Kuriya be pleased with my mother over a set of pans and I
worked out a week later on a road what he had been pleased about, and it
was not the pans.*

*And I watched Deguchi be pleased on the sixth of last month, right up
until I stopped him being pleased, and he was pleased at seven and a half
and he was not pleased at five two, and the mule was the same mule the
whole time.*

> ***A man who is pleased has paid less than he would have.***

She put her hands flat on the floor either side of her.

**The third step took four seconds and she did not want it.**

*So if Kadoya was pleased at three six, then three six was under. And if
three six was under, it was not the price of Sakuta.*

*It was what Kadoya was willing to say out loud in a room, and he was
willing to say a low one, because there was nobody in that room who knew
what he had got.*

*And what he had got was eleven years of names stopping. He had got two
more years of funding. He told him so.*

*The fee is not for who the man was.*

*The fee is for what the buyer has got.*

*And he has never once looked at the buyer, and he has told me he did not
know who they were nine times out of eleven, and he told me that the way
you say the price of bread.*

---

She sat on the floor with sixty-one sheets in front of her and did not
move for a while.

*He has been doing this for fifteen months and he is the best there is at
the part in the middle and he has never once looked at either end.*

*And he does not know. It is not that he has decided not to. He has not
got the question. He has got who the man was and he has run the whole of
his working life off it and it is wrong, and it has been wrong since the
ninth month of the year before last, and nobody has ever told him because
the only people who could tell him are the ones it is wrong in favour of.*

She looked across at him.

He had the road sheet on his knee and his thumb halfway down it, and he
was reading it for what she thought was the eleventh time that week, and
he had the face he had, which was no face at all.

---

She did not tell him.

She went at *why not* properly, sitting there, because she was not going
to let herself get away with anything and she never had.

*It is not that I want to keep it.*

*I have got one I keep and it is a different kind of thing and I know
exactly why I keep that one.*

*This one I am going to give him. There is no version where I do not.*

*But he is going to take it apart.*

She turned that over.

*He is going to take it apart in front of me, in order, out loud, and if
there is a hole in it he will find it in about a minute and a half, and
then it will be nothing, and I will have had one thing to say to him about
his own work and it will have been wrong.*

*So I am going to take it apart first.*

*Nine days. I have got until the nineteenth and there is nothing else on.*

*And if it is still standing on the nineteenth then it is standing, and
then I will give it to him, and he can do what he likes with it.*

---

There is one more thing and it happened while she was tying the bundles
back up, and it took her by the throat and she put it down and went and
did the fire.

She had been on the floor with the stack for about nine hours.

*I have not been bored.*

*Not once. Not for a minute of it, and it was sixty-one pieces of paper
about a boy walking to places.*

She got the tie round the short way, the way he had it.

*And that is everything he has ever done.*

*The whole of it. Fifteen months, sixty-one and nine and two he turned
down, and it is on this floor and it is in four bundles and I have read
every single one of them and he handed it to me because it took him forty
minutes and there was nothing in it.*

She put the last bundle on the stack and squared the corners, and she did
not have a word for what she had just had, and she did not go looking for
one.
