# Chapter 17, Scene 3 — He Does Not Have a No

*Act 2, year 1, month 4, the fourteenth to the seventeenth. North of Hane.
Nozaki Uzuki, age eleven. POV: Uzuki. ~1,900 words.*

---

She had eleven days and a boy she could not price and she used every one of
them.

That is not a nice sentence about her and she would not have argued with
it. What she had was a person sitting eight feet from the fire doing
nothing at all for four hours at a stretch, and a finding of her own that
had failed twice and could not be used, and a great deal of time.

*Everybody has a number.*

*That finding died in the second month and I replaced it and the
replacement was better and it does not cover this.*

She sat on the other side of the fire with the hat on her knee.

*The man at the fire had nothing, so there was nothing he wanted from me.
That one I got.*

*Tadayoshi has got everything and does not want anything, and I have had
two months on that and got nowhere.*

*And this one.*

She looked across at him.

*This one has got nothing and wants nothing and I have never in my life
heard of such a thing.*

---

She started on the fourteenth and she started small, because she was not
stupid.

"Are you cold?"

"No."

"You've been sat there four hours."

"Yes."

She waited. He did not add anything, and she had by now had four days of
that and had learned what it was: he had answered the question.

*He answers exactly the thing. Not less and not more.*

*Which is the same as Tadayoshi and it is not the same at all, because
Tadayoshi answers short because he is deciding what to give me.*

*This one is not deciding anything.*

---

She got the whole of it on the fifteenth off one question and it took her
about nine minutes to understand what she had.

She had asked, meaning nothing by it, in the middle of doing the water:

"What do you do?"

And he had said, "I do not know what you mean," which was not a refusal,
and she said, "When somebody wants something done. What do they get you
for."

And then he told her.

He told her for about four minutes, flat, in order, without one pause in
it. What his body does. What it is called, which is a word she had never
heard. What comes off him and where from. That the first was at eleven and
the sixth is through and the seventh has not started. That he has five
pieces in his shirt and their lengths in thumbs, largest first. That he
does not know how long a thumb is in anybody else's unit. That the number
he built for how much is left is dead because it is in bowls, and that he
has been carrying a dead number for four months and knows it.

Then he stopped, because he had finished, and looked at her, waiting for
the next one.

---

She sat with the water bucket in both hands and did not move for about
nine seconds.

*I did not ask for that.*

*I asked what he does. I asked what a person gets him for.*

*And I have got his clan, his hands, five measurements and the fact that he
has worked out roughly when he dies and cannot do the sum any more.*

She put the bucket down.

*And he does not know he gave me anything.*

---

She tested it on the sixteenth, and she is not going to be defended for
that, and she did not defend herself for it either.

She has been over the sixteenth more often than most days in her life. What
she has never been able to decide is whether the person she was on that
morning was a person finding out how a thing works or a person seeing what
she could get, and she has come down on both sides of it at different
ages, and the honest answer is that at eleven those were not two things
yet.

She asked him four questions and she made each one worse than the last on
purpose.

**One.** *"How long were you in the room?"* — and got two years, and the
dimensions in paces, and the drain, and the fact that the door opened
inward.

**Two.** *"Did they hit you?"* — and got: not often; twice in the first
month and not after; and that he had worked out it was because he did not
need it, and that he had not been pleased about that or unpleased about it,
he had put it down as a fact about them.

**Three**, and she knew when she said it that she should not: *"Who brought
you the food?"* — and got four descriptions of hands. Sizes. Which one
came in the morning. Which one had never once said anything. The one that
had held his shoulder for eleven seconds with no lamp in the dark on the
night before the ford.

She stopped there.

She had a fourth ready and it was the worst of the four and she did not ask
it, and the not asking is the only part of the sixteenth she has ever been
able to look at squarely.

---

She sat behind the bank for a while afterwards, which is where she went
when she had something.

*He has not got a no.*

She turned it over and it did not need turning over, it was simply true and
it had four demonstrations under it.

*It is not that he trusts me. He does not know me. He has known me nine
days.*

*It is not that he wants to tell me. He does not want anything, that is the
whole problem with him.*

*Somebody asks and he has got the answer and the answer comes out. There is
nothing in between. There is not a place in him where the deciding
happens.*

She put her hands flat on the grass.

*I have got one of those and it is not the same. I say everything because I
cannot be bothered not to, and I know I am doing it, and about four times
in my life I have not.*

*He has never not.*

---

**And then she had the thing that mattered, and she had it at about six in
the evening on the sixteenth, and she went cold in a way she had not gone
cold since a hall in the twelfth month.**

*Anybody can do that.*

*Not me. Anybody.*

*A man on a road. A woman in a shop. Somebody being kind to him at a door
with nothing in it at all.*

She sat very still.

*He would tell them what he is. He would tell them what his clan did. He
would tell them how long he has got and he would give them the working.*

*And he would not be frightened while he did it and he would not know
afterwards that he had done anything, and if you told him he had he would
say **they asked**, and he would be right, and there would be nowhere to go
from there.*

She got up.

*That is not a thing about him. That is a thing about **us**, and I am the
only person here who has got it.*

---

She did not tell Tadayoshi on the sixteenth or the seventeenth, and she
went at *why not* the way she went at everything, because she was not going
to let herself get away with it.

*Because I got it by doing something I would not do again.*

*And that is a reason not to be proud of it and it is not a reason not to
say it.*

She turned that over.

*So say it.*

And she did not, and the real reason arrived on the seventeenth and it was
smaller and worse than the one she had been expecting.

*Because I have not got a thing to do about it.*

*I have got: he answers everything. That is not a finding, that is a
description. A finding is a thing you can act on, and the only action is
**do not let anybody ask him anything**, and we live on roads, and people
ask.*

She sat with that.

*So I am carrying a true thing with nothing under it.*

*Which is the third one.*

**And she was right, and it stayed a description for eight days, and on the
twenty-fourth of the fourth month it became a finding for about ninety
seconds, and she acted on it, correctly, and it did not save anybody.**

---

There is one more thing on the seventeenth and she did not connect it to
any of the above for a very long time.

She was doing the fire and she stopped and looked at him.

She did that a good deal. She was aware that she did it. What she was not
aware of, and would have denied at eleven and admitted at about nineteen,
was what she was looking for, which was not information.

*He is going to be dead before I am fifteen.*

*He said the number and I was writing it down in my head while he said it
and I have not taken it out since.*

She put a stick on the fire.

*And I have got one thousand four hundred and twelve days, and he has got
fewer, and he told me his the way you tell somebody the price of a thing.*

She did not have anywhere to put that, and it did not go in the bag with
the findings, and she did not know what to call the place it went instead.

*Right.*

*So we had better not waste any.*

**She had no idea what she meant by it and she meant it more than she had
meant anything since a monastery, and it is the first entirely unpriced
sentence she has had about another person in her life.**
