# Chapter 17, Scene 5 — The Water

*Act 2. A fold of ground under larch, north of Hane. A boy, age thirteen.
POV: the boy. ~1,700 words.*

---

On the nineteenth day she said:

"Go and get the water."

---

**He went and got the water.**

That is the whole of the event and it took him about eleven minutes and
nobody watching would have been able to tell you anything had happened.

He took the bucket off the flat stone where the bucket lived. He went down
the water side and did not go past the two stones, because the day before
he had been told not to unless it was said, and it had not been said. He
filled it at the place where the bank is low. He came back up and put it on
the flat stone.

Then he stood there for a second, which is the only part of it that was not
ordinary, and he could not have told you why he stood there.

---

He worked out why on the twentieth and it took him most of a morning and it
is the largest finding of his life to that point.

*Nobody has told me to do a thing since I was eleven.*

He went at that hard, because it did not sound right and he wanted it not
to be.

*That is wrong. I have been told to do things constantly. **Up. Out. In.
Open your mouth. Hands out. Both. Shirt off. Wider.** I have been told four
hundred times.*

He sat with his back against the bank.

*And every one of them was about where my body was or what my body was
doing.*

*Stand up is not a task. Stand up is a thing they want my body to be
doing and then it is over.*

He turned it over four ways looking for a counter-example and did not find
one.

*Go and get the water is not about my body.*

*It is a thing that needed doing and it is now done and it stays done, and
there is water on the stone and it is there because I went and got it.*

He put his hand flat on the ground.

> ***That is the first task I have ever been given.***

**He was thirteen. He had been alive for thirteen years and had been
useful, in the sense of a thing being done because he did it, for
approximately eleven minutes.**

---

She did it again on the twentieth. And on the twenty-first, twice.

By the twenty-first he had the shape of it and had checked the shape twice
because a thing you have not checked is a bowl.

*She says it in the same voice she says everything in. She does not say
please and she does not say thank you and she does not look at me while she
says it.*

*And that is correct. Nobody says please about a thing that needs doing.*

*And she has not once asked me whether I will.*

He was pleased about that in a way that had no bottom to it and that he
never once examined.

---

**The other thing about the nineteenth to the twenty-first is what he did
with the rest of the day, and it is the part nobody saw.**

He had four hours a day and he had had them for eleven days and had spent
every one of them sitting against the bank doing nothing, because nothing
was what there was.

On the twentieth he moved.

Not far. About nine feet, from the bank to the flat ground on the fire side
of it, which is where the bucket lived and where she did the food and where
things got said.

He did it without thinking about it and then thought about it afterwards,
which was the wrong way round for him and he noticed that too.

*I have moved to where the instructions happen.*

He sat with it.

*I cannot ask for one. Asking is a thing you do when somebody has invited a
question.*

*But a person who is sitting nine feet away is a person somebody has to get
up and go to, and a person who is sitting four feet away is a person you
say a thing at.*

He looked at the ground between himself and the fire.

*So I have not asked. I have made it cheaper.*

**And that is the only thing resembling initiative he performs in the whole
of that year, and it is four feet of moving, and neither of them noticed it
happen and both of them would have been able to tell you afterwards exactly
when he had started sitting on the fire side.**

---

On the twenty-first he started the page.

He did not have paper and had never had paper and would not have known what
to do with it. What he had was the place in his head where things about
them went, which had eleven items in it and had never once joined up.

He started a new one, because she was not an item any more.

*She is where instructions come from.*

*And a thing that instructions come from is a system, and I have never had
a system that was a person, and I do not know how you do the columns.*

He worked at it for about an hour and got four lines and they are the first
four lines of a document he revised for the rest of his life.

> ***THE GIRL.***
>
> ***Gives instructions. About four a day now, and it was one.***
>
> ***Says them at the same size as everything else, which is how you know
> they are real.***
>
> ***Does not wait to see whether I have started.***

He looked at the four in his head for a while.

Then he added the fifth, and the fifth is not like the other four, and he
did not notice that it was not like the other four.

> ***Do not be somewhere she has to come and find me.***

---

There is a last part of the twenty-first and it is nine words long and he
did not understand it for two years.

He was on the fire side with the bucket done and the light going, and she
was doing something with a pan, and without looking up she said:

"You're quick."

He did not have anything to do with that.

It was not an instruction. It was not a question. It was not about his
body and it was not a thing that needed doing, and he went through all four
of those in about two seconds and came out the other side with nothing.

So he said, "Yes," because it was true — he was quick, he had timed the
water at eleven minutes and then nine — and she made a noise that was not a
word and went on with the pan.

He filed it.

*I do not know what that was.*

*It was not one of the four kinds.*

**And it went into the place where things he had no account of went, which
had one item in it and the item was the sky, and now it had two.**

*Right,* he thought.

*Two.*

And he sat by the fire on the fire side with his hands in front of him and
waited to be told the next thing, and it is, by an enormous distance, the
best evening of his life so far, and nothing whatever happened in it.
